Where to stay

Choosing where to sleep is two decisions, in this order: the neighbourhood, then the hotel. Our stay guides start every city with the first, what each area is like early and late and how far it is from the trains you will actually use, then name specific hotels at every budget, with the reason we would pick each.

Not sure which area? The Stay Finder ranks a city's areas for your budget and priorities, from these guides, with the reasons shown.

Book early: dates when hotels fill up

  • Niseko: the 2026/27 season opens on 28 November
  • Kyoto’s maples: peak in the last ten days of November
  • Hakuba: Goryu opens on 21 November, Happo-one on 1 December
  • Tokyo’s autumn leaves: ginkgo around 23 November, maples around 28 November
  • Sydney on New Year’s Eve: fireworks at 9pm and midnight on 31 December, with minimum stays
  • The Sapporo Snow Festival: 4 to 11 February 2027
  • Tokyo’s cherry blossom: usually opens around 24 March and peaks around 31 March
  • Kyoto’s cherry blossom: usually opens around 26 March and peaks around 4 April

Hotels in these guides are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and checked as open when the guide was last updated. When we review a stay first-hand, the guide says so at the top. No hotel pays to be included.
