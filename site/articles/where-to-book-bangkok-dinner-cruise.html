Where to Book a Bangkok Dinner Cruise: Klook, KKday or Viator?

By Casey, Gently Yonder editor

KKday has the cheapest Chao Phraya dinner cruises from around 880 baht; Klook carries the headline boats with clearer pier details. But the 17:00 sunset departure saves more than any platform does. Comparison table inside.

About 28 boats work the Chao Phraya, most of them running the same stretch of river twice a night. That means the platform matters less than which boat you pick and which departure you book — but the platform still decides what you pay and how clearly you can see what you’re getting.

The saving that dwarfs the platform difference

Before comparing platforms, compare departures. Most boats run the same route twice a night: an earlier sunset round around 17:00, and the headline dinner round around 19:00. On many ships it is the same vessel and the same buffet, and the earlier round costs roughly 30–50% less.

That is a bigger difference than any gap between Klook, KKday and Viator. Our guide to Bangkok’s river cruises works through the boat tiers and the pier logistics in full.

So the right order is: pick the departure, pick the boat, then compare platforms for that exact sailing.

KKday is where the cheapest entry points sit — listings from around ฿880, and around ฿990 for boats bundling unlimited beer and live music. If the budget is the constraint, start here.

Klook carries the well-known vessels and tends to be clearer about the practical part that trips people up: which pier, and where check-in actually is. Getting to the wrong pier in Bangkok traffic is the classic way to miss a boat, so that clarity is worth something.

Viator lists the most boats overall, with entry prices starting higher at around US$31. We have no affiliate relationship with Viator and are naming it anyway, because a comparison that hides a major option isn’t one.

What to check on the listing, before the price

  1. Which pier? Asiatique, ICONSIAM and River City are well apart. ICONSIAM has a free shuttle boat from Sathorn (Saphan Taksin BTS).
  2. Which departure? The ~17:00 sunset round is usually the same buffet for substantially less.
  3. Covered or open deck? In the May-to-October rainy season an open top deck can become unusable.
  4. How loud is the entertainment? Amplified music is standard on the big buffet boats. Read recent reviews for the words “music” and “karaoke” if you want conversation.
  5. Then compare the same boat on both platforms — the same vessel routinely differs in price between them.

You can check Chao Phraya dinner cruises on KKday, usually the cheapest entry point, and cross-check Klook for the headline boats and clearer pier details. Frequently asked questions

Which platform is cheapest for a Bangkok dinner cruise? KKday generally has the lowest entry prices, with listings from around ฿880 and about ฿990 for boats including unlimited beer and live music. Viator’s listings start higher, from roughly US$31. Prices vary per boat, so compare the specific sailing.

Is Klook or KKday better for Chao Phraya cruises? KKday for price; Klook for the well-known boats and for clearer information about which pier you board from, which is the detail most likely to go wrong in Bangkok traffic.

What is the cheapest way to do a Bangkok dinner cruise? Book the earlier sunset departure, around 17:00, rather than the 19:00 dinner round. On many boats it is the same vessel and the same buffet for roughly 30–50% less, and you still get Wat Arun in golden light.

Where do Bangkok dinner cruises depart from? Mainly Asiatique the Riverfront, ICONSIAM and River City Bangkok. They are well apart from one another, so check your booking and allow for traffic — or reach Sathorn pier by BTS to Saphan Taksin and take the free ICONSIAM shuttle.

Do I need to book a Chao Phraya dinner cruise in advance? Popular boats and weekend sailings fill, especially in high season. Booking a few days ahead is usually enough, and it lets you compare the same vessel across platforms rather than taking whatever is left.
