Three things separate one Tromsø aurora tour from the next, and the headline price is only one of them: how long you can cancel for, how big the vehicle is, and what is included in the price (thermal suit, boots, a hot meal, photos, hotel pickup). On the evening we priced, the same kind of chase cost from about €103 to about €167 a head, and the gap is mostly explained by those three lines.

One thing has to be said first, because it changes what you are buying. The aurora is never guaranteed. Visit Tromsø, the city’s tourism board, says the lights may be visible from late August to early April, but that clear skies and darkness are essential. The KKday listings we read say it themselves: sightings are not guaranteed. What a tour sells is a driver and guide who follow clear sky, plus the terms for when the sky does not cooperate. Those terms are what this page compares.

Which listing is the safe, quick buy

For most readers it is the KKday tour with photos, suits and a local guide. It is the cheapest of the listings we read, it has the longest free-cancellation window (2 days, against 24 hours on the other two), confirmation is instant, and thermal suits and photos are in the price. That combination matters most if a bad forecast makes you nervous about committing, because you can still walk away two days out.

Say where the others win, plainly. Viator is the one that states a vehicle cap (15 travellers) and includes a hot meal and boots, if you want a bigger night out for about €60 more. GetYourGuide has by far the most reviews on the listing we read (7,268), includes portraits and a hotel drop-off, and is the seller to pick if review volume is your test, at €167. What we could not read on any listing is a clear refund term for a night with no aurora, so treat all three as free cancellation up to a point, not as a weather guarantee.

What decides whether you see the lights

  • Season. Visit Tromsø says the lights may be visible from late August to early April. A December date is inside that window.
  • Clear sky and darkness. The same page says clear skies and darkness are essential. This is why the listings talk about driving out of town: a chase tour is a ride to a gap in the cloud, not a trip to a fixed viewing spot.
  • Give yourself more than one night. Nobody can say weeks ahead which evening will be clear. Booking an early night in your stay with a free-cancellation ticket leaves you a second try. The cancellation windows above (24 hours to 2 days) are what let you move it.
  • A long, cold night. The listings we read run from about 6 to 9 hours, and GetYourGuide notes the end time depends on driving distance, weather and aurora activity. Plan the next morning loosely.

Clothing, children and the small print

Thermal suits are in the price on all four listings we read, and Viator adds boots and a headlamp. Still wear layers: the Viator listing asks for wool rather than cotton underlayers, a hat and a thick sweater, and GetYourGuide asks for warm clothing, a hat, gloves and warm shoes.

Age limits differ by listing. GetYourGuide’s minibus chase is not suitable for children under 8; Viator says children must be accompanied by an adult; the KKday listings we read did not give a minimum age, so check the age line on the listing before paying for a family. GetYourGuide’s minibus chase and KKday’s bus tour are listed as not suitable for wheelchair users, and Viator says its tour is not wheelchair accessible; the other KKday listing did not say.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a northern lights sighting guaranteed on a Tromsø tour?

    No. Visit Tromsø says clear skies and darkness are essential, and the KKday listings we read say sightings are not guaranteed. Treat any tour as a better chance, not a promise.

    Which seller is the safest to book?

    Of the listings we read, the KKday tour with photos, suits and a local guide: €102.89 on 12 December 2026, free cancellation up to 2 days ahead, instant confirmation, thermal suits and photos included.

    What is the cheapest northern lights tour from Tromsø?

    When is the aurora season in Tromsø?

    Visit Tromsø says the lights may be visible from late August to early April, but clear skies and darkness are essential.
