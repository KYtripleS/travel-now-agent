New York’s hotel tax

Three taxes and a fee apply to every hotel room in the city:

Charge Rate
New York State and City sales tax 8.875% of the room rate
New York City hotel room occupancy tax 5.875% of the room rate
City occupancy charge, rooms of $40 or more $2 a room a night
New York State hotel unit fee $1.50 a room a night

Together that is 14.75% of the room rate plus $3.50 a room a night. By state rule the hotel must show the taxes separately on your bill, so compare totals, not headline prices.

Getting in from the airports, and around

  • From JFK, the AirTrain runs to Jamaica or Howard Beach stations for $8.75, and from there the subway or the Long Island Rail Road continues into Manhattan.
  • From LaGuardia, the Q70 LaGuardia Link bus is free from Terminals B and C to the Jackson Heights–Roosevelt Avenue and 74 St–Broadway subway hubs.
  • From Newark, the AirTrain to the airport’s rail station costs $8.75 unless you already hold an NJ Transit or Amtrak ticket; the train then runs to Manhattan.
  • In town, a subway or local bus ride is $3; tap a contactless card or phone at the gate.
  • By taxi or app, rides into, out of or within the Congestion Relief Zone, Manhattan at and below 60th Street, carry a per-trip charge: $0.75 in a yellow or green taxi and $1.50 in an app-based car such as Uber or Lyft.

Midtown and Times Square: the first-trip base

Midtown is the New York of Broadway’s theatres, Times Square, Bryant Park and the big department stores, and the Times Sq–42 St station is a hub for many subway lines. It is loud and bright, and that is the point.

Where to stay in Midtown

  • The Knickerbocker, splurge. At 6 Times Square, behind a historic Beaux-Arts façade, with rooms with 12-foot ceilings, the St. Cloud rooftop bar and Charlie Palmer Steak IV. Choose it for luxury on Times Square itself. Watch out: it is on the square, so the crowds and the lights are at the door. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Arlo Midtown, good value. On West 38th Street, a few blocks from Times Square and Broadway, with a restaurant, Altair, and a rooftop bar, ART Midtown, with views over the city.   • Pod Times Square, budget. The hotel offers free walking tours with Streetwise New York Tours. Choose it to keep money for the shows. Watch out: the Full Pod sleeps two but is recommended for one; two people should book a Queen or Bunk Pod. Check rates on Booking.com →

The Financial District: bridges and quiet evenings

The southern tip of Manhattan holds Wall Street, One World Trade Center and the approach to the Brooklyn Bridge. It is busy by day and much quieter at night and at weekends, which suits some travellers better than Midtown.

  • The Beekman, a Thompson Hotel, splurge. A 19th-century building on Nassau Street, close to the Brooklyn Bridge and One World Trade Center, now part of Hyatt’s Thompson brand. Choose it for history and design downtown. Watch out: price; when we checked, two weeknights in November cost more than $2,300. Check rates on Booking.com →

SoHo and Greenwich Village: village streets

Greenwich Village spreads around Washington Square Park, with its arch, and SoHo, to the south, is a shopping district of cast-iron buildings and boutiques. Both are walkable, with subway stations on all sides.

  • Soho Grand Hotel, splurge. On West Broadway in SoHo, with newly renovated rooms, terrace suites and two penthouse lofts. Choose it for SoHo at the door. Watch out: price. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • The Marlton, comfortable. A boutique hotel on West 8th Street, near Washington Square Park, with herringbone wood floors, brass fixtures and marble bathrooms. Choose it for character in the Village. Watch out: the Queen Deluxe rooms measure about 125 to 150 sq ft (12 to 14 m²). Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Washington Square Hotel, good value. A hotel since 1902 on Waverly Place, steps from Washington Square Park, with Art Deco architecture and rooms from a Double to a Deluxe Quad for four and a Penthouse King with views of the park. Choose it for the park at a lower price. Watch out: the hotel itself calls the Double “cozy”. Check rates on Booking.com →

Chelsea: galleries and the High Line

Chelsea, on the west side between the Village and Midtown, has the galleries, Chelsea Market and the High Line, the park built on an elevated freight line.

  • The High Line Hotel, comfortable. On Tenth Avenue at 20th Street, with an Intelligentsia coffee bar and bicycles for guests, and dogs welcome for $100 a stay of up to five nights. Choose it for a quieter base by the High Line. Watch out: the bedding contains feathers; ask for hypoallergenic bedding when you book. Check rates on Booking.com →

The Upper West Side: Central Park and the museums

The Upper West Side runs between Central Park and Riverside Park: residential, with the American Museum of Natural History and Lincoln Center. Midtown is a short subway ride away.

  • Hotel Belleclaire, good value. A historic hotel on Broadway, about half a mile from Central Park and a third of a mile from Riverside Park, by the hotel’s count, near the American Museum of Natural History and Lincoln Center. Choose it for the park and the museums at a lower price. Watch out: the theatres and Times Square are a subway ride away. Check rates on Booking.com →

Brooklyn: the skyline view

Across the East River, Brooklyn gives you the view of Manhattan rather than the view from it. Williamsburg is the neighbourhood of bars and restaurants, DUMBO and Brooklyn Bridge Park sit under the bridges, and Downtown Brooklyn is a transit hub. Manhattan is a subway ride away.

  • The William Vale, comfortable. In Williamsburg, with a private balcony in every room and suite, looking over Brooklyn and the Manhattan skyline, and the Westlight rooftop bar. Choose it for a balcony and the view. Watch out: Manhattan is across the river. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • 1 Hotel Brooklyn Bridge, splurge. In Brooklyn Bridge Park, the 85-acre park on the East River waterfront, with a rooftop pool, Harriet’s Rooftop and a spa. Choose it for the skyline across the water. Watch out: price; when we checked, two weeknights in November cost more than $2,500. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Ace Hotel Brooklyn, good value. In Boerum Hill, on the edge of Downtown Brooklyn, with rooms from the Cozy to skyline rooms and suites with a guitar and a turntable. Choose it for Brooklyn at a fair price. Watch out: the Cozy rooms are, as the name says, the smallest. Check rates on Booking.com →

How to book New York

  • Count the tax. Add 14.75% and $3.50 a night to every room rate: a $300 room costs $347.75.
  • Watch the season. When we checked the same two weeknights, mid-November cost more than February at every hotel here, and at most of them between one and a half and three times as much: Arlo Midtown $872 against $381, Hotel Belleclaire $799 against $244, and The Knickerbocker $1,465 against $769.
  • Check the room size. Some hotels here are small by design: The Marlton’s Queen Deluxe rooms are about 125 to 150 sq ft. If you need space, book the category above the cheapest.
  • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in New York for a first trip?

Midtown, near Times Square: Broadway's theatres are at the door and the Times Sq–42 St station is a hub for many subway lines. Choose SoHo or Greenwich Village for walkable streets, the Upper West Side for Central Park and the museums, and Brooklyn for the view of the Manhattan skyline.

How much is the hotel tax in New York City?

14.75% of the room rate, made up of 8.875% sales tax and the city's 5.875% hotel room occupancy tax, plus $3.50 a room a night: a $2 city charge on rooms of $40 or more and the state's $1.50 hotel unit fee. On a $300 room that is $47.75 a night.

How do you get from JFK to Manhattan?

Take the AirTrain to Jamaica or Howard Beach station, $8.75, then the subway, $3, or the Long Island Rail Road into Manhattan. A taxi or app ride that ends in Manhattan at or below 60th Street also pays a congestion charge: $0.75 in a yellow taxi and $1.50 in an app-based car.

Is there a free bus from LaGuardia Airport?

Yes. The Q70 LaGuardia Link bus is free from Terminals B and C to the Jackson Heights–Roosevelt Avenue and 74 St–Broadway subway stations, where you continue on a $3 subway ride.

When are New York hotels cheapest?

Of the two seasons we compared, February. When we checked the same two weeknights, mid-November cost more than February at every hotel in this guide, and at most of them between one and a half and three times as much.

How much is the subway in New York?

$3 a ride on the subway and local buses in 2026, paid by tapping a contactless card or phone at the gate; express buses cost $7.25.
