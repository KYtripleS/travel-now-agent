Ocean Park’s general admission ticket is not a dated ticket. It is valid for 180 days from the day you buy it, so the date you pick at checkout does not change the price, and a Saturday in December costs the same as a quiet weekday. What differs between sellers is the price, whether the ticket can be cancelled, and how soon the listing lets you book.

For our date, Saturday 12 December 2026, the lowest adult price we saw was HK$494 on KKday, with HK$247 for a child. KKday’s listing is an instant-confirmation e-ticket that you scan at the entrance, and it is the one we would buy for that date. It cannot be cancelled once bought, so if you want a way out, KKday lists a free-cancellation version at the same HK$494 that opens for booking on 27 November.     What each seller’s listing says

    oceanpark.com.hk

    The park’s ticket page lists General Admission as “good for a one-day adventure”, with adult (aged 12 or above) at HK$538, child (aged 3-11) at HK$269 and JoyYou Card holders (aged 60-64) at HK$100. It says ticket validity is 180 days from the date of purchase and that children under 3 are admitted free. The admission fee includes most facilities except skill games and coin-operated games. The park’s FAQ says visitors no longer need to make an online reservation before they visit, apart from the free birthday admission for Hong Kong residents.

    KKday

    Check this on KKday →

    Read 25 November 2026. The Admission Ticket (HK$494 adult, HK$247 child) is marked “Use instantly” and says it includes one 1-day standard admission ticket, adult or child, which you scan at the park entrance. The ticket is valid within 180 days of purchase except on park closure dates. The cancellation policy reads: once the order is completed, it cannot be cancelled, modified or refunded.

    The Admission Ticket (Free Cancellation) is listed at the same HK$494 with “Free cancellation up to 3 day(s) in advance” and “Booking starts: 2026-11-27”. For a 12 December visit, that would mean cancelling by 9 December if you bought it on or after 27 November.

    KKday’s reminders say that Ocean Park may close or adjust facilities without notice and that refunds are not accepted for partial closures. If the Hong Kong Observatory issues a Black Rainstorm Warning or a Tropical Cyclone Warning Signal No. 8 or above and the park closes for the whole day, KKday says it will arrange a refund according to your ticket type. Guests may be asked to show ID at the gate, and the listing says admission ticket terms are subject to the park’s official ticket terms.

    Rules that change the buying decision

    These come from the park’s own pages.

    • Open-date ticket, 180 days. The park says general admission ticket validity is 180 days from the date of purchase. A ticket bought on 25 November would run well past 12 December.
    • No online reservation. The park’s FAQ says visitors are not required to make online reservations before they visit, except for the free birthday admission offered to Hong Kong residents.
    • Ages. Adult is aged 12 or above and child is aged 3 to 11. Children under 3 are admitted free. Any child aged 11 or below must be accompanied by an adult aged 15 or above.
    • ID can be checked. The park reserves the right to verify the age, identity or ticket eligibility of guests, and says admission may be refused to anyone who cannot show valid identification when asked.
    • Ride closures are not refunded. The park says that, as a condition of sale, it will not consider a refund request, partial or full, because a ride or attraction is suspended or closed. Its rules add that there are no refunds or rain checks for inclement weather.
    • Re-entry. If you leave and come back on the same day, ask staff at the turnstile for a wristband, then show the wristband and your admission ticket.
    • Bags. Luggage, bags, coolers and containers over 56cm x 36cm x 23cm are not allowed in, and the park runs bag checks at the main entrance. A baggage rental counter is at the Waterfront.

    Which ticket suits which trip

    • You want the lowest price on a fixed day. KKday’s Admission Ticket was HK$494 adult and HK$247 child. The trade-off is that the order cannot be cancelled once completed.
    • You want a way out. KKday’s Free Cancellation version is the same HK$494 and says you can cancel up to 3 days ahead. Its booking start date is 27 November.
    • You want a listing marked official. GetYourGuide’s listing is marked “Official ticket” and shows HK$538 in Hong Kong dollars once you switch the currency.
    • You are travelling with a child. KKday’s child price was HK$247. GetYourGuide and Tiqets child prices were not read.
    • You want both parks. KKday’s 2-Park Combo is HK$587 and sold to visitors aged 12 or above only.

    Five checks before you pay

    1. Ticket type. Admission, combo and FasTrack are separate products, and FasTrack does not include admission.
    2. Cancellation. KKday’s standard ticket, GetYourGuide’s and Tiqets’ were all non-refundable on the day we read them.
    3. Ages and names. Adult is 12 or over; child is 3 to 11. The combo needs each guest’s name and date of birth.
    4. ID at the gate. Bring a passport or ID card.
    5. Currency. Tiqets charges in US dollars even when it shows Hong Kong dollars, and GetYourGuide’s currency may need switching.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is an Ocean Park Hong Kong ticket on 12 December 2026?

    On 25 November 2026 the lowest adult price we saw was HK$494 on KKday, with HK$247 for a child. GetYourGuide showed HK$538 and Tiqets about HK$536. The park's page lists HK$538 for an adult and HK$269 for a child. Prices change daily.

    Does the date I pick change the price?

    Not for general admission. The ticket is valid for 180 days from purchase, and KKday's listing says the selected date is for system reference only.

    Can I cancel an Ocean Park ticket?

    It depends on the listing. KKday's standard Admission Ticket says it cannot be cancelled once completed, while its Free Cancellation version says free cancellation up to 3 days in advance, with booking starting 27 November. GetYourGuide and Tiqets said non-refundable.

    Do I need to reserve a time or date to enter?

    The park says visitors are not required to make online reservations before they visit, except for the free birthday admission for Hong Kong residents. You show your ticket at the entrance.

    What ages count as child?

    Child is aged 3 to 11 and adult is aged 12 or above. Children under 3 are admitted free, and any child aged 11 or below must be accompanied by an adult aged 15 or above.

    Is Ocean Park open on Saturday 12 December?

    GetYourGuide's listing showed operating hours of 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM for that Saturday. Hours change, so the time on the day you go is the one that counts.
