    What each seller’s listing says

    Hong Kong Disneyland, direct

    The park’s Midweek Getaway Ticket page lists a Wednesday-only general admission ticket “starting from HK$ 535” and a child or senior ticket “starting from HK$ 399”, for purchase through 30 December 2026. You choose the visit date at purchase, and the page says that once the offer ticket is bought “the selected Validity Date may not be changed”. Signing in to a MyDisney HK account adds a free gift valued at HK$100 or more and two HK$50 vouchers, one for shopping and one for dining, each with a HK$500 minimum spend.

    Regular dated tickets are different. The park’s terms say a 1-day dated ticket can have its visit date changed, or be upgraded to a 2-day ticket, within 90 days of purchase and before 12:00 a.m. on the visit date, by signing in to MyDisney HK, with any price difference paid; if the new date is cheaper, nothing is refunded. The park’s own snack-ticket page lists general admission “starting from HK$ 669” and child or senior “starting from HK$ 499”.

The Wednesday-only ticket says it is valid “on the selected Wednesday only”, with applicable dates from 7 October to 30 December 2026 excluding 23 December, a valid photo ID such as a Hong Kong ID card or passport, and a park reservation made automatically for the date you choose. It says the date “cannot be changed once purchased”, and that unused tickets are void and not refundable.

    Rules that change the buying decision

    These come from the park’s own pages.

    • Dated tickets since 11 November 2026. The park’s terms have separate sections for 1-day dated tickets bought on or after 11 November 2026. A 1-day dated ticket is “valid for one guest to visit the Park on the date specified on the ticket only”.
    • Tier calendar. The park’s Ticket Tier Calendar has four tiers. For December it shows 1 to 3, 8 to 10 and 15 to 17 December as Tier 1, 4 to 7, 11 to 14, 18 to 22 and 28 to 30 December as Tier 2, and 23 to 27 and 31 December as Tier 3. The calendar shows operating days and not whether the day is fully reserved.
    • A park reservation goes with the ticket. The park’s notice says guests need a valid park reservation for the visit. The two sellers whose terms we read say it is made automatically for dated tickets.
    • Same person, photo at the gate. Each ticket must be used by the same guest. On entry the park takes a photo, or accepts the identification it requires, and the ticket, handstamp or photo is needed to re-enter. If you would rather not have a photo taken, the park says to ask a Cast Member before entry about an alternative.
    • Ages. Child and senior tickets are for ages 3 to 11 and 60 or over; children under 3 do not need park admission. Guests must be 18 or older to buy online.
    • Christmas season. The park’s Christmas season, “A Disney Christmas”, runs to 3 January 2027, and festive decorations and shows are part of a December visit.

    Which ticket suits which trip

    • You have a fixed Wednesday and want the lowest price. KKday’s Wednesday-only ticket was the lowest we saw. The trade-off is that the date is fixed and the order cannot be cancelled once completed.
    • You want a way out. GetYourGuide’s Wednesday ticket says free cancellation until 15 December, and its “reserve now, pay later” option holds the date without payment today.
    • Your date is not a Wednesday. KKday’s regular dated ticket lets you change the date through the park’s own app or website within 90 days, subject to availability, paying any difference.
    • You have a child or a parent over 60. The child or senior rate was HK$381 on KKday’s Wednesday ticket and HK$482 on its regular ticket on our date, with the other sellers’ child figures not read.

    Five checks before you pay

    1. Same date and same ticket type. A Wednesday-only ticket and a regular dated one are different products with different rules.
    2. Cancellation and date change. KKday said no cancellation; GetYourGuide said free until 15 December for our date; Viator said free until 24 hours before.
    3. Photo ID. KKday’s Wednesday ticket asks for a valid photo ID such as a Hong Kong ID card or passport.
    4. Child and senior ages. Child is 3 to 11 and senior is 60 or over, so check each traveller’s age on the date.
    5. The total in your own currency. GetYourGuide showed US dollars for us, so your card may add its own conversion.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is a Hong Kong Disneyland ticket on 16 December 2026?

    On 19 November 2026, the lowest adult price we saw for Wednesday 16 December was HK$511 on KKday's Wednesday-only ticket, with HK$381 for a child or senior. KKday's regular adult dated ticket was HK$641 (HK$482 child or senior). GetYourGuide showed US$72.53 and Viator HK$844.39. Prices change daily.

    Is there a cheaper ticket on Wednesdays?

    Yes. A Wednesday-only ticket is sold on selected Wednesdays from 7 October to 30 December 2026, except 23 December. The park lists it from HK$535 for an adult and HK$399 for a child or senior. KKday showed HK$511 and HK$381 for 16 December.

    Can I cancel or change the date of a Hong Kong Disneyland ticket?

    It depends on the seller and the ticket. GetYourGuide's Wednesday ticket said free cancellation until 15 December for a 16 December visit, and Viator said free cancellation until 24 hours before. KKday said completed orders cannot be cancelled or refunded, and that a regular dated ticket's date can be changed on the park's website or app within 90 days of purchase, before the visit date, paying any difference. The Wednesday-only ticket's date cannot be changed once bought.

    Do I need ID for a Hong Kong Disneyland ticket?

    The park takes a photo at entry, or accepts the identification it requires, and each ticket must be used by the same guest. KKday's Wednesday ticket asks for a valid photo ID such as a Hong Kong ID card or passport, and says staff may check a passport.

    What ages count as child and senior?

    The park's child and senior ticket is for ages 3 to 11 and 60 or over. Children under 3 do not need park admission, and guests must be 18 or older to buy tickets online.

    Are Hong Kong Disneyland tickets dated now?

    Yes. From 11 November 2026 the park sells 1-day dated tickets: you choose the date when you buy, and the ticket is valid on that date only.
