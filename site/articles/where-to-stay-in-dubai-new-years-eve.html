Dubai’s Burj Khalifa fireworks are free to watch from the public viewing areas, but they are not easy to reach: last year the roads around Downtown began closing at 4pm, and the Dubai Media Office said the closures would widen until 11pm. The hotel bill is different too. A Dubai room carries the Tourism Dirham, AED 7 to 20 a night, and hotels list a 7% municipality fee, a 10% service charge and 5% VAT on top of the rate. That changes how you choose, because where you sleep decides whether you walk to the fireworks or ride the Metro to them.

31 December 2026: what has been published, and what has not

When we checked in November 2026, we found no official plan for 31 December 2026 from the Dubai Media Office, Emaar or the Roads and Transport Authority (RTA). Two hotel groups have started to publish. Atlantis describes a New Year’s Eve 2026 gala that welcomes 2027, and Address Hotels + Resorts says the Burj Khalifa fireworks bring midnight in Dubai as 2027 begins.

Dubai’s organisers call last year’s event “New Year 2026” because it welcomed 2026. To avoid confusion we call it 31 December 2025. Last year Emaar announced its programme and opened ticket sales on 24 November 2025, and the Dubai Media Office published the security plan on 26 December, so the 2026 details may arrive between late November and late December. That is one year’s pattern, not a promise. Below is last year’s plan, labelled as such.

Emaar’s own pages set the rules that matter for a hotel guest. Its public viewing page says road closures begin as early as 4 PM and to plan to arrive before then. Its FAQ says a hotel guest’s badge is valid only for their own hotel, and that the Metro to Dubai Mall will run but may close depending on crowd conditions. Burj Park is the ticketed option: Emaar sold last year’s tickets through its official website, and its terms make them non-refundable and non-transferable, so buy only from Emaar.

Last year the RTA reported 2,836,859 public transport passengers on the night, 13% more than the year before, 1,249,636 of them on the Metro’s Red and Green Lines. Plan for crowds, not for a quiet ride.

Staying in Downtown Dubai: at the foot of the show

Downtown is where the closures are. Last year Emaar’s guidance was to arrive before the roads close, and the roads on the Media Office’s list include Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard, Burj Khalifa Street and Al Mustaqbal Street. A hotel here means you may already be inside when the streets shut, but ask the hotel in writing how guests are let through, because none of the hotels below publishes that.

Armani Hotel Dubai, inside the Burj Khalifa, is not an option this year. Gulf News reported on 14 September 2026 that it closed on 1 April 2026 for a full-scale refurbishment, with a full reopening to guests in March 2027.

    Dubai’s hotel fees in 2026

    The Tourism Dirham is set by Dubai’s Executive Council and charged per occupied room, per night, by the hotel’s classification.

    Hotel category Tourism Dirham per room per night
    Five-star or higher hotel or resort AED 20
    Four-star hotel or resort AED 15
    Three-star hotel or resort AED 10
    Two-star hotel AED 10
    One-star or budget hotel AED 7
    Luxury hotel apartment AED 20
    Deluxe hotel apartment AED 15
    Standard hotel apartment AED 10
    Guest house AED 7

    It is charged for a maximum of 30 consecutive nights, to the main guest in the room, and per room, not per person. The percentages are the larger part. A Dubai decree of 2019 lowered the municipality fee on hotel sales from 10% to 7%, the UAE’s VAT is 5%, and hotels’ own rate terms list both, with a 10% service charge, on each room night. Together that is at least 22% of the room rate: on an AED 800 room, at least AED 176 a night before the Tourism Dirham, by our arithmetic. A couple spending four nights in a four-star hotel at AED 15 a night pays AED 60 in Tourism Dirham on top.

    A 2026 change to watch. In May 2026 Dubai’s government approved a second package of economic incentives that exempts establishments registered with the Department of Economy and Tourism from collecting the Tourism Dirham and the sales fees on hotel rooms. The package’s measures run for three to 12 months, and we could not find a published end date for this one. In November 2026, Millennium Airport Hotel Dubai’s offer terms, valid to 31 December 2026, still list the 7% municipality fee, the 10% service charge and 5% VAT. Ask the hotel what will be on your bill. Hotel taxes around the world compares the fees in other cities.

    How to book New Year’s Eve in Dubai

    • Choose how you will watch first, then the hotel. Free public viewing, Burj Park or a hotel gala each means a different base. Buy a Burj Park ticket only from Emaar’s official website, as the terms say they are non-transferable.
    • Read the cancellation terms and the exemption. Airspace can close at short notice, so choose a rate you can cancel, and ask whether the 2026 fee exemption applies to your stay.
    • Ask about minimum stays and the closures. Atlantis says a minimum length of stay applies and requires a Gala or restaurant experience. Apart from that, none of the hotels here publishes a minimum stay or says how guests get through the closed streets that we found, so ask.
    • Count the fees. Add the Tourism Dirham, AED 7 to AED 20 a room a night, and at least 22% in municipality fee, service charge and VAT.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are the Burj Khalifa fireworks free to watch in 2026?

    Last year they were. Emaar's Downtown Dubai public viewing areas for 31 December 2025 were free on a first-come, first-served basis, with no tickets or badges required. Emaar also sold ticketed Burj Park viewing at AED 997.5 for adults including VAT. We found no official 2026 plan or prices when we checked in November 2026, so confirm both on Emaar's site.

    When will Dubai announce its New Year's Eve 2026 plan?

    We could not find it in November 2026. Last year Emaar announced its programme and opened Burj Park ticket sales on 24 November 2025, and the Dubai Media Office published the security plan on 26 December, so the details may arrive between late November and late December. That is one year's pattern, not a promise.

    What time do the roads close around the Burj Khalifa on New Year's Eve?

    Last year the Dubai Media Office said road closures around event areas would begin at 4pm and gradually expand until 11pm, and Emaar warned they could start as early as 4 PM. The streets named included Al Asayel Street, Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard, Burj Khalifa Street and Al Mustaqbal Street. The 2026 closures had not been published.

    Does the Dubai Metro run all night on New Year's Eve?

    Last year the Metro Red and Green Lines ran from 5am on 31 December to 11:59pm on 1 January, 43 hours, and the Tram from 6am on 31 December to 1am on 2 January. Emaar's FAQ said the Metro to Dubai Mall may close depending on crowd conditions. The 2026 timings had not been published.

    Do Dubai hotels charge a tourist tax?

    Yes. The Tourism Dirham is AED 7 to AED 20 per room per night, set by the hotel's star category, and it is charged for a maximum of 30 consecutive nights. Hotels' own rate terms also list a 7% municipality fee, a 10% service charge and 5% VAT, at least 22% of the room rate. In May 2026 Dubai approved an exemption from collecting the Tourism Dirham and sales fees for registered hotels, so ask the hotel what will be on your bill.

    Do I need to book a gala or a minimum stay at Atlantis for New Year's Eve?

    Atlantis's New Year's Eve 2026 page says guests staying at Atlantis, The Palm or Atlantis The Royal over New Year's Eve must choose the Under The Stars Gala Dinner or a restaurant experience, booked in addition to the room, and that a minimum length of stay applies without stating it. Gala prices for ages 14 and over run from AED 8,500 to AED 14,500.
