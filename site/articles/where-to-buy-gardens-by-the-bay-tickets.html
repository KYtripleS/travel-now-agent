For a Tuesday 15 December visit, the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest ticket cost almost exactly the same on Tiqets, GetYourGuide and Viator: SGD 45.99, SGD 46.00 and SGD 46.00. The difference is the terms. Viator is the one to buy if you want free cancellation, because its listing says you can cancel up to 24 hours before; the Tiqets and GetYourGuide tickets were non-refundable. KKday showed the lowest figure we saw, SGD 28.29, but only for dates up to 7 December. This guide prices the conservatory tickets and the larger combinations for 15 December. What the tickets cover

Gardens by the Bay sells its attractions separately. The ones people buy for are the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest, which the gardens price as one two-conservatory ticket, with Jurassic World: The Experience running inside the Cloud Forest. Floral Fantasy, the OCBC Skyway and the Supertree Observatory are separate attractions with their own admission. The Supertree Grove and the outdoor garden areas are not ticketed, and the listings we read say so.

A seller’s “combo” is its own bundle, so read the list of attractions before paying. The Viator and GetYourGuide Supertree versions both limit entry to before 4:00 pm. The four-attraction ticket adds Floral Fantasy and a shuttle inside the gardens, and GetYourGuide shows shorter hours for it (9:00 am to 8:00 pm for 15 December).

Gardens by the Bay, gardensbythebay.com.sg

The gardens’ FAQ says tickets can be bought on its website, at the ticketing counters at The Canopy or in its mobile app. Its price table lists the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest ticket at SGD 46 for a non-resident adult and SGD 32 for a child, and the four-attraction ticket with the shuttle at SGD 73 and SGD 51. Prices are in Singapore dollars and include GST. On changing a date, the FAQ says only: “please kindly contact us for further assistance.”

The gardens’ own rules that change the buying decision

These come from gardensbythebay.com.sg only.

  • Reseller tickets need no time slot. The FAQ says: “Booking of time slot is no longer required for those who have already purchased their tickets.” It names Klook and travel agencies as sources of tickets and asks you to present the original ticket at the attraction.
  • Resident and non-resident rates differ. The Singapore Resident rate applies to citizens, permanent residents and people living in Singapore, and “Proof of residency may be required”. Tourists should buy the non-resident ticket.
  • Maintenance closures. The Flower Dome page says it will be closed on maintenance days, with the next closure on 12 January 2027. Block-out dates apply to all day passes. Nothing on the page we read showed a closure on 15 December.
  • Hours. The Flower Dome page lists 9:00 am to 9:00 pm daily, last admission 8:30 pm. KKday’s listing gives the Cloud Forest’s last admission as 8:00 pm.
  • Children. The gardens’ child rate is for ages 3 to 12; KKday’s listing says children aged 0 to 2 are free, as does Tiqets.
  • Christmas Wonderland. The gardens’ pages list it from 27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, evenings, at the Supertree Grove and The Meadow. It is a separate event and not part of the conservatory tickets.

Which partner to book

  • The two conservatories with flexibility: Viator. SGD 46 for 15 December and a free-cancellation line on the listing.
  • The conservatories at the lowest dated price: Tiqets, by one cent, with no cancellation.
  • With the Supertree Observatory: Viator. SGD 54 against SGD 60.
  • Four attractions and the shuttle: GetYourGuide. SGD 65.00, with e-tickets emailed within a business day.
  • A date before 8 December: KKday. SGD 28.29 per adult, non-cancellable.

Five checks before you pay

  1. The attractions list. A conservatory ticket does not include Floral Fantasy, the Skyway or the Observatory.
  2. The cancellation line. For 15 December only Viator’s listing offered it.
  3. The delivery. GetYourGuide emails the tickets within one business day; KKday and Tiqets said instant.
  4. Resident or non-resident. Choose the non-resident ticket as a visitor.
  5. The date. KKday’s calendar closed at 7 December on the day we read it.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Gardens by the Bay tickets?

Viator for the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest if you want to be able to cancel: SGD 46.00 per adult for Tuesday 15 December, read on 22 November 2026, with free cancellation up to 24 hours before on its listing. Tiqets was SGD 45.99 and GetYourGuide SGD 46.00, both non-refundable. Klook was not checked.

Which is cheapest?

It depends on the date. For 15 December the conservatory ticket was SGD 45.99 to SGD 46.00 on three sellers. KKday showed SGD 28.29 per adult, but its calendar only offered dates from 22 November to 7 December.

Is the Supertree Observatory included?

Not in the standard conservatory ticket. Viator and GetYourGuide sell a version with entry before 4:00 pm, at SGD 54 and SGD 60 for 15 December.

Do I need to book a time slot with a reseller ticket?

The gardens' FAQ says time-slot booking is no longer required for tickets already bought, and that you present the original ticket to admission staff.

Can I cancel a Gardens by the Bay ticket?

It depends on the seller. Viator's listing says free cancellation up to 24 hours before; Tiqets, GetYourGuide and KKday showed their tickets as non-refundable or not cancellable.

Do children need a ticket?

Children aged 3 to 12 pay the child rate, SGD 32 on Tiqets and the gardens' price table. Tiqets and KKday say children aged 0 to 2 are free.
