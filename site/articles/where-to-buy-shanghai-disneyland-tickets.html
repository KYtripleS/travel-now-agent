    What each seller’s listing says

    Shanghai Disney Resort, direct

    The official one-day ticket is a “One-day Dated Ticket”: you choose the visit date from a calendar and can enter only on that day. Age bands: standard is 12 to 59, child is 3 to 11 and senior is 60 and over, all counted on the day of the visit. Under-3s are admitted free, though a guardian must still bring the child’s original ID.

    Refunds: free before 23:59, seven days before the visit date, which for 16 December means 23:59 on 9 December. From 00:00 six days before until 23:59 the day before, a service fee of RMB 80 per ticket applies. From 00:00 on the visit date there is no refund. This holds only if no official coupon was used and no date change was made.

    Date changes: the official ticket page says it can be exchanged twice within its validity, with the change made at least 48 hours before 00:00 on the original date, subject to availability. It says registered ID details cannot be changed after purchase, and tickets cannot be transferred. The page also says that after a date change no refund requests are accepted unless required by law.

    Entry rules that decide how you buy

    Shanghai Disney Resort’s real-name ticketing policy, from the official site, applies to every ticket however you buy it.

    • Your own ID, one ticket per ID per day. Each guest must use their own valid government ID to book their own ticket, and one ID can buy only one ticket for the same visit date.
    • Foreign visitors use a passport. The policy says other guests “are required to use a valid foreign passport or PRC Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card”. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan residents use the mainland travel permits the policy lists, and mainland adults use their Resident ID Card.
    • At the gate, show the original. The policy says the original physical copy of the ID used at purchase is the only valid credential for park entry. Copies in any form or a digital version are not accepted.
    • ID details cannot be changed once submitted, so type the passport number exactly as printed.
    • Under-3s need ID too: their guardians must also show the child’s original ID, even though admission is free.
    • Child tickets run on age alone: 3 to 11 inclusive on the day of the visit. The official page adds that guests under 16 must be accompanied by someone aged 16 or older.
    • Re-entry: a one-day ticket allows entering and re-entering the same day.

    The official site also states which channels it treats as authorised: its own website, app and WeChat account, its reservation centre, official Jingdong and Fliggy stores and a published list of travel partners. That list names companies by legal name, such as “Shanghai Ctrip International Travel Service Co.,Ltd”, and we could not match every brand name above to an entry, so do not read this article as saying a given seller is on it. The resort also says Disney Premier Access is sold only through four channels: its website, app, WeChat account and Fliggy store, and only after you hold a park ticket.

    Five checks before you pay

    1. Same date, same age band, same days. A 2-day price is not comparable with a 1-day one.
    2. Plain ticket or bundle? Several listings mix in transfers, Premier Access or guides.
    3. Passport number. It must match the passport you will carry on the day, and it cannot be edited afterwards.
    4. The cancellation date. Put it in your diary, in local time.
    5. Compare the total in your own currency, after any fee shown at checkout.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is a Shanghai Disneyland ticket on 16 December 2026?

    The official calendar showed ¥475 for an adult and ¥356 for a child or senior on that date. KKday showed HK$531 for an adult, about ¥451, and HK$387, about ¥329, for a child or senior, on 19 November 2026. Prices change daily and by date.

    Which seller was cheapest?

    KKday, in our table, on every row we could compare. GetYourGuide and Viator showed higher figures for their listings, and Trip.com had no plain ticket. Klook was not checked.

    Can I cancel a Shanghai Disneyland ticket?

    The official ticket is free to refund until 23:59 seven days before the visit, then RMB 80 per ticket until 23:59 the day before. GetYourGuide and Viator said free cancellation until 15 December for a 16 December visit; KKday's ticket cannot be cancelled once the order is complete.

    What ID do I need?

    Each guest books with their own ID. Foreign visitors use a valid passport, and the original physical passport used at purchase must be shown at the entrance; copies and digital versions are not accepted.

    Do children and seniors pay less?

    Yes. Child tickets are for ages 3 to 11 and senior tickets for ages 60 and over, counted on the visit day. Under-3s are free but their guardians must show the child's original ID.

    Does a reseller ticket include Disney Premier Access?

    A plain day ticket does not. The resort says Premier Access is sold only on its website, app, WeChat account and Fliggy store, after you hold a park ticket. GetYourGuide's plain listing excludes it, and bundles that include it are separate products.
