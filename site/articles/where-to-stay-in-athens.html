Hotels around Plaka, with prices

The map shows the hotels and apartments around Plaka that booking sites such as Booking.com and Expedia list, with nightly prices; our own picks follow further down. It comes from our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book through it.

Athens hotel bills carry a charge set by the room, not the guest: the climate crisis resilience fee, from €2 to €15 a night between April and October, depending on the hotel’s stars. It does not change with the number of guests. Four nights in a four-star hotel in July add €40 to the bill. Where you stay decides the rest, in walking time and in how you reach the airport and the ferries.

This guide covers six areas, from Plaka to Piraeus, 12 hotels, the airport links and the fee. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in October 2026. No hotel pays to appear here.

Athens’s climate crisis resilience fee in 2026

Greece’s climate crisis resilience fee replaced the old accommodation tax on 1 January 2024. The rates for hotels are set in article 44 of Law 5177/2025 (Government Gazette, issue A’ 21, 14 February 2025), and the tax authority, AADE, still cites that article in its guidance of January 2026. The fee is charged per room per night, and the amount depends on the hotel’s stars and on the month:

Hotel category April to October November to March
1 and 2 stars €2 €0.50
3 stars €5 €1.50
4 stars €10 €3
5 stars €15 €4

The hotel collects the fee from the guest after the stay and before departure, and issues a separate receipt for it; for a stay that runs across two months, AADE says one receipt is issued for each month. The fee carries no VAT, and it is not charged when the stay itself is free. We found no amendment to these rates after the January 2026 guidance, but Greek rules change, so confirm with the hotel.

Worked example (our arithmetic): four nights in a four-star hotel in July cost 4 × €10 = €40, whether one person or three share the room. The same four nights in January cost 4 × €3 = €12. Among our picks, Hotel Attalos describes itself as a three-star (€5 a night in season), the Herodion as a four-star (€10) and Electra Palace as a five-star (€15).

Getting in from the airport, and around

Athens International Airport, Eleftherios Venizelos (ATH), is east of the city. The fares below are from OASA, the Athens urban transport authority, and we checked them on 19 October 2026.

  • By metro, line 3 runs from the airport to downtown Athens. The airport ticket costs €9 one way, or €16 for a return valid for 30 days. It is valid for 90 minutes on all OASA transport except the airport express buses and the X80 bus, and also on the suburban railway. You can tap a contactless card or phone for the €9 fare instead of buying a ticket first.
  • By bus, the X95 express runs 24 hours to Syntagma and costs €5.50. OASA estimates the journey at about 60 minutes, depending on traffic. Tap a contactless card or phone once, on boarding.
  • By suburban railway, Hellenic Train runs between the airport, Athens Railway Station and Piraeus. The metro airport ticket is valid on it between Piraeus and the airport.
  • By taxi, a Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport decision, reported by the Greek press in mid-September 2026, raised the fixed fare from the airport to central Athens from €40 to €50, and from €55 to €65 between midnight and 5am. We could not open the gazette text itself, so confirm the fare at the rank.
  • In town, a 90-minute ticket is €1.20 and a 24-hour ticket €4.10. The 3-day tourist ticket costs €20 and includes one trip each way to and from the airport by metro or express bus. At these prices, a €16 airport return plus three 24-hour tickets at €4.10 comes to €28.30, against €20 for the tourist ticket (our arithmetic).

Plaka: the first-trip base

Plaka is the old quarter under the Acropolis: narrow streets, small museums and tavernas. OASA puts it 450 m from Acropolis metro station and 650 m from Monastiraki. It is the area to choose if you want to walk to most of the old city and come back to a quiet street.

  • Electra Palace Athens, splurge. A five-star hotel in the heart of Plaka, with a roof garden on the tenth floor that looks at the floodlit Acropolis, and the Herod Atticus theatre ten minutes’ walk away. Syntagma: Parliament and the metro hub

Syntagma Square is where the X95 airport bus arrives, and it has its own metro station. OASA lists the Hellenic Parliament 300 m from the station, the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier 130 m and the Benaki Museum 650 m. It is central and easy to reach from the airport, but it is a city-centre square, not a village quarter.

Where to stay in Syntagma

  • Hotel Grande Bretagne, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Athens, splurge. A landmark open since 1874, with views of the Acropolis, Syntagma Square and Lycabettus; it was restored in 2003 and has a 400 m² Royal Suite. Choose it for the grandest address on the square. Watch out: a five-star pays the €15 fee in season, and you add it to the room rate. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • NJV Athens Plaza, comfortable. At the edge of Syntagma Square, steps from Parliament, with 159 rooms and 21 suites, some with Acropolis views. Choose it for a modern hotel on the square. Watch out: it is a large hotel with meeting spaces for up to 260 guests, so it is a business hotel as much as a leisure one. Check rates on Booking.com →

Monastiraki and Psyrri: rooftops and nightlife

Monastiraki is the square around the metro station. OASA lists the Ancient Agora 220 m from the station, Psyrri 230 m and Plaka 650 m, and A for Athens counts Psyrri, Monastiraki and Plaka among the nightlife districts within five minutes’ walk of the hotel. It suits you if you want evenings out close to your bed.

Where to stay in Monastiraki and Psyrri

  • A for Athens, comfortable. Next to Monastiraki metro station, with a rooftop bar and a terrace with a view of the Acropolis, and rooms from a 25 m² Standard Double to suites of up to 50 m². Choose it for the Acropolis from the roof and the metro at the door. Watch out: the rooftop bar is open until the early morning hours, in a nightlife district. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Attalos, good value. A three-star of 78 rooms on Athinas Street, the lively street between Monastiraki and Omonoia, with a roof garden and bar that look at the Acropolis and Lycabettus. Choose it for the old centre at a lower price. Watch out: the Economy Double is an interior room, with a window onto the inside of the building. Check rates on Booking.com →

Koukaki and Makrygianni: the Acropolis Museum

Koukaki borders the southern section of the Acropolis, Philopappos Hill and Plaka, and Makrygianni is the adjoining area around the Acropolis Museum. OASA lists the museum 130 m from Acropolis metro station. It is the base to choose for the museum and the walk up to the rock.

Where to stay in Koukaki and Makrygianni

  • Herodion Hotel, comfortable. A four-star of 90 guest rooms, 100 m from the Acropolis Museum and 200 m from Acropolis metro station, by the hotel’s count, with a roof garden with two hydromassage bathtubs. Choose it for the Acropolis on your doorstep. Watch out: the rooms measure only 16 to 21 m², and Syntagma Square is a 20-minute walk. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Marble House Pension, budget. A family-run pension on a quiet dead-end street in Koukaki, ten minutes’ walk, by the hotel’s count, from the Acropolis, Plaka and the museum. Choose it for the lowest price near the Acropolis. Watch out: the Budget Double with shared bath is 12 m². Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Athens Backpackers, budget. Greece’s first hostel, open since 2003, with capsule beds, studios and apartments, a roof bar, Sunnies, and, by its own count, three minutes’ walk from the Acropolis and the new museum. Choose it for a bed on the Acropolis side of town on a small budget. Watch out: it is built around the social side, with its own bars, so it suits sociable travellers more than light sleepers. Check rates on Booking.com →

Kolonaki: boutiques and Lycabettus

Kolonaki lies at the foot of Lycabettus Hill, with designer boutiques, independent galleries and café terraces, by the account of St. George Lycabettus. OASA lists the War Museum 250 m from Evangelismos metro station, the Byzantine and Christian Museum 300 m and the Museum of Cycladic Art 550 m. It is the area to choose for museums, shopping and a hill walk, rather than the ancient sites.

Where to stay in Kolonaki

  • St. George Lycabettus Lifestyle Hotel, splurge. Family-owned since 1974, with 154 rooms and 15 suites, the Vertigo rooftop and the forest of Lycabettus just behind it; by the hotel’s count, Syntagma Square is a 15-minute walk. Choose it for the hill at the door and a view across the city. Watch out: the Premium and Signature rooms with a balcony are about 15 to 20 m². Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Periscope, comfortable. A design hotel of 22 rooms in a 1950s concrete building on Haritos Street, with art installations throughout. Choose it for a small hotel with a point of view. Watch out: with only 22 rooms there is little choice if your dates are taken. Check rates on Booking.com →

Piraeus: for the ferries

Piraeus is Athens’s port, a city of its own on the coast. It makes sense only if you sail the next morning: OASA lists the port 450 m from Piraeus metro station. The X96 airport express runs to Piraeus in about 90 minutes, by OASA’s estimate, and the suburban railway also links the airport with the port. From May to October the X80 bus runs between Piraeus, the Acropolis and Syntagma from 07:00 to 21:30 every 30 minutes, and OASA says you can use it only with a daily or tourist ticket.

Where to stay in Piraeus

  • Piraeus Theoxenia Hotel, comfortable. A boutique five-star in the centre of Piraeus, close to the main port, with Guest Rooms of 30 m² and rooms with a pool view. Choose it for an early ferry. Watch out: Piraeus is not the old centre, and as a five-star the hotel adds the €15 fee in season. Check rates on Booking.com →

How to book Athens

  • Count the fee. It is per room, not per person: add €2 to €15 a night from April to October, according to the stars. A four-star room for four nights in July adds €40.
  • Watch the switch. The fee falls on 1 November: for a four-star hotel from €10 to €3 a night, and for a five-star from €15 to €4.
  • Check the room size. Several hotels here are small: the Herodion’s rooms are 16 to 21 m² and St. George Lycabettus’s Premium rooms 15 to 20 m². If you need space, book the category above the cheapest.
  • Choose by what you will do. For the Acropolis and the old streets, pick Plaka or Koukaki; for the airport bus and Parliament, Syntagma; for an early ferry, Piraeus.
  • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and direct booking against online agencies weighs the hotel’s own site.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Athens for a first trip?

Plaka, the old quarter under the Acropolis, which OASA puts 450 m from Acropolis metro station and 650 m from Monastiraki. Choose Syntagma for the X95 airport bus, Koukaki and Makrygianni for the Acropolis Museum, Kolonaki for boutiques and Lycabettus Hill, and Piraeus only if you sail the next morning.

How much is the hotel fee in Athens?

The climate crisis resilience fee is €2 for a 1 or 2-star hotel, €5 for 3 stars, €10 for 4 stars and €15 for 5 stars per room per night from April to October, and €0.50, €1.50, €3 and €4 from November to March. Four nights in a four-star hotel in July add €40.

Is the Greek climate resilience fee charged per person?

No. It is charged per room per night, so the amount does not change with the number of guests. The hotel collects it after the stay and before departure, issues a separate receipt, and adds no VAT; it is not charged when the stay itself is free.

How do you get from Athens airport to the centre?

By metro line 3 to downtown Athens: the airport ticket is €9 one way or €16 for a return valid for 30 days. The X95 express bus runs 24 hours to Syntagma for €5.50 and takes about 60 minutes, by OASA's estimate. The suburban railway also links the airport with Athens Railway Station and Piraeus.

How much is a taxi from Athens airport to the city centre?

A Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport decision, reported by the Greek press in mid-September 2026, raised the fixed fare from the airport to central Athens from €40 to €50, and from €55 to €65 between midnight and 5am. We could not open the gazette text, so confirm the fare at the rank.

Where should you stay in Athens for the ferries?

Piraeus. OASA lists the port 450 m from Piraeus metro station, and the X96 airport express takes about 90 minutes to Piraeus. Piraeus is not the old centre, so stay there only if you sail early; from May to October the X80 bus runs between Piraeus, the Acropolis and Syntagma.
