A luxury night in Kyoto now carries a line on the bill that did not exist in this size a year ago. Since 1 March 2026, Kyoto City’s accommodation tax has had much higher bands for expensive rooms: ¥4,000 per person per night once the room costs ¥50,000 to ¥99,999 per person, and ¥10,000 per person from ¥100,000. Some hotels add it at the desk; at least one builds it into the rate. For a couple on a special night, that is a real number to plan around, so this guide shows it before the hotels.

This guide covers three parts of the city, then nine places to stay: international luxury hotels along the Kamo River and in Higashiyama, ryokan, and a spa retreat in the north. They are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in November 2026. No hotel pays to appear here. We do not quote nightly room rates, because they change by the hour; the figures below are taxes, fares and published facts only.

What makes a luxury bill in Kyoto different

The lodging tax now scales with the room. The official table is below. On a luxury room for two it is the line that most surprises people.

Ryokan bills depend on the meal plan. A ryokan usually sells a night with dinner and breakfast in a set menu, served at a set time. Hotels sell the room alone. Compare like with like.

Three kinds of bath, not one. At a ryokan or an onsen-style hotel, check which you are getting:

  • An in-room bath (often called a rotenburo when it is open to the air) is in your room and only your party uses it.
  • A reservable private bath (kashikiri) is a shared-building bath you book by the slot, then use alone.
  • A shared bath is a large public bath used by many guests at set hours, usually by gender.

A property that says “private bath” may mean any of the first two. We say which only where the property’s own site does, and otherwise we tell you to check the room type before you pay.

The lodging tax: the official bands, and a worked example

Kyoto City’s official travel guide gives the rates from 1 March 2026, per person per night:

Accommodation fee (per person, per night) Tax per person, per night
Under ¥6,000 ¥200
¥6,000 to ¥19,999 ¥400
¥20,000 to ¥49,999 ¥1,000
¥50,000 to ¥99,999 ¥4,000
¥100,000 or more ¥10,000

Worked example (our arithmetic). Take a room at ¥240,000 for the night, two guests. If the room fee is shared equally, each guest’s fee is ¥120,000, which is in the top band: ¥10,000 each, so ¥20,000 for the night, ¥40,000 over two nights. A room at ¥120,000 for two is ¥60,000 each, so ¥4,000 each, ¥8,000 a night, ¥16,000 over two nights. These are examples, not rates; the city page does not define how the fee is split between guests, so the hotel’s own statement decides.

Hotels differ on how they show it. The Ritz-Carlton Kyoto states that the tax is included in the room rate. Hotel Okura Kyoto states that it is not included and is paid separately at the hotel. Before you compare two totals, find out which one you are looking at.

Getting there

From Kansai Airport: the JR Haruka limited express to Kyoto takes about 75 minutes, per the airport’s own page. JR West’s one-way Haruka ticket to Kyoto is ¥2,700 for adults on tickets sold from 1 November 2026. Our guide to Japan eSIMs covers getting online on arrival.

From Tokyo: the Shinkansen takes about two hours twenty minutes, according to Hotel Okura Kyoto’s access page; see Tokyo to Kyoto, compared. From Kyoto Station, the hotels below are a taxi or short subway ride away. Okura says it is about 15 minutes from JR Kyoto Station by car.

    How to book a luxury stay in Kyoto

    1. Count the tax. Use the table above for your room fee per person, then add the tax if the hotel does not include it.
    2. Pick the bath on purpose. If an open-air bath in your room is the point, choose the room type that states it, not the hotel in general. Our booking sites comparison shows where to book once you have chosen.
    3. Watch the season. Cherry blossom and autumn leaves push rates and sell the best rooms out first; see Kyoto in autumn.
    4. Check the room size and the cancellation line before you pay, and for a ryokan the dinner and check-in times.
    5. Book the Kyoto night, then the rest. If you add Tokyo or Osaka, fix the Kyoto night first.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is Kyoto's accommodation tax on a luxury room?

    Since 1 March 2026, per person per night: ¥4,000 when the room fee is ¥50,000 to ¥99,999 and ¥10,000 when it is ¥100,000 or more. On our worked example, a ¥240,000 room for two (¥120,000 each) carries ¥20,000 a night.

    Is the tax included in the room rate?

    It depends on the hotel. The Ritz-Carlton Kyoto states that it is included in the room rate; Hotel Okura Kyoto states that it is not and is paid separately at the hotel.

    Which luxury hotel suits a first trip to Kyoto?

    The Ritz-Carlton Kyoto, with 134 rooms on the Kamo River bank and Gion and Teramachi within walking distance. Four Seasons Hotel Kyoto suits a garden stay in Higashiyama, and Hotel Okura Kyoto a classic hotel at the lower end of this range.

    What is the difference between an in-room bath and a private bath?

    An in-room bath is in your room and used only by your party. A reservable private bath (kashikiri) is a building bath you book by the slot. A shared bath is used by many guests at set hours. Check which one the room type states.

    How do I get from Kansai Airport to a Kyoto hotel?

    The JR Haruka limited express takes about 75 minutes to Kyoto. JR West lists the one-way Haruka ticket to Kyoto at ¥2,700 for adults on tickets sold from 1 November 2026. A taxi or the subway covers the last stretch.

    Should I stay in Arashiyama or Higashiyama?

    Higashiyama puts the old lanes at your door and suits the first night or two. Arashiyama is packed by day and calm at night, and suits one or two nights of quiet rather than a whole stay.
