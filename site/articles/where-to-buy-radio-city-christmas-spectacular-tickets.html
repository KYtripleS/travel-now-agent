What you are actually choosing

The Christmas Spectacular is one 90-minute show at Radio City Music Hall with no intermission, according to the show’s FAQ. Every listing sells admission to that show and nothing else, so the real choices are the show time and the seat category.

On Tuesday 15 December Tiqets showed four times: 11:00 am, 2:00 pm, 5:00 pm and 8:00 pm. The 11:00 am show started at $104 and the other three at $129. For the 11:00 am show it listed six categories: Third Mezzanine $104, First Mezzanine $116, Orchestra Rear $122 and $135, and Orchestra Mid $129 and $214. GetYourGuide said it offered 11 seating categories for the date, with Third Mezzanine at $104 for the 11:00 am show; we did not open the other ten.

The show’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from rockettes.com only.

  • All sales are final. The FAQ says “All ticket sales are final – no refunds or exchanges.” That is why every listing above says the same.
  • Ticketing partner. The FAQ sends ticketing questions to Ticketmaster. GetYourGuide says its tickets are issued through Ticketmaster; Tiqets sends a separate link to retrieve them.
  • Children. “Children under 2 years old do not need their own ticket, but they must sit on a parent or guardian’s lap and cannot occupy their own seat.” Anyone older needs a ticket. We priced one adult.
  • Length. The show runs 90 minutes with no intermission.
  • Ticket limit. The ticket policy limits any single customer to 14 tickets across a season.
  • Resellers. We read the FAQ, the ticket policy and the MSG terms of use and found no wording that refuses or voids tickets bought from other sellers. The listings we compare issue the ticket for a chosen date and time to you.

Which to pick

  • Main pick: GetYourGuide. It matched the cheapest price ($104), shows by far the most reviews, and tells you plainly that the best available seats in your category are assigned after booking.
  • Second pick: Tiqets, for a reader who wants to choose a category from a seat map, or who wants a later show with its price shown. Its 2:00, 5:00 and 8:00 pm shows are listed with a $129 starting price.
  • If you want free cancellation, none of the listings we read offer it, so choose the date with care.

Five checks before you pay

  1. The show time. The price belongs to it: $104 at 11:00 am against $129 later on Tiqets.
  2. The category. From Third Mezzanine at $104 up to $214 for the 11:00 am show on Tiqets.
  3. The cancellation. Every listing we read says no refunds.
  4. How the ticket arrives. A claim link by email, then the Ticketmaster app on GetYourGuide; a separate retrieval link on Tiqets.
  5. Your party size. GetYourGuide caps a transaction at 8 tickets.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Radio City Christmas Spectacular tickets?

GetYourGuide for most readers. On 25 November 2026, for a Tuesday 15 December show, it listed Third Mezzanine at $104 for the 11:00 am show, matching Tiqets, with 4.8 from 1,058 reviews. Klook was not checked.

How much are Christmas Spectacular tickets?

For Tuesday 15 December, Tiqets showed $104 to $214 for the 11:00 am show by seat category, and $129 as the starting price for the 2:00, 5:00 and 8:00 pm shows. GetYourGuide showed $104 for Third Mezzanine at 11:00 am.

Can I get a refund on Christmas Spectacular tickets?

The show's FAQ says "All ticket sales are final – no refunds or exchanges." GetYourGuide and Tiqets both state their tickets are non-refundable, and KKday's listing says "No cancellation".

Can I choose my seat?

You choose a category. On GetYourGuide the best available seats in that category are assigned after booking. Tiqets shows a seat map to pick the category and does not say how seats within it are chosen. KKday's notes say seats are assigned randomly.

Do children need a ticket?

The show's FAQ says children under 2 do not need their own ticket but must sit on a lap. The listings we read do not state their own age rules. We priced adults only.

How long is the show?

90 minutes with no intermission, according to the show's FAQ.
