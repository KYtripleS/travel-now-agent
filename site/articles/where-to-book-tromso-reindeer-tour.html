A Tromsø reindeer tour is not one product, and for Thursday 24 December 2026 the date decides which version you can buy. When we looked, every listing that includes a reindeer sled ride was not on sale for that day, while the feeding-and-Sami-culture versions were: GetYourGuide’s Tromsø: Reindeer Sledding, Feeding & Sami Culture Experience had its feeding option at €139.50 with a 09:00 start, KKday’s Experience the Sami culture at a reindeer camp was €165.00 with 10:00 and 13:00 starts, and Viator’s Reindeer Feeding and Sami Culture Including Lunch from Tromso was €179.93 at 10:00. The other differences sit in a few lines on every listing: whether a sled ride is included, whether a meal is included, where the coach leaves from, and how old a child has to be.

Which listing is the safe, quick buy

For 24 December it is GetYourGuide’s Tromsø: Reindeer Sledding, Feeding & Sami Culture Experience, if you want the lowest price we read: €139.50, 4.7 from 3,188 reviews, free cancellation until 23 December. Two things to know before you pay. On that date it is the feeding option, with no sled ride, and food is not included, only hot drinks. The page also marked the date “Likely to sell out”.

KKday also offers a second start time (13:00) and instant confirmation. Viator’s listing shows 4.7 from 547 reviews and lets you reserve now and pay nothing until 22 December.

If a sled ride matters more than the date, GetYourGuide’s Reindeer Sledding & Feeding with a Sami Guide is the one with the most reviews we saw (4.8 from 7,055), a lunch and a choice of a 10-minute or 25 to 30 minute ride. We read it on 18 December; it showed nothing from 23 to 26 December.

What the sled ride and the feeding give you

  • Sled ride. The listings give minutes, not distances: 10 minutes on the shortest options, 25 to 30 minutes on the longest we read. On the Skansegata listing, sledding is two people per sled with a 125 kg limit per person.
  • Feeding and the tent. Every listing we read describes feeding reindeer and time inside a Sami tent (lavvu, lavvo or gamme in the listings’ own words) with stories. Several describe a joik, a traditional Sami song, performed by the guide.
  • Meals. The Prostneset-departure listings on Viator, KKday and GetYourGuide’s sled-ride version include a hot traditional meal or lunch; GetYourGuide’s Skansegata listing lists hot drinks only and says food is not included.

Children, clothing and the small print

Ages differ and are stated unevenly. Viator and KKday price adults at 13 and over, children 3 to 12 and infants 0 to 2. The two GetYourGuide listings state no minimum age on the pages we read. The Prostneset listings ask for at least two layers of warm clothing, gloves, a hat and good winter boots; KKday adds that warm overalls and snow boots are provided if needed. The Viator listings do not take baby car seats on the coach, and GetYourGuide’s Skansegata listing asks you to bring your own baby seat. The Viator listing is not wheelchair accessible.

Frequently asked questions

Which seller is the safest to book for a reindeer tour in Tromsø on 24 December?

Of the listings we read, GetYourGuide's Tromsø: Reindeer Sledding, Feeding & Sami Culture Experience at €139.50 for one adult on 24 December 2026, 09:00 start, free cancellation until 23 December. On that date it is the feeding option, with no sled ride and no meal.

Can I ride a reindeer sled on 24 December?

Not on the listings we read. GetYourGuide's sledding option and Viator's sled-ride listing were not on sale for the 24th (next date shown 5 January 2027), and KKday's 10-minute sled listing showed as sold out.

What is the cheapest Tromsø reindeer tour on 24 December?

€139.50 on GetYourGuide, feeding only, with hot drinks but no meal. With a meal, KKday's €165.00 was lower than Viator's €179.93 for the same four-hour trip from Prostneset.

Is a meal included?

It depends on the listing. Viator and KKday include a hot traditional meal in the tent; GetYourGuide's Skansegata listing includes hot drinks and says food is not included.

Can children go?

Viator and KKday price children aged 3 to 12 and infants aged 0 to 2 separately; the two GetYourGuide listings state no minimum age on the pages we read.

How late can I cancel?

GetYourGuide's two listings allow free cancellation up to 24 hours ahead; Viator's listing until 10:00 the day before the 24th; KKday's up to 2 days ahead.
