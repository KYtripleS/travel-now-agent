For a loop around the South Island, a car is the practical choice: the lakes, passes and viewpoints that make the trip are spread along roads where buses are thin, and a rental gives you the timetable. For Queenstown town itself you do not need one. Our Queenstown stay guide puts the lakefront, restaurants and tour operators of the town centre within a short walk, and says the Orbus public bus runs from the airport to the town centre.

This guide starts from Queenstown Airport (ZQN), prices one week-long hire from the airport back to the airport, and sets DiscoverCars against two big firms read on their own sites. DiscoverCars is a partner and pays us a commission if you book through it; Apex and GO Rentals pay us nothing, and we say plainly where they are the better buy.

Insurance and the excess

Read this before you pick the cheapest line, because the excess is where the three sellers differ.

Apex shows its excess on each car in the “View includes” panel. For the cars above, the basic rate carried a NZ$4,000 or NZ$5,000 excess, and a separate line offered “$0 Excess (Maximum)”. On the Tucson that option read NZ$1,497.36 for eight days against NZ$1,169.84 for the basic rate, a difference of NZ$327.52 by our arithmetic. Apex’s page also says it charges no airport location fee, booking fee or extra-driver fee.

GO Rentals says its rate includes 24-hour AA roadside assistance and third-party cover, and calls its basic cover “free basic car rental insurance”. Its insurance page says the hirer pays the excess only, “if conditions under the agreement are met”, that the excess “is dependent on the vehicle selected”, and that a bond is taken at pick-up and returned if no damage is caused. It sells two upgrades for excess reduction; the per-day fee was not shown on the page, and GO says the upgrade is a non-refundable premium. A credit card in the main hirer’s name is needed.

DiscoverCars’ Full Coverage is a separate, optional product. Its help page describes it as a reimbursement policy that refunds the deductible (excess) and eligible costs you pay the rental company. The steps it gives: buy it when booking; leave the normal deposit at the desk, because the supplier cannot see DiscoverCars’ cover; pay the supplier for any damage first and keep the paperwork; then file a claim in “My Booking” within 90 days of drop-off. It says Full Coverage does not include third-party liability. Read the page before relying on it; we are quoting its terms, not advising.

Rules of the road that change the booking

  • Licence and time limit. NZTA says you must have a current and valid overseas driver licence or international driving permit. If you last arrived in New Zealand on or after 1 November 2026, you can drive on your overseas car licence for 12 months. If you last arrived before that date, the limit is 18 months for a car licence.
  • Not in English? NZTA says that if your overseas licence is not in English, you must also have an accurate English translation or an international driving permit (IDP). The IDP can be current or expired up to 6 months, for car licences only, and you must carry your current overseas licence with you. NZTA lists approved translators on its site.
  • The left. NZTA’s visitor page opens its list with “We drive on the left-hand side of the road”, and adds that roads are narrow, winding and sometimes steep, that most are two-way with one lane in each direction, and that not all railway crossings have active warnings. Seat belts are required for everyone and phone use while driving is illegal.
  • One-lane bridges. NZTA’s road code says all one-lane bridges are signposted. A round sign with a red border means you give way to vehicles coming towards you; a blue rectangle with a big white arrow means others should give way to you, but you should still slow down and check for vehicles already on the bridge.
  • Milford Road (State Highway 94). An NZTA release dated 24 August 2026 says the road between Te Anau and Milford Sound had closed overnight for snow and was scheduled to close again for avalanche risk, and that the Milford Road Alliance runs avalanche control on it. The release was written for winter and spring; check the current state for your day. On 19 November 2026 NZTA’s Journey Planner listed for State Highway 94 two road works, one area warning and four message signs. The Milford Road site named in our brief, milfordroad.co.nz, would not open in our test browser, so we did not read it.
  • Fatigue and pace. NZTA tells visitors to rest before a long drive after arrival and to take regular breaks. If traffic builds up behind you, it says to find a safe place to pull over and let it pass.

When not to rent

  • A few nights in Queenstown with one or two day trips. The rental sits unused while you are in town. Our Queenstown stay guide covers the Orbus airport bus and says the town centre puts the lakefront and restaurants within a short walk.
  • If you will not drive on the left. The change is the main thing NZTA flags for overseas drivers, and a tour driver removes it.
  • The Milford Sound day. The road is the part of the trip most likely to be disrupted, and a coach or tour operator carries the planning. We have no Milford tour comparison on the site yet, so check the operator’s own cancellation terms.
  • Long one-way trips with a different end point. None of the sites we read showed a one-way fee for our same-airport return, so we have no figure; get the fee in writing before booking.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a car in Queenstown?

For the town itself, no. For a loop through the wider South Island a car is the practical choice, and NZTA says distances can look short on a map but travel times are often longer than you think.

What licence do I need to drive in New Zealand?

NZTA says a current and valid overseas licence or an IDP. If you last arrived on or after 1 November 2026 you can drive on an overseas car licence for 12 months. If it is not in English you need an accurate English translation or an IDP, and you must carry your own licence.

Is DiscoverCars cheaper than renting direct?

On 19 November 2026, for 21 to 29 November from Queenstown Airport, DiscoverCars' cheapest offers were well below Apex and GO Rentals in the small automatic and mid-size SUV rows. In the AWD row we have no DiscoverCars figure. Check the inclusions and your own dates.

What happens at a one-lane bridge?

NZTA says the signs show who gives way: a round red-bordered sign means you give way, and a blue rectangle with a white arrow means you may go first after checking the bridge is clear.

Can the Milford Road close?

Yes. An NZTA release of 24 August 2026 describes overnight snow closures and avalanche-risk closures on State Highway 94. Check NZTA's Journey Planner on the day.

Does DiscoverCars' Full Coverage mean I skip the deposit?

No. Its help page says you still leave the supplier's deposit, pay for damage first and then claim a refund in My Booking within 90 days.
