What each seller’s listing says

All read on 26 November 2026, with 24 December selected.

KKday: Rovaniemi: Reindeer, Huskies & Santa Claus Village. €187.96 an adult and €108.98 a child for the 08:45 session (a 09:00 session also exists but was greyed out for the date). Children are ages 4 to 13 and adults 14 and over; infants 0 to 3 are a third category, and the listing’s own summary says children under 6 are free with no seat. Free cancellation up to 2 days in advance, instant confirmation, guaranteed departure. The listing says transport runs from the city centre of Rovaniemi, with a tour guide, a husky and reindeer farm visit, a 500-metre reindeer sleigh ride, a 500-metre husky sleigh ride, winter clothing, a salmon soup lunch (a vegetarian soup is offered), free time in Santa Claus Village, and a 10-minute mini-snowmobile ride for children under 150 cm. Group size and the exact meeting point are not stated. Not suitable for wheelchair users. The duration is 6 hours on the matching GetYourGuide listing; KKday’s page did not state it.

GetYourGuide: Rovaniemi: Reindeer, Huskies & Santa Claus Village. $206.33 an adult and $119.63 a child for 24 December, 08:45, flagged “likely to sell out”. The listing text is word for word the same as KKday’s: 6 hours, a live English-speaking guide, pickup included, the same includes list, and the same 500 m rides. It shows a 4.6 rating from 2,424 reviews, free cancellation until 23 December (24 hours ahead) and reserve now and pay later. Children are ages 4 to 13 and adults 14 to 99; the booking panel says children 3 and younger join free but must still be added. The Includes list names transportation from the city centre of Rovaniemi and, separately, shows hotel pickup and drop-off as not included, so expect a central meeting point rather than your hotel door. Not suitable for wheelchair users. Group size is not stated.

Which listing is the safe, quick buy

For most families it is the KKday six-hour package: Reindeer, Huskies & Santa Claus Village. On 24 December it was the lowest euro price we read for a full day, it has the longest free-cancellation window of the listings that were open on our date (2 days, against 24 hours elsewhere), confirmation is instant, and winter clothing and a hot lunch are in the price. Children aged 4 to 13 pay €108.98 against the adult €187.96.

Say where the others win. GetYourGuide’s identical six-hour listing carries 2,424 reviews at 4.6 and a pay-later option, if review volume is your test, though it is priced in dollars. GetYourGuide’s four-hour package with Santa is the one to pick if a scheduled meeting with Santa and hotel pickup matter more than the price, but its Christmas Eve figure was close to double the lead-in price. Viator’s combo safari states a 16-traveller cap and a fixed meeting point; read its recent reviews about cancelled husky rides first.

What changes on Christmas Eve

  • Availability. Two Viator listings had no seat on 24 December, and the GetYourGuide six-hour tour was flagged “likely to sell out”. Book early if this date is fixed.
  • Other dates. The listings priced above were all open on 24 December. If your dates are flexible, Viator showed 26 and 27 December as the next open dates on the two listings that were shut.
  • Prices move. GetYourGuide’s four-hour package showed “from $219” in its heading and $384.13 for our date. Read the figure for your own date at checkout, not the lead-in price.
  • Start times. The GetYourGuide four-hour listing warns that morning pickup in November and December may fall between nine and eleven.

Clothing, children and the small print

Winter clothing is listed as included on the six-hour package. Viator lists winter overalls as not included on its combo safari, and the GetYourGuide four-hour listing does not mention warm clothing, so check before you assume.

Child ages differ: 4 to 13 on the six-hour package, 4 to 12 on the GetYourGuide four-hour listing, and 3 to 12 on Viator’s combo. The six-hour package adds a 10-minute mini-snowmobile ride for children under 150 cm. None of the listings we read is suitable for wheelchair users where the point was stated (GetYourGuide’s six-hour, KKday’s six-hour and Viator’s combo all say so).

Ride lengths are short on every listing: 500 m by reindeer and 500 m by husky on the six-hour package, a 400 m reindeer ride mentioned on Viator’s. Treat these as a taste of each, not a safari.

Frequently asked questions

Which Santa Claus Village tour from Rovaniemi is the safest to book for 24 December?

Of the listings we read, the KKday six-hour package, Reindeer, Huskies & Santa Claus Village: €187.96 an adult and €108.98 a child, free cancellation up to 2 days ahead, instant confirmation, winter clothing and a salmon soup lunch included.

How much is a child's ticket?

On 24 December 2026 we read €108.98 on KKday, $119.63 on GetYourGuide's six-hour package, $378.64 on GetYourGuide's four-hour package and €185 on Viator's combo safari. Child ages are 4 to 13, 4 to 12 and 3 to 12 depending on the listing.

Is hotel pickup included?

It depends on the listing. GetYourGuide's four-hour package and Viator's combo include pickup (Viator also offers a meeting point at Scandic Rovaniemi City); the six-hour package lists transport from the city centre of Rovaniemi and shows hotel pickup as not included.

Can I cancel for free?

Yes on every listing we read, within limits: up to 2 days ahead on KKday, and up to 24 hours ahead on GetYourGuide and Viator, which on 24 December meant before 23 December.

Are Christmas Eve tours open?

Some are. Two Viator listings had no seat on 24 December, and the GetYourGuide six-hour tour was flagged likely to sell out.

How long are the reindeer and husky rides?

About 500 m each on the six-hour package, and a 400 m reindeer ride on Viator's combo. The GetYourGuide four-hour listing does not state lengths.
