Four things separate one Seine cruise from the next, and the headline price is only one of them: which boat company runs it, where it leaves from, whether the ticket is for a fixed time or open for the day, and how late you can cancel. For the same Saturday, the one-hour sightseeing cruises we read run from about $18.65 to $20.12 a head, while a three-course dinner cruise runs from about $95 to $140 and a four-course dinner with live music from about $126 up.

Two things to know before you pick. The three big names are not interchangeable: Bateaux Parisiens leaves from the foot of the Eiffel Tower, Bateaux Mouches from the Pont de l’Alma, and the Vedettes boats from other piers, so the pier decides your first hour more than the price does. And a dinner cruise is a different product from a sightseeing cruise: the three-course dinners we read range from about 75 minutes to about three hours, with drinks included on some and not on others.

What the boat companies’ rules mean when you buy

Read from the operator’s own sightseeing page on 22 November 2026: the Bateaux Parisiens one-hour sightseeing cruise leaves from the Eiffel Tower and includes an audio guide, and its online adult price was €18 with a child price of €9. The page also lists dinner and seasonal cruises on 24 and 25 December and on 31 December, so 12 December is an ordinary date. We did not read the operator’s dinner prices.

What that means for buying:

  • Most one-hour tickets are open for the day. Tiqets, GetYourGuide and KKday describe the Bateaux Parisiens and Bateaux Mouches tickets as needing no timeslot, so you choose the date and turn up. GetYourGuide’s Vedettes du Pont Neuf listing is the exception: it shows set departure times and says “likely to sell out”.
  • Check the winter timetable on the listing you pick. The listings disagree on Bateaux Parisiens departures between October and March (every 30 minutes on Tiqets, every hour on GetYourGuide), and Tiqets says the first departure moved to 13:00 from 3 September 2026. Bateaux Mouches departures in winter are every 45 minutes until 16:00 and every 30 minutes after.
  • Sunset in December is about 17:00, according to the GetYourGuide Bateaux Parisiens listing, so a late-afternoon or evening departure is after dark. Dinner sailings start at 6:15 pm or later on the listings we read.
  • Dinner cruises have a dress and arrival rule. Viator says smart casual and arriving 15 minutes early; KKday says formal clothing at night and that boarding closes 15 minutes before departure.
  • Dinner prices include different things. Tiqets includes wine or soft drinks, water and coffee; the GetYourGuide 6:45 pm sailing excludes drinks. Compare the “included” line, not just the price.

Frequently asked questions

Which seller is cheapest for a one-hour Seine cruise?

On 12 December 2026 the lowest dollar price we read was $18.65 for the Bateaux Mouches cruise on Tiqets and GetYourGuide, and $19.75 for the Bateaux Parisiens cruise on Tiqets, GetYourGuide and KKday. Viator showed $20.12 for Bateaux Parisiens.

Which boat leaves from the Eiffel Tower?

Bateaux Parisiens boards at Port de la Bourdonnais, pontoon 3, at the foot of the tower. Bateaux Mouches boards near the Pont de l'Alma, and Vedettes du Pont Neuf from Pont Neuf.

Do I need to pick a time for the one-hour cruise?

Not on the Bateaux Parisiens and Bateaux Mouches tickets we read: Tiqets and KKday say no timeslot is needed, and GetYourGuide's Bateaux Mouches ticket is valid for 365 days. GetYourGuide's Vedettes du Pont Neuf listing shows set departure times.

Can I cancel?

Tiqets allows cancellation until 11:59 pm the day before, GetYourGuide up to 24 hours before, KKday up to two days before, and Viator's dinner cruise before 8:30 pm the day before. Check the line on the option you choose.

Are drinks included on a dinner cruise?

It depends. Tiqets lists wine (one bottle between four people) or soft drinks, water and coffee in the three-course dinner. The GetYourGuide 6:45 pm sailing excludes drinks and serves coffee or tea after the meal. Viator lists wine or kir in the Bateaux Parisiens dinner.

How much is a dinner cruise on 12 December?

The three-course dinners we read were $95.44 (GetYourGuide, 6:45 pm), $108.63 (Tiqets) and $139.37 (Viator, 8:30 pm). Tiqets' four-course dinner with live music was $126.19.
