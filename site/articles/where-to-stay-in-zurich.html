Hotels around Old Town, with prices

The map shows the hotels and apartments around Old Town that booking sites such as Booking.com and Expedia list, with nightly prices; our own picks follow further down. It comes from our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book through it.

Zurich’s City Tax is not a percentage of the room: it is a flat CHF 3.50 per guest per night, collected by the hotels for the city’s tourist board, and it has been at that level since May 2023. VAT on a hotel room is low, a special rate of 3.8% against the normal 8.1%. What decides the rest of the cost is the room itself, often small in the centre, and how far you are from Zürich HB, the main station, where the airport trains arrive.

This guide covers five areas, from the Old Town to Zürich-West, 12 hotels, the airport links and the tax. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in October 2026. No hotel pays to appear here.

Zurich’s City Tax in 2026

Zurich’s City Tax is not set as a percentage, and it is not a tax the city itself levies. Hotels in Zurich and the surrounding region collect it on each night, under an agreement with the Zurich hoteliers’ association, and it goes to Zürich Tourismus, which uses it to market the city; Airbnb hosts take part in the same system. By the City of Zurich’s own figures, the City Tax pays for 65% of Zürich Tourismus.

Charge Rate
City Tax, since May 2023 CHF 3.50 per guest per night
City Tax, before May 2023 CHF 2.50 per guest per night
Swiss VAT on accommodation, overnight stay with breakfast 3.8%, the special rate
Swiss VAT, normal rate (for example, restaurant meals) 8.1%

The City Tax counts people, not the price of the room: a couple pays the same CHF 7 a night in a CHF 180 room as in a CHF 900 suite. It does not come with a free transport pass; the Zürich Card is a separate, paid product of Zürich Tourismus.

Our arithmetic: two adults in a CHF 250 double for three nights pay CHF 750 for the room and CHF 21 in City Tax (2 guests × 3 nights × CHF 3.50), so CHF 771 in all. If the CHF 250 rate includes VAT at 3.8%, about CHF 9.15 of each night is VAT.

Getting in from the airport, and around

  • By train. Trains run from Zurich Airport to Zürich HB every 5 to 10 minutes between 5 am and midnight and take 10 to 15 minutes. A single costs CHF 7, valid for an hour, and a 24-hour pass CHF 14. The fare is three zones: the airport is zone 121, and the city of Zurich, zone 110, counts double.
  • By tram. Line 10 runs from the airport to the main station in about 35 minutes, on the same ticket.
  • In town, a single ticket for zone 110 is CHF 4.60 and valid for an hour; a 24-hour ticket for the city is CHF 9.20. Both cover trams, buses and the S-Bahn inside the city zone.
  • The Zürich Card costs CHF 29 for 24 hours and CHF 56 for 72 hours. It covers public transport in the city zones, including the trip between the airport and Zürich HB, free entry to many museums and the short lake and Limmat river cruises. New prices apply from 13 December 2026, set by the start date you choose, not the date you buy.

The Old Town and Niederdorf: the first-trip base

District 1 is the Old Town on both banks of the Limmat, with the Bahnhofstrasse, the guild houses, the Grossmünster and Fraumünster churches and the Opera House. The east bank holds the Niederdorf, lanes of restaurants and bars that stay busy late. Most of a first visit is a walk away, and rooms here cost most.

Where to stay in the Old Town

  • Storchen Zürich, splurge. On the Weinplatz, in a house first mentioned in 1357, with 64 rooms and suites, the La Rôtisserie restaurant and a terrace on the Limmat with a view of the Grossmünster, open from May to October in fine weather. Choose it for the Old Town from the water’s edge. Watch out: the view decides the room: the Old Town View Double is 18 m², and a river view is its own category, from the River View Double at 18 to 25 m². Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Adler Zürich, good value. A three-star-superior hotel at Rosengasse 10, in the Niederdorf, with air-conditioning in every room, the Swiss Chuchi restaurant for fondue, and Heidi, the hotel’s cow, standing on the balcony over the square. The hotel puts the main station 7 minutes’ walk away. Choose it for the Niederdorf at a fair price. Watch out: the Small Double is 18 m² with a 140 cm bed; book the Standard Double, 21 m² with a king bed, if two of you need space. Check rates on Booking.com →

Around Zürich HB: the trains at the door

Zürich HB is the largest and busiest station in Switzerland, with the airport trains, the S-Bahn and trains across the country. Staying beside it saves time on arrival and on day trips; the Bahnhofstrasse starts across the square, and the Old Town is a short walk.

Where to stay around Zürich HB

  • Hotel Schweizerhof Zürich, splurge. A five-star hotel at Bahnhofplatz 7, directly opposite the main station, open since 1877, with 92 rooms and suites, triple-glazed windows, adjustable air-conditioning and the La Soupière restaurant. Standard doubles measure 20 to 25 m². Booking direct includes a complimentary City Ticket for public transport, the hotel says. Choose it for the station at the door without the noise. Watch out: it faces the station square; the windows deal with the sound, not the bustle outside. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Montana Zürich, good value. On Konradstrasse, 300 metres behind the main station, a 3-minute walk by the hotel’s count, with 74 rooms, a breakfast buffet, quiet rooms on the inner courtyard and its own underground car park. Choose it for a short walk to the trains at a lower price. Watch out: the Comfort Double Queen is 18 m² with a bed of 140 by 200 cm; the Deluxe rooms, 22 to 26 m², suit two better.

Seefeld: the lake on the east shore

District 8 starts behind the Opera House and runs along the east shore of Lake Zurich to the Seebad Tiefenbrunnen, with boutiques, villas, the Chinawiese and the Seebad Utoquai. It is calm at night, the lakeside promenade is on the doorstep, and the Old Town is a walk or a short tram ride away.

Where to stay in Seefeld

  • La Réserve Eden au Lac Zurich, splurge. On the Utoquai, on the lakeshore, a hotel first opened about a century ago and redesigned by Philippe Starck, now with 40 rooms and suites, a restaurant and a rooftop bar. Every Lake View Room has a king-size bed, and most face south over the lake. Choose it for Lake Zurich from your window. Watch out: the entry category is the Standard Courtyard Room; the lake view is a category of its own, so book it by name. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Sorell Hotel Seefeld, comfortable. At Seefeldstrasse 63, in the middle of the district, with 65 rooms with Art Deco touches, box-spring beds and Soeder toiletries from Zurich, and the FLOR Café Bar. Choose it for a calm, local-feeling base near the lake. Watch out: the Standard Single is at least 14 m²; the Grand Standard Double, at least 24 m², is the room for two. Check rates on Booking.com →

Enge and Wollishofen: the left shore

District 2 is the west shore of the lake: Enge station, built of Ticino granite, Museum Rietberg in its park, the Seebad Enge lido and, further south in Wollishofen, the Rote Fabrik cultural centre. It is a few minutes by train or tram from Zürich HB and the Bahnhofstrasse, and it holds two of the better-value picks in this guide.

Where to stay in Enge and Wollishofen

  • Motel One Zurich, good value. At Stockerstrasse 61, not far from Paradeplatz and a short stroll from the lake shore, with designed rooms, box-spring beds, free Wi-Fi, and local food and drink at breakfast and at the bar. Choose it for a designed room near the centre at a chain price. Watch out: the rooms are compact and standard across the chain; expect comfort rather than character. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • B2 Boutique Hotel Zürich, comfortable. A four-star hotel in what was once Switzerland’s largest brewery, on the Hürlimann site, with 60 rooms and suites under high ceilings, a library of 33,000 books where breakfast is served, a free 24-hour gym, and the Giesshübel stop, 3 minutes by train from the main station, about 300 metres away. Choose it for space and character a few minutes from the centre. Watch out: the Boutique Double, 24 to 28 m², has an open-plan bathroom, and the Hürlimannbad thermal baths below the hotel are a public spa with their own entry. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Zurich Youth Hostel, budget. A modern hostel of Swiss Youth Hostels at Mutschellenstrasse 114 in Wollishofen, open all year, with 76 rooms and 280 beds, a restaurant, a bar and a 24-hour reception, within walking distance of the lake. Tram 7 runs from the main station to Morgental, the nearest stop. Choose it for a budget base by the lake. Watch out: it is in the south of the city; most of what you came for is a tram ride away. Check rates on Booking.com →

Langstrasse and Zürich-West: bars and the old industrial quarter

District 4, around the Langstrasse, was long regarded, in the tourist board’s words, as a den of iniquity; it is now known for its restaurants and nightlife. District 5, Zürich-West, is the former industrial quarter where nightclubs, cultural institutions and universities cluster around the Prime Tower, the Viadukt and the Freitag Tower. Both are lively after dark, and both are close to Zürich HB.

Where to stay in Langstrasse and Zürich-West

  • 25hours Hotel Langstrasse, comfortable. At Langstrasse 150, between the Langstrasse and the Europaallee, a short walk from the main station, with the NENI restaurant on the ground floor and a sauna and gym on the 7th floor, looking over the station. Choose it for the nightlife district at the station’s back door. Watch out: the hotel itself plays on the street’s double life, banking by day and the red-light district by night; light sleepers should ask for a quieter room. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Greulich Design & Boutique Hotel, comfortable. A Bauhaus-style design hotel in District 4 with 18 rooms around a green inner courtyard beside its birch grove: 10 Design Rooms with a 160 cm bed and a shower, and 8 Design Double Rooms on two levels with a 180 cm bed and a bathtub. Choose it for a quiet courtyard in the middle of District 4. Watch out: with 18 rooms it is small, so book early; an extra bed in a Design Double costs CHF 60 a night. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • 25hours Hotel Zürich West, comfortable. In the old industrial quarter, designed by Alfredo Häberli on a sports theme, with 123 rooms and suites, the Ribelli restaurant, a gym and a heated 25-metre outdoor pool, open daily in summer. Tram 17 from the main station stops at Fischerweg nearby. Choose it for Zürich-West and a swim. Watch out: the pool closes in the colder months, and the Old Town is a tram ride away.

How to book Zurich

  • Count the City Tax. Add CHF 3.50 for every guest, every night: a couple staying four nights pays CHF 28 on top of the room.
  • Check the bed, not just the room. Several central rooms are small: the Old Town View Double at Storchen Zürich, the Small Double at Hotel Adler Zürich and the Comfort Double Queen at Hotel Montana Zürich are each 18 m², and the last two have a 140 cm bed. Two people should book the category above.
  • Price the transport. A 24-hour city ticket is CHF 9.20 and the airport single CHF 7; Hotel Schweizerhof Zürich includes a City Ticket when you book direct. The Zürich Card pays if you plan museums; its prices change on 13 December 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Zurich for a first trip?

The Old Town and the Niederdorf, District 1 on both banks of the Limmat: the Bahnhofstrasse, the guild houses, the churches and the Opera House are a walk away. Stay around Zürich HB for the trains, Seefeld for the lake, Enge and Wollishofen for the left shore and value, and Langstrasse or Zürich-West for bars and nightlife.

How much is the city tax in Zurich?

CHF 3.50 per guest per night, up from CHF 2.50 in May 2023. Hotels in Zurich and the surrounding region collect this City Tax for Zürich Tourismus, so it depends on the number of guests, not the room price: two adults staying three nights pay CHF 21.

What is the VAT rate on hotels in Switzerland?

Accommodation, the overnight stay with breakfast, is taxed at the special rate of 3.8%, against the normal Swiss VAT rate of 8.1%.

How do you get from Zurich Airport to the city centre?

By train: trains run to Zürich HB every 5 to 10 minutes between 5 am and midnight and take 10 to 15 minutes. A single costs CHF 7, valid for an hour, and a 24-hour pass CHF 14. Tram 10 takes about 35 minutes on the same ticket.

Does the Zurich City Tax include a free transport card?

No. The Zürich Card is a separate, paid product of Zürich Tourismus: CHF 29 for 24 hours and CHF 56 for 72 hours, with public transport in the city zones, the trip between the airport and Zürich HB, and free entry to many museums. New prices apply from 13 December 2026.

How much is a public transport ticket in Zurich?

In the city zone, 110, a single ticket costs CHF 4.60 and is valid for an hour, and a 24-hour ticket costs CHF 9.20. The city zone counts as two zones, so the trip to the airport, zone 121, is a three-zone ticket at CHF 7.
