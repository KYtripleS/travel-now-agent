Hotels around Bahnhofstrasse, with prices

The map shows the hotels and apartments around Bahnhofstrasse that booking sites such as Booking.com and Expedia list, with nightly prices; our own picks follow further down. It comes from our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book through it.

Zermatt has no cars, so where you sleep is really a choice of which lift, funicular or railway you want near your door. The village’s visitor tax, the Kurtaxe, is a flat CHF 4.00 per adult per night for every type of accommodation, added on top of the room rate. The lift operator’s winter season starts on 1 November 2026, but two big sides of the mountain open later, on 28 November.

This guide covers the season and the lift pass, five areas by lift, 12 hotels from a youth hostel to a resort at 2,222 m, the Kurtaxe, and the way in through Täsch. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked on its own website in October 2026. Where a hotel publishes its winter opening dates we give them, because several are in their autumn break and reopen in late November or December. No hotel pays to appear here.

The 2026/27 season and the lift pass

Zermatt Bergbahnen’s ski-pass page gives the winter season as 1 November 2026 to 3 May 2027, with the Sunnegga/Rothorn and Gornergrat areas opening on 28 November 2026 and closing on 11 April and 18 April 2027. Our reading is that before 28 November only the glacier side, reached from the Matterhorn Express, is open, so an early-season trip suits a hotel on that side of the village.

The winter ski pass is priced with “from” rates, not a single price. The operator says its lowest price applies from 1 to 27 November 2026 and from 19 April to 2 May 2027, and that the Gornergrat railway is not included in those periods. Dates in between cost more and are shown only in its web shop, so treat the table as the floor. Prices include Swiss VAT and can change.

Half day, from 12:15 CHF 68 not sold

Children under 9 ski free with a photo pass, children aged 9 to 15 pay 50% less, youths aged 16 to 19 pay 15% less, and on Saturdays the Zermatt pass is free for children under 16. The Peak Pass, sold by the tourist office from CHF 212, is a sightseeing pass for the railways, cable cars and local bus, and its page says it is not valid on ski and chairlifts, so skiers need the ski pass.

Bahnhofstrasse and the station: the village centre

Bahnhofstrasse is Zermatt’s main street, and the hotels along it put you among the shops, restaurants and bars, with a walk to the lifts rather than a lift at the door. Zermatt Tourism lists a free e-bus on two routes, the Ski-Bus and the Winkelmatten line, as well as electric taxis.

Sunnegga: the funicular at your door

The Sunnegga underground funicular runs from the edge of the village up to the Sunnegga, Blauherd and Rothorn side of the ski area, which opens on 28 November. Both hotels here describe themselves by their distance to it.

Where to stay near Sunnegga

  • Hotel National Zermatt, comfortable. Matterstrasse 39, a four-star hotel that its own site places 20 metres from the Sunnegga valley station, with a spa and Standard and Deluxe doubles, family rooms and a Maisonette Suite. Choose it to be next to the funicular with a spa to return to. Watch out: its website still carried a summer and autumn 2025 promotion when we checked, so confirm winter dates and rates before you rely on it. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Holiday, good value. Gryfelblatte 4, in what it calls a quiet quarter, 200 metres from the Sunnegga funicular, with the Gornergrat railway and the Klein Matterhorn bus stop a few minutes’ walk away. Rooms run from a 20 m² Standard Double to a 28 m² family room, the hotel taxi collects guests free on arrival and departure days, and its site lists offers for January and March to April 2027. Choose it for a short walk to the lifts at a lower price. Watch out: a Standard Double is 20 m², so a family should look at the larger rooms. Check rates on Booking.com →

Steinmatte and Winkelmatten: the Matterhorn Express side

The Matterhorn Express is the lift line that climbs from the south end of the village via Furi to the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise, and the hotels around its valley station sit on the hillside at the village’s edge. The Matterhorn Focus says the e-bus stop in front of its entrance, on the red Winkelmatten line, is called Matterhorn Glacier Paradise, and that is the line to use for the village. On our reading of the opening dates, this is the side to be on in early November.

Where to stay near the Matterhorn Express and in Winkelmatten

  • Hotel Matterhorn Focus, comfortable. Schluhmattstrasse 131, a four-star superior design hotel that says it stands directly next to the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise valley station. It lists 11 room categories, including a 30 m² Futura Room with a balcony, and a spa. Choose it for the glacier lift on your doorstep. Watch out: the hotel puts the village under ten minutes away on foot, so evenings in the centre mean a walk or the e-bus. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Jägerhof, good value. Steinmattstrasse 85, a family-run three-star hotel with 49 rooms, from singles to family suites for up to six, and a restaurant and bar. Its FAQ puts the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise valley station about ten minutes away on foot, Sunnegga, Gornergrat and the railway station about 15, and the ski bus three. A free shuttle runs from 7:30 to 21:00. Choose it for a lower price with the lifts in walking range. Watch out: only some rooms have a balcony or a Matterhorn view. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Berghof, comfortable. Winkelmattenweg 18, a family-run four-star hotel with 28 rooms, a 600 m² spa and two restaurants or bars. Zermatt Tourism’s listing puts the Matterhorn Express valley station less than two minutes’ walk away. Choose it for a spa hotel beside the glacier lift. Watch out: the hotel’s own pages give no distance to the lifts or the centre. Check rates on Booking.com →

Riedweg: slopes beside the hotel

CERVO Mountain Resort, on Riedweg, describes itself as a ski-in, ski-out hotel. The trade-off is timing: it is closed until the end of November.

Where to stay on Riedweg

  • CERVO Mountain Resort, comfortable. Riedweg 156, open from 27 November 2026 to 11 April 2027. Nomad rooms are four-star, with Hästens beds, and Alpinist and Huntsman rooms are five-star. Its ski-in, ski-out page says guests live directly next to the slopes, and it has the Atman Mountain Spa and après-ski on its terraces. Choose it for slopes beside the hotel and a lively house. Watch out: the terrace après-ski includes DJ sets and live sessions, so it is not the quietest option, and it opens too late for an early-November trip. Check rates on Booking.com →

Riffelalp: a resort at 2,222 m

Riffelalp Resort 2222m lists its winter opening as 11 December 2026 to 4 April 2027. You take the Gornergrat Railway from Zermatt to Riffelalp station and walk a short way in winter, and luggage goes up by skidoo. The last train up leaves Zermatt at 23:30, according to the hotel.

Where to stay on the mountain

  • Riffelalp Resort 2222m, splurge. A five-star ski-in, ski-out resort at 2,222 m; the hotel says every stay includes spa access and mountain-guide experiences. Choose it to ski from the door and be above the village at night. Watch out: there is no parking, you travel by train, and it is open only from 11 December to 4 April. Check rates on Booking.com →

The Kurtaxe and Swiss VAT

Zermatt’s visitor tax is set by the municipality’s Kurtaxen regulation, which the municipality lists with the date 1 November 2022. It applies per night to every type of accommodation, and the hotel collects it; Grand Hotel Zermatterhof’s FAQ confirms it is charged in addition to the room rate.

Guest Kurtaxe per night
Child under 9 free
Child aged 9 to 15 CHF 2.00
Adult, 16 and over CHF 4.00

Residents of Zermatt and some others, such as pupils at recognised Valais schools during term, are exempt. Swiss VAT is separate: the federal tax administration lists a special rate of 3.8% for overnight stays with breakfast, against a normal rate of 8.1%.

A worked example, our arithmetic: two adults and a ten-year-old for four nights pay 2 × CHF 4.00 × 4 = CHF 32 for the adults and CHF 2.00 × 4 = CHF 8 for the child, CHF 40 in all. On a room we have assumed at CHF 500 a night, four nights come to CHF 2,000, so the Kurtaxe for the two adults alone is 1.6% of the bill, and CHF 40 for the family is 2%. Getting to Zermatt and around

Zermatt is car-free. Zermatt Tourism says private vehicles can go only as far as Täsch, 5 km short of the village, that electric cars are treated the same as petrol ones, and that large fines follow if you drive in without authorisation.

  • By train. Zermatt Tourism puts the journey at three and a half hours from Zurich Airport and four hours from Geneva or Basel airports, with trains to Zermatt every half hour. Our Zurich guide covers hotels if you break the journey there, and a Europe rail pass may pay if you plan several rail days.
  • By car. Park at Täsch. The Matterhorn Terminal has 2,100 covered spaces and sits under the same roof as the railway station, and private car parks in Täsch add more than 1,000. We could not confirm a daily rate from an official page, so check it before you go.
  • The Täsch to Zermatt shuttle. Trains run every 20 minutes from 5:55 to 21:55, then hourly in the evening until 00:30 or 01:00, and all night at weekends. The ride takes 12 minutes, a return costs CHF 17.20, half-fare and children aged 6 to 15 pay half, and the Swiss Travel Pass is valid.
  • Once there. Zermatt Tourism lists electric taxis, horse-drawn carriages, bicycles, walking and a free e-bus on two routes, the Ski-Bus and Winkelmatten.

How to book Zermatt

  • Count the Kurtaxe. Add CHF 4.00 per adult per night, and CHF 2.00 per child aged 9 to 15.
  • Match the hotel to the opening dates. Sunnegga/Rothorn and Gornergrat open on 28 November, and CERVO, Matthiol and Riffelalp open on 27 November, 4 December and 11 December. For an early-November trip, choose a hotel that is open and, on our reading, on the Matterhorn Express side.
  • Price the pass for your dates. The prices above are floors; the cheapest windows are 1 to 27 November and 19 April to 2 May.
  • Check room size. Hotel Holiday’s Standard Double is 20 m², Matterhorn Focus’s Futura Room 30 m².
  • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book; Whistler and Niseko are the other ski-season guides in this series.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best area to stay in Zermatt for skiing?

    It depends on the lift. Bahnhofstrasse and the station put you among the shops and restaurants, Sunnegga has two hotels about 200 m or less from the funicular, the Matterhorn Express side suits early-season glacier skiing, Riedweg has CERVO Mountain Resort, which describes itself as ski-in, ski-out, and Riffelalp Resort sits at 2,222 m on the Gornergrat line.

    When does the Zermatt ski season start in 2026/27?

    Zermatt Bergbahnen gives the winter season as 1 November 2026 to 3 May 2027. The Sunnegga/Rothorn and Gornergrat areas open on 28 November 2026, and close on 11 April and 18 April 2027.

    How much is a Zermatt ski pass in 2026/27?

    The operator lists from-prices: CHF 89 for one day, CHF 329 for five days and CHF 436 for seven days in the Zermatt area. The lowest price applies from 1 to 27 November 2026 and 19 April to 2 May 2027; other dates cost more and are shown in its web shop. Children under 9 ski free. The Peak Pass is a sightseeing pass and is not valid on ski and chairlifts.

    How much is the tourist tax in Zermatt?

    The Kurtaxe is CHF 4.00 per adult per night for every type of accommodation, CHF 2.00 for children aged 9 to 15 and free under 9, charged on top of the room rate. Two adults and a ten-year-old for four nights pay CHF 40, by our arithmetic.

    Can you drive to Zermatt?

    No. Zermatt is car-free, and private vehicles can go only as far as Täsch, 5 km away. The Matterhorn Terminal there has 2,100 covered spaces, and the shuttle train to Zermatt runs every 20 minutes from 5:55 to 21:55 and takes 12 minutes. A return costs CHF 17.20.

    How long does it take to get to Zermatt from the airport?

    Zermatt Tourism puts the train journey at three and a half hours from Zurich Airport and four hours from Geneva or Basel airports, with trains to Zermatt every half hour.
