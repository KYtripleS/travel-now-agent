Amsterdam’s New Year’s Eve has changed this year. Since 1 August 2026 a national ban covers consumer fireworks, and the city says it is organising fireworks shows on Museumplein and at other locations. It had not published times or a programme when we checked. The other thing that shapes your night is the transport: last year’s published timetables had trams, buses and the metro stopping at about 20:00, with a night network from 01:30. The bill carries two charges on top of the room rate: 21% VAT and a 12.5% tourist tax on the price before VAT.

This guide covers what the city has published for 31 December 2026, six areas, 12 hotels and the terms that catch people out. 31 December 2026: what the city has published

On 2 November 2026 the City of Amsterdam published a page headed “Vuurwerkverbod in heel Nederland”. It says a national ban has applied since 1 August 2026: you may not set off banned consumer fireworks, and you may no longer buy, sell or keep them at home. Amsterdam had its own local lighting ban in recent years, but the new rule is national. The city says light F1 fireworks, such as sparklers, are still allowed, and that no exemptions are granted in Amsterdam.

The same page says the municipality will again organise fireworks shows on Museumplein and at a number of other locations. It does not give a date, a time, a programme or a list of locations, and the page we read does not mention firework-free zones, so we cannot tell you where else to look. We also found nothing official about Nieuwmarkt for this year. Treat the city’s show as likely but unconfirmed in detail, and do not choose a hotel on the strength of a view.

For comparison, I amsterdam’s New Year’s Eve page, last updated on 27 December 2025, described last year’s arrangements: an electric fireworks display on Museumplein designed for up to 60,000 spectators, live acts on the main stage from 23:00, the countdown beginning at 23:50, and a children’s programme from 19:00. It also listed a show at Sloterplas from 00:00 to 00:10. That is last year’s plan, and we label it as such; the 2026 version may differ.

Getting around on the night

  • Public transport has not been published for 2026. GVB’s holiday page showed only the April and May timetable when we checked, so we cannot give you this year’s hours.
  • Last year’s GVB page is no longer online. The latest we could read was the page for 2024-25, kept in the Internet Archive. It said buses, trams and metros ran until about 20:00 on 31 December, and that at 01:30 the night network started with metro 52 and 54, night buses and ferries F3 and F4. Night buses carried a separate night fare. Treat this as a pattern, not a promise.
  • Trains to the airport stopped early last year. Schiphol’s notice of 22 December 2025 said there were no trains to or from the airport between 20:00 and 01:00, except trains between Hoofddorp and Amsterdam Centraal, which ran until about 22:00, and no buses between 20:00 and 01:30.
  • NS has said the same for years. A 2022 NS release said no trains run after 20:00 on 31 December “as every year”, and that night trains resumed from 01:00. Check the NS journey planner and GVB’s holiday page before you go.
  • Plan to walk. If the pattern repeats, a hotel you can reach on foot from Museumplein or the centre is worth more than a cheaper bed on a tram line.

    Oud-Zuid and the Museum Quarter: closest to Museumplein

    This is the quiet, grand part of Amsterdam, with the Rijksmuseum, the Van Gogh Museum and the Stedelijk around Museumplein. It is the area to pick if the city’s show is held there again and you would rather walk home than queue for a tram. We cannot say how close the crowds will come to each door, so ask the hotel what it advises.

    Centrum and the Grachtengordel: canals on foot

    The canal ring has many hotels in converted canal houses, where rooms can be small. From here you can reach the Dam, the Spui and the Jordaan on foot, which is the point on a night when trams may stop at 20:00.

    Where to stay in Centrum and the Grachtengordel

    • The Dylan Amsterdam, splurge. A five-star boutique hotel in a historic building at Keizersgracht 384, whose Restaurant Vinkeles has two Michelin stars, by the hotel’s own account. Choose it for a canal-side splurge with a serious restaurant. Watch out: its homepage mentions no New Year’s Eve package or minimum stay, so ask before you book. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Hotel Estheréa, comfortable. A four-star hotel at Singel 303 to 309 with 91 rooms and two suites; Classic rooms are at least 13 m², Luxury rooms 20 m² and Suites 30 or 55 m², and the hotel says sizes vary in the historic building. It advertises a “Bubbles, breakfast and holiday cheer” package from 15 November 2026. Choose it for a long-established canal hotel on the Singel. Watch out: that package page does not mention New Year’s Eve or give a minimum stay, and Classic rooms start small. Check rates on Booking.com →

    The Jordaan: village streets west of the canals

    The Jordaan is the neighbourhood of small canals and narrow streets west of the canal ring. Expect people in the streets on the night; we found no published plan for the neighbourhood.

    Where to stay in the Jordaan

    • Hotel Mercier, comfortable. At Rozenstraat 12, in the heart of the Jordaan by its own description, with 48 rooms and suites across four floors of the main building and two of the annexe. Its site shows a best-price guarantee of at least 10% off when you book direct. Choose it for a design-led stay on a Jordaan street. Watch out: the site gives no room sizes and no New Year’s Eve terms. Check rates on Booking.com →

    De Pijp: restaurants and a tower on the edge

    De Pijp lies south of the canal ring. We name only one hotel here, and it sits at the neighbourhood’s edge, away from the canal ring; if you need a tram or metro to reach the centre, remember that last year’s service stopped at about 20:00.

    Where to stay in De Pijp

    • Hotel Okura Amsterdam, splurge. At Ferdinand Bolstraat 333, with 120 completely renewed rooms and suites, and a site that cites three Michelin stars across its restaurants, including Ciel Bleu and Yamazato. Its end-of-year page is for corporate events. Choose it for the food, in one building. Watch out: it publishes no New Year’s Eve package or minimum stay for guests, and it is away from the canal ring. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Oost: a park, and a published package

    East of the Amstel, Oost is more residential, with the Oosterpark and the Amstel station as its landmarks. It is the area where one hotel publishes a dated New Year’s Eve package, which is rare in this guide.

    Where to stay in Oost

    • Pillows Grand Boutique Hotel Maurits at the Park, splurge. A five-star hotel at Mauritskade 61, beside the Oosterpark, in a building from 1908. Its festive-season page lists “New Years at VanOost Restaurant” on 31 December 2026: a six-course dinner, wine pairing, an aperitif on arrival, a glass of champagne at midnight, house drinks in Bar VanOost until 01:00, a live DJ, the overnight stay and an all-in à la carte breakfast. A lighter “New Years at Spring Cafe Brasserie” package for the same date offers a three-course lunch or dinner, the stay and breakfast. Choose it for a packaged night with no tram needed. Watch out: the page shows no prices and says to book the restaurant separately, and it states no minimum stay. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Hotel V Fizeaustraat, good value. At Fizeaustraat 2, right behind Amstel Station, which the hotel counts as 400 m or about five minutes’ walk away; Comfort Rooms are 22 to 25 m² with a king-sized bed. Choose it for generous room sizes by a major station. Watch out: it is east of the centre, and its page publishes no New Year’s Eve terms. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Noord: across the IJ

    Noord sits across the river from Centraal, reached by free GVB ferries. Last year’s GVB page said ferries F3 and F4 ran in the night network from 01:30, and that some ferry lines kept running later on New Year’s Eve, but this year’s timetable is not out.

    Where to stay in Noord

    • Sir Adam, comfortable. At Overhoeksplein 7, in the A’DAM Tower, which the hotel puts a 3-minute, free 24/7 ferry ride from Central Station, with a rooftop that has 360-degree views. Choose it for the view and the ferry. Watch out: every trip into the centre starts with the ferry, and its site publishes no New Year’s Eve terms. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • ClinkNOORD, budget. A large hostel at Badhuiskade 3 with mixed dorms of 1 to 14 beds, female-only dorms and private en-suite rooms for 1 to 6. Choose it for the lowest price in this guide. Watch out: check-out is by 10am, and the page lists no New Year’s Eve event. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Amsterdam’s hotel tax: 21% VAT and a 12.5% tourist tax

    Charge Rate in 2026
    VAT on a hotel room 21% from 1 January 2026
    Tourist tax 12.5% of the overnight price, excluding VAT

    The Belastingdienst says the 21% rate applies to hotel stays from 1 January 2026, and the City of Amsterdam’s tourist tax page gives 12.5% of the overnight price excluding VAT. On a room that costs 200 euros before tax, VAT is 42 euros and the tourist tax is 25 euros, so a night costs 267 euros, and two nights cost 534 euros (our arithmetic from the two rates). Check whether the price you are shown includes both.

    The tax may change in 2027. On 23 September 2026 the city executive agreed an amendment to the tourist tax ordinance that proposes a uniform rate of 16% of the overnight price in 2027. AT5 reported that the council votes on the 2027 budget on 11 and 12 November 2026. We found no outcome and no official page tying that vote to the 16% rate, so treat it as a proposal. The proposal is for 2027, so we read the night of 31 December 2026 as falling under the current 12.5%, but if your stay runs into January, ask the hotel which rate applies.

    Schiphol says a train runs eight times an hour between Amsterdam Centraal and the airport, in just 17 minutes.     How to book New Year’s Eve in Amsterdam

    • Decide how you will get home. If trams and metros stop at about 20:00 again, choose a hotel you can reach on foot or by ferry, and check GVB’s holiday page and the NS planner in December.
    • Ask about minimum stays. Only Pillows publishes a dated New Year’s Eve package among these 12, with no minimum stay stated; for the other eleven we found none, so ask.
    • Do not book for a view. The city has published that it will organise shows, but no time or place we can send you to, so no room is sold here on a view.
    • Count the tax. Add 21% VAT and the 12.5% tourist tax to a rate quoted without them, as in the worked example above.
    • Check room size. Canal-house rooms are small by design: Estheréa’s Classic rooms start at 13 m², while Hotel V’s Comfort Rooms are 22 to 25 m².
    • Compare totals. Our hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and hotel taxes around the world sets Amsterdam beside other cities.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a fireworks show in Amsterdam on New Year's Eve 2026?

    The City of Amsterdam says it will again organise fireworks shows on Museumplein and at a number of other locations, but the page we read gives no date, time or programme. Last year's I amsterdam page described an electric fireworks display on Museumplein with a countdown from 23:50; that is last year's plan, not a 2026 confirmation.

    Can I set off fireworks in Amsterdam in 2026?

    No, not banned consumer fireworks. A national ban has applied since 1 August 2026, covering lighting, buying, selling and keeping them at home. The city says light F1 fireworks such as sparklers are still allowed and that no exemptions are granted in Amsterdam.

    Do trams and the metro run on New Year's Eve in Amsterdam?

    GVB had not published its 2026 timetable when we checked. Its 2024-25 page said buses, trams and metros ran until about 20:00 on 31 December and that a night network started at 01:30 with metro 52 and 54, night buses and ferries F3 and F4. Schiphol's notice for 2025 said no trains or buses to the airport between 20:00 and 01:00 or 01:30, apart from some Hoofddorp to Amsterdam Centraal trains. Treat these as last year's pattern.

    Where is the best area to stay in Amsterdam for New Year's Eve?

    If the city's show is on Museumplein again, the Oud-Zuid and Museum Quarter hotels (Conservatorium, Avani, Max Brown) are closest, and the Centrum and Jordaan hotels are on foot from the centre. Because last year's transport stopped at about 20:00, choose a hotel you can walk or take a ferry to. Pillows in Oost is the one hotel we found that publishes a 31 December 2026 package.

    How much is the tourist tax in Amsterdam for New Year's Eve 2026?

    The City of Amsterdam charges 12.5% of the overnight price excluding VAT, and hotel VAT is 21% from 1 January 2026. On a 200-euro room that is 42 euros VAT and 25 euros tourist tax, 267 euros a night (our arithmetic). A rise to 16% is proposed for 2027; the council vote on the 2027 budget was due on 11 and 12 November 2026, and we found no outcome.

    Do Amsterdam hotels require a minimum stay on New Year's Eve?

    Among the 12 hotels here, only Pillows Maurits at the Park publishes a dated 31 December 2026 package, and it states no minimum stay or prices. The other eleven publish no New Year's Eve minimum that we found, so ask the hotel before booking.
