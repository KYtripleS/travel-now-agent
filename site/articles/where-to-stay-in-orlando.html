Hotels around Lake Buena Vista, with prices

The map shows the hotels and apartments around Lake Buena Vista that booking sites such as Booking.com and Expedia list, with nightly prices; our own picks follow further down. It comes from our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book through it.

An Orlando hotel bill depends on which side of a county line you sleep. Hotels in Orange County, which holds Orlando, International Drive and Winter Park, add 12.5% in taxes to the room rate; hotels in Osceola County, which holds Kissimmee, add 13.5%. On a $200 room that is $25 or $27 a night before any resort fee, and one hotel in this guide publishes a taxable resort fee of $48 a night.

This guide covers five areas, from Walt Disney World to Winter Park, 12 hotels, the airport links and the tax. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in October 2026. No hotel pays to appear here.

Orlando’s hotel tax and resort fees

Three charges apply to a hotel room in Orlando, and the county decides two of them:

Charge Orange County Osceola County
Florida state sales tax 6% 6%
County discretionary sales surtax 0.5% 1.5%
Tourist Development Tax 6% 6%
Together (our arithmetic) 12.5% 13.5%

The Florida Department of Revenue lists the rental of living or sleeping accommodations, which it calls transient rentals, among the sales that carry the county surtax, and its county table shows the Tourist Development Tax in Orange and Osceola at 6%, in addition to the state’s 6%. Orange County describes its tax as 6% on hotel stays and short-term rentals of less than six months; the Osceola Tax Collector gives 6% for rentals of under 180 days. The Department’s surtax table shows Orange at 0.5%, extended from 1 January 2026, and Osceola at 1.5%, and lists no change to either rate for 2026.

The county follows the hotel’s address. Kissimmee is the seat of Osceola County, so its hotels are in the 13.5% column; Orlando and Winter Park are in Orange County. The oversight district that governs Walt Disney World’s land spans both counties, so check the tax lines on your confirmation. On a $200 room, our arithmetic gives $225 a night in Orange County and $227 in Osceola.

Resort fees are separate. We quote one only where the hotel publishes it: Margaritaville Resort Orlando states a taxable resort fee of $48 plus tax per room, per night, and the Drury Plaza, Rosen Centre and Rosen Inn at Pointe Orlando say they charge none. We found no fee amount on the Marriott, Disney or Universal pages we read, so ask before you book. Since 12 May 2025 a federal rule has required a mandatory resort fee to be included in the total price shown, while taxes and other government charges may be left out of the upfront total. We make no judgement about any hotel’s compliance.

  • By bus, LYNX stops at Terminal A, Level 1. A single ride is $2.00 and an all-day pass is $4.50, which you ask the operator for when boarding.
  • By taxi, cabs are metered, and the airport says flat rates are not authorised, so the metered fare may differ from the table.
  • By app, ride-hailing pick-up is at the Arrivals curbs: Level 2 at Terminals A and B, 24 hours a day, and Level 6 at Terminal C.
  • By car, the airport lists on-airport and off-airport rental companies, with free shuttle buses to the off-airport ones.
  • By train, a Brightline station at the airport, open daily 5am to 11pm, runs south to West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Fort Lauderdale, Aventura and Miami. It is useful only if you are continuing to South Florida.
  • Around the parks, hotels run their own transport. Disney’s Pop Century lists buses and the Skyliner gondola; Universal lists a complimentary shuttle bus for its hotel guests, with water taxis and walking paths at select hotels.

Walt Disney World and Lake Buena Vista: the first-trip base

Disney says guests at its resort hotels, and at select other hotels, can enter its theme parks 30 minutes early every day of a stay, including check-in and check-out days. Valid admission is needed, not every attraction runs during early entry, and Disney’s page says the benefit continues through 2027.

Where to stay at Walt Disney World and Lake Buena Vista

How the hotel links work: a hotel’s name goes to its own website. “Check rates” opens it on Booking.com through our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book, at no extra cost to you.

  • Disney’s Grand Floridian Resort & Spa, splurge. A Victorian-style Deluxe Resort at 4401 Floridian Way on the Seven Seas Lagoon, one stop from the Magic Kingdom on the complimentary Resort Monorail, with a pool with a waterslide and a spa. It is on Disney’s early-entry list. Choose it for the monorail ride to the Magic Kingdom. Watch out: Disney’s page carries a notice that the resort is undergoing refurbishments.
  • Disney’s Pop Century Resort, good value. A Value Resort at 1050 Century Drive styled on the fads of the 1950s to the 1990s, with the Hippy Dippy Pool and the Skyliner gondola to EPCOT and Disney’s Hollywood Studios. It is on Disney’s early-entry list. Choose it for Disney’s own transport at a lower category. Watch out: Disney’s page carries a notice that the resort is undergoing construction and refurbishment.
  • Drury Plaza Hotel Orlando - Disney Springs Area, good value. On Hotel Plaza Boulevard, half a mile from Disney Springs, an official Walt Disney World hotel by its own description, with free shuttles to all four Walt Disney World theme parks, a free hot breakfast, the free 5:30 Kickback with evening snacks, a saltwater pool and no resort fee. Disney’s early-entry page names a Drury Plaza Hotel Orlando Lake Buena Vista among its select other hotels. Choose it for breakfast and shuttles included. Watch out: parking is $28 a night. Universal Orlando: the parks at the door

Universal says guests at its hotels can enter one of its four theme parks up to an hour before opening with Early Park Admission; valid theme park admission is required, and only select attractions run. It names three hotels, Loews Portofino Bay Hotel, Hard Rock Hotel and Loews Royal Pacific Resort, as giving free Universal Express Unlimited.

  • Loews Portofino Bay Hotel, splurge. A replica of an Italian seaside village, with three pools and water taxis to the parks, by Universal’s account, and free Universal Express Unlimited. Choose it for the fullest set of Universal perks. Watch out: Early Park Admission covers select attractions only, and it needs valid admission. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Universal Cabana Bay Beach Resort, good value. Retro style inspired by classic Florida beach resorts of the 1950s and 60s, with two pools, a winding river, a food court and a bowling alley (fees apply). Universal lists it among its Value Inns and Suites. Choose it for a themed resort at a lower tier. Watch out: Universal’s list of free Universal Express Unlimited hotels does not include it. Check rates on Booking.com →

International Drive: the visitor strip

International Drive runs past ICON Park, Pointe Orlando and the Orange County Convention Center, so it suits conventions and a base with restaurants on the doorstep. It is not on the theme-park grounds: Rosen Centre puts Universal Orlando at 10 minutes (3.5 miles) and Walt Disney World at 14 minutes (6 miles).

Where to stay on International Drive

  • Rosen Centre Hotel, comfortable. At 9840 International Drive, with 1,254 deluxe rooms and 80 suites, connected to the Orange County Convention Center, a complimentary Universal Orlando shuttle that guests sign up for at the concierge desk, and no resort fee. Choose it for a big hotel at the convention centre. Watch out: self-parking is $40 plus tax. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Rosen Inn at Pointe Orlando, budget. Across International Drive from Pointe Orlando, a 20-minute walk or short drive from the convention centre and ICON Park, with three pools, no resort fees, and free scheduled shuttles to Universal Orlando Resort and SeaWorld as a Universal Partner Hotel. Choose it for a budget base on the strip. Watch out: transport to Disney is paid. Check rates on Booking.com →

Kissimmee: resorts with room to spread out

Kissimmee, the seat of Osceola County, is where the 13.5% rate applies. Its resorts trade distance for facilities: Margaritaville says it is 10 to 15 minutes from Walt Disney World and 20 to 25 minutes from SeaWorld and Universal, and the airport’s table lists a taxi to the Kissimmee hotels at $45 to $75.

Where to stay in Kissimmee

  • Gaylord Palms Resort & Convention Center, splurge. At 6000 West Osceola Parkway, with a 4.5-acre garden atrium, the Cypress Springs Water Park with seven waterslides, and a full-service spa. Choose it for a resort you do not need to leave. Watch out: Marriott’s page lists self-parking at $40 a day and valet at $55, and states no resort fee amount, so check the total before booking. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Margaritaville Resort Orlando, comfortable. At 8000 Fins Up Circle, with 265 guest rooms and suites, floor-to-ceiling windows and private balconies, plus cottages, and complimentary theme-park shuttles with reservations required. Choose it for a pool resort near Disney. Watch out: the taxable resort fee is $48 plus tax per room, per night, self-parking is $20 plus tax, check-in is for guests aged 21 and over, and the hotel has no direct airport shuttle. Check rates on Booking.com →

Downtown Orlando and Winter Park: a city break

Downtown Orlando holds Lake Eola, the Dr. Phillips Center for the Performing Arts and the Kia Center. Winter Park lies three miles north of Orlando, in Orange County, with Rollins College and brick-paved streets. Neither is near the theme parks: the airport’s table lists a taxi to downtown at $38 to $45 and to Winter Park at $45 to $55, and you will need a car or an app to reach Disney or Universal.

Where to stay in Downtown Orlando and Winter Park

  • The Alfond Inn, comfortable. At 300 East New England Avenue, Winter Park, a boutique hotel that opened in 2013, with contemporary art from the Rollins College collection installed throughout, a resort pool, and rooms of 323 sq ft (Classic King) to 358 sq ft (Classic Queen). Choose it for art and Winter Park’s streets. Watch out: the free house cars run only around Winter Park, from 11:00 to 20:00, first come first served. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Beauden, Autograph Collection, comfortable. At 325 South Orange Avenue, steps from the Dr. Phillips Center and the Kia Center, with the Altira Pool + Lounge, a rooftop, and nightly Steinway piano performances in the Garden Rotunda. Marriott’s pages show both Hotel Beauden and Grand Bohemian for this address, so search under either name. Choose it for the arts district. Watch out: valet parking is $42 a day. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Aloft Orlando Downtown, good value. At 500 South Orange Avenue, steps from City Hall, the Dr. Phillips Center and the Kia Center, with an outdoor pool, a bar with live music and a 24-hour pantry. Choose it for downtown at a lower price. Watch out: parking is valet only, at $30 a day. Check rates on Booking.com →

How to book Orlando

  • Count the tax. Add 12.5% in Orange County or 13.5% in Osceola: a $200 room costs $225 or $227 before any resort fee.
  • Check the fee and the parking. The hotels here that state them range from no resort fee to $48 a night, and parking from $20 to $55 a night.
  • Match the hotel to the parks. Early entry only helps if you hold valid admission, and Universal’s free Express goes with three hotels, not the rest.
  • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and direct booking against online agencies covers the choice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Orlando for a first trip?

For a first trip built around Disney, Walt Disney World and Lake Buena Vista: Disney says guests at its resort hotels, and at select other hotels, can enter its theme parks 30 minutes early every day of a stay, with valid admission. Universal Orlando suits Universal's parks, International Drive suits the convention centre and attractions, Kissimmee suits a resort with a water park, and Downtown Orlando or Winter Park suits a city break.

How much is the hotel tax in Orlando?

12.5% in Orange County, which holds Orlando, International Drive and Winter Park: 6% state sales tax, 0.5% county surtax and 6% Tourist Development Tax. In Osceola County, which holds Kissimmee, it is 13.5%, because the surtax is 1.5%. On a $200 room that is $225 or $227 a night, before any resort fee.

How do you get from Orlando airport to a Disney hotel?

Orlando International Airport lists approximate rates to the Disney resorts area: a local bus at $4, a shuttle van at $23 or $37 per person, and a taxi at $52 to $68. Ride-hailing pick-up is at the Arrivals curbs, Level 2 at Terminals A and B and Level 6 at Terminal C.

Do Disney and Universal hotels get early entry to the parks?

Yes, with valid admission. Disney says its resort hotels and select other hotels get 30-minute early entry every day of a stay. Universal says guests at its hotels can enter one of its four theme parks up to an hour before opening with Early Park Admission, for select attractions. Free Universal Express Unlimited goes with only three Universal hotels: Loews Portofino Bay, Hard Rock and Loews Royal Pacific.

Do Orlando hotels charge resort fees?

Some do. Of the 12 hotels in this guide, Margaritaville Resort Orlando states a taxable resort fee of $48 plus tax per room, per night, while Drury Plaza, Rosen Centre and Rosen Inn at Pointe Orlando say they charge none. Since 12 May 2025 a federal rule has required a mandatory resort fee to be included in the total price shown.

How much is the bus from Orlando airport?

A LYNX single ride is $2.00 and an all-day pass is $4.50, which you ask the operator for when boarding. The airport's table lists $2 to most areas and $4 to the Disney resorts area. The bus stops at Terminal A, Level 1.
