Where to Stay in Vienna (2026): Four Areas and 10 Hotels, Compared

By Casey, Gently Yonder editor

Choose the area based on your interests, then the hotel. Innere Stadt, Leopoldstadt, Landstraße and Mariahilf compared for a first stay, with 10 researched hotels and the trade-offs of each.

Last updated 17 Oct 2026 · 15 min read

Build My Trip Checklist All guides

Gently Yonder’s verdict: For a first trip, stay in Innere Stadt or the area around Stephansplatz to be in the heart of Vienna's imperial history. For a quieter, local experience, choose Mariahilf. Always prioritize a hotel within a ten-minute walk of a U-Bahn station (U1, U3, U4) for the easiest exploration.
