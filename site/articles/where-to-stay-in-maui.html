A Maui hotel room can carry up to 18.712% in tax: Hawaii’s 11% state tax, Maui County’s 3% tax and up to 4.712% in general excise tax. On a $300 room that is $56.14 a night, before any resort fee. Where you stay decides the rest of the bill, because Maui is a driving island and its resort areas are far apart.

This guide covers five areas, from Kaanapali to Paia and Upcountry, 12 hotels, the airport and the tax, with a careful note on Lahaina. Maui’s hotel tax

Three taxes apply to a hotel room on Maui:

Charge Rate
Hawaii transient accommodations tax (state) 11% of the room rate, from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2030
Maui County transient accommodations tax 3% of the room rate
General excise tax, with the Maui surcharge 4.5% a hotel owes; at most 4.712% may be added to your bill

The state rate was 10.25% until the end of 2025; Act 96 of 2025, known as the “green fee”, raised it. Maui County has charged its own 3% tax since 1 November 2021, and the County says it may be visibly passed on to the guest. The general excise tax is a business tax: it is 4% plus a 0.5% Maui County surcharge, in force from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2030, and 4.712% is the most a business may add to your bill. These are the same three layers as in our Honolulu guide, with a county tax of the same size.

Our arithmetic for a $300 room, if the hotel passes on everything: $33.00 state tax, $9.00 Maui County tax and $14.14 general excise tax, $56.14 in all, so $356.14 a night. Resort fees and parking are extra, and hotels are not required to list them in the same place as the room rate, so read the total.

Getting in from the airport, and around

  • Kahului Airport (OGG) is the main gateway. The airport’s page lists nine car-rental companies: Alamo, Avis, Budget, Dollar, Enterprise, Hertz, National, Sixt and Thrifty.
  • By Maui Bus, the County runs a public transit system that, in the airport’s words, provides limited service to the airport. Two routes stop there: Route 40, the Upcountry Islander, and Route 35, the Haiku Islander, each every 90 minutes. Luggage is limited to one medium-sized suitcase, duffle bag or carry-on that fits under your seat or on your lap.
  • The fare on the Maui Bus is $2 per boarding, and a monthly general pass is $45; no transfers are given on the airport routes.
  • A car is the practical choice: the airport routes are the Upcountry and Haiku lines only, so plan on hiring one and book it early.
  • Haleakala sunrise: the National Park Service requires a reservation to reach the summit between 3am and 7am. It costs $1.00 per vehicle, on top of the park entrance fee, and is booked on recreation.gov. Reservations open 60 days ahead, at 7am Hawaii time; without one, cars cannot enter the park until 7am.

    Kaanapali: the first-trip base

    Kaanapali is a resort strip along one long beach in West Maui. The hotels here carry Lahaina postal addresses, but they sit along the coast, not in the town itself. It is the easiest area for a first trip: the beach is at the door, and the resorts have their own restaurants.

    Kapalua and Napili: a small bay

    North of Kaanapali, Napili Bay has small resorts on it, with the Kapalua Coastal Trail and the Kapalua golf courses close by. It is a small-resort area, not a strip of big hotels.

    Where to stay in Kapalua and Napili

    • Napili Kai Beach Resort, comfortable. At 5900 Lower Honoapiilani Road, an oceanfront boutique resort in Napili Bay with one-bedroom suites and Ocean View Studios, the Sea House Restaurant and the Makai Hale Snack Bar. Choose it for a small beach resort. Watch out: it is a small property, and the page we read lists two places to eat on site, so plan to drive for variety. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Napili Surf Beach Resort, good value. Steps from Napili Beach, with recently renovated studios and one-bedrooms that have fully equipped kitchens, private lanais and air conditioning, and walking distance to dining and the Kapalua Coastal Trail. Choose it for a kitchen by the beach. Watch out: the draw is the kitchen, so budget for groceries and cooking time. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Wailea: polished resorts and calm water

    Wailea is South Maui’s resort district, with large and small hotels along the coast.

    Where to stay in Wailea

    • Grand Wailea, splurge. At 3850 Wailea Alanui Drive, with Nobu Grand Wailea among its restaurants, the Kilolani Spa & Salon and a lūʻau. Choose it for a big resort with everything on site. Watch out: the hotel’s offers say rates do not include taxes, gratuities, the daily resort fee, valet parking or incidentals, and the page does not show the fee’s amount. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Hotel Wailea, splurge. Set on 15 acres with just 72 suites, adults only, and The Birdcage Japanese Grill on site; the hotel calls itself Hawaii’s only Relais & Châteaux property. Choose it for quiet and no children. Watch out: it is adults only, so it is no choice for a family. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Kihei: kitchens and a lower price

    Kihei runs along South Kihei Road, on the south-western shore of Maui, with Wailea and Makena a short drive to the south by one hotel’s count. Several hotels here have kitchens.

    Where to stay in Kihei

    • Maui Coast Hotel, good value. In Kihei, a short stroll from Kamaole Beach Park I, with a pool, the classic Hōkū Tower and the Kai Tower, which opened in 2024. Choose it for a hotel with a pool near the beach. Watch out: the two towers differ, the Hōkū Tower classic and the Kai Tower modern, so check which you are booking. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Mana Kai Maui, comfortable. At 2960 South Kihei Road, on the mile-long Keawakapu Beach, with ocean-front condominiums and hotel-style units, and free parking, free Wi-Fi and maid service among its listed services. Choose it for the beach at the door. Watch out: the hotel’s notice says renovation work on its Beach House is in progress, with noise and some disruption. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Kohea Kai Maui, good value. At 551 South Kihei Road, with rooms and suites that have full-size kitchens and California king beds, and a penthouse suite with views of the pool. Choose it for a kitchen at a fair price. Watch out: Booking.com lists it as Kohea Kai Hotel Maui, so search both names. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Paia and Upcountry: surf-town streets and the road to Hana

    Paia is a surf town on the North Shore, on the way to the road to Hana, with galleries, boutiques and beaches. Makawao and Upcountry Maui sit inland. Neither has the resort strips of the south and west.

    Where to stay in Paia and Upcountry

    • Paia Inn, comfortable. A boutique hotel in the centre of Paia, with 500-thread-count sheets, air conditioning, mini fridges and in-room safes, and what it calls a private beach. Choose it for the town and the North Shore. Watch out: the page we read does not show prices or fees, so compare the total with the tax added. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Lumeria Maui, comfortable. At 1813 Baldwin Avenue in Makawao, an educational retreat centre with 24 guest rooms in a compound first built in 1910, the Wooden Crate restaurant and a spa. Choose it for a quiet, inland stay. Watch out: it is a retreat centre first, and its page says rooms are offered to students and visitors on retreat, so expect a drive to any beach. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Lahaina, and what the recovery means for a visit

    Lahaina is the town that burned in the August 2023 wildfire, and it is still being rebuilt. This guide names no hotel in the burned area. The County of Maui’s Office of Recovery said in July that Front Street would reopen to vehicles on 1 August 2026, from Pāpalaua to Shaw Street, with Dickenson and Prison streets. It also said Market Street, Papelekane Street and Mokuhinia Place would stay closed for ongoing work, including the state’s dredging of Lahaina Harbor.

    The County says that rebuilding throughout the commercial district will continue, that temporary road closures may still be needed, and that for safety the public is encouraged to stay off private property and out of fenced-off areas. We have not seen a County update confirming how the reopening went, so check the County’s recovery site, Maui Recovers, before you go. Treat Lahaina as a town where people live and are rebuilding.

    How to book Maui

    • Count the tax. Add up to 18.712% to every room rate: a $300 room costs up to $356.14 a night.
    • Read the resort fee. Only two hotels here show one on their own pages: Grand Wailea says its rates exclude a daily resort fee and valet parking, and Royal Lahaina mentions resort fees in its offers. Neither page shows the amount, so ask before you book. For every other hotel, ask what is charged on arrival.
    • Check the unit. Several picks are condominium-style, with kitchens, and some hotels have two towers or more than one room category; read which one the price covers.
    • Book the car and the sunrise first. Without a car the resort areas are hard to reach from the airport, and the Haleakala sunrise reservation opens 60 days ahead.
    • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best area to stay in Maui for a first trip?

    Kaanapali, in West Maui: one long beach with resorts along it and restaurants on site. Choose Wailea for polished South Maui resorts, Kihei for a kitchen at a lower price, Kapalua and Napili for a small bay, and Paia for surf-town streets and the road to Hana.

    How much is the hotel tax in Maui?

    Up to 18.712% of the room rate: Hawaii's 11% state transient accommodations tax from 1 January 2026, Maui County's 3% tax, and general excise tax, at most 4.712% if the hotel passes it on. On a $300 room that is $56.14 a night, our arithmetic, before any resort fee.

    Do you need a car on Maui?

    Plan on one. The Maui Bus gives only limited service to Kahului Airport, on the Upcountry Islander and Haiku Islander routes every 90 minutes, with a luggage limit of one medium suitcase or bag. The airport lists nine car-rental companies.

    Is Lahaina open to visitors?

    The County of Maui said Front Street would reopen to vehicles on 1 August 2026, while some streets near the harbour would stay closed for work. Rebuilding continues, and the County asks the public to stay off private property and out of fenced-off areas. Check Maui Recovers before you go.

    Do you need a reservation for the Haleakala sunrise?

    Yes, to reach the summit between 3am and 7am. It costs $1.00 per vehicle on top of the park entrance fee, is booked on recreation.gov, and opens 60 days ahead at 7am Hawaii time. Without one, cars cannot enter the park until 7am.

    Do Maui hotels charge resort fees?

    Some do. Grand Wailea says its rates exclude a daily resort fee and valet parking, and Royal Lahaina mentions resort fees in its offers; neither page shows the amount. Ask every hotel what is charged on arrival and compare totals.
