Florence is a city best explored on foot. For travelers, this means there is no single "center" that fits all interests. Where you sleep determines whether you wake up to artisan workshops, bustling markets, or Renaissance masterpieces — and how many cobblestone streets you walk to reach the next wonder. Choosing the right neighbourhood is the single most important logistical decision of your trip.

First, choose the area

Florence's historic center is compact but distinct. To make your sightseeing stress-free, prioritize hotels near these key zones:

  1. The Duomo zone: The absolute core for the cathedral, baptistery, and campanile.
  2. Oltrarno: Across the Arno River, known for artisan workshops and piazza Santo Spirito.
  3. San Lorenzo: The market district north of the Duomo, with the Medicean Chapels.
  4. Santa Maria Novella: The train station area west of the historic center.

Being within a ten-minute walk of the Duomo will save you hours over a week-long trip.

Oltrarno: artisan and authentic

Oltrarno is Florence's most beloved neighborhood for its atmosphere. You are on the south bank of the Arno, surrounded by artisan workshops, ivy-clad buildings, and restaurants that have served locals for generations. It is connected to the center via several bridges, most notably the Ponte Vecchio.

Santa Maria Novella: station area

Santa Maria Novella is where you find the main train station, conveniently located just west of the historic center. It is one of Florence's most practical neighbourhoods, filled with business hotels and quick-bite eateries.

San Lorenzo: market and tradition

San Lorenzo is where you find the bustling San Lorenzo Market, the Medici Chapels, and some of Florence's best trattorias. It's the best area for those who want to eat like a local and avoid the tourist crowds of the Duomo.

Duomo: the heart of Florence

The Duomo area is where you find the cathedral, baptistery, Giotto's Campanile, and the Piazza del Duomo. It's the best area for those who want to be immersed in Renaissance art and architecture from the moment they wake up.
