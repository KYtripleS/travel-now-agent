Where to Stay in Sapporo for the Snow Festival 2027: Areas, Dates and When to Book

By Casey, Gently Yonder editor

The 2027 Sapporo Snow Festival runs from 4 to 11 February at Odori, Susukino and Tsudome. Four areas by what you can walk to, 10 hotels, Sapporo's new hotel tax, and one hotel that closes after the festival.

Choose Sapporo Station for the airport train, Susukino for the ice sculptures and late nights, and Nakajima Park for quiet. The dates are fixed: book now.

The 77th Sapporo Snow Festival runs from 4 to 11 February 2027, the dates its organisers announced in July 2026, at three sites: Odori Park, Susukino and the Tsudome dome in the east of the city. In 2026 the Odori and Tsudome sites drew 2.539 million visits between them, 212,000 more than the year before. It all happens in the coldest month: by the Japan Meteorological Agency’s normals, a February day in Sapporo peaks at 0.4°C and drops to −6.2°C at night.

The 2027 festival: dates, sites and hours

All three sites open from Thursday 4 February to Thursday 11 February 2027, a national holiday, eight days in all:

Site Where Hours Getting there
Odori Odori Park, from West 1 to West 11 Lit until 10pm Odori Station, where all three subway lines meet
Susukino Ekimae-dori, from South 4 to South 6 Lit until 11pm Susukino Station (Namboku line)
Tsudome The Tsudome sports dome, Higashi-ku 10am to 4pm Sakaemachi Station (Toho line), then a 15-minute walk or the shuttle bus

The Odori site has the five large snow sculptures, at West 4, 5, 7, 8 and 10, with smaller ones between them and the International Snow Sculpture Contest at West 11. Tsudome is the family site, with slides and snow rafting, and has no public parking. The organisers say the full 2027 programme will be posted from mid-December 2026.

Odori: the sculptures outside the door

The Odori site fills the park from West 1, below the TV Tower, to West 11. Stay here and you can walk the sculptures early or late, and be back in your room in minutes when the cold gets to you. The Sapporo Ekimae-dori underground walkway runs north from Odori to Sapporo Station, so the trains are a walk away without going outside.

Sapporo Station: the airport train and a covered walk

Sapporo Station has the JR trains to New Chitose Airport: the fastest take 33 minutes, and in the middle of the day six an hour run between them. The underground walkway runs south to Odori. If you are flying in and out around the festival, this is the base that keeps the cold walks short. The area around the station is being redeveloped, so expect building works.

Where to stay at Sapporo Station

Susukino: the ice sculptures and late nights

Susukino is Sapporo’s eating and drinking district, and the festival’s ice sculptures line Ekimae-dori between South 4 and South 6, lit until 11pm. It is one stop south of Odori on the Namboku line, and an easy walk from it.

Where to stay in Susukino

  • Mercure Sapporo, comfortable. 285 rooms at South 4 West 2, a minute from Hosui-Susukino Station and three from Susukino Station, a block from the ice sculptures. Choose it for the ice and the restaurants on foot. Watch out: Susukino is busy late at night. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • OMO3 Sapporo Susukino by Hoshino Resorts, good value. 226 rooms, opened on 28 January 2022, about five minutes from Susukino Station, and built around the idea of “happy late nights” in the neighbourhood. Choose it for a friendly base for eating out. Watch out: it is on the west side of Susukino, a few blocks from the ice sculptures.

Nakajima Park: a quieter base

South of Susukino, Nakajima Park is quieter, one stop further on the Namboku line, and still a walk from the ice sculptures.

  • Sapporo Park Hotel, comfortable. Opened on 10 July 1964, with 216 rooms beside Nakajima Park, a minute from Nakajima-koen Station. It closes on 28 February 2027 to be rebuilt, so the 2027 festival is the last in this building. Choose it for calm after the festival crowds, and a last stay in a 1964 hotel. Watch out: you ride or walk to the sculptures. Check rates on Booking.com →

How to book festival week

  • Book now, with free cancellation. The dates are fixed, so there is no waiting for a forecast; check each rate’s cancellation deadline.
  • Count the tax. Since 1 April 2026, Sapporo and Hokkaido both charge an accommodation tax, together ¥300 a person a night on rooms under ¥20,000, ¥400 from ¥20,000 and ¥1,000 from ¥50,000. It applies to stays from that date even if you booked earlier. See Japan’s tourist taxes in 2026 for the rest of the country.
  • Give it three nights or more. Tsudome closes at 4pm and the two city sites are lit late, so one day will not cover them.
  • Stay near the subway. The three lines meet at Odori; a hotel near a station keeps the walks short when it is −6°C at night.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Sapporo Snow Festival in 2027?

From Thursday 4 February to Thursday 11 February 2027, eight days, at all three sites: Odori, Susukino and Tsudome. The organisers announced the dates in July 2026 and say the full programme will be posted from mid-December 2026.

What is the best area to stay in Sapporo for the Snow Festival?

Odori, if you want the snow sculptures outside the door: the Odori site runs along Odori Park from West 1 to West 11, and all three subway lines meet at Odori Station. Sapporo Station suits travellers flying in and out, and Susukino suits the ice sculptures and late nights.

How cold is Sapporo during the Snow Festival?

By the Japan Meteorological Agency's 1991–2020 normals, a February day in Sapporo peaks at 0.4°C and falls to −6.2°C at night, with 116 cm of snowfall over the month.

What are the Sapporo Snow Festival opening hours?

The Odori site is lit until 10pm and the Susukino site until 11pm. The Tsudome site, in Higashi-ku, is open from 10am to 4pm and is reached from Sakaemachi Station on the Toho line, by a 15-minute walk or the shuttle bus.

Does Sapporo have a hotel tax?

Yes, since 1 April 2026. Sapporo and Hokkaido each charge one, together ¥300 a person a night on rooms under ¥20,000, ¥400 from ¥20,000 and ¥1,000 from ¥50,000. It applies to stays from that date even if you booked earlier.

How do you get from New Chitose Airport to Sapporo?

By JR train to Sapporo Station: the fastest take 33 minutes, and in the middle of the day six run each hour.
