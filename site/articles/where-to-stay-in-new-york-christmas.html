New York’s Christmas sights cluster in Midtown: the Rockefeller Center tree will be lit on Wednesday 2 December 2026, and its own site says it stays on view through mid-January 2027. Radio City Music Hall’s Christmas Spectacular runs through 4 January, and the Winter Village at Bryant Park has its rink and shops open from 28 October. The hotel bill adds 14.75% in tax on the room plus $3.50 a room a night. Our expectation, not a measured figure, is that December is the dearest month to sleep in Midtown; we checked no live rates, so we quote none.

Each date below comes from the organiser’s own site.

Rockefeller Center’s page says the 2026 tree is a 76-foot Norway spruce from West Edmeston, New York, lit with more than 50,000 multicolored LED lights beneath a Swarovski star. Bryant Park’s page describes the rink as 17,000 square feet, and says the Holiday Shops are a European-inspired open-air market. Some Winter Village experiences, such as the igloos and the Overlook, are booked separately, and the rink listing notes tickets for peak dates.

Three things are not settled. We could not find a first performance date for the Christmas Spectacular on the Rockettes’ page, only the closing date, so check the box office before you plan. We also did not confirm how crowds are managed on the night of the lighting, so check Rockefeller Center’s page nearer the date. And we have left Dyker Heights, the Brooklyn neighbourhood known for house displays, out of the dates because we did not find an official or city source for them. New York’s hotel tax

Three taxes and a fee apply to every hotel room in the city, according to the NYC Department of Finance and New York State:

Item Rate
New York State and City sales tax 8.875% of the room rate
New York City hotel room occupancy tax 5.875% of the room rate
New York City charge on rooms of $40 or more $2.00 a room a night
New York State hotel unit fee $1.50 a room a night

Together that is 14.75% of the room rate plus $3.50 a room a night. The state says the invoice must show the taxes and the unit fee separately, so compare totals, not headline prices.

Our arithmetic, for illustration only: a $400 room carries $59 in percentage tax plus $3.50, so it costs $462.50 a night, and four nights from 2 to 6 December come to $1,850 before extras.

Getting in from the airports, and around

  • From JFK, the AirTrain runs to Jamaica or Howard Beach stations for $8.75, and from there the subway or the Long Island Rail Road continues into Manhattan.
  • From LaGuardia, the Q70 LaGuardia Link bus is free from Terminals B and C to the Jackson Heights–Roosevelt Avenue and 74 St–Broadway subway hubs.
  • From Newark, the AirTrain to the airport’s rail station costs $8.75 unless you already hold an NJ Transit or Amtrak ticket; the train then runs to Manhattan.
  • In town, a subway or local bus ride is $3, paid by tapping a contactless card or phone.
  • By taxi or app, rides into, out of or within the Congestion Relief Zone, Manhattan at and below 60th Street, carry a per-trip charge: $0.75 in a yellow or green taxi and $1.50 in an app-based car. Midtown’s Christmas streets are inside it.

    How to book New York at Christmas

    • Book early and read the terms. We did not check live availability or prices; the tiers reflect category and facilities, not rates.
    • Count the tax. Add 14.75% and $3.50 a night to every room rate: a $400 room costs $462.50.
    • Check the dates. The tree is lit on 2 December and the Rockettes run through 4 January; a stay before the lighting misses the ceremony but not the tree.
    • Check room size. Midtown rooms can be small, so look at the room type if you have luggage.
    • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is the Rockefeller Center Christmas tree lit in 2026?

    Rockefeller Center says the Tree Lighting Ceremony is on Wednesday 2 December 2026, from 7:00 to 10:00pm. The tree arrives on 7 November and, per its page, stays on view through mid-January 2027.

    When is the Bryant Park Winter Village in 2026?

    Bryant Park lists the Bank of America Winter Village rink from 28 October 2026 to 28 February 2027, with free-admission skating and rental skates, and the Holiday Shops from 28 October 2026 to 3 January 2027.

    How long does the Radio City Christmas Spectacular run?

    The Rockettes' page says the Christmas Spectacular is at Radio City Music Hall through 4 January. We did not find a first performance date on that page, so check the box office.

    What is the best area to stay for Christmas in New York?

    Midtown, around Rockefeller Center and Fifth Avenue, puts the tree and Radio City on foot, with the Warwick, the Peninsula and the Lotte New York Palace. Bryant Park and Times Square suit the rink and Broadway.

    What taxes are added to a New York hotel room in 2026?

    The sources give 8.875% State and City sales tax, 5.875% City hotel occupancy tax, $2.00 a room a night on rooms of $40 or more and a $1.50 State unit fee: 14.75% plus $3.50 a room a night. A $400 room comes to $462.50 a night.

    Is there a cheaper way to stay near the Christmas sights?

    Midtown West has lower tiers: Arlo Midtown and Moxy NYC Times Square as good value, Pod Times Square and YOTEL New York Times Square as budget picks. YOTEL's own FAQ puts Times Square at a 15 to 20 minute walk.
