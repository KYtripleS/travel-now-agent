Paris at Christmas is not a German or Alsace market town. The official listings describe a market of 18 chalets in the Tuileries Garden with a Ferris wheel and a rink, one very large village of 300 to 350 chalets at La Défense, and a scatter of smaller markets, plus the department-store windows and the lights. Several museums are shut on 25 December, and the hotel bill carries a per-person, per-night tourist tax that went up on 1 January 2026.

This guide covers five areas, 12 hotels, the 2026 market dates, the closures on 25 December, the tax and the way in from the airports. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in November 2026. No hotel pays to appear here.

Christmas 2026 in Paris: dates and what is on

Paris je t’aime, the city’s tourist office, lists the 2026 markets, and the Ville de Paris gives the La Défense details. We use only dates an official page prints.

What Dates and hours published
La Magie de Noël, Tuileries Garden 14 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, every day 11:00 to 23:45; 18 chalets, a food market, rides, a 250 m² rink and a Ferris wheel
Champs-Élysées market 22 November to 4 January 2027, avenue des Champs-Élysées
Notre-Dame market 26 November to 27 December 2026, Square René Viviani
Place Saint-Michel market 4 December 2026 to 4 January 2027
Place de l’Étoile and Grande Armée market 20 November to 23 December 2026
La Défense, Parvis 12 November to 31 December 2026 per the Ville de Paris, with 300 to 350 chalets; its “Le Sommet” rink and festive area runs to 3 January 2027
Galeries Lafayette Haussmann Windows and tree from 12 November 2026, rooftop rink from 24 November to 3 January 2027 (press reports; see below)

Two caveats. The tourist office’s own page mentions a Saint-Germain-des-Prés market but we read no 2026 date for it, so we give none. And the Galeries Lafayette dates come from a Paris press report, because the store’s own pages would not load for us; the store’s opening hours on 24, 25 and 31 December are not something we confirmed, so check them before planning a day around the windows.

What is closed on 25 December

The two big museums are both shut on Christmas Day, which falls on a Friday in 2026.

  • The Louvre. Its page says the museum is closed on 1 January, 1 May and 25 December. It is closed every Tuesday, and open 9:00 to 18:00 on most days and to 21:00 on Wednesdays and Fridays. It also says booking is compulsory for everyone from 19 December 2026 to 4 January 2027. It gave no special hours for 24 or 31 December.
  • The Musée d’Orsay. Closed every Monday, on 1 May and on 25 December; open 9:30 to 18:00, with a late opening on Thursdays to 21:45.
  • Transport. We could not find a festive timetable from RATP for 24 or 25 December 2026, so confirm the service on the RATP app or site before you plan an evening. Do not assume the last metro.

The practical effect: put the Tuileries market, the Notre-Dame market (open to 27 December), a walk along the avenue and a long lunch on the 25th, and take the museums on the 24th or 26th. If you stay on to 31 December, our New Year’s Eve guide covers that night.

Paris’s hotel tax: the taxe de séjour in 2026

France’s service-public site says the Paris tourist tax changed on 1 January 2026. It is charged per adult, per night, and the totals below include the additional taxes.

Category Total per adult per night, 2026
Palace €15.93
5 stars €11.70
4 stars €8.45
3 stars €5.53
2 stars €3.25
1 star, guest rooms, collective inns €2.60
Unclassified or pending 5% of the per-person nightly cost before tax, plus additional taxes, capped at €15.93

Our arithmetic, for illustration only: two adults in a 4-star hotel for four nights, 23 to 27 December, pay 2 × 4 × €8.45 = €67.60 in tax, usually collected by the hotel on top of the room price or at the desk. The same stay at a palace comes to 2 × 4 × €15.93 = €127.44. The service-public table is for adults (“personne majeure”); it does not say whether children are exempt, so ask the hotel. Check whether a booking site’s total already includes the tax.

Getting in from the airports, and around

Île-de-France Mobilités sells one ticket for both airports: the Paris Région <> Aéroports ticket, €14 a journey (€7 reduced), from 1 January 2026. Orly is reached by metro line 14 or Orlyval, and Charles de Gaulle by RER B. It loads on a Navigo Easy pass or in the official app. We did not confirm times for 24 and 25 December, so check before a late arrival.

    How to book Paris at Christmas

    • Count the tax. It is per adult per night, up to €15.93 at a palace.
    • Check the closures. The Louvre and Orsay are shut on 25 December; Louvre booking is compulsory from 19 December.
    • Check the market dates. The Notre-Dame market ends on 27 December and Place de l’Étoile on 23 December.
    • Check the metro. We found no festive timetable for 24 to 25 December.
    • Check room size. Several city-centre rooms are small.
    • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and direct booking versus online agencies covers when to go direct.

    Frequently asked questions

    When are the Paris Christmas markets in 2026?

    Paris je t'aime lists the Tuileries market from 14 November 2026 to 3 January 2027 (daily 11:00 to 23:45), the Champs-Élysées market from 22 November to 4 January, Notre-Dame from 26 November to 27 December and Place Saint-Michel from 4 December to 4 January. The Ville de Paris gives La Défense as 12 November to 31 December 2026.

    What is the best area to stay for the Paris Christmas markets?

    Tuileries and the Louvre puts you beside the Tuileries market, with Hôtel Regina Louvre and Hôtel Brighton facing the garden. Champs-Élysées and Concorde suits the avenue's market, and the Left Bank is nearest the Notre-Dame market, though we found no official walking times.

    Are the Louvre and Musée d'Orsay open on 25 December?

    No. The Louvre says it is closed on 1 January, 1 May and 25 December, and the Musée d'Orsay is closed every Monday, on 1 May and on 25 December. The Louvre also says booking is compulsory for everyone from 19 December 2026 to 4 January 2027.

    How much is the Paris tourist tax in 2026?

    Service-public says the 2026 total per adult per night is €15.93 for a palace, €11.70 for 5 stars, €8.45 for 4 stars, €5.53 for 3 stars and €3.25 for 2 stars. By our arithmetic, two adults in a 4-star hotel for four nights pay €67.60.

    How do I get from the Paris airports to the centre?

    Île-de-France Mobilités sells one Paris Région <> Aéroports ticket at €14 a journey (€7 reduced) from 1 January 2026. Orly is reached by metro line 14 or Orlyval and Charles de Gaulle by RER B.

    Does the metro run normally on 24 and 25 December 2026?

    We could not confirm it. We found no festive RATP timetable for those dates, so check the RATP app or site before planning an evening.
