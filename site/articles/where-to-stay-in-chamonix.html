Hotels around Chamonix centre, with prices

The map shows the hotels and apartments around Chamonix centre that booking sites such as Booking.com and Expedia list, with nightly prices; our own picks follow further down. It comes from our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book through it.

Chamonix is a valley, not a single base, and the lifts sit in different villages, so where you sleep decides how much of your ski day is spent on a bus. The hotel bill also carries the taxe de séjour, a per-person, per-night tourist tax that the valley’s local authority sets by star rating: €2.60 per adult per night for a four-star hotel in 2026, added on top of the room rate. The lift operator’s own live page on 30 October 2026 shows the main areas reopening between 28 November and 19 December.

This guide covers the lifts and the season, five areas, 12 hotels from a two-star chalet hotel to a five-star Relais & Châteaux estate, the tax and the guest card, and the way in from Geneva. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked on its own website in October 2026. Where a hotel publishes winter dates we give them; where it does not, we say so, because some are closed this month or open only from December. No hotel pays to appear here.

The 2026/27 season and the lift pass

Mont-Blanc Natural Resort, the Compagnie du Mont-Blanc’s visitor site, had not published a 2026/27 winter brochure or day-pass prices when we looked on 30 October 2026: its winter section still pointed to the 2025/26 press kit. What it does show is a live status page, and that page gives these reopening dates, all subject to snow.

Area Reopening on the operator’s live page
Flégère (Les Praz side) Partial reopening on 28 November, snow permitting
Grands Montets (Argentière) 5 December
Balme (Le Tour and Vallorcine gondolas) 12 December
Les Houches - Saint-Gervais 19 December
Brévent No winter date given; its lifts were on autumn opening hours on 30 October

Two season-pass products are on sale in a limited offer running from 20 October to 30 November 2026. CHAMONIX Le Pass covers Brévent-Flégère, Balme and the Grands Montets, plus the beginner areas. MONT BLANC Unlimited adds Les Houches - Saint-Gervais, Evasion Mont Blanc and sites including Aiguille du Midi, and its adult season pass is listed at €1,466 in the offer. We saw no 2026/27 day-pass price.

Chamonix centre: the main street and the buses

The centre puts you among the shops, restaurants and the SNCF station, with the Chamonix Mobilité bus station here too. The cable car to the Aiguille du Midi also leaves from the centre. Several hotels sit along the Allée du Majestic by the tourist office, and the Park Hôtel Suisse & Spa puts the bus stops for the villages and ski areas under 100 m from its door.

Le Bouchet and Les Praz: the quieter edge

Le Hameau Albert 1er is on the Route du Bouchet and calls itself close to the centre of Chamonix; Les Praz is the hamlet towards Argentière, with its own station on the Mont-Blanc Express and the Flégère lifts above it. Both suit a stay where you ski the Flégère side and come home to quiet.

Where to stay in Le Bouchet and Les Praz

  • Le Hameau Albert 1er, splurge. 38 route du Bouchet, a five-star Relais & Châteaux hotel on a 2.5-acre estate that it describes as close to the centre of Chamonix. Rooms run from 20 m² to 47 m², with two 70 m² family suites, and it has a heated indoor-outdoor pool, a hammam and a jacuzzi. Choose it for an estate with space around it. Watch out: its site says it is closed from 1 November to 4 December 2026, so it is a December-onwards choice. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hôtel Le Labrador, good value. 101 Route du Golf, Les Praz, a three-star hotel that puts itself 250 m from the Flégère lifts and about 3 km from the centre, with the Les Praz station 500 m away. Rooms start at 17 m², and a spa with a jacuzzi, sauna and hammam opens from 16:00 to 21:30. Choose it for the Flégère lifts at a lower price. Watch out: it is 3 km from the centre, and its site gives no 2026/27 winter dates. Check rates on Booking.com →

Argentière: the Grands Montets lift

Argentière is the village at the foot of the Grands Montets, the area the operator lists as reopening on 5 December. It sits on the Mont-Blanc Express line, so the valley’s other villages are a train ride away.

Where to stay in Argentière

  • Les Grands Montets Hotel & Spa, comfortable. 340 chemin des Arbérons, a four-star hotel with 39 rooms, an indoor pool, a sauna, a steam room and an outdoor hot tub. Its access page puts it at the start of the car park for the Grands Montets cable car and ten minutes’ walk from the village; skibus 15 runs from the stop opposite the station. Its winter 2027 ski package includes one ski pass per person per night, with 10% off the room, for direct bookings only, and a €3 fee per card. Choose it to be beside the Grands Montets lift. Watch out: its offers page quotes a local tax of €2.50, against €2.60 in the 2026 grid for four-star hotels, and the lifts reopen on 5 December. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hôtel La Couronne, good value. 285 rue Charlet Straton, a three-star hotel built around 1863 to 1865 that says it is the oldest hotel complex in the valley still operating. It has 69 rooms of 15 to 23 m², a sauna and hammam for guests, a ski and bike room, and a paid underground car park of about 30 spaces, and reception is open 24 hours. Choose it for a village base with a restaurant on site. Watch out: its homepage still carries 2025 details, so confirm winter 2026/27 opening and rates. Check rates on Booking.com →

Les Houches and Les Bossons: free parking and a ski shuttle

Les Bossons and Les Houches lie on the Saint-Gervais side of the valley, on the same railway line as the centre. Les Houches reopens last, on 19 December, so this side suits a Christmas or January trip.

Where to stay in Les Houches and Les Bossons

  • Hôtel Aiguille du Midi, good value. 479 chemin Napoléon, Les Bossons, a Logis three-star hotel with 40 rooms and an apartment, the Alpine rooms being 19 to 26 m², and a sauna, bubbling baths and a massage room. Its winter opening is 18 December 2026 to 29 March 2027, and its FAQ puts the bus and train stops 150 m away, the Les Houches and Brévent-Flégère areas about 5 km off, and parking free. Choose it for free parking and a quiet village. Watch out: it is closed until 18 December, and the outdoor pool runs only from June to September. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Chalet Hôtel Les Campanules, budget. 450 route de Coupeau, Les Houches, a two-star chalet hotel with 43 rooms for one to four people, 400 m from the Les Houches station. It says it is open all year, with free parking, a sauna and hammam, a free shuttle for skiers to the Les Houches slopes, and ski passes sold at reception. Choose it for the lowest-priced Les Houches base with a shuttle. Watch out: it does not publish shuttle times, and it is not listed on Booking.com.
  • RockyPop Chamonix - Les Houches, budget. 1476 avenue des Alpages, Les Houches, a hotel with a ski shop, a brasserie with a terrace, a cocktail bar, live concerts, games and free parking. Rooms sleep two up to six, and a penthouse sleeps 12. Choose it for a lively base for groups on a budget. Watch out: the live music and bar make it the least quiet pick here, and its site gives no distance to the lifts. Check rates on Booking.com →

Le Tour and Vallorcine: the far end

Balme is the ski area at the northern end, reached by gondola from Le Tour or from Vallorcine, and the operator lists both gondolas as reopening on 12 December. We found hotels in Vallorcine but none whose own website confirmed winter 2026/27 opening, so we have left them out. The guest card covers the Mont-Blanc Express to Vallorcine, so a day out from the centre works.

The taxe de séjour and the guest card

The taxe de séjour is set by the Communauté de communes de la Vallée de Chamonix Mont-Blanc, which covers Chamonix-Mont-Blanc, Les Houches, Servoz and Vallorcine, in a deliberation of 3 June 2025 that applies from 1 January 2026. It is charged per person per night, and your host collects it on top of the room rate.

Accommodation Tax per person per night, 2026
Palace €4.90
5-star hotel €3.60
4-star hotel €2.60
3-star hotel €1.70
2-star hotel €1.00
1-star hotel, guest house €0.80
Unclassified or awaiting classification 5% of the pre-tax nightly cost per person, capped at €4.90

Minors, seasonal workers employed in the commune, people in emergency housing and people occupying premises with rent under €5 a night are exempt. The Communauté’s grid, deliberation and poster list these rates and no departmental surcharge. We did not find a Haute-Savoie decision on one, so check your invoice against the grid. We read the 2026 grid; a grid for stays from January 2027 was not on the page we read, so check it for a trip then.

A worked example, our arithmetic: two adults and a ten-year-old for four nights in a four-star hotel pay 2 × €2.60 × 4 = €20.80, because the child is exempt. On a room we have assumed at €400 a night, four nights come to €1,600, so the tax is 1.3% of the bill. In an unclassified hotel with a room at an assumed €300 before tax for two, the per-person cost is €150, 5% of that is €7.50, the cap of €4.90 applies, and the tax is 2 × €4.90 × 4 = €39.20. See hotel taxes around the world and the 2026 tourist-tax data report for comparisons.

The guest card, the carte d’hôte, is handed to you by your accommodation host on arrival. The tourist office says it gives free travel on the SNCF Mont-Blanc Express between Servoz and Vallorcine while the card is valid, and discounts of up to 50% on Chamonix Mobilité bus passes. It is not a bus ticket, it has no discount on single tickets or on night buses after 21:00, and each traveller needs one except children under 4. The tourist office’s examples are €5 for a 2-day bus ticket, €8 for 5 days and €10 for a week. If you hold a valid lift pass, you scan that on the bus and need no ticket.

Getting to Chamonix and around

Geneva Airport is the usual gateway, 88 km away according to the tourist office. The office adds that winter rules apply on the road: from 1 November to 31 March, light vehicles must carry snow chains or run four winter tyres.

  • Airport transfer. Mountain Drop-offs lists shared door-to-door transfers from Geneva Airport from €45.50 per person, a journey of around 1 hour 15 minutes, all year round. Alpybus offers shared and private transfers, and its booking form lists Les Houches, Les Praz, Argentière, Le Tour and Vallorcine as destinations. The tourist office names FlixBus as running daily between Geneva Airport and Chamonix.
  • By train. The tourist office gives Geneva Cornavin to Chamonix as about 2 hours 40 minutes by rail, and says the Léman Express links Geneva Airport to Saint-Gervais-Le Fayet, where the Mont-Blanc Express takes over. The Mont-Blanc Express serves Les Bossons, Les Houches and Servoz one way, and Les Praz, Argentière and Vallorcine the other. The tourist office lists SNCF timetables for 14 September to 12 December 2026 and works timetables for 28 September to 6 November; we could not open SNCF’s own pages, so check frequencies there. If you break the journey in Switzerland, our Zurich guide covers hotels, and a Europe rail pass may pay on several rail days.
  • Once there. Chamonix Mobilité runs the valley buses, including SKIBUS, PROXIBUS and CHAMONUIT lines, and validation is compulsory every time you board.

    How to book Chamonix

    • Count the tax. Add €2.60 per adult per night for a four-star hotel, less for lower categories, more for five-star and palace.
    • Match the hotel to the date. Hameau Albert 1er is closed to 4 December and Hôtel Aiguille du Midi opens on 18 December, while Flégère, Grands Montets, Balme and Les Houches reopen on 28 November, 5, 12 and 19 December.
    • Ask for the guest card. It is free from your host and saves on the valley buses and trains.
    • Check room size. Standard rooms are 19 m² at Hôtel Mont-Blanc, 17 m² at Heliopic and 10 to 11 m² for a single at Hôtel Lyret.
    • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book; Zermatt, Whistler and Paris are other guides in this series.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best area to stay in Chamonix for skiing?

    It depends on the lift. Chamonix centre puts you among the restaurants, the station and the bus station, Le Bouchet and Les Praz are the quieter edge with the Flégère lifts close, Argentière has hotels at the foot of the Grands Montets, and Les Houches and Les Bossons have hotels with free parking or a ski shuttle. We could not confirm a winter 2026/27 hotel opening on its own website in Le Tour or Vallorcine, so none is listed there.

    When do the Chamonix ski areas open in 2026/27?

    The operator had not published a 2026/27 winter brochure on 30 October 2026, but its live status page gave these reopening dates, all subject to snow: partial reopening of Flégère on 28 November, Grands Montets on 5 December, Balme on 12 December and Les Houches - Saint-Gervais on 19 December. It gave no winter date for Brévent.

    How much is a Chamonix ski pass in 2026/27?

    We saw no 2026/27 day-pass price, because the operator had not published its winter brochure. Its limited offer from 20 October to 30 November 2026 lists the adult MONT BLANC Unlimited season pass at €1,466. CHAMONIX Le Pass covers Brévent-Flégère, Balme and the Grands Montets.

    How much is the tourist tax in Chamonix?

    The 2026 taxe de séjour is set per person per night by star category: €4.90 for a Palace, €3.60 for five stars, €2.60 for four stars, €1.70 for three, €1.00 for two and €0.80 for one star. Unclassified accommodation pays 5% of the pre-tax nightly cost per person, capped at €4.90. Minors are exempt, so two adults and a ten-year-old for four nights in a four-star hotel pay €20.80, by our arithmetic.

    What does the Chamonix guest card give you?

    The carte d'hôte is handed over by your accommodation host on arrival. The tourist office says it gives free travel on the SNCF Mont-Blanc Express between Servoz and Vallorcine and discounts of up to 50% on Chamonix Mobilité bus passes. It is not a bus ticket, and it gives no discount on single tickets or night buses after 21:00.

    How do you get from Geneva Airport to Chamonix?

    Geneva Airport is 88 km away, according to the tourist office. Mountain Drop-offs lists shared door-to-door transfers from €45.50 per person, taking around 1 hour 15 minutes, and the tourist office names FlixBus as running daily. By rail, the Léman Express links the airport to Saint-Gervais-Le Fayet, where the Mont-Blanc Express continues to Chamonix.
