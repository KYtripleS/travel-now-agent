Flight

Long flights are more bearable with a proper fatigue strategy.

Before you go: checklist

  1. Set your sleep schedule a few days before departure
  2. Wear compression socks and stay hydrated
  3. Move your legs and adjust your seatbelt regularly
  4. Use a neck pillow and eye mask for quality rest

Why this helps

Small preparation steps before a trip may reduce avoidable stress and make travel days feel smoother.
