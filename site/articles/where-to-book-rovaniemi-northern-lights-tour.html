Three things separate one Rovaniemi aurora tour from the next, and none of them is the headline price: how the tour treats a cloudy night, how many people share the minibus, and whether the winter overalls are included. The same evening can cost anywhere from about €79 to about €200 a head, and the cheap end is not always the one you can cancel.

One thing has to be said first, because it changes what you are buying. The aurora is never guaranteed. Visit Rovaniemi, the city’s tourism office, says plainly that you never know when the Northern Lights will appear, and the tours we read say the same. What you can buy is a better chance (a guide who follows clear sky) and clear terms for when the sky does not cooperate. Those terms are what this page compares.

Which listing is the safe, quick buy

For most readers it is the Viator Arctic GM Signature Small Group tour. It is not the cheapest. It is the listing where the terms are easiest to read and the most generous: a group of eight at most, pickup, overalls, free cancellation until the evening before, a full refund if the operator calls the tour off for conditions, and a money-back term if nothing is captured on camera. If a cloudy week makes you nervous about committing, that is the combination that matters.

Say where the others win, plainly. KKday is cheaper at €85.44, with free cancellation up to two days ahead, if you are two or more adults and want a three-hour small-group tour. Tiqets is cheaper still at €79, but only because its ticket cannot be cancelled or moved. If your dates are fixed and you accept that, it is a fair price for a tour that includes overalls and a barbecue. For a snowmobile, Viator is the one we read with a price and free cancellation.

What decides whether you see the lights

  • Season. Visit Rovaniemi says the Aurora Borealis is visible in Rovaniemi from late August until mid April. A December date is well inside that window.
  • Cloud, not just solar activity. A clear sky matters as much as a strong display, which is why the Arctic GM listings stress following clearer skies, crossing into Sweden when needed. A small group that can move quickly is easier to redirect than a large coach.
  • The forecast. The Finnish Meteorological Institute publishes geomagnetic disturbance readings from magnetometer stations. Its page says that when the upper threshold is exceeded, it is very likely one can see auroras, and when only the lower threshold is crossed the aurora is possible but usually dim. It is a live indicator of activity, not a forecast made weeks ahead, so do not plan around it from home.
  • Give yourself more than one night. Nobody can say months ahead which night will be clear. Booking an early evening in your stay, with a free-cancellation or reschedulable ticket, leaves a second try. The non-refundable tickets above do not.

Clothing, children and the small print

Check clothing first. The Viator Signature tour and the Tiqets barbecue, sleigh and hunt listings include or offer overalls and boots; the Viator coach option does not include thermal overalls, and KKday tells you to bring warm clothing. Wear layers under whatever is lent to you.

Age limits differ by listing. KKday’s small-group tour is adults only and not suitable under 14. On the Viator snowmobile safari, adults are counted from age 14 and children from 4 to 13 appear as a separate fare; children without a licence ride in the sledge. The Viator Signature tour counts ages 0 to 12 as children and offers infant seats. If you are travelling with young children, read the age rule on the specific listing before paying.

Reindeer, husky and camp-evening versions

Visit Rovaniemi lists aurora excursions that use a car, a snowmobile or a reindeer. The tours we could price on our partner sellers fall into three shapes: minibus chases, snowmobile safaris and sleigh-and-fire evenings. A reindeer sleigh under the stars is its own product. Frequently asked questions

Is a northern lights sighting guaranteed on a Rovaniemi tour?

No. Visit Rovaniemi says you never know when the Northern Lights will appear, and the listings we read say sightings cannot be guaranteed. Some tours offer a refund if no aurora is captured on camera; read that term as a money-back offer, not a promise.

Which seller is the safest to book?

Of the listings we read, the Viator Arctic GM Signature Small Group tour: max 8 guests, pickup and overalls included, free cancellation until 19:00 the day before, and a full refund if the operator cancels for conditions. It was €199 a head on 12 December 2026.

What is the cheapest northern lights tour from Rovaniemi?

On 12 December 2026 the lowest prices we read were €79 on Tiqets (barbecue tour and sleigh ride, both non-refundable) and €85.44 on KKday (small group, free cancellation up to 2 days ahead).

When is the aurora season in Rovaniemi?

Visit Rovaniemi says the aurora is visible from late August until mid April.

Do I need my own winter clothes?

It depends on the listing. The Viator Signature tour includes overalls and the Tiqets barbecue and sleigh tours include overalls and boots, while the Viator coach option does not include thermal overalls and KKday asks you to bring warm clothing.

Can children join a northern lights tour?

Rules differ. KKday's small-group tour is adults only and not suitable under 14, while the Viator snowmobile safari lets children without a licence ride in the sledge and the Viator Signature tour offers infant seats.
