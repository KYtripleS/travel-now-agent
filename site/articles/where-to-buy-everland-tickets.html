Everland sells one product that matters to most visitors, the one-day all-day pass, and the park prices it by month, not by weekday. What differs between sellers is the price, the cancellation line and one December detail: the park is running a buy-one-get-one “1+1” all-day pass, and the sellers package it differently. For a Saturday visit on 12 December 2026, on the day we checked, a single adult all-day pass cost ₩39,520 on KKday and ₩47,850 on GetYourGuide, against the park’s published December adult price of ₩55,000.

KKday is the safe, quick buy for one adult on that date: it was the lowest figure we saw, it gives instant confirmation with a QR code for direct entry, and its 1-Day Pass package panel allows free cancellation up to the day before (its purchase notice is stricter; see below). GetYourGuide’s ticket is non-refundable. This guide sets out the figures, the terms of each listing and the one rule you must know before paying: KKday’s listing says its ticket is for foreign visitors. How to read it:

  • KKday was lowest in both rows. Our arithmetic: ₩8,330 below GetYourGuide for the single pass, and ₩7,500 below GetYourGuide for the 1+1 set. KKday’s own page crosses out ₩68,000 beside its ₩39,520; that is a list price on KKday’s page, not the park’s current December price, so we compare with ₩55,000.
  • GetYourGuide showed ₩47,850 for the single pass. Its page crosses out ₩55,000, which matches the park’s December adult price, and shows a 13 per cent reduction. It first displayed dollars, so we switched its currency selector to won before reading the figure.
  • The single pass and the 1+1 pass are close in price on GetYourGuide. The 1+1 option was ₩47,500, only ₩350 under the single pass, and covers two people. If you are travelling with one other person, the 1+1 option is the one to choose on either site.
  • The park’s price is monthly. The official page lists adult and youth at ₩71,000 in October, ₩65,000 in November and ₩55,000 in December, and child and senior at ₩61,000, ₩55,000 and ₩45,000. Seasons “are subject to change without prior notice”.
  • Promo codes were advertised but not applied. KKday’s page showed USD 9 and USD 25 off banners and a 5 per cent app code. We did not test them, so the price at checkout may differ.

What each seller’s listing says

Everland, the park’s own price page

The English price page, read on 28 November 2026, lists the all-day pass by month. In December an adult or teenager pays ₩55,000 and a child or senior pays ₩45,000. The page defines a child as 36 months to 12 years, a youth as 13 to 18 and a senior as 65 and over, says infants under 36 months are admitted free (some infant facilities need a separate ticket bought at the booth) and says staff may ask for documents proving age at the entrance. It adds that some facilities may incur separate fees and that these prices cannot be combined with other promotions or discounts.

The park’s homepage on the same day carried a banner, “Everland All-day Pass 1+1 Special”, dated 10 November to 31 December 2026. We did not find the official 1+1 price, so the table leaves it blank.

KKday

Check this on KKday →

The listing is titled “Everland E-Ticket”, rated 4.8 from 2,500 reviews, with 20,000 or more sold. It has two admission packages for 12 December:

  • 1-Day Pass, ₩39,520. Package terms: free cancellation up to 1 day in advance; “Cancellations 1 day(s) (inclusive) before the selected date are allowed” and cancellation is not allowed on the day itself. Instant confirmation, and the ticket type is an e-ticket with direct entry by QR code.
  • Buy 1 Get 1 Free_1-Day Pass, ₩40,000 per set. The package is “for 1 set, which can be used by 2 people”. Its terms read: “Once the order has been completed, it cannot be canceled, modified, or refunded”, and the voucher must be used on the selected date and within operating hours.

The purchase notice on the same page adds that once the QR code is used it cannot be rescheduled or refunded, and also says “Tickets cannot be canceled once issued”; the 1-Day Pass package panel says otherwise, so read the cancellation panel for the exact package on the day you book. The park’s own rules that change the buying decision

  • Monthly pricing, not a date calendar. The park’s price depends on the month, so a December date is ₩55,000 for an adult on the official list, and a January date may differ. KKday’s calendar showed ₩39,520 for every December date we looked at.
  • 1+1 runs to the end of the year. The park’s banner gives 10 November to 31 December 2026. If you go later, the 1+1 option may not be on sale.
  • Age bands. Child is 36 months to 12 years, youth 13 to 18 and senior 65 and over; the official page says children and seniors pay the lower price and staff may ask for proof of age.
  • Hours vary by day. The park’s homepage showed 10:00 to 21:00 on 28 November; GetYourGuide’s 12 December card showed 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and KKday’s page lists 10:00 to 21:00 with last entry at 19:00. Check the day’s hours in the park’s app.
  • Some rides need a waiting pass. KKday says that for attractions such as Lost Valley, Safari World, T Express, Amazon Express and Thunder Falls a waiting pass must be taken after you enter, and you must ride at the time on it.
  • Foreigners-only tickets. KKday’s listing sets its ticket for foreign visitors; bring the passport you booked with.

Which seller for which trip

  • One adult, a fixed date, and you want to be able to cancel: KKday 1-Day Pass. ₩39,520, free cancellation up to the day before.
  • Two people going together: KKday Buy 1 Get 1 Free_1-Day Pass. ₩40,000 for the set, about ₩20,000 each by our arithmetic. It cannot be cancelled after ordering, so check the forecast and your date first.
  • You prefer GetYourGuide’s account and app: GetYourGuide. ₩47,850 single or ₩47,500 for two; 4.5 from 37 reviews; non-refundable.
  • Before you pay, either way: both listings we read are for one day only on the date you pick, and the GetYourGuide ticket cannot be refunded.

Five checks before you pay

  1. Same date, same pass. Pick 12 December (or your date) in the calendar; the single pass and the 1+1 set are different packages.
  2. One ticket, two entries. The 1+1 voucher admits two people on one QR code, so you must go together.
  3. Your cancellation line. KKday’s single pass can be cancelled up to a day before; its 1+1 set and GetYourGuide’s ticket cannot.
  4. Your passport. KKday’s ticket is for foreigners; keep the passport you booked with to hand.
  5. Compare in won. GetYourGuide opened in dollars for us; switch the currency to see the won total.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the cheapest place to buy Everland tickets for 12 December 2026?

On 28 November 2026, for a single adult, KKday showed ₩39,520 and GetYourGuide ₩47,850; the park's published December adult price is ₩55,000. Klook and Viator were not checked and we found no Tiqets listing. Prices change daily.

Is there a buy-one-get-one ticket?

Yes, in December. The park's homepage advertises an all-day pass 1+1 special from 10 November to 31 December 2026. KKday sold a set for two people at ₩40,000 and GetYourGuide a 1+1 option at ₩47,500 for 12 December.

Can I cancel an Everland ticket?

KKday's 1-Day Pass package allows free cancellation up to one day before the date; its 1+1 set cannot be cancelled or refunded. GetYourGuide's listing says the activity is non-refundable.

Is the KKday ticket only for foreigners?

KKday's listing says "This ticket is for foreigners only" and lists South Korea as an ineligible nationality or region. GetYourGuide's page did not state a nationality rule.

What does a child pay?

The park's December price for a child (36 months to 12 years) or senior is ₩45,000, and infants under 36 months are free. KKday's listing shows one traveller price and no child fare, and we did not read a child fare on GetYourGuide.

Does the ticket include transport or the fast pass?

No. KKday says standard admission does not include priority boarding and GetYourGuide lists transportation as not included. Some rides need a waiting pass from a kiosk inside the park, according to KKday's listing.
