What you are actually choosing

Edge is the outdoor observation deck at 30 Hudson Yards, on the west side of Manhattan. The entrance is on Level 4 of The Shops & Restaurants. Every ticket type includes the indoor immersive installations and the outdoor sky deck with its glass floor and angled glass wall. The general ticket is timed; other ticket types on some sellers’ pages are for a date only.

So the decision is the slot and the cancellation terms. The sellers we read show the same figures for the same slot, and where they differ is in how they let you change your mind. Sunset slots are the busiest and the dearest; the sunset time on your date is worth checking before you pick, because the price steps up in the hours before it and falls again afterwards.

Edge’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from edgenyc.com only, read on 27 November 2026.

  • Tickets are for the date and time shown. Its terms say tickets are “valid for the date and/or time specified on your ticket”. A timed ticket stays valid “for up to 30 minutes after your scheduled time”; after that, staff try to place you in the next available slot, which is not guaranteed. It recommends arriving 10 to 15 minutes before your entry time.
  • Children. “Tickets are required for all visitors to Edge NYC ages 6 and over.” Children aged 5 and under do not need a ticket.
  • Refunds. Standard tickets bought on its own site are non-refundable. For a ticket bought from a seller, the seller’s cancellation terms apply; that is why the cancellation line in each listing above matters.
  • Weather. The outdoor sky deck may close temporarily “during extreme or severe weather at our discretion”; the indoor areas stay open. Dress for the outdoor deck.
  • Bags. Normal-size backpacks and bags are permitted; large bags and luggage are not.
  • Sunset. It says sunset is the most popular time and slots around golden hour sell out quickly.
  • Passes. It accepts New York Pass, Go City and New York C3 and C-ALL by CityPASS.

Which seller to book

  • Most readers: Tiqets. The same slot prices as the others, cancellation up to your slot, and a price shown for every time of day.
  • A different reader: the seller with the most reviews, or someone who wants to pay later. GetYourGuide has 24,037 reviews, “Reserve now & pay later” and free cancellation until 29 December. It is also the listing that shows the champagne ticket with a price ($72.77 at 8:00 am).
  • A different reader: someone who may not know their time. Viator lists a Flexible Time Admission ticket ($90.37) beside the timed one.
  • Viator if you want the Vessel combination on the same page ($101.26).

Five checks before you pay

  1. The slot. The price belongs to the time; 7:00 am and 4:30 pm differed by more than $20 on 30 December.
  2. The cancellation date. Tiqets until your slot, GetYourGuide until 29 December, Viator before 7:00 am on 29 December, KKday none.
  3. The product. A general ticket is timed; flexible and priority tickets are different, dearer products.
  4. Children. Edge says 6 and over need a ticket; seller pages differ on the rest.
  5. Arrive on time. The ticket holds for 30 minutes after your slot, and entry after that is not guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Edge NYC tickets?

Tiqets for a dated general ticket. On 27 November 2026, for Wednesday 30 December, it showed $47.91 at 7:00 am and $69.68 at 4:30 pm, the same as GetYourGuide to within rounding, with cancellation possible until your selected timeslot and 4.6 from 2,526 reviews. Klook was not checked.

Is Edge a timed ticket?

The general ticket is. Edge says tickets are valid for the date and time on the ticket and stay valid for up to 30 minutes after your slot. Tiqets, GetYourGuide and Viator all list slots from 7:00 am on 30 December.

Does the price change by time of day?

Yes. On 30 December Tiqets showed $47.91 from 7:00 to 8:45 am, $59.88 from 9:00 am to 1:30 pm, up to $77.30 at 3:30 pm and $60.97 from 5:15 pm.

Can I cancel an Edge ticket?

It depends on the seller. Tiqets said cancellation is possible until your selected timeslot, GetYourGuide free until 29 December, Viator free before 7:00 am on 29 December, and KKday not allowed (date changes permitted). Edge says tickets bought from a reseller are handled by that vendor.

Do children need a ticket?

Edge says tickets are required for visitors aged 6 and over; children aged 5 and under do not need one. We priced one adult only, and Viator and KKday mention child rates we did not read.

Can I add champagne?

GetYourGuide listed General Admission plus a glass of champagne at $72.77 for the 8:00 am slot on 30 December, for guests aged 21 or over. Tiqets and KKday list champagne options too; we did not read their prices.
