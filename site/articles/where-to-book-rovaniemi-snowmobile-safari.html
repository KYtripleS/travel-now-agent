Two snowmobile safaris from Rovaniemi can carry the same name and cost the same, and still be different outings. On Christmas Eve 2026 the listings we read ran from €89 to €119 per adult for a shared snowmobile, and the gap between them lies in four lines: how much of the 3 hours you actually spend driving (the listings say about 1 hour to 1.5 hours), who must hold a driving licence, how much you owe if the machine is damaged, and whether anyone collects you from your hotel. Two points before you compare. A snowmobile is shared by two guests who swap the driving, so the price is per person for half the machine; riding alone costs extra (a €50 supplement on one listing, a second ticket on another). And every listing here puts under-15s or under-6s in a sled pulled by the guide, so check the child lines below before you book a family.

What each seller’s listing says

All read on 26 November 2026 for 24 December 2026.

GetYourGuide: Rovaniemi: Arctic Snowmobiling Ride with Local Guide. Operator Lapland Royal Adventures, rated 4.6 from 987 reviews. Four options were open on 24 December: an afternoon departure at 17:00 or 17:30 for €89 (3 hours, with pickup and drop-off within 10 km of the city centre), the standard ride at 08:30 for €99 (3 hours), the “Arctic Aurora Chase by Snowmobile” at 20:00 for €119 (4 hours, a Lappish barbecue and hot drinks by a fire; the listing says aurora sightings are not guaranteed), and a snowmobile and ice fishing option at €149. All show free cancellation until 23 December and reserve now and pay later, with prices “all taxes and fees included”. Licence and age: drivers must be at least 18 and hold a valid driver’s licence in Finland, as the listing words it; we do not interpret that. The listing says it is suitable for children over 2: children ride in a sled pulled by the guide, and those over 140 cm can sit behind an adult on the snowmobile at the adult price. Liability: all drivers carry a maximum personal liability of €1,000 for damage, which can be cut to €300 by paying a €20 supplement at the office. Included: thermal clothing, transfers to the base from the office, an English-speaking guide, a snowmobile shared by two guests, fuel and traffic insurance (maximum personal liability applies), hot drinks and a snack. The listing puts riding at 1 to 1.5 hours. Meeting and group: the office is at Maakuntakatu 31 (Sampokeskus Mall); arrive 15 minutes before the start. Group size is not stated. Solo riding is an extra €50 per person, for adults with a driving licence. At least 2 participants are needed; if not met you get a full refund or a new date. Recent reviews mention large groups riding in single file at the pace of the slowest rider.

Choosing between a shared and a solo snowmobile

  • Shared. Two guests share each machine and swap the driving. On the Arctic GM listing you drive for about 1 hour in total, so about half an hour each is a fair reading of the listing, though it does not say so.
  • Solo. Lapland Royal Adventures lists a €50 supplement for adults with a licence; Viator’s Arctic GM listing says sole use of a snowmobile requires purchasing two tickets (€238 for one rider on 24 December, our arithmetic); the KKday listing says single driving is subject to availability. The GetYourGuide Arctic GM listing does not state a solo price.
  • Passengers. Under-15s ride in a guide’s sled on the Arctic GM listings. The Lapland Royal listing lets guests over 140 cm sit behind an adult at the adult price. KKday’s listing offers a seat in the sleigh to guests without a licence; check the exact wording on any listing before you book.
  • Damage. The cap is €950 on the Arctic GM listings and €1,000 on the Lapland Royal listing, which can be reduced to €300 for a €20 supplement paid at the office. The Arctic GM listing says travel insurance is not included.

Clothing and the small print

Thermal overalls are listed as included on the GetYourGuide and Viator Arctic GM listings, thermal clothing on the Lapland Royal listing and thermal gear on the KKday one; wear warm layers underneath. Check the meeting-point line: Arctic GM’s listing says hotel pickup is not included and starts at Valtakatu 26, Lapland Royal’s afternoon option offers pickup within 10 km of the centre, and Lapland Welcome offers hotel-reception pickup. Operators say they may reschedule if the minimum of two riders is not met, and one Arctic GM reviewer said the tour was moved because of mild weather.

Frequently asked questions

Which seller is the safest to book for a Rovaniemi snowmobile safari?

Of the listings we read, GetYourGuide's Arctic Snowmobiling Ride with Local Guide: €89 at 17:00 or €99 at 08:30 on 24 December 2026, free cancellation until 23 December, reserve now and pay later.

How much does a snowmobile safari in Rovaniemi cost on 24 December?

The listings we read ran from €89 to €119 per adult for a shared snowmobile. The lowest was €89 on GetYourGuide at 17:00; the Arctic GM safari was €119 on both GetYourGuide and Viator.

Do I need a driving licence?

To drive, yes on every listing we read: 18 or over with a valid licence, worded as Class B on the Arctic GM and KKday listings and as valid in Finland on the Lapland Royal and KKday listings. We do not interpret that wording. Passengers and sled riders are covered in the child lines above.

What am I liable for if the snowmobile is damaged?

The Arctic GM listings cap the driver at €950 per person; the Lapland Royal listing caps it at €1,000, reducible to €300 for a €20 supplement paid at the office. The Lapland Welcome and KKday listing pages we read did not state a figure.

Can children join?

Rules differ. Arctic GM takes children from 6 and puts under-15s in a guide's sled; Lapland Royal says suitable for over-2s in the sled, with over-140 cm riders behind an adult at the adult price; KKday says not suitable for children aged 3 or under.

Is there a northern lights snowmobile ride on 24 December?

GetYourGuide's Arctic Aurora Chase by Snowmobile showed €119 at 20:00. Viator's Lapland Welcome ride was sold out that day. Both say sightings cannot be guaranteed.
