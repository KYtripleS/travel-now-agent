    What each seller’s listing says

    The cancellation dates below were read on 19 November 2026 for a 16 December visit; the other terms are as we read them on 31 October 2026.

    Disneyland Paris, direct

    Disney sells dated and undated tickets. Dated tickets of one to four days are free to cancel up to 3 days before the visit date; Disney’s own example is that a 4 June ticket can be cancelled until midnight on 1 June. You cancel online in “My Bookings” in a Disney account, and Disney says that to change your date you cancel and buy again, subject to availability on the new date.

    The 1-day undated ticket is a different product. Disney lists it as valid for one year from the day after purchase (except 31 October and 31 December), cancellable only under the 14-day right of withdrawal, and you “must register your visit date in advance”, subject to park capacity. It is not a walk-up ticket, and in our checks it appeared only on Disney’s own page. Disney also says park tickets generally cannot be bought at the entrance.

    Disney’s other terms on the ticket page: children aged 3 to 11 pay the child rate, and under-3s go free. The one-day dated ticket can cover one or two parks; Disney’s configurator offered no one-park choice for two days. Disney also runs a Price Match Promise: if you find a lower price for the exact same ticket within 24 hours, you can claim the difference, but the lower price must be in the same currency and country (£, United Kingdom for us), have the same cancellation conditions, and be claimed within 72 hours of booking. Its page does not say whether resellers count, so do not rely on it to match one.

    GetYourGuide

    The 1-day listing is labelled “Official ticket” and describes a ticket for one park, or both, “on a pre-booked date”. Its terms: “Cancel up to 3 days in advance for a full refund”, and for our 16 December visit the options said “Cancel for free until Dec 13”. The two-park option states entry to both parks “on the same pre-booked date”. GetYourGuide has other flexible-date and transport-bundle listings; we did not price them.

    Viator

    The listing we used lets you choose one or two parks and one to four days. For two parks it says “full park-hopping”; for one park, access to one of the two parks. On the page we read: “Free cancellation before 13 Dec (local time)” for the 16 December visit, and “no amendments can be made, including name changes”, with ID that may be asked for at the gate. It also says to sync Viator tickets in the Disneyland Paris app to add Disney Premier Access. Viator sells a separate 1-Day/1-Park listing, which is where the £66.65 came from (it too showed free cancellation before 13 December).

    “Park hopper”, Extra Magic Time and what a ticket does not include

    Disneyland Paris does not use the word “hopper” on the pages we read. The idea is a two-park ticket: Disney’s ticket page offers a “2 Parks experience” ticket covering Disneyland Park and Disney Adventure World, and Viator’s listing says “park-hopping” for its two-park option. If you want to switch parks in a day, buy two parks; a one-park ticket is one park. Tiqets and GetYourGuide both describe the two-park day as entry to both on the same date.

    Extra Magic Time is the early entry before the official opening time. Disney’s page says: “Guests staying at a Disney Hotel, Les Villages Nature Paris, or with a Disneyland Pass Gold can enter one or both Disney Parks before the official opening time.” That is the whole list. A day ticket bought from any seller is not on it, and the page adds that the opening time, duration and parks can vary, are shown up to three months ahead, and can change until the day of your visit. If early entry is the reason you are comparing sellers, you are comparing the wrong thing: it comes from where you sleep, which is covered in where to stay for Disneyland Paris.

    Which partner to book

    • You have a fixed date and want the lowest price: Tiqets or GetYourGuide. Both were below Disney on all three rows that day, by £4.55 to £10.25. Viator was below by only 34p on one park.
    • You want free cancellation: GetYourGuide or Viator. Both said free cancellation until 13 December for a 16 December visit; Tiqets said until 72 hours before.
    • You want to see the price in another currency before paying: Tiqets or Viator. Both let you switch the displayed currency on the page, but Tiqets says the currency you pay in may differ, so check the total at checkout.

    Five checks before you pay

    1. Same date, same park count, same days. A one-park price is not comparable with a two-park one.
    2. The cancellation date. For a 16 December visit, the resellers we read said 13 December. Put it in your diary.
    3. Name and ID. Tickets are personal, and Viator says no name changes are possible.
    4. Is it dated or undated? An undated ticket still needs a registered visit date.
    5. Check the total in your own currency, after any fee shown at checkout.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where should I buy Disneyland Paris tickets?

    Tiqets or GetYourGuide for a dated ticket. On 19 November 2026, for a Wednesday 16 December visit, Tiqets, GetYourGuide and Viator were all below Disney's own listed price on the three ticket types we priced. For a one-day, two-park adult ticket the figures were £92.66 on Tiqets, £92.64 on GetYourGuide and £94.42 on Viator, against £99 listed by Disney. Klook was not priced. Prices change daily, so the figure at checkout for your date is the one that counts.

    Can I cancel a Disneyland Paris ticket?

    Disney's dated 1 to 4-day tickets can be cancelled and refunded up to 3 days before the chosen date. Tiqets said free cancellation until 72 hours before, and GetYourGuide and Viator said until 13 December for a 16 December visit. The undated ticket can be cancelled only under the 14-day right of withdrawal, on Disney's page.

    What is the difference between a dated and an undated ticket?

    A dated ticket is for the day or days you choose when you buy. Disney's undated 1-day ticket is valid for one year from the day after purchase, except 31 October and 31 December, and you must register your visit date in advance, subject to park capacity.

    Does a ticket from Klook, Tiqets, GetYourGuide or Viator give Extra Magic Time?

    Disney's Extra Magic Time page names three groups: guests of a Disney Hotel, guests of Les Villages Nature Paris, and Disneyland Pass Gold holders. A day ticket bought from any seller is not on that list, and the times can change until the day of your visit.

    Do I need a two-park ticket to see both parks in one day?

    Yes. A one-park ticket covers one park, either Disneyland Park or Disney Adventure World. The two-park ticket covers both on the same date, and Viator's listing calls this park-hopping.

    Does Disneyland Paris match a lower price?

    Disney's Price Match Promise refunds the difference if you find a lower price for the exact same ticket within 24 hours of booking, in the same currency and country and with the same cancellation conditions, and you claim within 72 hours. Its page does not say whether resellers count, and Tiqets and GetYourGuide were already below Disney's price on the dates we read.
