eSIM

Landing abroad with no data is stressful.

Before you go: checklist

  1. Check if your phone supports eSIM
  2. Know your carrier's international roaming rates
  3. Have a physical SIM vendor's location saved
  4. Pick the right option for your trip length

Why this helps

Small preparation steps before a trip may reduce avoidable stress and make travel days feel smoother.
