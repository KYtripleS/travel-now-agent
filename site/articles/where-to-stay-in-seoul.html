Myeongdong and City Hall: the first-trip base

Myeongdong is the Seoul most first-timers picture: cosmetics shops, crowds, and street-food stalls in the evening, with Myeongdong Station on Line 4 at one end and Euljiro 1-ga and City Hall on Line 2 at the other. It is central in the literal sense. Namsan and N Seoul Tower rise just to the south, and the palaces of Jongno are a short ride or a long walk to the north. The trade-off is the crowd, which is the point of the place and also the reason some people would rather sleep a district away.

Jongno, Insadong and Gwanghwamun: the palaces on foot

This is old Seoul. Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung palaces, the hanok lanes of Bukchon, Insadong’s galleries and craft shops and the Jogyesa temple are all within walking distance, and Anguk Station on Line 3 sits in the middle of them. It is quieter after dark than Myeongdong, which suits anyone who wants to be at a palace gate when it opens. Gwanghwamun, at the southern end, is the grander, more modern edge, with the big square in front of Gyeongbokgung.

Where to stay in Jongno, Insadong and Gwanghwamun

  • Four Seasons Hotel Seoul, splurge. Open since 1 October 2015 by Gwanghwamun Square, with 317 rooms (43 of them suites), some looking out towards Gyeongbokgung Palace and the mountains, and a Korean sauna on the ninth floor. Hongdae: nightlife, cafés and the airport train

Hongdae takes its name from Hongik University, and it has a student district’s energy: live music, street performers, cafés, vintage shops and bars that stay busy late. Hongik University Station is a genuine asset, where Line 2, the Gyeongui–Jungang line and the airport railway’s all-stop trains from Incheon meet. The trade-off is distance from the palaces and the noise that comes with the fun.

Where to stay in Hongdae

  • RYSE, Autograph Collection, comfortable to splurge. Opened in 2018 with 272 rooms, about 300 m from Exit 9 of Hongik University Station, and built around art and design. Choose it for style in the middle of Hongdae. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • L7 Hongdae by Lotte, comfortable. About 335 rooms a minute’s walk from Hongik University Station, with a rooftop bar and a seasonal outdoor pool. Choose it for the airport railway at your door. Check rates on Booking.com →

Seoul Station and Itaewon: trains, then dinner

Seoul Station is where the AREX express from Incheon Airport ends and where KTX trains leave for Busan (is a KORAIL Pass worth it? prices that trip), so it is the practical base for early flights and trips out of the city. Itaewon, a short ride east below Namsan, is Seoul’s best-known international district, with restaurants from all over the world. Neither is where most people want to spend all their evenings, but for a first night or a last one they are hard to beat.

Where to stay near Seoul Station and in Itaewon

  • Four Points by Sheraton Josun, Seoul Station, comfortable. 342 rooms, linked to Seoul Station by an underground passage from Exit 12, so the AREX and the KTX are a walk away without going outside. Choose it for early trains and easy airport runs. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Mondrian Seoul Itaewon, comfortable. 295 rooms and suites, with a seasonal outdoor pool that costs extra to use. Choose it for design and Itaewon’s restaurants. Watch out: it is about a 15-minute walk from central Itaewon. Check rates on Booking.com →

Gangnam and Jamsil: south of the river

Gangnam and Jamsil are the modern Seoul south of the Han River: office towers, COEX, department stores, and Lotte World Tower. They make sense if your business, shopping or concerts are there, and less sense for a first trip, because the palaces, Insadong and Myeongdong are a long subway ride away.

Where to stay in Gangnam and Jamsil

  • Josun Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Gangnam, splurge. Opened on 25 May 2021 with 254 rooms and suites, carrying on the name of the 1914 Chosun Hotel. Choose it for luxury south of the river. Watch out: it is about an 18-minute walk from Gangnam Station. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Signiel Seoul, splurge. 235 rooms on the 76th to 101st floors of Lotte World Tower, open since April 2017. Choose it for the view. Watch out: Jamsil is the furthest of these areas from the palaces. Check rates on Booking.com →

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Seoul for a first trip?

Myeongdong or around City Hall: central, with the shopping streets, street food and several subway lines on foot. Choose Jongno and Insadong instead if the palaces and Bukchon's hanok lanes are the priority.

Is it better to stay in Myeongdong or Hongdae?

Myeongdong for a central first-trip base with the shopping streets and subway lines at hand; Hongdae for nightlife, cafés and the airport railway at Hongik University Station, at the cost of being further from the palaces.

Is Gangnam a good place to stay in Seoul?

If your business, shopping or concerts are south of the Han River, yes. For a first trip it is less convenient, because the palaces, Insadong and Myeongdong are a long subway ride away.

How do I get from Incheon Airport to central Seoul?

The AREX express runs non-stop to Seoul Station in about 43 minutes from Terminal 1 (about 51 from Terminal 2) for ₩13,000, or ₩12,500 for AREX members. The cheaper all-stop trains also call at Hongik University Station for Hongdae.

What is The Grand Lotte Seoul?

The former Lotte Hotel Seoul, renamed in August 2026 after a room renovation of about 15 months that cut the hotel from 1,015 rooms to 868. Some booking sites still list it under the old name.

Where can I stay in a hanok in Seoul?

In Bukchon Hanok Village, between Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung palaces. Rakkojae Seoul, restored in 2003 by the master carpenter Chung Young-jin, has rooms in two hanok compounds there.
