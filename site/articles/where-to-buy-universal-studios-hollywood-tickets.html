Universal Studios Hollywood, the park’s own terms

The park’s ticket FAQ describes the four products this way. General Admission is a one-day park ticket valid on the selected date only. A standard Universal Express ticket “includes 1-Day General Admission and one-time (per day) express access” to each ride (excluding Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift), attraction and seated show. The Universal Express Unlimited ticket has the same admission with unlimited per-day express access. The VIP Experience adds a guided tour, a gourmet meal and valet parking to unlimited express access.

Which ticket: General Admission, Express, Unlimited or VIP

General Admission is the ride, show and Studio Tour day, with the standard queues. It suits a quiet weekday like our example.

Universal Express adds one use of the express line per ride, attraction and seated show. The park’s own wording on its price is that Express bought in advance with admission should equal General Admission plus the Express Add-On bought separately. On 9 December the park’s figures were $99.95 and $219.95, so Express was $120 more per adult than General Admission.

Universal Express Unlimited is the same, with unlimited express use per ride rather than one. It was $269.95 on the park’s calendar for 9 December, $50 above standard Express.

The VIP Experience is a different product. The park’s page describes a tour of at least 5 hours with an expert guide, access to Studio Tour sets on foot, unlimited express access that runs all day (including after the tour ends), a gourmet meal in a private dining room, a VIP lounge and complimentary valet parking. Groups are capped at 13 guests per guide. Children must be at least 5 to attend, and at least 17 to attend without an accompanying adult. On 9 December it was $389.95 on the park’s calendar, $385 on Tiqets and $398.92 on Viator.

The park’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from the park’s ticket FAQ, read on 21 November 2026.

  • Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift is the exception. It is included with theme park admission, but express access to it is not in Universal Express, Universal Express Unlimited or the Express Add-On. The FAQ lists the exceptions: the VIP Experience, the R.I.P. Tour products and the Guided Theme Park Tour Add-On, plus a separate timed-access add-on. Express tickets bought before 31 August 2026 are unaffected. Every reseller listing we read that mentioned Hollywood Drift said the same.
  • Ages. The online ticket store prices by age: ages 3 to 9 qualify for the child price, and children aged 2 and under enter free with no ticket. At the front gate the discount is by height instead (under 48 inches, or 122 cm). The park says the height discount cannot be combined with any other offer.
  • Sell-outs. The park advises checking its ticket store for a “Sold Out” date before you leave, and says tickets may be unavailable at the booths when the park reaches capacity. A dated ticket bought ahead fixes your date before you travel.
  • Tickets bought outside the park’s own store. The FAQ says the park “strongly” recommends buying “directly through us or our authorized ticketing partners”, that it is “unable to verify or guarantee the validity or authenticity of tickets not purchased directly through an authorized sales channel”, and that invalid, duplicate or counterfeit tickets are refused at entry without compensation and may be confiscated. That page does not name which sellers it counts as authorised, and we cannot confirm for any of the five partners above whether Universal lists them. Nothing on that page says resold tickets are void. The practical points are to keep the booking confirmation and to carry the ID the ticket may ask for.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Universal Studios Hollywood tickets?

Tiqets for the lowest price on a fixed date, Viator if you want free cancellation on Universal Express. For a Wednesday 9 December 2026 visit, read on 21 November, a one-adult General Admission ticket was $98 on Tiqets and $99.96 on Viator, Universal Express was $217 and $225.02, and the VIP Experience was $385 and $398.92. Klook, KKday and GetYourGuide were not priced. Prices change daily, so the figure at checkout for your date is the one that counts.

What is the difference between General Admission and Universal Express?

General Admission is one day in the park. Universal Express adds one-time per-day express access to each ride, attraction and seated show, but not to Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift. On 9 December the park's figures were $99.95 and $219.95.

Can I cancel a Universal Studios Hollywood ticket?

It depends on the seller and the option. Tiqets said its tickets are non-refundable with no rescheduling. Viator's Universal Express option said free cancellation before 8 December for a 9 December visit. GetYourGuide's General Admission listing said non-refundable, and KKday's options said free cancellation up to one day in advance.

Is the VIP Experience worth it?

It is a guided tour of at least 5 hours with unlimited express access, a gourmet meal and valet parking, for $385 on Tiqets and $398.92 on Viator on 9 December, against $99.95 for General Admission. It suits visitors who want the tour and the meal as well as the shorter lines. Children must be at least 5, and at least 17 to attend without an adult.

Do express tickets include Fast & Furious: Hollywood Drift?

No. The park says Universal Express, Universal Express Unlimited and the Express Add-On do not include express access to it. The VIP Experience does include it.

Are tickets from a seller other than the park accepted at the gate?

The park says it recommends buying from itself or its authorised ticketing partners, cannot verify tickets bought through other channels, and refuses invalid, duplicate or counterfeit tickets without compensation. Its page does not list which sellers it counts as authorised, so keep your confirmation and any ID the ticket asks for.
