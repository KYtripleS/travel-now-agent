Four things separate one London to Stonehenge coach tour from the next, and the headline price is only one of them: whether Stonehenge admission is in the price, how long you get at the stones, where the coach leaves from and how long you can cancel for. On the date we priced, Wednesday 30 December 2026, the half-day coach tours with admission came to about £66 to €78 a head, and a full day with Bath added came to £85.

The ticket matters more than usual here, so it comes first. English Heritage runs Stonehenge and sells its own entry tickets. Its booking terms say that if anyone tries to enter with a ticket that has been resold without English Heritage’s written consent, it may refuse entry and pay no compensation. The terms do not name any authorised reseller. English Heritage does run its own group booking route for tour operators and travel-trade businesses, which is how a coach tour can include admission. For that reason this page compares only tours whose listings state that Stonehenge admission is part of the tour price, and it leaves out listings where entry is an optional add-on we could not confirm. Read the “Includes” line of whichever listing you choose.

Stonehenge’s own rules and opening times

These come from English Heritage’s pages, read on 27 November 2026.

  • Opening on 30 December. English Heritage’s page lists 09:30 to 17:00 on every day of the weeks it showed, apart from Christmas Day. Its date selector had not yet opened 28 to 31 December when we read it, so we cannot show you a published line for 30 December itself. The tour listings above were selling that date.
  • Christmas. The calendar shows Thursday 24 December as open 09:30 to 17:00 and Friday 25 December as closed. It adds that opening times on the winter solstice, 22 December, are subject to change.
  • Last entry. English Heritage says last admission is 2 hours before the advertised closing time, which on a 17:00 close means 15:00. A coach tour that arrives at opening is well inside that.
  • Tickets are named and dated. The booking terms say tickets are non-transferable and non-refundable except where English Heritage cannot provide entry for reasons outside its control, and that a ticket collected at the admission tills needs the booking reference. A tour that includes admission handles that step for you; this is why we list the “Includes” lines above in full.
  • Members. English Heritage and National Trust (England) members enter free, which is not relevant to a tour price but is worth knowing if you hold either card.

What to expect on a winter day

  • An early start. The listings we read leave at 7am (Bath day trip), 07:30 (Day Tours London half-day) or 08:30 (Viator Morning Tour). The Day Tours London listings say their coaches are usually among the first groups when the site opens at 9.30am.
  • A long day. The half-day tours run about 6 to 7 hours door to door, the Bath trip about 11 hours. Drop-off times are estimates: the Evan Evans listing says the journey can vary with traffic, and the Bath day trip says its 6pm return is subject to traffic.
  • Dress for open ground. Stonehenge sits on Salisbury Plain, and the walk to the stones is outdoors. Layers, a hat and gloves are sensible in late December.
  • No food on the coach. The GetYourGuide listings say food and drinks are not allowed in the vehicle, so eat before the meeting point.
  • Children. The two Day Tours London listings say not suitable for children under 4. The Viator and Evan Evans listings did not state a minimum age, so check before booking for a family.
  • Access. The Viator listing says it is not wheelchair accessible, and the Bath day trip is not suitable for people with mobility impairments. The other half-day listing did not say.

Frequently asked questions

Is Stonehenge entry included in a London day tour?

On the listings we priced, yes: the GetYourGuide half-day trips and Stonehenge and Bath day trip, and the Viator half-day tour, each list admission to Stonehenge in the price. Several Windsor and Bath listings sell entry only as an option, so check the Includes line.

Which seller is the safest to book?

Of the listings we read, the GetYourGuide morning half-day trip with admission: £66 for the 07:30 start on 30 December 2026, admission included, free cancellation up to 24 hours ahead, 4.8 from 4,113 reviews.

Is Stonehenge open on 30 December 2026?

English Heritage lists 09:30 to 17:00 on every day of the weeks its calendar showed, except Christmas Day, but its date selector had not yet opened 30 December when we read it. Last admission is 2 hours before closing.

Is Stonehenge closed at Christmas?

English Heritage's calendar shows Friday 25 December 2026 as closed and Thursday 24 December as open 09:30 to 17:00.

What is the cheapest Stonehenge tour from London?

On 30 December 2026 the half-day prices we read were £66.00 (GetYourGuide, Day Tours London), €75.06 (Viator) and €78 (GetYourGuide, Evan Evans), in different currencies, so we do not name one as lowest. The Stonehenge and Bath day trip was £85.

Can I visit Windsor Castle on this date?

One Windsor tour listing says Windsor Castle is closed on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and 30 December 2026 is a Wednesday, so check that line before booking a Windsor tour for that date.
