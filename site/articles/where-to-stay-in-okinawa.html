Okinawa’s hotel bill is about to change, but not yet. The prefecture’s accommodation tax, 2% of the room price, applies to nights from 1 February 2027, so a stay in 2026 pays only the room rate and Japan’s 10% consumption tax. The choice that matters more is the one the island forces on everyone: base yourself in Naha, where a monorail runs from the airport, or on the resort coast to the north, where most visitors want a car.

This guide covers five areas, from Naha’s Kokusai-dori to the Churaumi Aquarium, 12 hotels, the tax and the airport links. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in October 2026. No hotel pays to appear here. Ishigaki and the other islands are separate trips and are not covered.

Okinawa’s accommodation tax

Okinawa Prefecture’s accommodation tax is not in force in 2026. The prefecture’s own page gives 1 February 2027 as the start, for nights from that date, and says it applies even to rooms booked before then. The rate is 2% of the room-only price per person per night, with the tax capped at ¥2,000.

The base is the price you pay minus anything that is not the room: breakfast, activities and the consumption tax. It is rounded down to the nearest ¥1,000 first, and capped at ¥100,000. Hotels collect the tax; the prefecture says it is paid at the property, although it may be included in what you pay a booking site or travel agent.

Room price per person per night, before consumption tax Prefecture tax from 1 February 2027
¥8,500 (counted as ¥8,000) ¥160
¥15,000 ¥300
¥30,000 ¥600
¥60,000 ¥1,200
¥100,000 or more ¥2,000

The first row is the prefecture’s own example; the others are our arithmetic. For a worked example, also ours: a couple pays ¥33,000 a night for a room only, including the 10% consumption tax. Before that tax the room costs ¥30,000, or ¥15,000 each, so the tax is ¥300 each, ¥600 a night and ¥2,400 over four nights.

Some places will add a town tax. The prefecture says six municipalities plan their own: Motobu, Onna, Chatan, Nago, Ishigaki and Miyakojima, including the areas in this guide. Where a municipality charges too, the prefecture’s page splits the same 2% into 0.8% for the prefecture, capped at ¥800, and 1.2% for the municipality, capped at ¥1,200. Naha is not on the prefecture’s list.

On top of the room rate, Japan’s standard consumption tax rate is 10%, and Japanese hotel rates are normally quoted with it included.

Getting in from Naha Airport, and around

  • Into Naha by monorail. The Yui Rail (Okinawa Urban Monorail) starts at Naha Airport station. To Asahibashi it takes 11 minutes and costs ¥290; to Kenchomae (Prefectural Office), 13 minutes and ¥290; to Makishi, 16 minutes and ¥320, by the operator’s fare table.
  • To the resorts by limousine bus. Okinawa Bus runs the Airport Limousine Bus from the airport’s domestic terminal. Its fares from 1 April 2026 are ¥1,000 to Chatan Gate Way and the Chatan hotels, ¥1,700 to most of the Onna stops in areas B and C, and ¥1,800 for the stops from Manza Beach to Seragaki. Its timetable is not a train timetable, so read it before you plan an arrival. In its sample runs the bus takes about 80 minutes to Chatan and about an hour to the first Onna stops, and Monterey’s own page still quotes an older fare of ¥1,530, so check the operator.
  • To Motobu by bus or car. Okinawa Bus lists no limousine route to Motobu: its DE area ends on 31 March 2026, and its E area is suspended. The Orion Hotel Motobu’s own page lists three scheduled buses from the airport, taking about 2 hours 10 minutes to 2 hours 30 minutes, at ¥2,000 to ¥2,880, and about 60 minutes by car.
  • By car. Okinawa’s official tourism site says there are many rental companies across the islands, and the Renaissance and the Orion both sell stays bundled with a rental car. Chatan is about 40 minutes from the airport by car, Onna about 60 to 70 and Motobu about 60 to 100, by the hotels’ own figures. See our first-timers guide for driving rules.
  • Typhoon season. The Japan Meteorological Agency’s 1991 to 2020 averages put typhoon approaches to the Okinawa region at 1.5 in July, 2.2 in August, 1.9 in September and 1.1 in October. Pack for it with our Okinawa packing guide.

Naha: Kokusai-dori and Makishi

For a first trip without a car, this is the base. Kokusai-dori is Naha’s main street, with Makishi Public Market and the covered arcades beside it, and three Yui Rail stations (Kenchomae, Miebashi and Makishi) are within a walk. You can reach most of Naha on foot or by monorail and add a hired car only for the days you leave town. See things to do in Okinawa for what to fit in.

Naha: Asahibashi

Asahibashi is the transport end of Naha: a monorail station, the Naha Bus Terminal, where the limousine buses stop, and a shorter ride from the airport than the stations farther up the line. It is a quieter base than Kokusai-dori, with the street about ten minutes on foot.

Where to stay in Asahibashi

  • OKINAWA NaHaNa HOTEL, comfortable. A five-minute walk from Asahibashi station and about ten minutes by car from the airport, with Kokusai Street and Naminoue Beach each ten minutes on foot, by the hotel’s own page. Choose it for a walkable, central base. Watch out: its 50 parking spaces are first-come, and cannot be booked ahead. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Torifito Naha Asahibashi, good value. A hotel at 1-11-19 Nishi, about an eight-minute walk from Asahibashi station, with a laundromat on the ground floor. Choose it for a simple, well-placed base at a modest price. Watch out: the Economy Double is 15.6 square metres. Check rates on Booking.com →

Chatan: American Village

Chatan sits on the west coast, about 40 minutes from the airport by car, and mixes a beach, shops, restaurants and a US-themed district known as American Village. It suits a stay that is half city and half beach, and a visitor with a car will find it easiest to reach.

Where to stay in Chatan

  • Hilton Okinawa Chatan Resort, splurge. In Mihama, with a lagoon pool, a cascade pool and an indoor pool with a sauna, ocean-view and city-view rooms, and an executive lounge on the 9th floor; guests can also use the two outdoor pools of the DoubleTree next door. Choose it for pools and space on the west coast. Watch out: parking has been charged since 15 May 2026. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Vessel Hotel Campana Okinawa, good value. Every room has a balcony, though the hotel notes that some do not face the sea, and the rooftop pool on the annex’s 12th floor faces the East China Sea. Choose it for sunset views at a fair price. Watch out: the rooftop pool’s 2026 season runs from 20 March to 31 October. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • La’gent Hotel Okinawa Chatan, budget. A West Coast America-themed hotel in Mihama, with a hostel area of eight-bed, single-sex dormitories and group dormitory rooms for five to seven. Parking costs ¥550 a night. Choose it for a cheap bed beside American Village. Watch out: the dormitories share their showers and bathrooms.

Onna: the resort coast

Onna village is where Okinawa’s big beach resorts line the west coast, about an hour from the airport. This is the place for a few days of pool, sea and room service, not for sightseeing on foot, and you will need the limousine bus or a car to see anything beyond your hotel’s grounds.

Where to stay in Onna

  • Halekulani Okinawa, splurge. 360 rooms facing a mile of coastline in Nakama, and five villas, each with a private pool and a natural hot spring. Choose it for the most luxurious beach stay in this guide. Watch out: from 10 October 2026 the Ocean Terrace Pool and the hot spring beside the indoor pool are closed until further notice. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Renaissance Okinawa Resort, comfortable. All 377 rooms have an ocean view and a balcony, and there are nine restaurants and bars and dolphin activities. Choose it for a family beach holiday. Watch out: it is about an hour from the airport by car. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hotel Monterey Okinawa Spa & Resort, comfortable. A British Colonial-style resort over Tiger Beach in Fuchaku, with a natural hot-spring bath, saunas, a wave pool and an infinity pool. Parking is ¥500 a night. Choose it for a spa day without leaving the resort. Watch out: its page quotes an older ¥1,530 limousine fare; the operator’s table says ¥1,700. Check rates on Booking.com →

Motobu: the Churaumi Aquarium

The Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium is in Ocean Expo Park on the Motobu Peninsula, far to the north. Staying beside it puts the aquarium on your doorstep, but it is a long way from everything else: the hotels put it at 60 to 100 minutes from the airport by car, and the buses take more than two hours.

Where to stay in Motobu

  • The Orion Hotel Motobu Resort & Spa, comfortable. Next to Ocean Expo Park and the aquarium, and the official hotel of Junglia Okinawa. Every room has an ocean view and a balcony; the Ocean Wing’s rooms are over 50 square metres, and the Club Wing is all suites, with a lounge and an infinity pool. Choose it for a resort next door to the aquarium. Watch out: the buses from the airport take more than two hours. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Royal View Hotel Churaumi, good value. By its own account the hotel nearest the aquarium, within walking distance of Emerald Beach, with a self-serve drink lounge for guests and free parking for 90 cars. The Standard Twin is 28.5 square metres and sleeps up to four. Choose it for a family stay at a lower price. Watch out: the garden pool is open from 1 April to 31 October only. Check rates on Booking.com →

How to book Okinawa

  • Count the tax for your dates. A stay with a night on or after 1 February 2027 pays the prefecture’s tax per person per night. A stay in 2026 does not.
  • Check the pool and facility dates. Several pools here shut for the winter or for maintenance: the Vessel’s rooftop pool and the Royal View’s garden pool close after 31 October, and the Halekulani’s main pool is closed for now.
  • Decide on a car first. If you are not renting one, stay in Naha and use the monorail, or pick a resort on a limousine-bus route and plan to stay put.
  • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and Where to stay in Japan puts Okinawa beside the mainland cities.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Okinawa for a first trip?

Naha, near Kokusai-dori, if you are not renting a car: the Yui Rail monorail runs from the airport, and Kokusai-dori, Makishi Public Market and three monorail stations are close. Choose Chatan for a beach and American Village, Onna for beach resorts and Motobu for the Churaumi Aquarium; those areas are easiest with a car.

Does Okinawa have an accommodation tax in 2026?

No. Okinawa Prefecture's accommodation tax starts on 1 February 2027, for nights from that date, including rooms booked earlier. It is 2% of the room-only price per person per night, rounded down to the nearest ¥1,000 first, with the tax capped at ¥2,000.

How much is the accommodation tax in Okinawa?

From 1 February 2027, 2% of the per-person, per-night room price before consumption tax and meals, capped at ¥2,000. On a room of ¥30,000 before tax for two people, that is ¥300 each, ¥600 a night. Where a municipality such as Chatan, Onna or Motobu also charges, the prefecture's page splits the 2% into 0.8% for the prefecture and 1.2% for the municipality.

How do you get from Naha Airport to Naha by monorail?

The Yui Rail starts at Naha Airport station. It takes 11 minutes and costs ¥290 to Asahibashi, 13 minutes and ¥290 to Kenchomae (Prefectural Office), and 16 minutes and ¥320 to Makishi, by the operator's fare table.

How do you get from Naha Airport to the resorts?

Okinawa Bus runs the Airport Limousine Bus: from 1 April 2026 the adult fare is ¥1,000 to Chatan Gate Way and the Chatan hotels and ¥1,700 to most Onna stops. In the operator's sample runs the bus takes about 80 minutes to Chatan and about an hour to the first Onna stops. Okinawa Bus lists no limousine route to Motobu; the Orion Hotel Motobu's page lists scheduled buses of more than two hours, and about 60 minutes by car.

Do you need a car in Okinawa?

Not in Naha, where the Yui Rail and walking cover most of the centre. For Chatan, Onna and Motobu a car is the easiest way to move around: Okinawa's tourism site says there are many rental companies across the islands, and some hotels sell stays bundled with a rental car. By the hotels' figures the drive from the airport is about 40 minutes to Chatan, 60 to 70 to Onna and 60 to 100 to Motobu.
