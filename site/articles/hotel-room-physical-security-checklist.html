Hotels

Hotel rooms have physical vulnerabilities most guests ignore.

Before you go: checklist

  1. Test the door lock and chain/deadbolt
  2. Check windows and balcony doors lock
  3. Place a door stop alarm for extra security
  4. Stow valuables in a hotel safe or hidden pouch

Why this helps

Small preparation steps before a trip may reduce avoidable stress and make travel days feel smoother.
