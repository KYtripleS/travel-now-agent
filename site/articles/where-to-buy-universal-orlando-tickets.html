    How to read it:

    • Tax is the catch. Universal’s own prices are “plus tax”. Viator’s listing says its price includes “6.5% Florida Sales Tax”. On our arithmetic, 6.5% on Universal’s $200 is $213.00, and on its $341.99 two-day price is $364.22, which is the same as Viator’s $364.22. So for the two-day ticket Viator and Universal are the same price once tax is counted. KKday’s and Tiqets’s listings did not say whether tax is in their figure, so we cannot say how they compare after tax.
    • KKday was $2 under Universal on the one-park ticket. Its listing does not say whether tax is in the figure. If it is, KKday’s $205 park-to-park ticket is also under Universal’s $213 with tax (our arithmetic); if it is not, compare the totals at checkout.
    • Tiqets’s park-to-park ticket was $225. Tiqets says its prices may be rounded, its ticket is non-refundable, and it is not valid for residents of the USA, Canada or the UK.
    • The date matters more than the seller. Universal’s December calendar for the one-day park-to-park ticket runs from $185 on 1 December to $245 from 23 December. The same ticket on 9 December was $200. Epic Universe’s one-day ticket showed “Sold Out” for 28 December on the day we looked.
    • The official site also prints a “starting from” price ($124.99 for one park, $134.99 for Epic Universe, $179.99 for park-to-park). Those are the lowest dates of the year, not the price for your date.

    What each seller’s listing says

    Universal Orlando, direct

    Universal says “Prices and availability vary by date and are subject to change at any time. Lowest prices are available on select dates.” Its ticket store lists adult tickets for ages 10 and over and child tickets for ages 3 to 9. On the one-day tickets the child price was $5 lower than the adult price on the “starting from” figures.

    On refunds, the FAQ on its tickets page says: “Tickets are non-refundable (except with Cancel For Any Reason product if purchased and eligible), but most may be eligible for exchange to a different date.” It adds that an additional fee may apply, because ticket prices vary by date, and gives Guest Services as the contact. We did not price the Cancel For Any Reason product.

    What park-to-park means, and how Epic Universe works

    These rules come from Universal’s own tickets page and ticket store.

    • A one-park ticket is one park for the day. Universal’s FAQ calls these “One-Park-Per-Day” tickets, sometimes called base tickets: one day at Epic Universe, Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure or Volcano Bay.
    • A park-to-park ticket lets you move between parks in one day. The one-day park-to-park ticket on the store covers Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure. Guests with that access can also ride the Hogwarts Express between Hogsmeade and Diagon Alley; Universal’s store lists that ride under the park-to-park ticket, not the one-park ticket.
    • Epic Universe needs a ticket that includes it. The FAQ says Epic Universe “can be added to your Park-to-Park or multi-day ticket” and to select an option that includes it. It is also sold as its own one-day, one-park ticket.
    • Universal’s multi-day packages split the parks in particular ways. The 3-day special ticket is described as “Valid for one day per park, with each day used at a different park.” The 2-park-plus-Epic offer is two days of park-to-park access to Studios and Islands of Adventure plus one day at Epic Universe. On 9 December, a 3-day ticket starting that day with park-to-park access to Epic Universe, Studios and Islands of Adventure showed $483.99 for an adult, and a 2-day park-to-park ticket (Studios and Islands of Adventure) showed $341.99 (children aged 3 to 9: $473.99 and $331.99). We did not compare the 3-day ticket with resellers because their 3-day products did not clearly match.
    • Volcano Bay is closed. Universal says it is “temporarily closed for maintenance and has a planned reopening on or before April 1, 2027”, so a December visit cannot include it.
    • Express Pass is separate. Universal’s FAQ says separate theme park admission is required, and that the room-key Express access for guests at three of its hotels is not applicable at Epic Universe or Volcano Bay.
    • Multi-day tickets have a window. The ticket store’s calendar says that after the first day of a 2-day ticket, the following visit must be within 4 days.

    Which seller for which trip

    • One park, lowest price: KKday. $143 on 9 December, with free cancellation up to two days before the date.
    • Park-to-park, lowest price: KKday. $205, against $225 on Tiqets and $230.53 on Viator with tax included.
    • Dates that might change: KKday or Viator. KKday allows free cancellation up to two days before; Viator’s 1-day option read “before 8 Dec” for a 9 December visit. Tiqets says non-refundable.
    • Two days park-to-park: Viator. $364.22 with tax included (US and Canadian residents only); KKday’s two-day price was not compared.
    • A visitor from outside the US, Canada and the UK: KKday or Tiqets. Tiqets’s ticket is for that audience; the residency line on the listing decides.

    Five checks before you pay

    1. Same date, same parks, same days. A one-park price is not comparable with a park-to-park price, and an Epic day is priced separately.
    2. Tax. Universal quotes before tax. Viator says its price includes 6.5% sales tax. KKday’s and Tiqets’s listings do not say, so confirm the total at checkout.
    3. Residency. Tiqets excludes US, Canadian and UK residents; Viator’s listing is for US and Canadian residents.
    4. The cancellation date. KKday says 2 days before; Viator’s listing said 8 December for a 9 December visit. Universal says non-refundable.
    5. Child ages. Universal’s child ticket is for ages 3 to 9; age 10 and over pays the adult price.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where should I buy Universal Orlando tickets?

    KKday. For a Wednesday 9 December 2026 visit, read on 16 November 2026, its one-park adult ticket was $143 and its one-day park-to-park ticket $205 (its listing does not say whether tax is included), against $225 on Tiqets and $230.53 on Viator with tax included. KKday also allows free cancellation up to two days before the date. Klook and GetYourGuide were not checked. Prices change daily.

    Can I get a refund on Universal Orlando tickets?

    Universal's FAQ says tickets are non-refundable, except with its Cancel For Any Reason product if purchased and eligible, but most may be eligible for exchange to a different date, possibly with a fee. KKday's listing allows free cancellation up to two days before the date, and Viator's 1-day option said free cancellation before 8 December for a 9 December visit. Tiqets says its ticket is non-refundable.

    What is the difference between a one-park ticket and a park-to-park ticket?

    A one-park ticket covers one park for the day. A park-to-park ticket lets you move between parks in the same day, and with access to Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure it also includes the Hogwarts Express between Hogsmeade and Diagon Alley.

    How do I get into Epic Universe?

    You need a ticket that includes it. Universal says it can be added to a park-to-park or multi-day ticket, and it is also sold as a one-day, one-park ticket, which cost $155 for an adult on 9 December 2026. Universal's 3-day special ticket is one day per park, each at a different park.

    Who can buy Tiqets and Viator Universal tickets?

    Tiqets's listing says its ticket is not valid for residents of the USA, Canada or the UK. Viator's listing we read is for US and Canadian residents only. KKday's listing asks you to present your e-voucher and passport on site and carries no residency line that we read.

    What are the child ticket ages?

    Universal's ticket store lists adult tickets for ages 10 and over and child tickets for ages 3 to 9.
