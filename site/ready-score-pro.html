Gently Yonder · Ready Score Pro

Know you are ready before you fly.

Ready Score Pro is an offline trip-readiness workbook: a score that stays red until the checks that can stop a trip are done, a do-by date for every task, and one place for passports, bookings, money and packing. It saves in your browser and prints to a clean plan.

The free Ready Score on our homepage gets you started. Pro is the workbook you keep for the trip you are actually taking.

A score that will not flatter you

The checks that can stop a trip are marked: passport validity, the visa, the entry form or authorisation, travel insurance and the first night's bed (plus children's documents on a family trip, and business-visit rules on a work trip). Until they are done the score stays red and names them, however high the percentage.

36 destinations, with dated notes

The same 36 destinations as the free Ready Score. Each one's entry step links to the official site, and Korea, the UK, Canada and the Schengen countries carry short notes with the month we checked them. Open the file more than six months later and it says so, because a downloaded file cannot update itself.

It tells you what is due

Every check has a do-by date counted back from the day you leave. The workbook shows only what is due in the next two weeks, overdue first. On the trip it keeps only what is still open; back home it says the trip is done.

Passports, one per traveller

Each passport is checked against your return date and the six months many countries ask for after it.

Bookings with their references

Flights, rooms, trains and tours with their dates and references, earliest first, ready to print.

Estimate, then what you spent

Set an estimate, log what you spend, and see what is left or how far over you are.

A packing list that starts itself

One tap loads a starter list for your trip style and destination, with the right plug type. It adds only what is missing and never removes your own items.

Your own checks, and more than one trip

Add checks of your own with their own do-by day, scored like the rest. Keep a separate workbook for each trip.

Yours, offline and private

One file that works without internet and saves in your own browser. No account, nothing uploaded. If your browser will not save (a private window, or full storage) it tells you. A backup file restores it anywhere, and it prints to a clean plan.
