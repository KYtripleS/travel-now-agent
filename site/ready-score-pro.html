Why not just use a planner app or a template?

Because they answer a different question. Trip-planner apps organise what you have already booked, or help you build a day-by-day itinerary. Notion-style planner templates give you a handsome set of empty tables to fill in. Both are useful. Neither of them knows anything: a template cannot tell you that Japan changed its tax-free process in November 2026, that Korea's K-ETA waiver runs to the end of 2026, or that the EU's ETIAS probably does not apply to your trip yet. And none of them will tell you that at 45 days out, the passport check you have not done is already late.

This is not an itinerary builder and it is not a journal. It answers one question properly: are you actually going to be allowed on this trip, and what is running late? One file, no account, no subscription, works on a plane.
