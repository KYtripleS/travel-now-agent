What teamLab’s own help page says about sellers

We read the official ticket store’s help page on 17 November 2026. Two passages matter.

Named sellers. Under “Other Websites and Ticket Agencies”, the page says tickets can also be bought through “the following ticket agencies” and lists three: Lawson Ticket (Loppi), Seven Ticket and Klook. No other online seller is named. The official FAQ also says tickets are not sold on site, and that tickets bought through travel agencies or convenience stores must be changed through those sales outlets, not through teamLab.

Resold tickets. In its answer on illegal resale of QR tickets, the page says that when a ticket is found to be resold on a ticketing site or an internet auction site, “the ticket is invalidated”. It adds that an invalidated ticket cannot be used to enter, cannot have its date or time changed, and is not refunded.

Our reading, and its limits. The wording is unclear. It does not say which sellers it means, and it does not define “resold”. It could be aimed at individuals reselling store tickets on auction sites rather than at travel platforms. We cannot tell, and we have not seen teamLab say either way. Because only three sellers are named, we read the passage as a risk for tickets from sellers the page does not name. We are not saying that KKday or Tiqets tickets are invalid. We are saying they are not on teamLab’s list, and that their listings carry stricter terms than the official store’s (below). Klook is the online seller the page names.

What each seller’s listing says

Each entry gives what the listing said on 17 November 2026.

Official ticket store (teamlabplanets.dmm.com). - Tickets can be changed up to 3 times, no later than 2 hours before the admission time. Tickets bought with a coupon, partial changes within one order and sold-out new slots cannot be changed. - Tickets cannot be cancelled or refunded once bought, apart from the change option. - The store says it charges no fees beyond the ticket price, and prices include tax. - Payment is by card (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express, Diners), PayPay or UnionPay; as a DMM member, cards only.

Klook. Named on the official help page. We could not read the listing or its cancellation terms, so we say nothing about them. See the Klook teamLab Planets listing.

Tiqets. The standard entry ticket listing says “This ticket is nonrefundable” and that rescheduling is not possible. It lists the supplier as LINKTIVITY Inc. and says you show a smartphone ticket at the entrance. Included: access to teamLab Planets TOKYO; not included: transport. It says the venue is explored barefoot, water can be knee-deep, and eating, drinking and smoking are not allowed.

KKday. The listing says “No cancellation” and that you present a QR code on site. It says you book a date and time slot on KKday and enter without DMM registration. Youth tickets need a student ID and a photo ID, and elementary-school children must be accompanied by an adult. Its Premium Pass is a single price for all ages; children aged 3 and under are free.

The museum’s own rules that change the buying decision

All from teamLab’s official pages, read 17 November 2026.

  • Entry is timed. The time on your ticket is the admission time, and the official help page says 30 minutes from it is the entry preparation time. It also warns you may wait 30 to 60 minutes. Arrive within the window.
  • Late arrival. teamLab’s FAQ says you can still enter the same day during open hours if you ask staff; last entry is one hour before closing.
  • Hours and closures. The ticket store lists 8:00 to 22:00, shorter on 31 December to 2 January (8:00 to 20:00). It lists closures on Thursday 3 December 2026 and Thursday 14 January 2027. The calendar shows no price for those dates.
  • Prices move by date. The calendar showed ¥3,800 for 1 to 18 December (except 3 December), ¥4,200 from 19 December, ¥4,600 from 26 December and ¥3,600 in late January. Tickets for February go on sale in late November.
  • Ages. Under 3 free; children 4-12 ¥1,500; junior high and high school ¥2,800; adults 18 and over from ¥3,800.
  • The Premium Pass costs ¥12,000 for adults and children aged 4 and above, and skips the entrance queue, not the queues inside.
  • Closing date. teamLab’s own page shows the run as “July 7, 2018 - End of 2027”, so Planets is advertised to run until the end of 2027. We found no more exact date, so we state none.

Which resellers does teamLab name?

Its ticket store's help page names Lawson Ticket (Loppi), Seven Ticket and Klook. It names no other online seller.

Can I change or cancel a teamLab Planets ticket?
