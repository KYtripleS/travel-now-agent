The 86th floor and the 102nd floor: what you are choosing

The two decks are different places. The building’s own FAQ says: “The Empire State Building’s 102nd Floor Observation Deck is enclosed and the 86th Floor Observation Deck is open to the air.” The 86th floor is at 1,050 feet and the 102nd at 1,250 feet. You reach the 102nd by a glass elevator from the 86th, so a combo ticket always includes both.

Every ticket includes the museum on the second and 80th floors. The express ticket skips those galleries and the elevator lines, though the building says guests may still visit them. That is the real difference between the standard and express columns below: you are paying for time, not for a higher floor.

Sunrise and AM/PM tickets

These two are the ones most readers ask about, and they are not like the standard tickets.

Ticket Official Tiqets GetYourGuide Viator KKday
Sunrise (86th floor, early entry) from $135, listed as seasonal from $137.80 not available 9 December; next date shown Friday 18 December from $147 not read
AM/PM (two visits in one day) from $67 not listed not listed not listed not read
  • Sunrise. Tiqets describes it as entry to the 86th floor before the deck opens to the public, with Starbucks Reserve coffee and pastries, and says it is limited to 40 visitors a day. Its listing says the ticket is “offered only on Saturday mornings”, yet the calendar we saw showed dates on 28 November and 18, 19, 25 and 26 December, none of them 9 December. Tiqets’s cancellation line for it is “Cancel for free until 24 hours before your visit date and get a full refund”, and you arrive 15 minutes before the start. The 102nd floor is not included. Because the dates are few, choose the date by the sunrise calendar, not by the rest of your trip.
  • AM/PM. The building lists “Two Visits in One Day” for day and night views from $67. We did not see it as a separate option on the Tiqets, GetYourGuide or Viator pages we read, so if you want it, check that the ticket you are about to buy says AM/PM.

What each seller’s listing says

All terms below were read on 21 November 2026 for a 9 December visit.

Empire State Building, esbnyc.com

The building’s ticket policy says: “Tickets purchased via the Website are non-refundable.” Its FAQ adds that tickets can be changed: “you can change your reservation” through the Manage My Booking portal, and some changes, such as a sunset upgrade, may cost extra. “All visits require a timed reservation”, and the ticket is only valid for the date and time you pick. The policy also says you may be asked for proof of identity such as a passport, driver’s licence or credit card, and that the building may close the observatories early on very busy days.

The building’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from esbnyc.com only.

  • A timed reservation is required. The FAQ says reservations are required for entry and that “All visits require a timed reservation”. If your plans are loose, the building sells a Flex ticket for a chosen date with arrival at any time during operating hours; GetYourGuide’s flex option was $71.86 for 9 December.
  • Late arrival. If you are late, you may have to change to the next available slot.
  • Children and others. “Toddlers (5 or younger) are admitted free when accompanied by an adult ticket holder”; discounted tickets are listed for children aged 6 to 12 and for seniors aged 62 and over, and active military in uniform get free admission. We priced adults only.
  • Express. The express pass skips the exhibits and the elevator lines. It does not add a floor on its own: there is an express ticket for the 86th floor and one for the 102nd.
  • Bags and security. Large bags, tripods, glass items and outside food are not allowed, and there are no lockers.
  • Hours. The building is open every day; the page we read said 10 am to 10 pm with sunset at 4:34 pm.

Which partner to book

  • The 86th floor on a fixed date: Tiqets, GetYourGuide or KKday. All showed $54.44 for 9 December.
  • Both decks: Tiqets. It was $89.82, with GetYourGuide $5.99 above it and Viator $10.34 above it.
  • The most flexible terms: GetYourGuide. Free cancellation until the day before, pay later, and a flex option.
  • KKday is the one to look at if you want a four-day cancellation rule and are happy to give backup dates; its combo and express figures are “from” prices.

Five checks before you pay

  1. The floor count. A $54 ticket is the 86th floor only; the 102nd is a separate, dearer product on every seller.
  2. The time slot. Prices differ by slot; check the figure for the time you choose.
  3. The cancellation date. For 9 December, GetYourGuide and Viator said 8 December; KKday said four days before.
  4. Express or standard. Express skips the exhibits and the elevator lines; it is not a higher floor.
  5. Sunrise dates. Sunrise appears on very few dates, and not on 9 December.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Empire State Building tickets?

Tiqets or GetYourGuide for a dated ticket. On 21 November 2026, for a Wednesday 9 December visit, both showed $54.44 for the 86th floor and $99.08 for the express ticket. For both decks, Tiqets showed $89.82 and GetYourGuide $95.81. Klook was not checked.

What is the difference between the 86th and 102nd floors?

The 86th floor is the open-air deck at 1,050 feet; the 102nd floor is enclosed, at 1,250 feet, behind floor-to-ceiling glass and reached by a glass elevator from the 86th. A combo ticket includes both. On 9 December, the combo was $89.82 on Tiqets, $95.81 on GetYourGuide and $100.16 on Viator.

Can I cancel an Empire State Building ticket?

It depends on the seller. GetYourGuide said "Cancel for free until Dec 8" for a 9 December visit, Viator said free cancellation before 10:00 AM on 8 December, and KKday allows cancellation up to four days before. Tiqets showed "Free cancellation options available" for the standard ticket without a dated cut-off, and 24 hours for sunrise.

Is the sunrise ticket available?

On the dates we read it was limited. It was not available on 9 December on GetYourGuide, and Tiqets showed dates on 28 November and 18, 19, 25 and 26 December. Tiqets lists it from $137.80 with a capacity of 40 visitors a day, and Viator from $147.

What is the AM/PM ticket?

The building lists it as two visits in one day, for day and night views, from $67. We did not see it as a separate option on the Tiqets, GetYourGuide or Viator pages we read.

Do children need a ticket?

The building's policy says children aged 5 or younger are admitted free when accompanied by an adult ticket holder. Discounted tickets are listed for ages 6 to 12 and for seniors aged 62 and over. We priced adults only.
