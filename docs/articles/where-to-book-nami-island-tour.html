For Saturday 12 December 2026, the same island is sold as at least five different days from Seoul, and the price swings with what is bolted on. A Nami Island and Petite France coach day was $35 on GetYourGuide. Nami with the Garden of Morning Calm and its winter light festival was $54.08 on GetYourGuide and £39.21 on Viator. KKday’s Nami, Petite France and Garden day was $72.85. The winter extras that fill December search results, Eobi Ice Valley and the Elysian Gangchon ski package, are mostly not on sale for that date: only one listing we opened offered Eobi on 12 December, at $89.55.

What differs between sellers is less the island than the shape of the day: which stops are included, whether you leave at 7:20 or at midday, whether lunch is included (on every listing we read it was not), how many travellers a departure needs, and how late you can cancel. Several listings were sold out or had no seat on our date, so availability matters more than a few dollars.

What the island itself says

These are Nami Island’s own rules, read on 22 November 2026 on its English site.

  • Entry fee. The regular adult fee is ₩19,000 with the round-trip ferry fare included. The discount rate is ₩16,000 for middle and high school students and for ages 70 and over; the special rate is ₩13,000 for children from 36 months to elementary school age. Children aged 36 months and under, and tour guides, enter free.
  • Evening rate. The special ₩13,000 rate also applies in December to March to tickets bought after 18:01, and to early-bird tickets bought and boarded before the 08:00 ferry.
  • Open all year. The island says it is open throughout the year, so a winter date is not a closed date. Opening hours and the ferry timetable are on its own pages and change by season.

Every day-tour listing above states that Nami admission is included, so the fee is not an extra on top of those prices.

How the days differ

Stops and time on the island. The shortest days give Nami roughly two to four hours. The listings spell it out: Viator’s Garden day gives Nami 4 hours and the Garden 2; KKday’s winter day gives Nami 2 hours; GetYourGuide’s Petite France itinerary lists the island for 1 hour. If Nami itself is your reason for going, read the minutes on the listing, not the title.

Start time and the light festival. In December the Garden of Morning Calm days start late, around midday on GetYourGuide’s and Viator’s Garden listings, so the Garden can be seen after dark. The Petite France-only day starts at 8:40 or 9:15. KKday’s winter day starts from 7:50 and reaches the Garden at 17:30.

Rail bike. Adding the Gangchon rail bike moves the start to between 7:10 and 9:10 and the price up by roughly $22 to $44 on GetYourGuide, depending on the base day. The $76.53 and $79 prices are for different combinations, so compare them with the base day they sit on, not with each other.

Four checks before you book

  1. Which stops do you want? Nami and Petite France for the lowest price, Nami and the Garden for the light festival, all three at KKday, or Eobi if you want the ice valley on 12 December.
  2. How long can you cancel? Until 11 December on GetYourGuide and Viator, 2 days before on KKday.
  3. Is lunch in the price? On every listing we read it was not. Budget about KRW 15,000 to 20,000, the figure GetYourGuide gives.
  4. Does the departure run? GetYourGuide’s Petite France day needs 8 people and says so; the Garden listing says no minimum; KKday guarantees departure.

You can see the Nami and Petite France day on GetYourGuide, open the Nami and Garden of Morning Calm day, look at the Viator version, or check KKday’s three-stop day. Frequently asked questions

Which seller is cheapest for a Nami Island day tour from Seoul?

On 12 December 2026 GetYourGuide showed $35 for Nami and Petite France, the lowest price we read. For Nami and the Garden it showed $54.08 and Viator £39.21; we did not convert between currencies.

Is the Nami Island ticket and ferry included?

Every listing we read includes Nami admission, and Nami's own fee of ₩19,000 already includes the round-trip ferry. GetYourGuide's Garden listing says ferry and entry are included.

Is lunch included?

No listing we read included lunch. GetYourGuide puts a meal at KRW 15,000 to 20,000, paid to the restaurant on the day.

Can I see Eobi Ice Valley or ski on 12 December?

GetYourGuide's $89.55 day included Eobi on 12 December. KKday and Trip.com showed Eobi days from 15 December, and KKday's Elysian Gangchon ski package from 25 December.

How late can I cancel?

GetYourGuide and Viator showed free cancellation until 11 December for the 12 December departures. KKday allows cancellation up to 2 days before.

Is the island open in winter?

Yes. Nami Island says it is open throughout the year, and it charges a lower ₩13,000 special rate in December to March for tickets bought after 18:01.
