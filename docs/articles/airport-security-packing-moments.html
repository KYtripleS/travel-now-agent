Carry-on

Carry-on packing gets easier when you separate things by airport moments.

Before you go: checklist

  1. Liquids near the top
  2. Laptop easy to remove
  3. Passport in the same pocket
  4. Charger in your personal item

Why this helps

Small preparation steps before a trip may reduce avoidable stress and make travel days feel smoother.
