    Aspen’s room rate is only the start of the bill. The City of Aspen says that from 2026 the combined sales and lodging taxes on a night in a traditional lodge come to 12.35%, and the Town of Snowmass Village’s own tax guide puts a Snowmass hotel night at 13.05%. The season has its own wrinkle: Aspen Mountain and Snowmass are scheduled to open on 26 November 2026, Aspen Highlands and Buttermilk on 12 December, and the Aspen/Pitkin County Airport is due to close from 4 April 2027, while Aspen Mountain is still scheduled to be skiing until 18 April.

    The 2026/27 season

    The resort’s own FAQ gives the scheduled dates for each of its four mountains, and they depend on weather and conditions.

    Mountain Scheduled opening Scheduled closing
    Aspen Mountain 26 November 2026 18 April 2027
    Snowmass 26 November 2026 11 April 2027
    Aspen Highlands 12 December 2026 11 April 2027
    Buttermilk 12 December 2026 4 April 2027

    Two details are worth knowing before you book. First, the resort’s general “visit” page shows a single window of 26 November to 18 April for the whole resort, but the lift-ticket FAQ, which sets the dates mountain by mountain, closes Snowmass on 11 April. Second, one lift ticket covers all four mountains, according to Snowmass Village’s tourism office, and Ikon Pass holders can ski up to seven days across the resort.

    The bigger issue for a spring trip is the airport. Aspen/Pitkin County Airport says it will close from 11:00 pm on Sunday 4 April 2027 until 7:00 pm on Friday 19 November 2027 for a full runway rebuild. That leaves two weeks of Aspen Mountain’s scheduled season with no flights into Aspen itself, so an April trip means flying into one of the regional airports described below.

    Downtown Aspen: restaurants, galleries and the gondola

    Downtown is where most visitors want to be, with dining and shopping a walk away, and one of the three hotels here puts the Silver Queen Gondola within walking distance. They run from a hotel that has stood since 1889 to a boutique hotel that includes breakfast.

    • Hotel Jerome, splurge. 330 East Main Street, which the hotel says has been the social hub of Aspen since 1889, with Deluxe Rooms, Junior Suites, Luxury Suites and Residential Suites, and Yarrow Spa. It carries one Michelin Key. Choose it for a historic address in the middle of town. Watch out: when we looked, its page said online booking was temporarily unavailable and asked guests to call 855.331.7213.

Choose it for a boutique base. Watch out: it describes shuttle service to and from the airport or around town but does not say whether it is free, and its winter offer is listed for stays through 17 April 2027. Garmisch Street, which the hotel describes as within a short walk of downtown Aspen, with all four ski areas easily accessible, and a cafe serving breakfast and lunch. Choose it for a smaller base a short walk from downtown. Watch out: its page gives no distance to Aspen Mountain and does not mention a pool or fees, so confirm both before you book.

    The base of Aspen Mountain: two luxury resorts

    At the foot of Aspen Mountain, two resorts describe themselves as at the base, and both are splurges. They suit a short, ski-focused stay.

    Where to stay at the base of Aspen Mountain

    • The Little Nell, splurge. 675 E Durant Avenue, which the hotel calls a five-star ski-in, ski-out hotel at the base of Aspen Mountain, with Aspen’s only slopeside spa according to its own page, First Tracks skiing before the mountain opens to the public, and six Luxury Suites. Choose it for the slopes at the door. Watch out: the Aspen Chamber says, as of summer 2026, that The Little Nell will close for renovations while the airport is shut (from 4 April 2027), so for a trip after early April, check that it is taking bookings. Its home page also gives no room total and no fee list. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • The St. Regis Aspen Resort, splurge. 315 East Dean Street, with 154 guestrooms, 22 suites and 3 specialty suites, a 24-hour heated pool and hot tubs, and the RAKxa Wellness Spa. Choose it for a large resort with a night-time pool. Watch out: its Pool Bar & Patio was shown as closed for the season, so the pool is there but not the poolside bar. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Snowmass at the lifts: ski-in, ski-out

    Snowmass Village is a separate resort village about 6 miles from the airport and, according to the village’s tourism office, about 9 miles from Aspen, with free shuttles linking the two. The village’s tourism office says there is no need for a car once you are there. These three hotels each publish direct lift access.

    Where to stay in Snowmass at the lifts

    • Viceroy Snowmass, splurge. 130 Wood Road, which the hotel calls the only ski-in, ski-out luxury resort in Snowmass, with guest suites and residences, a 7,000-square-foot spa and an outdoor pool terrace that stays open year-round. Choose it for suite space and a spa at the slopes. Watch out: the Town’s tax guide names the Base Village Master Association as the contact for a special lodging assessment and resort fee, so ask whether one applies to your booking. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Limelight Hotel Snowmass, comfortable. 65 Wood Road, with ski-in, ski-out access at the base of the Elk Camp Gondola, heated spa pools, a full breakfast buffet included, a kids’ room and a five-story climbing wall. Choose it for families, with breakfast covered. Watch out: its winter offer is listed for stays through 10 April 2027, a day before Snowmass’s scheduled closing. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Viewline Resort Snowmass, comfortable. 100 Elbert Lane, a slopeside resort in the Autograph Collection with ski-in, ski-out suites directly off Fanny Hill and newly reimagined rooms. Choose it for a ski-in room off Fanny Hill. Watch out: its home page gives no room count or fee list, so ask about resort charges before you compare totals. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Snowmass, lower-priced: Stonebridge Inn and Mountain Chalet

    Two Snowmass hotels sit lower on the price scale than the resorts above, and each still describes lift access.

    Where to stay in Snowmass, lower-priced

    • Stonebridge Inn, good value. 300 Carriage Way, a fully renovated inn with 93 guest rooms and suites that the hotel describes as nearly slopeside and a short walk from the lifts. Choose it for a refreshed room in the village. Watch out: it says “nearly” slopeside, not ski-in, ski-out, so look at the resort map before you book. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Mountain Chalet Snowmass, good value. 115 Daly Lane, with 64 rooms a flight of stairs from the Snowmass Mall, an outdoor heated pool and hot tub, complimentary breakfast and lunch, a heated parking garage, and the free “Skittles” gondola down to Base Village. Choose it for a meal-inclusive stay at a lower price. Watch out: its online booking applied a minimum-stay rule to some January 2027 dates when we looked, and the main site has an old copyright date, so confirm directly. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Down valley: Basalt and Glenwood Springs

    Staying down the Roaring Fork Valley costs a bus ride each way. Glenwood Springs is 41 miles from Aspen on Highway 82, according to Aspen Snowmass.

    Where to stay down valley

    • Element by Marriott Basalt - Aspen, good value. 499 Market Street, Basalt, with a kitchen, a free hotel breakfast, an indoor pool and complimentary on-site parking. Choose it for a self-catering base. Watch out: it is a ride from Aspen, and the RFTA fare from the Basalt, Willits and El Jebel zone is $3 each way. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Hotel Colorado, good value. 526 Pine Street, Glenwood Springs, a historic hotel that has welcomed guests since 1893, in downtown Glenwood Springs and steps from Glenwood Hot Springs. Choose it for the hot springs after a ski day. Watch out: the RFTA fare from Glenwood Springs to Aspen is $5 each way, and the town is 41 miles from Aspen, so this is the longest commute in the guide.

    The lodging tax

    Aspen and Snowmass Village each add their own taxes on top of the room rate. We found official figures for both; the down-valley towns set their own rates, which we did not verify, so check the total at checkout there.

    Where Sales taxes Lodging tax Total on a hotel night
    City of Aspen, traditional lodge 10.35% 2.00% 12.35%
    Town of Snowmass Village 10.65% 2.40% 13.05%

    The City of Aspen lists its 2026 sales tax as 10.35%: the city 2.70%, Pitkin County 3.60%, the Roaring Fork Transportation Authority 0.40%, the State of Colorado 2.90%, the Aspen Fire Protection District 0.50% and the Confluence Early Childhood Development Service District 0.25%. Its two lodging taxes are 1.00% each. Snowmass Village’s guide lists its sales tax as 10.65%, with the town at 3.50%, the state at 2.90%, the county at 3.60%, the transportation authority at 0.40% and Confluence at 0.25%, plus a 2.40% town lodging tax.

    If you rent a house or condo instead of a hotel room in Aspen, the rate is higher. The city’s page lists 17.35% for an owner-occupied or lodge-exempt short-term rental and 22.35% for an investment or second-home “classic” permitted property.

    A worked example, our arithmetic: a US$500 room is US$61.75 more in Aspen at 12.35%, so US$561.75, and US$65.25 more in Snowmass Village at 13.05%, so US$565.25. The same US$500 in a classic short-term rental in Aspen at 22.35% would come to US$611.75. We have not verified whether either place applies the tax to extra fees such as a resort charge, so ask the hotel whether any added fee is taxed too. For how this compares, see hotel taxes around the world.

    Getting in, and around

    By air. Aspen/Pitkin County Airport (ASE) is 3 miles east of downtown Aspen on Highway 82, according to Aspen Snowmass, and about 6 miles from Snowmass Village, a drive of roughly 15 minutes. The airport says free RFTA bus service runs into Aspen and Snowmass Village, and taxis and hotel shuttles meet guests outside Baggage Claim. Rental cars are at the airport from Avis and Budget.

    Other airports. Aspen Snowmass lists the distances to downtown Aspen as 70 miles from Eagle County Airport (EGE), 125 miles from Grand Junction (GJT) and 220 miles from Denver (DEN), all via I-70 and Colorado 82. The airport’s closure FAQ names Denver, Grand Junction, Montrose and Vail/Eagle County as the airports to use while ASE is closed in 2027, and says transport between them and Aspen had not been arranged when it was written, though rental cars stay available. Roaring Fork Express, listed on the Aspen Snowmass site, runs scheduled shuttles between Aspen and Snowmass and either Denver or Eagle; we could not confirm its fares.

    By road in winter. Highway 82 over Independence Pass, east of Aspen, is closed generally from late October through May, so the way in from Denver is I-70 to Glenwood Springs and then 41 miles on Highway 82.

    By bus. The RFTA fare table lists these adult single fares from the Aspen and Snowmass Village zone:

    To Fare
    Within Aspen and Snowmass Village, and Aspen to Snowmass Village Free
    Aspen Village $2
    Basalt, Willits, El Jebel $3
    Carbondale $4
    Glenwood Springs $5

    All City of Aspen routes are free, and the winter-only Four Mountain Connector, which links Aspen Mountain, Aspen Highlands, Buttermilk and Snowmass, is always free. Seniors 65 and over and children 5 and under ride free, and youths aged 6 to 18 pay $1 when they leave the free zone.

    How to book Aspen

    • Count the tax. Add 12.35% in Aspen or 13.05% in Snowmass Village to the room price, and ask about any resort or special assessment charge on top.
    • Watch the season. Aspen Mountain and Snowmass open on 26 November and Highlands and Buttermilk on 12 December, so an early-December trip has two mountains, not four.
    • Plan April around the airport. From 4 April 2027 there are no flights into Aspen, so price a flight to Eagle or Denver and the ground leg.
    • Check what the room includes. Limelight Aspen and Limelight Snowmass include breakfast, Mountain Chalet includes breakfast and lunch, and Element Basalt has free parking.
    • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and our Whistler guide and Zermatt guide are the other ski-season guides in this series.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the best area to stay in Aspen for skiing?

    For a first trip, Downtown Aspen: dining and shopping are a walk away and the Silver Queen Gondola is within walking distance of Limelight Hotel Aspen. The Little Nell and The St. Regis Aspen Resort describe themselves as at the base of Aspen Mountain, and Snowmass Village suits ski-in, ski-out stays at the lifts.

    When does the Aspen Snowmass ski season start and end in 2026/27?

    Aspen Mountain and Snowmass are scheduled to open on 26 November 2026 and Aspen Highlands and Buttermilk on 12 December. Scheduled closing dates are 18 April 2027 for Aspen Mountain, 11 April for Snowmass and Aspen Highlands, and 4 April for Buttermilk, all depending on weather and conditions.

    How much tax is added to an Aspen hotel room?

    The City of Aspen says the combined sales and lodging tax on a night in a traditional lodge is 12.35% from 2026: 10.35% sales tax plus two 1.00% lodging taxes. For example, a US$500 room becomes US$561.75, by our arithmetic. Snowmass Village's guide puts its total at 13.05%, so the same room comes to US$565.25.

    Is Aspen airport closing in 2027?

    Yes. Aspen/Pitkin County Airport says it will close at 11:00 pm on 4 April 2027 and reopen at 7:00 pm on 19 November 2027 for runway reconstruction. Aspen Mountain is scheduled to ski until 18 April, so an April trip means flying into Denver, Eagle County, Grand Junction or Montrose and travelling by road.

    How do you get around Aspen and Snowmass without a car?

    RFTA buses are free within Aspen and Snowmass Village, on all City of Aspen routes and on the winter-only Four Mountain Connector linking the four mountains. Down valley, the adult fare from Aspen is $2 to Aspen Village, $3 to Basalt, Willits and El Jebel, $4 to Carbondale and $5 to Glenwood Springs.

    How far is Eagle airport from Aspen?

    Aspen Snowmass lists Eagle County Airport (EGE) as 70 miles from downtown Aspen via I-70 and Colorado 82, Grand Junction as 125 miles and Denver as 220 miles. Aspen/Pitkin County Airport itself is 3 miles from downtown, and Highway 82 over Independence Pass is closed in winter, so the road from Denver runs through Glenwood Springs.
