Tourist Taxes Around the World 2026: City-by-City Data for 85 Cities

By Casey, Gently Yonder editor

Tourist and hotel taxes in 85 cities, 2026: the rule, what each adds to a 3-night stay, scheduled changes and official sources. Free to cite, with a CSV.

Last updated 30 Oct 2026 · 58 min read

A hotel’s quoted rate is rarely the whole bill. In most of the 85 cities below, a tourist, city or accommodation tax is added per person, per room or as a percentage of the price. This page puts them side by side: what each city charges, and what it adds to one standard stay, with the official source for every figure. Journalists and researchers may cite and reuse it with a link.

Key findings

  • The highest tax on our example stay (3 nights, 2 adults, one room at US$200 a night before tax, four-star where class matters): Chicago, about US$104; Washington, DC, about US$96; Santa Monica, about US$90; Boston, about US$90; Cambridge, MA, about US$87.
  • US cities top the table because their hotel taxes are percentages of the room price: 14 of the 55 cities we could compute are in the United States.
  • 25 cities charge no accommodation-specific tax in 2026 on the evidence we have, among them Copenhagen, Dublin, Kraków, London, Madrid, Seville, Stockholm, Bangkok (VAT, GST or a service charge may still apply; see each city).
  • 23 cities have a change scheduled or under discussion; the dated ones are listed under What changes next.

The example stay, and what counts

  • The stay: 3 nights, 2 adults, one room at US$200/night before tax, 4-star. The US$200 is converted to each local currency at the ECB reference rate for 2026-10-23, and the tax is converted back at the same rate.
  • What counts: taxes specific to accommodation (tourist taxes, city taxes, occupancy taxes, visitor levies). General VAT, GST and sales tax, and hotels’ service charges, are listed separately for each city and are not in the example, because they apply to most purchases, not only to rooms.
  • Where we did not compute: when the rule depends on something the example does not fix (which district, whether a band is per person or per room, whether the base includes VAT), we show the rule and leave the example blank rather than guess.
  • Sources: each rate comes from the city, regional or national government, or the tax authority, and is recorded in our facts ledger with the date we checked it. See how we verify.

What changes next

  • Amsterdam: The PRO and D66 coalition agreement (June 2026) plans to raise Amsterdam’s tourist tax from 12.5% to 16% in 2027 and then by one point a year to 20% in 2031; as of October 2026 we found no council decision adopting it.
  • Barcelona: Ordinance 2.2 (approved 30 January 2026) phases the city surcharge in: EUR 5 from 1 April 2026, EUR 6 from 1 April 2027, EUR 7 from 1 April 2028, reaching the EUR 8 maximum from 1 April 2029.
  • Copenhagen: A reported 2027 hotel tax for Copenhagen and Aarhus was left out of the government’s 2027 finance bill (October 2026).
  • Dubai: Charged for a maximum of 30 consecutive nights; possible exemption announced 21 May 2026 (no end date found).
  • Dublin: Dublin City Council has stated it lacks the legal capacity to introduce a bed tax without new national legislation; the Budget 2027 tax policy changes, announced 6 October 2026, include no visitor levy or accommodation VAT change.
  • Hakone: Hakone’s council passed an accommodation tax of JPY 350 per person per night on 2 October 2026; the town aims to start it in April 2028, once the Minister for Internal Affairs consents, and the bathing tax stays alongside it.
  • Hakuba: From 1 June 2029 Hakuba’s bands become JPY 300, JPY 500, JPY 1,000 and JPY 2,000, the prefecture’s share JPY 150 (and Nagano Prefecture’s own tax rises from JPY 200 to JPY 300).
  • Istanbul: Set at 1% until 31 December 2026 inclusive; GİB’s guide describes it as a cut from 2% valid 1 May-31 December 2026. The ledger does not state the rate from 1 January 2027.
  • Jeju: A visitor environmental contribution has been discussed in earlier years; none in force.
  • Kraków: A Polish amendment applies from 1 January 2027: a council may introduce a local fee in places with landscape value of up to 5.00 zloty a person a night (5.50 in spa-protection areas); no Kraków council resolution was found.
  • London: On 10 September 2026 the government said mayors of strategic authorities will be able to choose to charge a levy set as a percentage of the accommodation cost; it intends to legislate in the current Parliamentary session and expects local leaders to set out revenue plans by March 2028. No rate or date is set.
  • Los Angeles: On 2 June 2026 voters rejected Proposition TT (raising the city hotel tax from 14% to 16% through 2028, then 15%); no change.
  • Niseko: For stays from 1 November 2026 the tax becomes 3% of the room price in total (ministerial consent 27 March 2026).
  • Okinawa: Starts for stays from 1 February 2027: 2% of the room-only price per person per night, capped at JPY 2,000; the base is rounded down to the nearest JPY 1,000 and capped at JPY 100,000. Where a municipality also charges, the 2% is split 0.8% (cap JPY 800) prefecture and 1.2% (cap JPY 1,200) municipality; Motobu, Onna, Chatan, Miyakojima, Ishigaki and Nago are listed, Naha is not.
  • Porto: On 25 September 2026 Lusa reported that Porto council would vote on starting the administrative procedure to amend the tourist tax regulation; no value named and no decision found (in July the mayor had admitted raising the tax from three to four euros).
  • Queenstown: Proposed levy on hold; in August 2026 the National Party said it would not introduce a bed-night levy. No rate or date.
  • Richmond, BC: Renewed for five years through to 2027.
  • Rio de Janeiro: A bill for a R$15 ‘sustainable tourism fee’ on visitors staying in the city (up to R$22.50 by decree) was before the City Council’s committees in June 2026, with no plenary vote scheduled.
  • Seville: On 5 August 2026 Seville’s mayor said he would ask the Junta for a tourist tax or similar extra funding; the Junta maintains its refusal. It is a proposal, not law.
  • Stockholm: A 2025/26 Riksdag motion (2025/26:2817, V) asks the government to investigate letting municipalities introduce local bed fees (bäddavgifter); no change stated.
  • Tokyo: From 1 April 2027 Tokyo’s accommodation tax becomes 3% of the room charge on stays of JPY 13,000 or more per person per night, with cheaper stays exempt and licensed private lodging (minpaku) included.
  • Vienna: From 1 July 2027 Vienna’s Ortstaxe is 8% of the charge for the stay, excluding VAT and breakfast.
  • Washington, DC: The 15.95% rate is extended through September 30, 2027 (OTR notice of October 2025); the ledger does not state the rate after that date.

Asia: the rules, city by city

Shanghai tourist tax 2026

none. In the Shanghai government and tax-authority pages read, no separate per-night tourist or hotel occupancy tax is mentioned; this is a negative finding, not an official statement that none exists.

Also on the bill: China’s VAT Law (in force from 1 January 2026) sets VAT at 6% for services not listed at higher rates; the ledger notes the rate for hotel rooms is the editors’ reading of these texts, not a sentence in any one of them. VAT is a tax outside the price and must be shown separately.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 20 October 2026. Our guide: Shanghai.

Hong Kong tourist tax 2026

Hotel Accommodation Tax. Hong Kong’s Hotel Accommodation Tax has been charged again since 1 January 2025, at 3% of the room charge; the service charge, normally 10%, is not taxed, so a bill runs about 13% over the room rate.

Example stay: HKD 141.18 (about US$18.00). 3% of the room charge x 3 nights; the service charge is not taxed.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 4 October 2026. Our guide: Hong Kong.

Bali (Badung regency) tourist tax 2026

PBJT on hotel services (jasa perhotelan). Badung regency’s PBJT on hotel services (jasa perhotelan) is 10%, and on food and drink 10%, under Perda Kabupaten Badung 7/2023, in force from 1 January 2024; the 2025 amendment (Perda 8/2025) did not change these rates.

Example stay: IDR 1,086,480.00 (about US$60.00). 10% of the hotel price x 3 nights (the ledger’s own example takes 10% of the nightly price; it does not state whether the base includes service charge).

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 13 October 2026.

Fukuoka tourist tax 2026

accommodation tax (city and prefecture). Fukuoka City and Fukuoka Prefecture together charge an accommodation tax of JPY 200 per person per night on a room charge under JPY 20,000 and JPY 500 from JPY 20,000 (the prefecture’s share JPY 50 in both bands), since 1 April 2020.

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

Example stay: JPY 1,200.00 (about US$7.65). JPY 200 x 2 adults x 3 nights (band under JPY 20,000). A US$200 room is JPY 31,360, or JPY 15,680 per person per night shared by 2 adults; Fukuoka sets the band by the charge per person per night. With 10% consumption tax added the price per person is JPY 17,248, which falls in the same band.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 28 October 2026. Our guide: Fukuoka.

Hakone tourist tax 2026

bathing tax (nyūtōzei) on hot-spring stays. Hakone has no accommodation tax in 2026; guests at hot-spring facilities pay the town’s bathing tax of JPY 150 per person per night (JPY 50 per day visitor), children under 12 and school trips exempt.

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

Example stay: JPY 900.00 (about US$5.74). JPY 150 x 2 adults x 3 nights, at a hotel or ryokan with a hot-spring bath; a property without one charges no bathing tax.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 28 October 2026. Our guide: Hakone.

Hakuba tourist tax 2026

accommodation tax (Hakuba Village with Nagano Prefecture’s share). Since 1 June 2026 Hakuba Village charges an accommodation tax, collected together with Nagano Prefecture’s JPY 100 share, of JPY 200 per person per night on room-only prices (before consumption tax) of JPY 6,000-19,999, JPY 400 from JPY 20,000, JPY 900 from JPY 50,000 and JPY 1,900 from JPY 100,000, until 31 May 2029; from 1 June 2029 it is JPY 300, JPY 500, JPY 1,000 and JPY 2,000, the prefecture’s share JPY 150.

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

Example stay: JPY 1,200.00 (about US$7.65). JPY 200 x 2 adults x 3 nights (band JPY 6,000 to JPY 19,999). A US$200 room is JPY 31,360, or JPY 15,680 per person per night shared by 2 adults; Hakuba Village sets the band by the charge per person per night, room-only and before consumption tax.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 28 October 2026. Our guide: Hakuba.

Hiroshima tourist tax 2026

Hiroshima Prefecture accommodation tax. Hiroshima Prefecture has charged an accommodation tax since 1 April 2026: JPY 200 per person per night when the room-only price (including service charge, excluding meals and consumption tax) is JPY 6,000 or more, nothing below JPY 6,000.

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

Example stay: JPY 1,200.00 (about US$7.65). JPY 200 x 2 adults x 3 nights. A US$200 room is JPY 31,360 per room and JPY 15,680 per person, both above the JPY 6,000 threshold, so the result does not depend on whether the threshold is per room or per person.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 21 October 2026. Our guide: Hiroshima.

Kanazawa tourist tax 2026

accommodation tax. Kanazawa City’s accommodation tax is JPY 200 per person per night for a room charge of JPY 5,000 to under JPY 20,000 and JPY 500 from JPY 20,000, measured before consumption tax; stays under JPY 5,000 per person per night are exempt (exemption in force since 1 October 2024; tax introduced April 2019).

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

Example stay: JPY 1,200.00 (about US$7.65). JPY 200 x 2 adults x 3 nights (band JPY 5,000 to under JPY 20,000). A US$200 room is JPY 31,360, or JPY 15,680 per person per night shared by 2 adults; Kanazawa City sets the band by the charge per person per night, before consumption tax.

Kutchan accommodation tax (Hokkaido tax and town tax). Since 1 April 2026 Kutchan charges 3% of the room price in total: Hokkaido’s tiered tax plus the town’s tax for the rest, collected by the town; it was 2% until 31 March 2026.

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

Kyoto tourist tax 2026

accommodation tax. Since 1 March 2026 Kyoto City charges an accommodation tax per person per night by room rate: JPY 200 under JPY 6,000, JPY 400 from JPY 6,000, JPY 1,000 from JPY 20,000, JPY 4,000 from JPY 50,000 and JPY 10,000 from JPY 100,000.

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

A US$200 room is JPY 31,360, or JPY 15,680 per person per night shared by 2 adults; Kyoto City sets the band by the charge per person per night, the room-only price excluding meals and consumption tax. Niseko tourist tax 2026

Niseko Town accommodation tax. For stays from 1 November 2026 Niseko Town’s accommodation tax becomes 3% of the room price in total, the town collecting Hokkaido’s share within it; since 1 November 2024 it has been a tiered fixed amount from JPY 100 to JPY 2,000 per person per night by room price.

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

Example stay: JPY 2,822.40 (about US$18.00). Rule for stays from 1 November 2026: 3% of the room price in total x 3 nights. The ledger does not state whether consumption tax is in the base. The pre-1 November tier bands are not in the ledger, so the earlier rule cannot be computed.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 20 October 2026. Our guide: Niseko.

Okinawa tourist tax 2026

Okinawa Prefecture accommodation tax. Okinawa Prefecture’s accommodation tax starts for stays from 1 February 2027 (the night of 31 January to 1 February excepted), including rooms booked earlier; the rate is 2% of the room-only price per person per night, with the tax capped at JPY 2,000; it is not in force in 2026.

Also on the bill: Japan’s consumption tax has a standard rate of 10% and a reduced rate of 8%.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 12 October 2026. Our guide: New York.

Orlando tourist tax 2026

Tourist Development Tax. Orange County’s Tourist Development Tax is 6% of the lodging bill, on hotel stays and short-term rentals of less than six months, collected by the county; Osceola County’s is also 6% (since 1 July 2004).

Also on the bill: Florida applies its 6% state sales tax to transient rentals, plus a discretionary sales surtax of 0.5% in Orange County or 1.5% in Osceola County (12.5% and 13.5% in total with the Tourist Development Tax in the ledger’s arithmetic).

Example stay: USD 36.00 (about US$36.00). 6% of the room rate x 3 nights (same in Orange and Osceola counties).

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 21 October 2026. Our guide: Orlando.

San Diego tourist tax 2026

transient occupancy tax (TOT). From 1 May 2025 the City of San Diego transient occupancy tax is 11.75% in Tax Zone 1, 12.75% in Tax Zone 2 and 13.75% in Tax Zone 3, applied to stays of less than one month.

Example stay: not computed: the rate depends on the hotel’s tax zone (11.75%, 12.75% or 13.75%) and the example does not specify the property’s address.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 21 October 2026. Our guide: San Diego.

San Francisco tourist tax 2026

transient occupancy tax (hotel tax). San Francisco imposes a 14% transient occupancy tax (the hotel tax) on the rental of accommodations for stays of less than 30 days.

Example stay: USD 84.00 (about US$84.00). 14% of the room rate x 3 nights.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 19 October 2026. Our guide: San Francisco.

Santa Monica tourist tax 2026

transient occupancy tax. Santa Monica levies a 15% tax on hotel and motel rooms (from 1 March 2023); the 2026 Tourism Marketing District assessment is $2.30 a room night for hotels with an average daily rate of $100-$199.99, $6.25 for $200-$299.99 and $7.25 above $300.

Example stay: USD 90.00 (about US$90.00). 15% of the room rate x 3 nights.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 19 October 2026.

Washington, DC tourist tax 2026

sales and use tax on transient accommodations. DC’s Office of Tax and Revenue lists the sales and use tax rate on rooms, lodgings or accommodations as 15.95%; its notice raised the transient-accommodations rate from 14.95% to 15.95% from April 1, 2023, and its October 2025 notice extends the 15.95% rate through September 30, 2027.

Also on the bill: DC’s general sales tax rate remains 6% through September 30, 2027 (site banner); rooms carry the separate 15.95% rate above.

Example stay: USD 95.70 (about US$95.70). 15.95% of the room rate x 3 nights.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 30 October 2026. Our guide: Washington, DC.

West Hollywood tourist tax 2026

transient occupancy tax. West Hollywood’s transient occupancy tax is 12.5% of hotel rent; hotels in the West Hollywood Tourism Improvement District also pay a 3% assessment that may be passed on to the guest.

Example stay: USD 75.00 (about US$75.00). 12.5% of the room rate x 3 nights.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 19 October 2026.

Melbourne tourist tax 2026

none on hotels (Victoria’s short stay levy covers holiday rentals). Hotels in Melbourne carry no tourist tax in 2026. Since 1 January 2025 Victoria has charged a short stay levy of 7.5% of the total booking fee on stays under 28 days in entire homes, apartments and rooms that are not the host’s main residence, paid by the booking platform; hotels, motels and hostels are excluded.

Also on the bill: Australia’s GST is 10%, charged in full on hotel, motel and hostel stays of under 28 days.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 28 October 2026. Our guide: Melbourne.

Sydney tourist tax 2026

none. No tourist or hotel tax is in force in Sydney in October 2026: Revenue NSW’s list of state taxes, duties and levies includes none on accommodation, and we found no city levy.

Also on the bill: Australia’s GST is 10%, charged in full on hotel, motel and hostel stays of under 28 days.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 28 October 2026. Our guide: Sydney.

Marrakech tourist tax 2026

stay tax (taxe de séjour, Law 47-06). Article 73 of Law 47-06 sets legal ranges per person per night: guest houses, congress centres and luxury hotels MAD 15-30; riads and houses rented to tourists MAD 10-25; hotels 5 stars MAD 10-25, 4 stars 5-10, 3 stars 3-7, 1-2 stars 2-5; tourist residences 3-7; other tourist accommodation 2-5.

Also on the bill: The DGI’s VAT page lists accommodation and restaurant services at the 10% reduced rate; the standard rate is 20%.

Example stay: not computed: the law sets only a legal range for a 4-star hotel (a range per person per night); the rate is fixed by each commune after council approval and the Marrakech commune’s own rate was not found.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 20 October 2026. Our guide: Marrakech.

Queenstown tourist tax 2026

none. Queenstown Lakes District Council has proposed a levy on short-term visitor accommodation; the project is listed as on hold, it has not been introduced and nothing is charged on hotel bills in October 2026.

Also on the bill: New Zealand’s GST rate is 15% on most taxable supplies; for a GST-inclusive price the GST is 3/23 of the price (NZ$13.04 of NZ$100).

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 22 October 2026. Our guide: Queenstown.

Cape Town tourist tax 2026

TOMSA tourism levy. TOMSA is a 1% tourism levy charged on the room rate at accommodation businesses that take part; it is voluntary for businesses, compulsory for guests where it is collected, is administered by the TBCSA and passed to South African Tourism, and TOMSA says it is not a tax.

Also on the bill: South Africa’s standard VAT rate is 15% on supplies of goods and services by registered vendors; hotel-type accommodation is ‘commercial accommodation’, a taxable supply.

Example stay: not computed: TOMSA does not say whether its 1% is worked out before or after VAT (the ledger’s own arithmetic merely assumes before), and it is charged only at participating businesses.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 22 October 2026. Our guide: Cape Town.

Dubai tourist tax 2026

Tourism Dirham. Dubai’s Tourism Dirham is a flat fee per occupied room per night set by hotel classification: five-star or higher AED 20, four-star AED 15, three-star AED 10, two-star AED 10, one-star or budget AED 7, hotel apartments AED 20 (luxury), 15 (deluxe), 10 (standard), guest house AED 7; it is not charged per person.

Also on the bill: The UAE introduced VAT at a standard rate of 5%. Hotels’ own October 2026 rate terms list a 7% municipality fee, a 10% service charge and 5% VAT per room per night (Dubai Decree No. 27 of 2019 reduced the municipality fee on hotel sales from 10% to 7%).

Example stay: AED 45.00. Four-star AED 15 per room per night x 3 nights; not per person, independent of room price.

Official sources: 1 · 2 · 3. Checked 21 October 2026. Our guide: Dubai.
