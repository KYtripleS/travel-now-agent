A private-pool villa in Bali is priced in layers. There is the villa itself, and often a service charge on top; there is a 10% regional hotel tax in Badung regency, which covers Seminyak, Uluwatu, Jimbaran and Nusa Dua; and there is the IDR 150,000 tourist levy that every foreign visitor owes once a trip, plus the visa on arrival, IDR 500,000, for those who need one. The villa also has to be the right kind. “Pool villa” on a booking site can mean a pool of your own behind a wall, or a pool shared with a few neighbours, so what each property says about its pool is the first thing to read.

This guide covers four areas, from Ubud’s river valleys to the Bukit’s cliffs, 10 villa stays, the airport link, the levy and the tax. The properties are researched picks, chosen for location, what each one publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in November 2026. No property pays to appear here.

What “private pool” means in Bali

We use the word only where the property’s own site does. Three things get called a pool villa. A private pool sits inside the villa’s own walls or terrace, for the guests of that villa alone, and the site says “private” or “own”. A pool villa with no further words has a pool the site does not call private; ask before you pay for one. A shared pool serves a block of rooms or the whole resort. Where a site does not publish a villa’s size, we say so rather than guess, and where it lists a size, it may include the garden, so treat it as the property’s figure, not a measure of the rooms.

Bali’s tourist levy, visa fee and hotel tax

Three charges sit on top of the room. Only the hotel tax appears on the hotel’s bill.

Charge Amount How it is paid
Bali tourist levy, foreign visitors IDR 150,000 a person, once a trip Online through Love Bali before you travel, or at counters at the airport and port
Visa on arrival (e-VOA), where your passport needs one IDR 500,000 a person Online at the official e-Visa site, or on arrival
Hotel tax in Badung regency 10% of the hotel bill Added by the hotel

The tourist levy. The Bali provincial government charges IDR 150,000 to each foreign tourist, paid once while you are in Bali, before you leave Indonesia. Pay it on the official Love Bali website, lovebali.baliprov.go.id, or in the Love Bali app. Payment must be cashless: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, bank transfer, virtual account, UnionPay or QRIS. There are payment counters at the airport and the port in Bali, and registered hotels, travel agents and attractions can take it too. Diplomatic and official visa holders, transport crew, and KITAS and KITAP residence-card holders are exempt, as are family-unification, student and golden visa holders. We found no change to the amount for 2026 on the Love Bali site.

The visa. Indonesia’s visitor visa, the e-VOA, costs IDR 500,000 for a single entry and a stay of 30 days, according to the official e-Visa site, evisa.imigrasi.go.id; card payments there can add fees set by your card issuer. Every traveller needs their own, children included, and the passport must be valid for at least six months from arrival. The site also warns that websites offering to get you a visa for a fee are not connected to the government.

The hotel tax. Badung regency charges a 10% regional tax on hotel services and 10% on food and drink, under a regency regulation in force since 1 January 2024. Ubud villas, such as Hanging Gardens of Bali, Mandapa and Sanctoo, lie in Gianyar regency, which sets its own rates; the bill shows the line either way.

Read how the price is quoted. Some luxury properties publish rates with “++” after them, as Karma Kandara does, which leaves tax and a service charge to be added. Check which applies on the booking page before you pay.

For a couple on three nights in a Seminyak villa at IDR 10,000,000 a night, the Badung tax would be IDR 3,000,000, the levy IDR 300,000 and two visas IDR 1,000,000: IDR 4,300,000 on top of the room, before any service charge. That is our arithmetic, not an official figure.

Getting in from the airport

  • From Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), the airport says taxis serve the arrivals area and operate on a metered fare or fixed rates, according to each provider’s policy. Agree which before you set off.
  • By app or bus, the airport’s transport page also has Online Taxi and Bus sections; check them for current pick-up points and routes.
  • Included transfers. Kayumanis Jimbaran and Kayumanis Nusa Dua list a free return airport transfer with their villas, which is worth counting when you compare totals.
  • Journey times. The airport’s own page gives none, so we give none; the villas are on different parts of the island, and a long transfer is part of Ubud and the Bukit.

    Ubud: jungle, rivers and rice terraces

    Ubud is inland Bali: temples, craft villages and rice terraces, with no beach. Its luxury villas sit in the valleys around it, where the pool looks over forest or a river rather than the sea. Give it at least three nights; the villas below are set apart from the town, so you will use a driver.

    Seminyak: villas near the beach and the restaurants

    Seminyak, on the west coast, is the base for a private pool close to restaurants, shops and a long beach. Its villas are in the Badung regency, so the 10% hotel tax applies.

    Uluwatu and the Bukit: cliffs and sea views

    The Bukit is the limestone peninsula at Bali’s southern tip. Uluwatu and Ungasan stand on its cliffs, where villas look down on the surf and the sunset. It suits couples and groups who want the view and the pool and will not mind a drive to a restaurant.

    Where to stay in Uluwatu and Ungasan

    • The edge Bali, splurge. Perched on Uluwatu’s cliffs, with eight villa types from the one-bedroom “The villa” (65 m² inside, 260 m² outside) to the five-bedroom “The view”. The site names private pools and ocean views as a villa feature, and describes the two-bedroom The ocean as arranged around a private pool. Choose it for a cliff villa sized to your group. Watch out: the site does not say which other villas have a private pool, and it publishes no minimum age. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Karma Kandara, splurge. In Ungasan, on Jalan Villa Kandara, with Garden View and Ocean View pool villas of one to four bedrooms and a Kids Club for ages 4 to 12. Its larger Ocean View villas are described with a private infinity pool. Choose it for a choice of villa sizes in one resort. Watch out: the smaller villas are described only as having an infinity pool, so confirm “private” for the one you book; rates are quoted “++”; its One Bedroom Deluxe Villa is listed as having no pool.

    Jimbaran and Nusa Dua: calm bays and the airport link

    Jimbaran is a curved bay near the airport; Nusa Dua is a planned resort enclave on the east coast of the Bukit with a long white beach. Both suit a first or last night, or a quiet week.

    How to book a luxury stay in Bali

    • Count the extras. Add IDR 150,000 a person for the levy, IDR 500,000 for a visa if you need one, and 10% hotel tax in Badung. For a couple on three nights at IDR 10,000,000, that is IDR 4,300,000.
    • Ask “nett” or “++”. A “++” rate leaves tax and service to come.
    • Check the pool. Look for “private” in the villa’s own description, and ask for it in writing if a villa’s name says only “pool villa”.
    • Check the age line. Mandapa asks that the person checking in is 21. Others publish no rule, so confirm before you bring children.
    • Split long stays. An Ubud villa and a coast villa suit most trips better than one, because the drive between them is long.
    • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and hotel taxes around the world sets Bali beside other destinations.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does private pool mean for a Bali villa?

    A pool inside the villa's own walls or terrace, for that villa's guests alone. Sites vary: some say private, some say only pool villa. This guide uses the word only where the property's own site does, so check the villa's own description.

    How much is the Bali tourist levy?

    IDR 150,000 a person, paid once while you are in Bali and before you leave Indonesia. Payment is cashless, through the official Love Bali site or app, at counters at the airport and port, or through registered hotels, agents and attractions.

    How much is the Indonesian visa on arrival?

    The official e-Visa site lists the visitor visa at IDR 500,000 for a single entry and a 30-day stay, with card fees from your issuer extra. Each traveller, children included, needs their own.

    How much tax is added to a Bali villa bill?

    Badung regency, which covers Seminyak, Uluwatu, Jimbaran and Nusa Dua, charges a 10% regional tax on hotel services. Ubud villas are in Gianyar regency, which sets its own rate. Some villas also quote rates with ++, which leaves tax and service charge to come.

    Which Bali area is best for a private pool villa?

    Ubud for a pool over a forest or river valley, Seminyak for a pool near restaurants and the beach, Uluwatu for a cliff villa and Jimbaran or Nusa Dua for a quiet bay. Hanging Gardens of Bali, The Layar, The edge Bali and the two Kayumanis properties are examples.

    How do I get from Bali airport to a villa?

    The airport says taxis serve the arrivals area on a metered fare or fixed rates, depending on the provider. Kayumanis Jimbaran and Kayumanis Nusa Dua list a free return airport transfer with their villas. The airport page gives no journey times.
