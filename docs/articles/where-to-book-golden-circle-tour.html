Four things separate one Golden Circle day tour from the next, and the headline price is only one of them: whether Kerið crater is a stop, whether you board at a bus stop or at your hotel, whether Secret Lagoon is added, and how big the vehicle is. On the Saturday we priced, the same route cost from $76.79 to $168 a head, and almost all of the gap is explained by those four lines.

The same operators appear under several sellers’ names. The Kerið tour run by Bus Travel Iceland is on GetYourGuide and on Tiqets, and the Secret Lagoon minibus run by Arctic Adventures is on both as well, at prices a few dollars apart. That is why this page compares listings line by line rather than brand by brand.

All read on 22 November 2026.

GetYourGuide: Reykjavik: Golden Circle Full-Day Tour with Kerid Crater. Operated by BusTravel Iceland, rated 4.8 from 28,201 reviews and flagged “likely to sell out”. For 12 December it offered four options: an afternoon tour from Bus Stop 12 at 12:00 for $91.05, a morning tour from Bus Stop 12 at 8:00 for $99.82, and pickup versions at $104.21 (afternoon) and $112.99 (morning). The listing says “Cancel up to 24 hours in advance for a full refund”, with reserve now and pay later. Included: tour, guide, transport by air-conditioned bus, and pickup and drop-off if you choose that option. The important catch is in the listing itself: “We won’t be stopping at the Kerid volcanic crater lake for any departures at 12:00 pm and later”, so the cheapest afternoon option is the one without Kerið. The listing also says the operator may cancel in heavy rain or thunderstorms and would offer another date or a refund.

For 12 December: $76.79 for the 10:00 departure without hotel transfer, $88.85 with transfer; the 12:30 departure was also listed. Free cancellation until 11 December and reserve now and pay later. It lists a GPS-sensitive audio guide in ten languages, free Wi-Fi onboard and USB chargers, and a live guide in English. One reviewer wrote that a toilet on the bus would be welcome, so do not count on one.

Which listing is the safe, quick buy

For a classic tour with Kerið, the Tiqets Grand Tour is the cheapest listing we read that states the Kerið fee is included ($95), with free cancellation up to 24 hours ahead. It names the same operator, Bus Travel Iceland, as the GetYourGuide listing at $99.82, so the difference is a few dollars, not a different tour; KKday’s comparable listing is $96.56.

If you want pickup, the Viator Classic Day Tour is the cheapest pickup listing we read at $100.58, against $112.99 on GetYourGuide, and its cancellation line is the most specific: free until 10:00 on the day before. We did not read a Kerið line on that listing, so choose a GetYourGuide, KKday or Tiqets listing if the crater matters to you.

Say where the others win, plainly. GetYourGuide is the one with the most reviews on the listings we read (28,201 on the Kerið tour, 139 on the Secret Lagoon tour) and the cheapest Secret Lagoon small-group price, at $150.29. Viator states the smallest vehicle cap on its Secret Lagoon tour (17). KKday has the longest free-cancellation window on the listings we read (2 days) and the lowest Secret Lagoon price ($129.48), though that is a larger bus, not a small group.

What decides the day in winter

  • Short days. Safe Travel Iceland’s winter page says to “Expect limited daylight which might make it difficult to drive in various conditions.” The GetYourGuide Kerið tour drops the crater from departures at 12:00 and later.
  • Changing roads. The same page says “Conditions on the roads may change rapidly” and advises keeping plans flexible. GetYourGuide says its operator may cancel a tour for weather, with another date or a refund offered, so a listing with a clear cancellation window is worth more in December than $5 saved.
  • Check the start time. On the GetYourGuide Kerið tour it is the departures before 12:00 that include the crater.

Clothing, children and the small print

Dress for standing in wind at a waterfall and a geyser basin: GetYourGuide’s Secret Lagoon listing asks for warm waterproof clothing, headwear, gloves, sturdy shoes, your own lunch, a bathing suit and a towel. Food and drinks are not included on the Tiqets listings, on Viator’s classic tour or on GetYourGuide’s Secret Lagoon tour.

Age limits differ. GetYourGuide’s Kerið tour is not suitable for children under 2 and its Secret Lagoon tour sets a minimum age of 5. KKday’s Kerið tour does not allow children under 2 or under 15 kg. Tiqets lists its Kerið tour as wheelchair accessible; we did not see an accessibility line on the others, so check before paying.

Frequently asked questions

Which seller is the safest to book for a Golden Circle tour?

For a classic tour with Kerið, the Tiqets Grand Tour: about $95 on 12 December 2026, Kerið entrance included, free cancellation until 24 hours before. For hotel pickup at the lowest price we read, the Viator Classic Day Tour at $100.58.

What is the cheapest Golden Circle tour from Reykjavik?

On 12 December 2026 the lowest price we read was $76.79 for GetYourGuide's Gray Line express tour (no Kerið), then $89.41 for Viator's no-pickup option, about $95 on Tiqets and $96.56 on KKday for the Kerið tours.

Does the tour stop at Kerið crater?

It depends on the listing. The GetYourGuide Kerið tour says it does not stop there on departures at 12:00 or later; the Tiqets Grand Tour and KKday full-day tour list it.

How much more is a Golden Circle tour with Secret Lagoon?

On our date the Secret Lagoon tours cost $129.48 (KKday, larger bus) to $168 (Viator, small group), with GetYourGuide at $150.29 and Tiqets at about $151.

Is Secret Lagoon admission included?

On the GetYourGuide, Tiqets and Viator Secret Lagoon listings we read, yes. On the KKday listing we did not read an admission line, so check the package details.

Can I cancel if the weather turns?

The listings we read allow free cancellation from 24 hours (GetYourGuide, Tiqets) to 2 days (KKday) ahead, and GetYourGuide says its operator may cancel a tour in heavy rain or thunderstorms and offer another date or a refund.
