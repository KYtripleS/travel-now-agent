A Hakone day tour from Tokyo is sold under many names, and the price depends mostly on three things: whether Mt Fuji’s 5th Station is on the route, how you get back (a Shinkansen ticket, or a drop-off at Odawara or Hakone-Yumoto), and whether lunch is in the price. On the Saturday we priced, the yen listings ranged from ¥7,800 for a Fuji-lakes and Hakone bus route to ¥27,000 for a tour that includes the Shinkansen back, and the gap is explained by those lines, not by luck.

The most-booked version on the big platforms is the same operator’s tour, H.I.S., sold on both GetYourGuide and Viator: a bus from Shinjuku or Tokyo Station to Mt Fuji’s 5th Station, then Owakudani, the Hakone Ropeway and a Lake Ashi cruise. We compared that tour and its rivals for one date.

    Which listing is the safe, quick buy

    For most readers it is the H.I.S. Mt Fuji and Hakone tour on GetYourGuide. It has the most reviews of the listings we read (1,824), free cancellation until the day before, pay later, and every version of the route in one place: with or without lunch, ending at Odawara or with the Shinkansen back. Pick the drop-off version if you are heading on to Odawara or Hakone-Yumoto, and the Shinkansen version if you want to be back at Tokyo Station without a second ticket to buy.

    Say where the others win, plainly. KKday has the lowest figure by a wide margin, ¥7,800 against ¥18,500 for the H.I.S. tour on Viator, and the longest free-cancellation window of the four (2 days). It is a different route, though: no 5th Station, a one-way ropeway ride, an optional cruise and no meals, and the 12 December marathon note applies. Viator shows the H.I.S. tour in yen, which helps if you plan your budget that way. Tiqets is the one with a stated group cap (40) and a fixed 8:10 hotel check-in, and it has the Shinkansen in the price; its single review is too thin to rely on.

    Rules that change the booking

    • Weather and Mt Fuji. Viator says that if the 5th Station is inaccessible you visit the highest station possible, and that weather is not grounds for a refund. Clouds can hide the mountain, as the GetYourGuide listing itself says.
    • Where the tour ends. The H.I.S. bus listing says the return bus does not go back to Tokyo Station. The Shinkansen versions end for you at Odawara, and the guide does not ride with you.
    • Meeting time and place. The H.I.S. listings use two meeting points (the LOVE sculpture in Shinjuku and the Marunouchi Building at Tokyo Station). The GetYourGuide bus listing adds that boarding points cannot be changed on the day and that late arrivals cannot join midway.
    • Children. Viator says children up to 5 travel free without a seat or lunch; the GetYourGuide bus listing says up to 3. Check the age line on the version you book.
    • Saturday 12 December. KKday’s listing names this date as a marathon day. We found no equivalent notice on the other listings we read, so treat it as the one seller that has told us about it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which seller is the safest to book a Hakone day tour from Tokyo?

    Of the listings we read, the H.I.S. Mt Fuji and Hakone tour on GetYourGuide: 1,824 reviews at 4.2, free cancellation until 11 December and reserve now, pay later. It showed $113.53 ending at Odawara or Hakone-Yumoto and $143.17 with a Shinkansen ticket back on 12 December 2026.

    What is the cheapest Hakone day tour from Tokyo?

    On 12 December 2026 the lowest figure we read was ¥7,800 on KKday, for a Fuji-lakes and Hakone route with an optional cruise and no meals. The H.I.S. tour on Viator started at ¥18,500.

    Is Mt Fuji guaranteed to be visible?

    No. The GetYourGuide listing says clouds move fast and you might spot the peak, and Viator says that if the 5th Station is inaccessible you visit the highest station possible.

    Is lunch included?

    Only if you pick a with-lunch option. Viator's listing says lunch is included only when the lunch option is chosen, and KKday lists meals as not included.

    How do I get back to Tokyo?

    The H.I.S. tour offers a Shinkansen ticket from Odawara to Tokyo Station, or a drop-off at Odawara or Hakone-Yumoto. GetYourGuide says the guide does not accompany you on the train.

    Can I cancel?

    On the listings we read, yes, with a full refund up to 24 hours before on GetYourGuide, Viator and Tiqets, and up to 2 days before on KKday. Viator says weather is not grounds for a refund.
