Visit Japan Web: How to Register Before You Fly (2026)

What is Visit Japan Web, and Why Consider Using It?

Visit Japan Web is an online service provided by the Japanese government. Its purpose is to allow international travelers to pre-register their Disembarkation Card for Immigration and Customs Declaration before they even board their flight. Once registered, you’ll receive QR codes that can be scanned at designated kiosks upon arrival at major Japanese airports like Narita International Airport, Haneda Airport, and Kansai International Airport.

Before we dive in, let’s clarify what Visit Japan Web helps with – and what it doesn’t. It is a tool for streamlining paperwork, not a guarantee of entry. It does not replace the need for a valid visa if your nationality requires one, nor does it affect standard immigration procedures. Simply put, it converts paper forms into digital ones, helping to speed up your passage through immigration and customs. I’ve found that having my QR codes ready has always made for a more efficient and less stressful arrival, allowing me to start my slow morning with a local coffee in Tokyo or Osaka sooner rather than later.

The official portal for Visit Japan Web is the authoritative source for the latest information and registration steps. As digital systems evolve, specific screens and steps can change, so I always recommend referring to the official website directly.

Step-by-Step: Creating Your Visit Japan Web Account

The first step in using Visit Japan Web is creating an account. This is a straightforward process, but remember to always refer to the official Visit Japan Web portal for the most current instructions, as the user interface can be updated.

  1. Access the Official Portal: Begin by navigating to the official Visit Japan Web website. Be wary of unofficial sites; always confirm you are on the legitimate government portal.
  2. Create a New Account: Look for an option to “Create new account” or “Sign up.” You’ll typically need to provide an email address and set a password. It’s wise to use an email you’ll have access to while traveling.
  3. Email Verification: After submitting your details, you’ll likely receive a verification email. Click the link within the email to confirm your account. This is a common security step.
  4. Log In: Once verified, you can log in to your newly created account.

From this point, you’ll enter the main dashboard, which allows you to register your personal details and then add specific travel plans. Take your time with each step; there’s no rush. It’s a process designed to make your journey easier, not more complicated.

Registering Your Trip Information

With your account established, the next phase involves registering your personal details and then the specifics of your trip. This is where you input the passport and flight information you gathered earlier.

  1. Register Your Information (Your Details): This section asks for your passport details, including your full name, date of birth, nationality, passport number, and expiry date. Ensure these match your passport exactly.
  2. Declare Accompanying Family Members: If you are traveling with family who cannot complete the process themselves (e.g., young children), you can register them under your account. This is particularly convenient for families, as it streamlines the customs declaration process for everyone together.
  3. Register Your Plan (Trip Details): Here, you’ll enter your arrival flight number, the expected date of arrival, and the name and address of your first accommodation in Japan. If you’re staying at a well-known hotel like the Park Hyatt Tokyo or a small guesthouse in Dotonbori, Osaka, the system may auto-populate some address details, but always double-check for accuracy.
  4. Complete the Disembarkation Card and Customs Declaration: These are the two main forms Visit Japan Web helps you pre-fill.
    • Disembarkation Card for Immigration: This asks for basic travel details, your purpose of visit (typically tourism), and your accommodation address.
    • Customs Declaration: This section will ask a series of questions about goods you are bringing into Japan. Be honest and thorough.
    • After completing each section, you’ll generate a QR code. Make sure to save these. I usually take a screenshot and also keep the tab open on my phone until I’ve passed through.

Remember, the system’s exact layout can change, so always refer to the detailed instructions on the official Visit Japan Web portal as you proceed. It’s designed to be intuitive, but taking a moment to read the prompts carefully is always a good practice.

Navigating Arrival: The QR Codes at Japanese Airports

Once you’ve completed your registration and generated your QR codes, the final step is using them upon arrival. This is where the pre-planning truly pays off, especially at busy hubs like Narita, Haneda, and Kansai.

Upon disembarking your plane at airports such as Narita or Haneda, you’ll typically follow signs for “Immigration” and “Customs.”

  1. Immigration:
    • Look for the immigration counters. Many airports now have dedicated lanes or kiosks for travelers who have used Visit Japan Web.
    • At the immigration booth, you’ll present your passport. The officer may ask to scan your immigration QR code, or they may simply process your entry based on your passport. The system integrates your pre-registered details directly. In my experience, the process is noticeably quicker than filling out a paper form on the plane, allowing you to move through to baggage claim with ease.
  2. Customs:
    • After collecting your luggage, you’ll proceed to the customs area.
    • Here, you’ll find electronic kiosks. Scan your passport first, then present your customs QR code to the scanner. The kiosk will process your declaration.
    • An officer may still be present for a quick visual check or to answer any questions, but for most travelers with nothing to declare, this step is often seamless and very fast.

The quiet efficiency of Japanese airports is something I always appreciate, and using Visit Japan Web aligns perfectly with that experience. It’s a small step that can contribute significantly to a calmer start to your time in Japan.

Common Questions About Visit Japan Web

It’s natural to have questions when navigating new systems, especially before an international trip. Here are some common queries about Visit Japan Web, reflecting the practical concerns I often hear.

  • Is it truly optional? Yes, absolutely. If you prefer, or if you encounter technical difficulties, you can always fill out the traditional paper Disembarkation Card for Immigration and Customs Declaration forms. These are typically handed out on your flight or are readily available in the arrivals hall at airports like Narita, Haneda, and Kansai.
  • When should I complete the registration? I recommend completing it before you depart for Japan. This ensures you have access to a stable internet connection and can take your time. While it’s technically possible to do it upon arrival if you have Wi-Fi, having it done in advance reduces last-minute stress.
  • Can I register for my family? Yes, if you are the main applicant, you can register accompanying family members (those traveling with you who cannot complete the process themselves) for both immigration and customs declarations under your account. This is a very convenient feature for families.
  • What if I make a mistake or my plans change? The Visit Japan Web portal allows you to edit your registered information up until your arrival. If your accommodation changes, for instance, you can log in and update your details.
  • What if I forget my QR codes or my phone battery dies? Don’t worry. As mentioned, the paper forms are always an option. You can also print out your QR codes as a backup. While digital is convenient, having a physical plan B never hurts.

Frequently asked questions

Is Visit Japan Web mandatory for entering Japan?

No, Visit Japan Web is an optional service. You can still use traditional paper forms for immigration and customs declarations upon arrival in Japan if you prefer not to register online.

Does Visit Japan Web replace the need for a visa to enter Japan?

Visit Japan Web does not replace or affect visa requirements. It is a system for pre-registering immigration and customs information, not a visa application portal. Travelers must still ensure they have the correct visa or meet visa-free entry requirements for Japan.

When should I complete my Visit Japan Web registration?

It is generally recommended to complete your registration online before you depart for Japan. While you can technically access the portal upon arrival if you have Wi-Fi, having everything prepared beforehand allows for a much calmer and smoother airport experience.

Can a family register together on Visit Japan Web?

Yes, one account holder can register accompanying family members for both immigration and customs declarations. This streamlines the process for families traveling together, allowing them to use shared QR codes for customs.

What happens if I encounter technical issues or forget my QR codes?

If you experience technical issues, forget your QR codes, or prefer not to use the digital service, you can always complete the traditional paper immigration and customs forms provided on your flight or at the airport upon arrival. These forms are readily available at Japanese airports like Narita, Haneda, and Kansai.
