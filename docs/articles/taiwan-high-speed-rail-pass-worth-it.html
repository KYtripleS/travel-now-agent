Taiwan High Speed Rail: Pass, Early Bird, or the Tourist Discount?

By Casey, Gently Yonder editor

Taiwan offers foreign visitors three discounts on the high speed rail and they do not stack. Why the 3-Day Pass at NT$2,200 usually loses to Early Bird fares and the buy-one-get-one pairing, plus who is not eligible.

Last updated 13 Sep 2026 · 5 min read

Taiwan gives foreign visitors three different ways to pay less on the high speed rail, and they don’t stack. Picking the wrong one is the difference between a good deal and paying roughly 70% more for the same seats.

Short answer: the 3-Day Pass is the one most visitors buy and the one that most often loses.

Here is the arithmetic on the country’s flagship journey, Taipei–Kaohsiung:

How you pay Round trip, per person
Standard fare NT$2,980
3-Day Pass NT$2,200 (often NT$2,090–2,199 via resellers)
Early Bird round trip ≈ NT$1,938
Buy-one-get-one pair ≈ NT$1,266 each

The pass beats the standard fare comfortably. It loses to Early Bird, and it is beaten badly by the two-person deal.

The three schemes, and who each suits

1. Early Bird — the best value, if you can commit THSR discounts selected reserved trains by 10%, 20% or 35% when booked ahead. The catch is the usual one: specific trains, limited seats, and they go. But when a 20% or 35% fare is still available, it beats the flat foreign-tourist discount outright. If your dates and times are fixed, look here first.

2. The foreign-tourist discount — a flat 15% Foreign-passport visitors can buy one-way reserved standard tickets at 15% off, through the THSRC pass site or resellers. Less generous than a good Early Bird fare, but it applies to any train — so it’s the answer when your timing is uncertain or the Early Bird seats are gone.

3. The 3-Day Pass — unlimited travel, on a clock NT$2,200 officially, often a little less through resellers, for three consecutive days from your first designated travel date. “Consecutive” is the word to notice: unlike Korea’s pass, which went flexible in 2026, this one runs on a fixed three-day block.

So when does the pass actually win?

When you are moving a lot inside those three days. Specifically:

  • Two or more long journeys within the window — Taipei → Taichung → Kaohsiung → Taipei, for example, rather than one there-and-back.
  • Plans you want to keep loose across a short, dense trip.
  • When Early Bird seats are gone on the trains you need and you’re travelling alone, so the buy-one-get-one pairing isn’t available.

It loses when you are doing one round trip (Early Bird wins), when you’re travelling as a pair (the BOGO deal wins, and by a lot), or when your journeys are spread across more than three days — which is the trap, because a relaxed week in Taiwan does not fit a three-consecutive-day pass.

Check you are eligible before you plan around it

The pass is for short-term entry foreign visitors only. Visitors who entered Taiwan on an R.O.C. passport, or who hold a valid alien resident certificate, are not eligible — which catches people living in Taiwan and anyone travelling on a Taiwanese passport alongside foreign companions. If you are travelling as a mixed group, check each person separately; you may end up buying different products for the same journey.

How to decide, in order

  1. List your HSR journeys with dates and rough times.
  2. Check Early Bird availability on those exact trains first — it is the biggest discount when it exists.
  3. If you’re two people, price the buy-one-get-one pairing before anything else.
  4. Only if you’re making several journeys inside three consecutive days does the pass come into it.
  5. Confirm eligibility for every passport in your party.

You can compare THSR passes and tickets here or check the same products on a second platform — reseller prices for the 3-Day Pass sit slightly under the official NT$2,200, so it is worth the minute.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Taiwan High Speed Rail 3-Day Pass worth it?

Only if you make several journeys inside three consecutive days. On a single Taipei-Kaohsiung round trip the pass costs about NT$2,200 against roughly NT$1,938 on an Early Bird round trip and about NT$1,266 each on the buy-one-get-one pairing.

How much is the THSR 3-Day Pass?

NT$2,200 through the official international portal, and often around NT$2,090-2,199 through resellers. It covers three consecutive days from your first designated travel date.

What discount do foreign tourists get on Taiwan High Speed Rail?

A flat 15% off one-way reserved standard tickets through the THSRC pass site and resellers. Early Bird fares of 20% or 35%, where available, beat it.

Who is not eligible for the THSR tourist pass?

It is for short-term entry foreign visitors. People who entered Taiwan on an R.O.C. passport, or who hold a valid alien resident certificate, cannot use it, so check each traveller separately in a mixed group.

Are the THSR pass's three days flexible?

No. They are three consecutive days from your first designated travel date, unlike Korea's rail pass, which became flexible in 2026.
