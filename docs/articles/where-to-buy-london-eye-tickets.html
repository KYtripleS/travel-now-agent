    What the venue’s terms say about tickets

    The London Eye’s general terms and conditions, read on 22 November 2026 at https://www.londoneye.com/security-privacy/terms-conditions/, say: tickets are non-transferable, not for resale, and void on transfer. The terms do not name authorised sellers, and they do not say tickets bought from a booking platform are refused. They govern what a ticket holder may do with a ticket once they have one.

    In practice that means two things. Buy for the names and the date you will use, and do not buy from people reselling tickets. We have also applied a test of our own to each partner below: we include a partner only if its own listing shows a dated admission issued for the buyer, using words such as “official ticket” or “show your smartphone ticket”. A listing that talks about a voucher to be exchanged, or that is unclear, is marked “not included” with the reason in one line.

    Standard, Fast Track and Flexi Fast Track

    The three tiers are not three different rides. The venue’s ticket page, read on 22 November 2026, lists a Standard & Fast Track ticket and a separate Flexi Fast Track ticket. GetYourGuide describes the Fast Track option as one that includes “Fast-track entrance access”, and shows the standard option as “Not included” for that line.

    Viator’s Fast Track listing says it lets you “skip the majority of the queue” and lists “Guaranteed fast track access” among its inclusions. Tiqets’s standard ticket lists “Fast-track entrance” under “Not included” and tells you to go to the standard entrance, queue A, and show your smartphone ticket.

    The difference you are paying £15 for on 12 December (£29.00 against £44.00 at 10:00) is queue time, not a different capsule. Whether it pays off is your call; the Fast Track card on GetYourGuide was flagged “Likely to sell out” for that date.

    The venue’s own rules that change the buying decision

    These come from londoneye.com only.

    • Tickets are non-transferable. Tickets are non-transferable, not for resale, and void on transfer. Buy for the names and date you will use.
    • A date and a timed slot are chosen at purchase. The ticket is valid only on the date printed on it. If you are not sure of your plans, ask yourself whether the cancellation window on the listing you pick is long enough.
    • Children. The booking page says children aged 15 and under must be accompanied by an adult aged 18 or over, and that all children under 2 need a ticket, which is free of charge. We priced adults only.
    • Luggage. The booking page says suitcases and large items of hand luggage are not permitted for security reasons.
    • Opening hours. We did not read the opening hours for 12 December, so we give none. They change by season; the time slots shown on your listing are the ones you can book.

    Which partner to book

    • Standard ticket on a fixed date: GetYourGuide, Viator or Tiqets. All showed £29 for 12 December. GetYourGuide and Viator show a free-cancellation cut-off of 11 December; Tiqets cancels free until 24 hours before.
    • Fast Track: GetYourGuide or Viator. Both £44.00 at 10:00. GetYourGuide shows it as one of two options on the same page.
    • Pay later: Viator. Its listing says “Reserve now, pay nothing until Dec 10”.
    • Reschedule rather than cancel: Tiqets. Its standard ticket says rescheduling is possible until 24 hours before your visit.

    Five checks before you pay

    1. The tier. £29 is the standard ticket; Fast Track was £44 on 12 December.
    2. The slot. Prices we read were for 10:00. Check the figure for the time you choose.
    3. The cancellation date. GetYourGuide and Viator: 11 December. Tiqets: 24 hours before.
    4. The name. Viator’s listing says the lead traveller name cannot be changed once added.
    5. Who you buy from. Use a booking page that issues a ticket for your date and name, and do not buy from people reselling tickets.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where should I buy London Eye tickets?

    GetYourGuide. On 22 November 2026, for Saturday 12 December, it showed £29.00 for the standard ticket and £44.00 for Fast Track at 10:00, with free cancellation until 11 December. Viator showed the same two figures and Tiqets £29 for the standard ticket. Klook was not checked.

    What is the difference between standard and Fast Track?

    Fast Track includes fast-track entrance access; the standard ticket does not. On 12 December at 10:00, GetYourGuide and Viator showed £29.00 against £44.00.

    Can I cancel a London Eye ticket?

    It depends on the listing. GetYourGuide said Cancel for free until Dec 11 for a 12 December visit, Viator said free cancellation before 10:00 AM on 11 December, and Tiqets said free cancellation until 24 hours before the visit date.

    Can I resell or give away a London Eye ticket?

    No. The venue's terms say tickets are non-transferable, not for resale, and void on transfer. Buy for the names and date you will use, and do not buy from people reselling tickets.

    Do children need a ticket?

    The booking page says children aged 15 and under must be accompanied by an adult aged 18 or over, and all children under 2 need a ticket, free of charge. We priced adults only.

    What is Flexi Fast Track?

    It is a separate tier on the venue's ticket page. We did not find it as a separate option on the GetYourGuide, Tiqets or Viator pages we read, so check that the ticket you are about to buy names the tier you want.
