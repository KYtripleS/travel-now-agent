What you are actually choosing

SUMMIT One Vanderbilt is an observation deck with art installations on three levels, entered from the Main Concourse of Grand Central Terminal. The standard ticket covers all three levels, including Levitation, the glass skyboxes. Ascent, an all-glass elevator on the outside of the building, is an upgrade sold as its own ticket, and the Premium tour adds skip-the-line access, a guided visit and a digital photo package.

So the real decisions are the ticket type and the slot. Slots on 12 December started at 8:00 am on every listing we read. The price changes with the slot on some sellers, and the day price shown on the others is a starting price, so read the figure you see at the slot you pick.

The building’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from summitov.com only.

  • Dated and timed tickets. Tickets are “only valid for the date and/or time specified on ticket”, and admission “is not guaranteed and is subject to availability for guests running late”.
  • Hours. Open daily from 8:00 am to 12:00 am, with last entry at 10:30 pm.
  • Children. Children aged 5 and under do not need a ticket when accompanied by an adult ticket holder. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.
  • Bags and security. Suitcases, carry-on bags and wheeled bags are prohibited, as is anything larger than a standard backpack or purse. The building says guests may be asked for proof of identity such as a passport, driver’s licence or credit card for an order placed through its website.
  • Entrance. 45 E 42nd St, on the Main Concourse of Grand Central Terminal.
  • Weather and refunds. Ticket sales are final; the building says no refunds are given for weather or waiting times.

Five checks before you pay

  1. The slot. The price belongs to the time. GetYourGuide and Viator showed their 8:00 am price first.
  2. The cancellation date. For 12 December: Tiqets 10 December at 11:59 pm, Viator before 8:00 am on 11 December, GetYourGuide 11 December, KKday up to two days before.
  3. The product. The general ticket is not Ascent; Ascent is sold as its own ticket.
  4. Children. Seller wording differs: the building says 5 and under are free; KKday says under 5.
  5. Arrive on time. Late arrivals may wait for an open slot and pay a fee.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy SUMMIT One Vanderbilt tickets?

GetYourGuide for a dated general ticket. On 25 November 2026, for Saturday 12 December, it showed $59.88 at 8:00 am, the same as Viator, with 4.7 from 62,681 reviews and free cancellation until 11 December. Klook was not checked.

Is SUMMIT a timed ticket?

Yes. The building says tickets are valid for the date and time on the ticket, and Tiqets, GetYourGuide and Viator all list slots from 8:00 am on 12 December. KKday asks for a session time at booking.

Can I cancel a SUMMIT ticket?

It depends on the seller. Tiqets said free cancellation until 11:59 pm on 10 December, GetYourGuide until 11 December, Viator before 8:00 am on 11 December, and KKday up to two days before. The building itself says sales are final.

What is Ascent and is it included?

Ascent is an all-glass elevator on the outside of the building, sold as an upgrade. The general ticket does not include it. For 12 December, GetYourGuide showed $86.01 and Viator $86.02 for the Ascent ticket.

Do children need a ticket?

The building says children aged 5 and under do not need a ticket when accompanied by an adult ticket holder. Viator and KKday list children aged 6 to 12 at a discounted rate; KKday showed child from $47.91. We priced one adult only.

What time does SUMMIT open?

The building lists 8:00 am to 12:00 am daily, with last entry at 10:30 pm.
