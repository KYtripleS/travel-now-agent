For Monday 14 December 2026, its Evan Evans Tours listing showed £135 per adult against £140 on GetYourGuide and KKday, and it offered free cancellation until 11 December. What differs between sellers for this attraction is less the ticket than the package around it. The studio says tickets “must be sold as part of a package and cannot be re-sold directly on a standalone basis”, so every reseller listing below bundles the studio ticket with return coach travel from central London. That makes the choices about the departure point, how long you get inside, who handles your ticket on arrival, and how late you can cancel. This guide covers each of those, plus the studio’s own rules on timed entry and selling out, and the winter “Hogwarts in the Snow” season. The studio’s own rules that change how you buy

These come from the studio’s own pages, read on 21 November 2026.

  • Every ticket is timed and pre-booked. The studio says visitors “must purchase Tickets in advance and pre-book the time and date” of entry. It does not hold back tickets: “We do not hold back additional tickets for sale over the phone or on the day.” Do not plan on turning up without a ticket.
  • Dates are open well ahead. Its home page says 2027 tickets are available now, and its FAQ says tickets are available until 31 December 2027. It strongly suggests booking through an approved distributor as early as possible to secure your preferred date and time.
  • Sold-out dates stay sold out. In its words: “once a time slot is sold out, we cannot add any additional visitors.” A seller cannot change that, so the date you want decides whether anything is available at any price.
  • Arrive in your slot. Visitors “will be able to enter the Studio Tour at any time during their 30-minute timeslot”, though the terms ask you to arrive no later than 20 minutes before your allocated entry time. Coach packages set a departure time for you, so the slot is chosen by the package.
  • Ages. A person of 16 or older is an adult; 5 to 15 inclusive is a child; children aged 4 and under are free but must have a ticket. A family ticket covers two adults and two children, or one adult and three children. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult aged 16 or over.
  • Changes and refunds. The studio’s own tickets are non-refundable, and date changes cost £10. A reseller package follows the reseller’s terms, which is why the cancellation line in each listing matters.
  • Authorised sellers only. “Only Tickets purchased directly from us or one of our authorised distributors are valid for admission.” The studio’s approved list includes Klook, Golden Tours, Evan Evans Tours and Axis & Globe, among others. We did not check which approved distributor runs the GetYourGuide and KKday listings we read beyond what each page says, so confirm that on the listing you choose.
  • Getting there. Every package below includes return coach travel from central London to the studio, so there is no train or shuttle to plan.

The winter season: Hogwarts in the Snow

The studio lists Hogwarts in the Snow from 14 November 2026 to 17 January 2027, with the Great Hall dressed for Christmas, festive common rooms and a snow-covered castle model, and a new sound-effects show in the Backlot Stage this year. Tickets for the 2027 to 2028 run, 13 November 2027 to 16 January 2028, are already on sale. We saw no separate price for the season on the studio’s ticket page, and the 14 December date we priced falls inside it.

Later seasons are Hogwarts House Spirit (23 January to 19 April 2027), a summer feature (13 May to 6 September 2027) and Dark Arts (24 September to 7 November 2027).

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Warner Bros. Studio Tour London tickets?

Viator, for a ticket with return coach from central London on a fixed date. On 21 November 2026, for Monday 14 December, Viator's Evan Evans Tours listing showed £135 per adult against £140 on GetYourGuide and KKday, with free cancellation before 14:30 on 11 December. Klook and Tiqets were not priced. Prices change daily, so the figure at checkout for your date is the one that counts.

Can I buy tickets at the door?

No. The studio says all visitors must buy in advance and pre-book a date and time, and that it does not hold back tickets for sale on the day.

Why do resellers sell packages rather than plain tickets?

The studio says its tickets must be sold as part of a package and cannot be re-sold directly on a standalone basis, so the listings we read all include return coach travel from central London.

Is Hogwarts in the Snow included in the ticket?

The studio lists Hogwarts in the Snow from 14 November 2026 to 17 January 2027 and a 2027 to 2028 run from 13 November 2027 to 16 January 2028. We saw no separate price for the season on its ticket page, and a standard visit on a date inside it is what we priced.

How old does a child need to be to pay, and who goes free?

The studio treats 16 and over as adults, 5 to 15 as children, and children aged 4 and under as free but needing a ticket. A family ticket covers two adults and two children, or one adult and three children.
