    On New Year’s Eve the Las Vegas Strip stops being a road. Visit Las Vegas, the city’s official tourism site, says that every year more than 400,000 people gather on the Strip, and that Las Vegas Boulevard closes to traffic from the early evening until the early hours of 1 January. That changes how you choose a hotel: where you sleep decides how far you walk once taxis can no longer reach your door. The bill is different too. Hotels near the Strip add a 13.38% room tax, and most add a daily resort fee on top.

    This guide covers what Visit Las Vegas and the Fremont Street Experience have published for Thursday 31 December 2026, five areas matched to the night, 12 hotels, and the tax and transport facts that matter. For the rest of your trip, where to stay in Las Vegas covers six areas, and six of its hotels also appear here. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in November 2026. No hotel pays to appear here.

    31 December 2026: the fireworks, the closure and Fremont Street

    Visit Las Vegas had not published the 2026 plan for the Strip when we checked in November 2026, so the details below are from the show that rang in 2026 and are labelled as last year’s. Check visitlasvegas.com before you go.

    The Strip, last year (31 December 2025). Visit Las Vegas described an eight-minute fireworks show at midnight, launched from ten resort properties, with the first drone show on New Year’s Eve leading the countdown. From south to north the properties were MGM Grand, ARIA, Planet Hollywood, Caesars Palace, Palms, Treasure Island, The Venetian, Fontainebleau, Resorts World and The STRAT. Its fireworks designer said any spot with a view above those buildings gets at least four or five launch sites. The Strip closes to traffic between Russell Road and Sahara Avenue: entry points began closing as early as 5:30pm and shut entirely by 8pm, typically reopening fully by 2am on 1 January. The same site says parking garages often fill early, and that some hotels will ask for your room key to let you back in as midnight approaches.

    Downtown, this year. The Fremont Street Experience’s New Year’s Eve page lists Countdown Under The Canopy on 31 December from 7pm, with gates opening at 7pm and live performances by Hoobastank, Petey Pablo, Alien Ant Farm, Wuki and others. It is 21 or over only, early-bird tickets were $35 plus fees when we checked, and all ticket sales are final. The page gives no year; its lineup differs from last year’s, so it appears to be this year’s, but confirm that when you buy.

    The Plaza Hotel & Casino, at the start of Fremont Street, runs its own midnight fireworks, which it calls Downtown’s only. Its page for New Year’s Eve 2027 says prime viewing spots are on Main Street, which will be closed to vehicles.

    Room tax and resort fees in Las Vegas

    Where the hotel is Room tax (City of Las Vegas guide)
    Inside the Primary Gaming Corridor, which covers the Strip 13.38%
    Outside the Primary Gaming Corridor 13.00%

    The City of Las Vegas says room tax should be collected from the guest and shown as a separate charge. Resort fees are separate again, and each hotel sets its own. We state a fee only where the hotel’s own page shows one. Treasure Island’s room page says its daily resort fee is $49.95 plus tax, and the Plaza says its fee is $39.00 per room, per day, taxable by 13%. The other pages we read gave no amount, so check the total at booking.

    On New Year’s Eve rates are far above normal, so here is the arithmetic on an illustrative $200 room (our arithmetic, not a quoted price). With Treasure Island’s $49.95 fee and 13.38% on both, it comes to $283.39 a night. With the Plaza’s $39 fee and 13% on both, it comes to $270.07.

    Getting in from the airport, and around

    From Harry Reid International Airport (LAS), the Nevada Taxicab Authority lists flat zone fares to the Strip.

    • By taxi: $22.25 to Zone 1 (Sunset north to Tropicana), $26.25 to Zone 2 (Tropicana north to Flamingo) and $30.25 to Zone 3 (Flamingo north to the Stratosphere). The Authority also lists a $2.40 airport pick-up fee and a $3.00 card fee.
    • By bus: Route 108 and the Red Line serve the airport; the bus stop is at Terminal 1 on Level Zero. RTC lists the Strip and Downtown services (the Deuce and SDX) at $6 for two hours, $8 for 24 hours and $20 for three days.
    • Monorail: seven stations run from SAHARA to MGM Grand. A single ride is $5.75 as an eTicket and $6.25 at full fare. On 31 December, a Thursday, its regular hours are 7am to 2am; Visit Las Vegas said last year that it was expected to run all night, but we found no 2026 holiday notice.
    • On the night, last year: Visit Las Vegas said the city would offer free bus transportation from 6pm, detouring around the usual Boulevard stops. We could not read RTC’s own page, so confirm this year’s service on rtcsnv.com.
    • Taxis and rideshare cannot use Las Vegas Boulevard while it is closed to vehicles, so arrange your return before midnight and allow time.

    The Center Strip: inside the closure

    The Center Strip is the stretch most visitors picture, with Bellagio, The Cosmopolitan and ARIA close together. Inside the closure you can walk to the others, but expect crowds, so allow extra time. ARIA appears on last year’s list of launch sites, and Visit Las Vegas has not yet given this year’s.

    Where to stay on the Center Strip

    How the hotel links work: a hotel’s name goes to its own website. “Check rates” opens it on Booking.com through our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book, at no extra cost to you.

    • Bellagio, splurge. Its room page lists the Fountain View King at 510 sq ft. Choose it for a classic Center Strip address on the busiest night of the year. Watch out: its page gives no resort fee amount and no New Year’s Eve terms, so ask before you pay. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas, comfortable. Rooms start with the City Room Queen at 460 sq ft, and Terrace Studios of 730 sq ft include fountain-view versions. Choose it for a larger room with outdoor space. Watch out: the page gives no fee amount or New Year’s Eve terms. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • ARIA Resort & Casino, splurge. Deluxe King and Deluxe Two Queen rooms are 520 sq ft by its room page, and the resort was one of last year’s ten launch sites. Choose it for a modern tower inside the closure. Watch out: the fee and any New Year’s Eve terms are not on the page we read. Check rates on Booking.com →

    The South Strip: calmer at the Mandalay Bay end

    The south end sits inside the closure but away from the Bellagio crowds, and the monorail’s southern terminus is at MGM Grand, which was a launch site last year. Luxor and W Las Vegas are at the Mandalay Bay end, so expect a longer walk to the middle of the Strip.

    Where to stay on the South Strip

    • Luxor, budget. Pyramid rooms of 420 sq ft with slanted windows, or Tower rooms such as the 449 sq ft Tower Premier King. Choose it for a room inside the pyramid at a lower price. Watch out: the Pyramid rooms have slanted windows, and the fee amount is not on the page we read. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • W Las Vegas, comfortable. An all-suite hotel with 725 sq ft Premier King and Premier Two Queen Suites. Choose it for a large suite. Watch out: the hotel says its suites do not include pullout couch beds. Check rates on Booking.com →

    The North Strip: two launch sites

    The north end has two of last year’s launch sites, The STRAT and Treasure Island, and last year’s closure ran up to Sahara Avenue. The walk to the middle of the Strip is long.

    Where to stay on the North Strip

    • The STRAT Hotel, Casino & Tower, good value. At 2000 Las Vegas Blvd S, with rooms such as the 340 sq ft Elevate King, and a New Year’s Eve event at the STRAT that it sells tickets for. Choose it to be under a launch site at the north end. Watch out: the event ticket is not a hotel package, and the home page shows no fee amount or minimum stay. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Treasure Island, comfortable. Deluxe rooms of approximately 400 sq ft, a studio-style room with one king or two queens. Its room page states a $49.95 plus tax daily resort fee, and says fees are required apart from optional exceptions for certain members. Choose it for a Strip hotel that publishes its fee. Watch out: count the fee in the total. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Downtown and Fremont Street: a second party

    Downtown is about walking to one place: Fremont Street, with its ticketed party and the Plaza’s fireworks at midnight. It sits outside the Strip’s closure. Staying here suits you if the Fremont party is your plan, since you can walk back to bed. The Strip’s launch sites are a ride away, and the Strip is closed to cars.

    Where to stay Downtown

    • Plaza Hotel & Casino, good value. At the start of Fremont Street, with rooms of 325 sq ft and a mandatory $39.00 per room, per day resort fee that includes covered parking. Its New Year’s Eve hotel package offers up to 25% off room rates for travel from 28 December 2026 to 4 January 2027, with a two-night minimum stay, and must be booked by 26 December 2026. Choose it to be under Downtown’s fireworks. Watch out: the package needs two nights, and the fee is taxable by 13%. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Golden Nugget Las Vegas, comfortable. On Fremont Street, with Rush Tower rooms such as the Premier California King at 439 sq ft, and free parking for hotel guests by its home page. Choose it for a well-known Downtown address. Watch out: we could not find its resort fee or any New Year’s Eve terms on the pages we read. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • El Cortez Hotel & Casino, budget. On Fremont Street, with check-in from 2pm, and everyone on the property must be 21 or older with a valid ID. Its holiday offer for 1 October to 26 December needs a two-night minimum and has blackout dates. Choose it for a historic Fremont Street address at the budget end. Watch out: the offer does not say whether 31 December is a blackout date, and we found no New Year’s Eve package. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Off the Strip: a quieter bed

    The off-Strip options skip the closure altogether. The Palms is west of the Strip and joined last year’s list as the first off-Strip launch site, and Alexis Park is a suite resort with no casino. Plan your trip to the Strip before the roads close.

    Where to stay off the Strip

    • Palms Casino Resort, comfortable. At 4321 W Flamingo Rd, with Fantasy Strip View King rooms of 510 sq ft; Visit Las Vegas lists a Ghostbar on floor 55. Choose it for a launch site west of the Strip. Watch out: it is away from the Strip, and we found no resort fee amount or New Year’s Eve terms on the pages we read. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Alexis Park All Suite Resort, good value. It calls itself the largest off-Strip, non-gaming resort in Las Vegas, three minutes from the Strip, with Parlor, Monarch and LOFT Suites. Choose it for space and no casino. Watch out: “three minutes” will not hold when Las Vegas Boulevard is closed, so plan your return. Check rates on Booking.com →

    How to book New Year’s Eve in Las Vegas

    • Count the tax and the fee. Add 13.38% inside the Primary Gaming Corridor or 13% outside it, plus the resort fee, to compare totals.
    • Check minimum stays. The Plaza states a two-night minimum for its package, and El Cortez states one for its holiday offer. None of the other hotels here published a New Year’s Eve minimum that we found, so ask.
    • Settle your return. The Strip is closed to vehicles for hours, so choose a hotel you can walk back to or one outside the closure with a plan.
    • Book as early as your plans allow. We cannot tell you when each hotel sells out.
    • Compare totals. Our hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and direct booking against online agencies covers the choice. Hotel taxes around the world sets Las Vegas beside other cities.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the best place to stay in Las Vegas for New Year's Eve?

    It depends on the night you want. The Center Strip (Bellagio, The Cosmopolitan, ARIA) puts you inside the Strip's road closure, the North Strip has The STRAT and Treasure Island, which were fireworks launch sites last year, and Downtown puts you at Fremont Street's ticketed party and the Plaza's own fireworks. Off the Strip, the Palms was a launch site west of the Strip and Alexis Park is a quiet suite resort.

    When does the Las Vegas Strip close on New Year's Eve?

    Visit Las Vegas has not published the 2026 plan that we could find. Last year, entry points to Las Vegas Boulevard between Russell Road and Sahara Avenue began closing as early as 5:30pm and shut entirely by 8pm, typically reopening fully by 2am on 1 January. Some hotels asked for a room key to let guests back in.

    Is there a New Year's Eve party on Fremont Street?

    Yes. The Fremont Street Experience lists Countdown Under The Canopy on 31 December from 7pm, with live performers including Hoobastank and Petey Pablo. It is 21 or over only, early-bird tickets were $35 plus fees when we checked, and all ticket sales are final. The page gives no year, so confirm the date when you buy.

    Does Las Vegas have free transport on New Year's Eve?

    Last year Visit Las Vegas said the city would offer free bus transportation from 6pm, detouring around the usual Boulevard stops, and that the monorail was expected to run all night. We could not read RTC's own page or find a 2026 notice. The monorail's regular Thursday hours are 7am to 2am, so confirm before you rely on it.

    What is the hotel tax in Las Vegas?

    The City of Las Vegas states a room tax of 13.38% inside the Primary Gaming Corridor, which covers the Strip, and 13.00% outside it. Resort fees are separate and set by each hotel: Treasure Island's room page states $49.95 plus tax a day, and the Plaza states $39.00 per room, per day, taxable by 13%.

    Do Las Vegas hotels have a minimum stay on New Year's Eve?

    Only some state one. The Plaza's New Year's Eve package, for travel 28 December 2026 to 4 January 2027, requires two nights and must be booked by 26 December 2026. El Cortez's holiday offer needs two nights and has blackout dates. The other hotels in this guide published no New Year's Eve minimum that we found, so ask before you book.
