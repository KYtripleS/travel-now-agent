London’s Christmas has one fact that shapes the whole hotel choice: on Christmas Day, 25 December 2026, almost nothing runs. Transport for London says the only transport running that day will be taxi and private hire services, Santander Cycles, electric scooter rental trials in some boroughs, coaches, Dial-a-Ride for registered members and some bus routes run by other operators. There is no Tube, no Elizabeth line and no DLR in that list. Where you sleep therefore decides what you can reach on foot. The hotel bill itself has no city tax: London charges none, and VAT at 20% is normally already in the price.

Christmas in London: what runs on 24, 25 and 26 December

This is the part to read before you book. We opened Transport for London’s festive travel page; its dates cover Thursday 24 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027, and it says the vast majority of services and the road network will be open in that period. On Christmas Day it is blunt.

Day What is published
Christmas Eve, Thursday 24 December Not published. TfL’s page gave no last-train times and said more details would follow; check TfL Go
Christmas Day, Friday 25 December Only taxis and private hire, Santander Cycles, e-scooter trials, coaches, Dial-a-Ride and some buses run by other operators, per TfL
Boxing Day, Saturday 26 December Not published on the TfL page we read
Congestion Charge Not operating from Christmas Day to 1 January 2027 inclusive
ULEZ Not operating on Christmas Day; the Low Emission Zone runs every day

Three practical consequences follow. First, if you arrive on 25 December you cannot rely on rail from the airport: National Rail’s notice for the Paddington works says that for the Elizabeth line and Great Western Railway, as part of the closure of the railway on Christmas Day and Boxing Day, no trains will run, and Heathrow Express shows no service from 26 to 30 December; that page does not list 25 December for it. Plan a taxi or a coach, or arrive on the 24th. Second, the Winter Wonderland site is closed on Christmas Day, so if your trip is built around it, pick another day. Third, once you are in town, the walk from your hotel is your Christmas Day, which is the case for staying close to what you want to see.

London’s hotel tax: none, but 20% VAT

Charge Rate in 2026
VAT on a hotel room 20%, the UK’s standard rate
City or tourist tax None

UK room prices normally include VAT, so compare totals. To see how much of a price is VAT, take one-sixth of it: our arithmetic puts £41.67 of a £250 room at VAT, so a four-night stay from 23 to 27 December carries £166.67 of VAT in £1,000. The government said on 10 September 2026 that it will legislate for an overnight visitor levy that mayors in England can choose to charge. No levy has been set for London, so none applies to a December 2026 stay. Our main London guide covers it in full.

Getting around when the network stops

The airport routes in our main London guide apply on ordinary days, not on Christmas Day. For 24 to 26 December:

  • Christmas Day. TfL lists no Tube, Elizabeth line, DLR, Overground or tram service. Taxis, private hire and Santander Cycles run.
  • Heathrow. National Rail’s notice says no Elizabeth line or Great Western trains on Christmas Day and Boxing Day, and no Heathrow Express from 26 to 30 December. We found no published Piccadilly line service for 25 December and TfL does not list the Tube as running that day.
  • Check before you travel. TfL says some services will be affected by planned works and timetable changes, and points to TfL Go and the National Rail Christmas page for updates. For New Year’s Eve, see where to stay in London for New Year’s Eve.

    Covent Garden and the Strand: the West End on foot

    Covent Garden, the Strand and Trafalgar Square are neighbours, so the theatres, the piazza and the river are reachable on foot without the Tube. The area is busy and loud until late.

    Where to stay in Covent Garden and the Strand

    • The Savoy, splurge. On Savoy Court, off the Strand, beside the Thames since 1889, with 263 rooms and suites in Edwardian and Art Deco styles, the American Bar, afternoon tea under the glass dome of the Gallery and a pool. Choose it for a grand hotel at the edge of Covent Garden. Watch out: only some rooms face the river, so book a Thames view by name. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • One Aldwych, splurge. At 1 Aldwych, with Superior and Deluxe Bedrooms, twelve suites and a health club. Choose it for a smaller luxury hotel between the Strand and Covent Garden. Watch out: we did not check its festive packages, so ask about Christmas Day meals before you book. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Strand Palace Hotel, comfortable. On the Strand since 1909, renovated in 2019, with rooms from Classic Singles to Deluxe Studio Kings, the Gin Palace cocktail bar and room service from noon to 11pm. Choose it for a large hotel between Trafalgar Square and Covent Garden. Watch out: it is cashless, so cards and digital payments only. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • hub by Premier Inn London Covent Garden, budget. In Theatreland, a few minutes from the piazza, with Standard rooms of 11 sq m holding a double bed, underbed storage and a slide-out desk. Choose it for the West End at the lowest price here. Watch out: the hotel says its rooms offer about half the space of a standard Premier Inn room. Check rates on Booking.com →

    The South Bank and Bankside: the river and the market

    The South Bank Winter Market is on the riverside beside the Southbank Centre, open daily from 11am to 10pm except 25 and 31 December. The London Eye and the riverside walk are here too, and a walk along the Thames needs no ticket or train.

    South Kensington: museums and a quiet morning

    South Kensington sits on the south side of Hyde Park and Kensington Gardens, so the park is a walk away when the Tube is shut. It is calmer and more residential than the West End, which is the reason to pick it.

    Where to stay in South Kensington

    • The Kensington, splurge. A five-star hotel at 109-113 Queen’s Gate, moments from the Royal Albert Hall, the V&A and Kensington Gardens, with drawing rooms with open fires, afternoon tea, the K Bar and a 24-hour gym. Choose it for a townhouse-style stay near the museums. Watch out: the entry rooms are Cosy and Classic rooms, and a rollaway bed for a guest aged 13 or over costs £70 a night. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • The Ampersand Hotel, comfortable. At 10 Harrington Road, a minute’s walk from South Kensington station, with each room type themed, from astronomy in the Singles and Small Doubles to birds in the Deluxe Rooms, a Science Afternoon Tea and the Apero restaurant under Victorian cellar arches. Choose it for the museums, with children or without. Watch out: the hotel itself calls the Singles and Small Doubles intimate. Check rates on Booking.com →

    How to book London at Christmas

    • Choose by what is within walking distance. On 25 December you have no Tube, so pick the area that holds what you want to do.
    • Arrive on the 24th, or plan a taxi. Rail to Heathrow is not running on Christmas Day, and last-train times for the 24th were not published when we looked.
    • Book Winter Wonderland ahead. Entry slots sell out, and it is closed on the 25th.
    • Count VAT, not a city tax. There is no city tax; make sure the total includes VAT and any resort or service charge.
    • Check room size. Compact rooms are 11 sq m at hub hotels, so look at the room type if you have luggage.
    • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is open and running in London on Christmas Day 2026?

    Transport for London says the only transport running on Friday 25 December 2026 will be taxi and private hire services, Santander Cycles, electric scooter rental trials in some boroughs, coaches, Dial-a-Ride for registered members and some bus routes run by other operators. It lists no Tube, Elizabeth line or DLR service. Hyde Park Winter Wonderland and the South Bank Winter Market are closed that day.

    When is Hyde Park Winter Wonderland in 2026?

    Its own site says it opens every day from 19 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, apart from Christmas Day. TfL's page gives 19 November 2026 to 1 January 2027 and says you need to pre-book, so check the ticket page for the day you want.

    Which area is best to stay in for Christmas in London?

    Stay where you can walk to what you want, because there is no Tube on 25 December. Mayfair and Marylebone put you near Hyde Park, Regent Street and Oxford Street, Covent Garden and the Strand are the West End on foot, and the South Bank is beside the Winter Market and the river.

    Is there a tourist tax on London hotels?

    No. London charges no city tax on hotel rooms; VAT at 20% is normally already in the price. The government said on 10 September 2026 that it will legislate to let mayors in England charge an overnight visitor levy, but none has been set for London.

    Can I get from Heathrow to central London on Christmas Day?

    Not by the Elizabeth line or Great Western: National Rail says no trains will run on Christmas Day and Boxing Day. Its notice shows no Heathrow Express from 26 to 30 December and does not list 25 December for it. TfL lists no Tube that day, so plan a taxi or a coach, or arrive on 24 December.

    When do the Regent Street Christmas lights switch on?

    Time Out reported a switch-on on Thursday 5 November 2026, with the street closed to vehicles from 5pm to 9pm. Regent Street's own site lists Christmas events but gave no date, and we could not confirm a 2026 date for Oxford Street.
