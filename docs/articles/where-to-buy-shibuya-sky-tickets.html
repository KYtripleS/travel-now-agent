Why this comparison is only four sellers

Most of our ticket comparisons set five or six sellers side by side. This one cannot, because the venue draws a line. Shibuya Sky’s FAQ, read on 22 November 2026, says it strictly prohibits ticket resale and that tickets bought from anywhere other than the official ticket site or an authorised reseller (正規販売代理店) cannot be used for entry. The same FAQ names its authorised resellers: Klook, KKday and Trip.com. Other sellers may list a Shibuya Sky ticket, but the venue does not name them, and a ticket from one of them may be refused at the door. So the table below covers the three authorised sellers and the venue’s own price.

That is a fact about the venue’s rule, not a judgement on any other seller.

What each seller’s listing says

All terms below were read on 22 November 2026 for a 5 December visit.

Shibuya Sky, the venue

The venue’s FAQ says the ticket is a timed one, with an entry time every 20 minutes, so you choose a date and time when you buy. Date and time cannot be changed; you cancel and buy again. Web tickets that are eligible can be cancelled free until the day before the visit; on the day, you can cancel only under the roof-closure service. If the roof is closed at your entry time, the FAQ allows free cancellation on that day only, and says the indoor gallery on the 46th floor stays open. Tickets already used cannot be cancelled. The FAQ lists Klook, KKday and Trip.com as the authorised resellers whose cancellation steps differ from the venue’s own site.

The venue’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from the venue’s own pages only.

  • Release window. Tickets go on sale at 00:00 Japan time, 14 days before the visit date. If your trip is three weeks away, you cannot buy yet. Set a reminder for the date 14 days out.
  • Sell-outs. The venue says that if adult web tickets are sold out, the counter is sold out too. Children’s tickets are sold at the counter only.
  • Passport. For tickets bought from the official ticket site by overseas customers, or from Klook, KKday or Trip.com, every person entering must carry their own passport, and entry is by passport check. The venue says QR codes will not be accepted for these tickets, and that if the passport details you entered at booking differ from the passport you show, you will not be admitted. KKday’s listing repeats the rule: its page shows “Admission from November 19: Present original passport”.
  • Counter prices. At the 14th-floor counter an adult ticket is ¥3,000 up to 15:00 and ¥3,700 from 15:00. Counter tickets cannot be cancelled.
  • Roof closures. The roof can close for strong wind, rain, snow, approaching thunderclouds, photochemical smog and abnormal heat. Bags, backpacks, tripods, selfie sticks, hats and caps, earphones, food and drink cannot go onto the roof; they are kept in lockers on the 46th floor. Phones and pocket-sized cameras can.
  • Strollers. Only the venue’s own loan strollers may go on the roof, and part of the roof is closed to them.

Which partner to book

  • KKday. It is the one we could read in full. It showed ¥2,700 at 11:00 and ¥3,400 at 18:00 for 5 December, confirms instantly, allows cancellation up to the day before, and gives a form for a closed roof. The venue names it as an authorised reseller.
  • Klook and Trip.com are also named by the venue as authorised, but we could not read either listing, so we make no price or terms claim about them.

Five checks before you pay

  1. The date. Only dates up to 14 days ahead are on sale; on 22 November, KKday’s calendar ran to 6 December.
  2. The slot. 14:59 or earlier is ¥2,700; 15:00 or later is ¥3,400.
  3. The name. Enter your name as it appears on your passport, without a dash, as KKday’s listing instructs.
  4. The passport. Bring the original passport of everyone entering; a QR code will not do.
  5. The cancellation date. Free cancellation ends the day before your visit.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Shibuya Sky tickets?

KKday. On 22 November 2026, for Saturday 5 December, it showed ¥2,700 at 11:00 and ¥3,400 at 18:00, with instant confirmation and free cancellation up to the day before. Klook and Trip.com were not checked.

Which sellers does Shibuya Sky accept?

The venue's FAQ names Klook, KKday and Trip.com as authorised resellers, along with its own ticket site, and says tickets bought from anywhere else cannot be used for entry.

How far ahead can I book?

Tickets go on sale at 00:00 Japan time, 14 days before the visit date. On 22 November, KKday's calendar ran to 6 December.

Does the slot change the price?

Yes. Entry up to 14:59 is ¥2,700 and entry from 15:00 is ¥3,400 for adults. On 5 December, KKday showed ¥2,700 at 11:00 and ¥3,400 at 18:00.

Do I need my passport?

Yes. For tickets from the venue's overseas ticket site, Klook, KKday or Trip.com, each person entering must show their own passport, and QR codes are not accepted. The passport details must match what you entered at booking.

What if the roof is closed?

The venue allows free cancellation on the day if the roof is closed at your entry time. With KKday, submit the request by 11:59 PM Japan time on the day of your visit; the listing says refunds take 7 to 14 business days. The indoor 46th-floor gallery stays open.
