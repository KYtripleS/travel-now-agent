Power

Modern devices need the right adapter for each country's outlets.

Before you go: checklist

  1. Identify the plug types for your destinations
  2. Choose an adapter with USB-C PD and USB-A ports
  3. Check if it handles 100-240V automatically
  4. Bring a small extension lead for hotel rooms

Why this helps

Small preparation steps before a trip may reduce avoidable stress and make travel days feel smoother.
