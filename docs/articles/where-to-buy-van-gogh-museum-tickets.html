Which sellers the museum names

The museum’s ticket page, read on 25 November 2026, names its sellers: “tickets.vangoghmuseum.com or our official resellers Tours&Tickets, GetYourGuide, Tiqets, Musement, or Klook”, and warns that “Other platforms may offer fraudulent tickets.” So this comparison covers only sellers on that list. Of those, Tiqets and GetYourGuide are our partners and we could read both. Klook is also named but blocked our automated reader (see below). Tours&Tickets and Musement are named by the museum but are not our partners, so we did not price them. We left out Viator and KKday because the museum does not name them.

How to read it:

  • The price is level; the slots are not. Both sellers showed about $29 for the adult ticket. Tiqets showed 15 timeslots for 12 December, with several marked “1 left” to “4 left” (for example 9:15 AM “2 left”, 9:30 AM “1 left”, 12:30 PM “4 left”). GetYourGuide showed one start time, 9:15 AM, with “Only 2 spots left”.
  • GetYourGuide’s calendar showed 5, 6 and 7 December crossed out while 8 December onwards was open. Tiqets showed no price for those three days either.
  • Tiqets’s page header said “From $22.58” while its calendar and checkout showed $29 for the date. We use the date figure, and do not know what the lower header figure refers to.
  • The audio guide. GetYourGuide sells it as a separate option ($33.72 for 12 December, English). Tiqets lists “Audio Guide” under options in checkout; we did not read its price.

Klook is “not checked” for a plain reason. Its search page returned an HTTP 403 error to our automated browser on 25 November, with no content. We did not try to get round it.

The museum’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from the museum’s own pages.

  • Every visitor needs a timed ticket. The museum says “All visitors need a ticket with a start time to have access to the museum”, and that tickets are sold online only.
  • Adult price. €25, rising to €26 from 1 January 2027.
  • Children and discounts. “Visitors under 18: free.” Students pay €16 until 31 December 2026 on proof of enrolment. Museumkaart, Vincent’s Friends, ICOM and Rembrandt card holders and Stadspas holders are free but still need a ticket with a start time. The listings we read did not state child prices, so check that an under-18 ticket is what you are buying.
  • I amsterdam City Card. Since 1 June 2022 the museum is no longer affiliated with it.
  • Hours. Tiqets’s page showed 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
  • Museum ticket site. Tickets bought there are “non-refundable” and valid only on the selected date and time.

Which partner to book

  • Main pick: Tiqets. About $29 with 15 timeslots on 12 December, 4.8 stars, free cancellation until 24 hours before, and rescheduling to the same cut-off.
  • Second pick: GetYourGuide. For a reader who wants to pay later, add an English audio guide in the same booking, or prefers the listing with the most reviews (58,611). On 12 December it showed only the 9:15 AM slot, so pick a different date if you want a later start.

Before you pay

  1. The time. Slots on a December Saturday were thin: 9:15 AM had two places left on both sellers.
  2. The cancellation date. For 12 December, GetYourGuide said 11 December; Tiqets said 24 hours before.
  3. The audio guide. Decide before you pay; the museum sells it at €3.75 and GetYourGuide’s option was $33.72 against $29.61 for the ticket alone.
  4. Under-18s. The museum admits visitors under 18 free, but still needs a ticket with a start time.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Van Gogh Museum tickets?

Tiqets for a dated ticket. On 25 November 2026, for Saturday 12 December, it showed $29 and 15 timeslots; GetYourGuide showed $29.61 and one slot, 9:15 AM. Klook was not checked.

Which resellers does the museum name?

Its ticket page names Tours&Tickets, GetYourGuide, Tiqets, Musement and Klook as official resellers, and warns that other platforms may offer fraudulent tickets. We compared the two we could read.

Can I cancel a Van Gogh Museum ticket?

It depends on the seller. Tiqets: free cancellation until 24 hours before the visit date. GetYourGuide: Cancel for free until Dec 11 for a 12 December visit. Tickets bought on the museum's own site are non-refundable.

Is there a ticket with an audio guide?

On GetYourGuide, a Van Gogh Museum with Audio Guide option (English) was $33.72 for 12 December. Tiqets lists the audio guide as an option in checkout; we did not read its price.

Do children need a ticket?

The museum says visitors under 18 are free, but every visitor needs a ticket with a start time. Neither listing we read gave a child price.

Does the I amsterdam City Card include the museum?

No. The museum says it has not been affiliated with the card since 1 June 2022.
