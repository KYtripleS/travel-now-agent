What the castle’s terms say about third-party tickets

We read Royal Collection Trust’s pages on 25 November 2026 before pricing anything: its “Ticket bookings and enquiries” page, its terms and conditions (conditions of admission last updated 25 February 2026) and its information-for-ticketholders page.

RCT does name how third-party tickets are treated. Its ticket enquiries page says: “you are guaranteed entry if your voucher states a date and time of arrival”. It adds that RCT cannot guarantee entry for someone who does not arrive at that time, that changes to a third-party booking go to the third party, and that tickets bought through third parties are not valid for the 1-Year Pass. Its refund notice says the same about refunds: contact the agent or tour operator.

The conditions of admission also say an admission is personal to you and may not be resold or exchanged, and that a resold admission is void. In the same conditions, an admission can be cancelled by RCT or by the group organisers, travel agents or tour operators it was obtained from. In practice that gives one rule for every listing below: choose a listing that asks for a date and a start time when you book, because that is what RCT’s guarantee is written around, and never buy from someone reselling a ticket.

We did not buy anything, so we have not seen the voucher each seller issues. We included a listing only if it took a date and a start time at booking. KKday’s listing is left out on that test, for the reason in the table notes.

How to read it:

  • The castle ticket was level. GetYourGuide and Viator each showed £32.00 for 12 December, and Tiqets’s page showed “from £32.00”. The date matters more than the seller: the castle’s own price list moves to £33.00 for adults from 1 January 2027.
  • The day trips matched to the penny. GetYourGuide and Viator both showed £155.70, £121.50 and £71.10 for the three options we compare. We cannot tell from the pages whether one operator runs both, so we do not claim it.
  • The day-trip price is mostly the entries. The coach-only price is £71.10. Adding all three entries takes it to £155.70, a difference of £84.60 that covers the castle, Stonehenge and the Roman Baths.
  • Tiqets sells the castle ticket and a few Windsor trips; we did not read a Windsor, Stonehenge and Bath trip on it, so that row says “not read”.

The castle’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from rct.uk only.

  • Prices. The 2026 price list shows adult £32.00 in advance and £36.00 on the day, child (5-17) £16.00 and £18.00, young person (18-24) £21.00 and £24.00, and under 5 free. From 1 January 2027 the adult advance price is £33.00.
  • Timed tickets. Where a time is stated on the ticket, the conditions say you will not be admitted if you arrive late unless a later time becomes available that day, and that no refund is made. Arrive early and you wait until the stated time.
  • Closures at short notice. The conditions say that as working royal palaces the residences can change opening arrangements at short notice, and that RCT may cancel and refund or offer another date. For a third-party ticket, RCT’s refund notice sends you to the seller.
  • Children. The conditions say children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult over 17, who also needs an admission. We priced adults only.
  • Bags. For safety, the conditions say you may be asked to leave bags, umbrellas, pushchairs and other items in the security cloakroom, and food and drink are not allowed inside except in the cafés.
  • St George’s Chapel. Listings say it is closed to visitors on Sundays. 12 December 2026 is a Saturday.
  • 12 December. RCT’s page lists a Windsor Castle Festive Weekend activity starting on 12 December, included with standard admission. The Great Kitchen Tour package runs from 27 December, so it does not apply to a 12 December visit.

Five checks before you pay

  1. The date. The castle’s price list changes on 1 January 2027, and Sunday tickets exclude St George’s Chapel.
  2. The start time. RCT’s guarantee is written around a date and a time of arrival, and late arrival is not covered.
  3. The cancellation cut-off. GetYourGuide: 11 December. Viator: before 10:00 on 11 December (castle) or 08:00 (day trip). Tiqets: 11:59 PM on 11 December.
  4. Which entries are in the day-trip price. £71.10, £121.50 and £155.70 are three different products.
  5. Who you buy from. Use a listing that issues a ticket for your date and time, and do not buy from people reselling tickets.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Windsor Castle tickets?

GetYourGuide: Windsor Castle Admission Ticket. On 25 November 2026, for Saturday 12 December, it showed £32.00 for one adult at 10:00, with free cancellation until 11 December. Viator showed £32.00 and Tiqets from £32.00. Klook was not checked.

Is a ticket from a booking site accepted at the castle?

RCT says: you are guaranteed entry if your voucher states a date and time of arrival. It cannot guarantee entry if you arrive at a different time, and changes go to the seller.

Which day trip includes Windsor Castle?

On Viator and GetYourGuide, the Windsor, Stonehenge and Bath trip with all three entries was £155.70 for 12 December at 08:00. The no-entry option was £71.10, so the entries are what separate the options.

Can I cancel a Windsor Castle ticket?

It depends on the listing. GetYourGuide said Cancel for free until Dec 11, Viator said free cancellation before 10:00 on 11 December, and Tiqets said free cancellation until 11:59 PM on 11 December. KKday's listing says tickets are non-refundable.

What do children pay?

RCT's 2026 price list shows child (5-17) at £16.00 in advance and under 5 free. Viator's castle listing shows a child band of 5-17 and infants 0-4 free; its day trip lists children aged 3-16. GetYourGuide's castle page did not show a child price in what we read.

Is St George's Chapel open on 12 December?

Listings say the chapel is closed to visitors on Sundays. 12 December 2026 is a Saturday.
