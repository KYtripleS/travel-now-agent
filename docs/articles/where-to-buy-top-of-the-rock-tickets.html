What you are actually choosing

Top of the Rock is one observation deck on three levels, the 67th, 69th and 70th floors. Admission covers all three. The extras are separate products: The Beam, SKYLIFT, and a VIP guided pass. The all-inclusive pass bundles admission with The Beam and SKYLIFT, and the VIP pass adds a guided tour, priority access, a Photo Pass and a champagne toast.

That makes the buying decision simpler than at some towers. The real choices are which product, and which time of day. On the building’s own page for 9 December, 9:00 am to 1:50 pm and 7:00 pm to 9:50 pm slots were $56, 2:00 pm to 2:50 pm and 5:20 pm to 6:50 pm slots were $68, the slots around 3:00 to 4:20 pm ran $71 to $83, and the last slots of the night fell to $53 and $49. Slots from 3:20 to 4:00 pm showed “Sold Out” on the day we read it.

How to read it:

  • At the same slot, the partners that showed a slot price matched. Tiqets and Viator both showed $60.97 for the 9:00 am slot and $99.08 for the all-inclusive pass at 9:00 am. Tiqets and GetYourGuide both showed $53.35 and $91.46 for 11:00 pm.
  • GetYourGuide’s page shows only the first slot it lists. For 9 December that was 11:00 pm, with the other slots behind the “Continue” step. The $80.57 and $201.43 figures are for the slot named in brackets, so do not compare them with a different slot’s price.
  • Viator’s figures are per adult with two travellers selected, read on the same date. Tiqets and KKday were read for one adult, and GetYourGuide for “Adult x 1”.
  • KKday does not price a slot. Its calendar showed “from $53.35” for 9 December. Its voucher is exchanged at the box office for a timed ticket, so the time is set on the day (see below).
  • Late slots are cheaper at every seller. Tiqets showed $60.97 at 9:00 am and $53.35 at 11:00 pm for the same ticket. If your plan is flexible, the time of day is a bigger lever than the choice of seller.

Klook is “not checked” for a plain reason. Its Top of the Rock page returned an HTTP 403 error to our automated browser on 22 November, with no page at all. We did not try to get round it, so Klook has no figure in the table.

The building’s FAQ says: “All sales are final and non-refundable.” It adds that tickets may be rescheduled up to 24 hours before the visit, subject to availability, that “Price differences may apply”, and that in poor visibility or weather tickets may be rescheduled for a future date. The building’s own rules that change the buying decision

These come from rockefellercenter.com only.

  • Timed admission. The ticket is for your selected date and admission time. The building says advance purchase is recommended and that tickets are also sold on site, subject to availability.
  • Hours. Open daily from 8 AM to 12 AM, with last entry at 11:10 PM, which is why the cheapest slots sit at the end of the evening.
  • Children and age bands. Children aged 5 and under receive free admission. The age bands on its ticket page are adult 13 to 61, child 6 to 12, and senior 62 and over. We priced one adult only.
  • Security and what you carry. There is a security checkpoint for all visitors. Outside food and drink, weapons and tripods are not allowed.
  • The Christmas tree. The building says the Christmas Tree and The Rink are not visible from the observation decks; both are reached at ground level after your visit. Its checkout page notes unobstructed tree views and nightly lightings from 2 December, and its VIP pass lists a Christmas Tree Experience for 3 December to 3 January.
  • Deck height. The deck is 850 feet above Midtown, on the 70th floor, and SKYLIFT raises visitors 30 feet above it.

Which partner to book

  • A fixed slot and visible slot prices: Tiqets. Every slot for 9 December is listed with its price, and free cancellation runs to 11:59 pm on 7 December.
  • The latest cancellation: GetYourGuide. Free cancellation until 8 December and pay later, with the five options on one page.
  • The all-inclusive pass: Tiqets or GetYourGuide. $91.46 at 11:00 pm on both; Tiqets also lists the 9:00 am price, $99.08, matching Viator.
  • KKday suits you if you are happy to fix the slot on the day at the box office; its date price starts at $53.35, and it cancels free to two days before.

Five checks before you pay

  1. The slot. The price belongs to the time. Late-evening slots were the lowest at every seller.
  2. The cancellation date. For 9 December: Tiqets 7 December, Viator 8 December before 9:00 am, GetYourGuide 8 December, KKday two days before.
  3. The product. Admission alone is not The Beam or SKYLIFT; those are separate or bundled.
  4. Fixed or box-office slot. Tiqets, GetYourGuide and Viator show times; KKday’s voucher is exchanged on the day.
  5. Height rules for The Beam if you travel with children.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Top of the Rock tickets?

Tiqets or GetYourGuide for a dated ticket. On 22 November 2026, for a Wednesday 9 December visit, both showed $53.35 for the 11:00 pm slot and $91.46 for the all-inclusive pass at that slot. Tiqets showed $60.97 for the 9:00 am slot, the same as Viator. Klook was not checked.

Does the price change by time of day?

Yes. The building's page for 9 December ran from $49 at 11:00 pm to $83 at 3:10 pm, and Tiqets showed the same pattern: $60.97 at 9:00 am, $74.04 at 2:00 pm and $53.35 at 11:00 pm.

Can I cancel a Top of the Rock ticket?

It depends on the seller. Tiqets said free cancellation until 11:59 pm on 7 December, GetYourGuide until 8 December, Viator before 9:00 am on 8 December, and KKday up to two days before. The building itself says sales are final and non-refundable, with rescheduling up to 24 hours before the visit.

What is the difference between admission and the all-inclusive pass?

Admission covers the observation deck on the 67th, 69th and 70th floors. The all-inclusive pass adds The Beam and SKYLIFT. On 9 December it was $99.08 at 9:00 am on Tiqets and Viator, and $91.46 at 11:00 pm on Tiqets and GetYourGuide.

Can I see the Rockefeller Center Christmas tree from the deck?

No. The building says the tree and The Rink are not visible from the observation decks; both are at ground level, reached after your visit.

Do children need a ticket?

The building says children aged 5 and under receive free admission. Its bands are child 6 to 12, adult 13 to 61 and senior 62 and over. We priced adults only.
