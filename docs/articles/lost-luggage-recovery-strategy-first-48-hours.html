Safety

Losing your luggage is stressful; knowing the first steps helps.

Before you go: checklist

  1. File a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) at the airport
  2. Keep all boarding passes and baggage tags
  3. Buy essential items and save every receipt
  4. Track the bag online and contact the airline daily

Why this helps

Small preparation steps before a trip may reduce avoidable stress and make travel days feel smoother.
