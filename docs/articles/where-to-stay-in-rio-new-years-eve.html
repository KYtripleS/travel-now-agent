Rio’s Réveillon is free, and that is what makes the hotel bill unusual. The city’s biggest night is a beach party that the Prefeitura puts on without a ticket, so you do not choose a seat, you choose how close you can sleep to a closed avenue. For last year’s edition the city expected more than 2.5 million people in Copacabana and shut Avenida Atlântica to cars from 7am on 31 December. Rio’s hotels carry no city tourist tax that we could find, so the extra cost is the date, not a levy.

This guide covers what Riotur and the city published for the 2025-26 Réveillon, which is the latest official plan, five areas matched to how you want the night to go, 12 hotels, and the hotel terms that catch people out. Thursday 31 December 2026 is the night, and the city had not published its 2026-27 programme or transport plan when we checked in November 2026; where we describe last year’s arrangements we say so. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in November 2026. No hotel pays to appear here.

What the city had published, and what it had not

We could not find a 2026-27 Réveillon programme, a Copacabana stage lineup, or a MetrôRio, SuperVia or CET-Rio plan for 31 December 2026 when we checked in November 2026. Last year, Riotur’s guide to the New Year parties was dated 27 November 2025, its page with the stage lineup was dated 4 December 2025, and the Prefeitura’s operations plan was dated 26 December 2025. So the official detail has tended to arrive in December, and a plan for the transport may come only days before the night. Riotur’s events calendar is the page to watch.

Here is what last year’s official plan said, which is the best guide to the shape of the night:

  • The stages: 13 stages across the city, with three in the Zona Sul on the beach: the Palco Rio in front of the Copacabana Palace, the Palco Samba Amstel at Rua República do Peru in front of Posto 6, and a gospel stage at Leme on Rua Anchieta in front of the Forte do Leme. On the Palco Rio last year the music ran from 6pm, with the countdown slot, the “Momento da Virada”, listed from 23h45 and a headline set at 00h12.
  • The fireworks: 19 barges along the beach and 12 minutes of fireworks.
  • Other fireworks: the city’s plan also named Flamengo, with three barges and 10 minutes, the Igreja da Penha at 8 minutes, and Parque Oeste and Parque Realengo at 6 minutes each. Barra da Tijuca is not on that list.
  • The crowd and the police: more than 2.5 million people expected in Copacabana, around 7,500 municipal agents from 11 bodies, and about 700 cameras watching the city’s parties.
  • Glass: street vendors were banned from selling glass bottles.

Closures and the Metro: last year’s arrangements

The closures decide how you arrive and how you leave. Last year the plan closed Avenida Atlântica completely to traffic from 7am on 31 December, and opened the lane beside the buildings from 10am on 1 January. Parking was banned on the avenue from 6am on 30 December, and the access points to Copacabana at Ipanema, the Cantagalo cut, the Túnel Velho and the Enseada de Botafogo were blocked. We could not load CET-Rio’s own page on the closures, so we rely on Riotur’s copy of the plan. If you arrive by car or taxi on the day, ask your hotel in writing how to reach it.

The Metro ran a special operation in Copacabana from 7pm on 31 December to 5am on 1 January. The Réveillon passport cost R$15 and covered the trip out and back; it was sold only through MetrôRio’s app or site, and after the passport was validated at the gate on the way to Copacabana, passengers were given a return wristband. Last year’s plan also had the Line 2 trains running between Pavuna and General Osório (Ipanema) on 31 December and 1 January, and the buses added to Copacabana’s routes from 6pm. Check MetrôRio’s app for the 2026 version, which had not been published, and do not assume it will match.

Copacabana’s metro stations are Cardeal Arcoverde, Siqueira Campos and Cantagalo, and Ipanema’s are General Osório and Nossa Senhora da Paz. Lines 1 and 4 run together between Uruguai and Jardim Oceânico, and Line 2 between Pavuna and General Osório.

A local news report of 6 June 2026 is our only source here, and it is not an official one. The fee that applies on Ilha Grande belongs to a different municipality, Angra dos Reis. The cost of the night shows up in the room rate and in party tickets, not in a levy, and most of the hotels here publish no New Year’s Eve rate at all. Compare totals, and ask for the New Year’s Eve rate in writing.

Getting in from the airport

  • From RIOgaleão (GIG): the airport’s own pages list the BRT Transcarioca Parador at R$5.00, from 4am to midnight, with a link to metro Line 2 at Vicente de Carvalho. A special Mobi-Rio bus runs from Terminal 2 to Terminal Gentileza for R$15. Regular and executive taxis leave from Terminal 2 arrivals at metered or city-hall-table fares, and Uber has its own pick-up points. We could not reload the airport pages on 5 November, so recheck the fares.
  • From Santos Dumont (SDU): the Windsor Asturias in our Carnival guide describes itself as a few minutes from this airport; none of the hotels here makes that claim, so use a taxi or an app.
  • Plan the last mile: with Avenida Atlântica shut from 7am, arrive in Copacabana before 31 December, or ask the hotel how its guests are let through.

    Copacabana: inside the party

    Copacabana is where the Palco Rio and the main fireworks were last year. A hotel on Avenida Atlântica puts you a few steps from the sand, and the avenue’s address numbers tell you where along the beach you are: the Copacabana Palace, in front of which the main stage stood, is at number 1702. The cost is the closures, the noise and the crowd, and the hotels with a stated New Year’s Eve package are here.

    Leme: the quieter end of the beach

    Leme is the northern tip of the same beach. Last year’s gospel stage stood here on Rua Anchieta, in front of the Forte do Leme, so it is not silent, but it is a short walk to the crowd without being in the middle of it.

    Where to stay in Leme

    • Windsor Leme, comfortable. A hotel of 224 apartments at Avenida Atlântica 656, with an infinity-edge pool. Its page lists Copacabana beach, Copacabana Fort, Leme beach and Leme Fort among the places nearby, and says dogs and cats up to 5 kg are accepted for a fee. Choose it for the quiet end of the avenue. Watch out: its promotions list Christmas but no New Year’s Eve package that we found. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Ipanema and Leblon: a calmer beach, a short trip

    Ipanema and Leblon lie beyond the Arpoador rock. Last year the plan blocked the Ipanema access to Copacabana, so you cannot count on driving or taking a taxi there on the night. The metro was the way: the Line 2 trains ran to General Osório in Ipanema, and the Metro’s special passport covered the journey to Copacabana. Plan to take the metro, not a car, and to be back before the last service.

    Where to stay in Ipanema and Leblon

    • Hotel Fasano Rio de Janeiro, splurge. A hotel of 89 rooms in five categories at Avenida Vieira Souto 80, a Philippe Starck hotel with an infinity pool above the beach. Choose it for the beach in Ipanema without the Copacabana crowd. Watch out: its offers page lists no New Year’s Eve package; its general offer says the last of four nights is free, which is not specific to the date. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • JANEIRO Hotel, splurge. In Leblon, a hotel whose page counts 51 rooms in categories from JANEIRO and MASTER to SUITE, DELUXE and THE ROOM, with a pool overlooking the mountain and a pool bar. Choose it for a small, quiet Leblon base. Watch out: its page lists no New Year’s Eve offer, and Leblon is the farthest Zona Sul beach from the Copacabana stage. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Botafogo and Flamengo: the bay’s own fireworks

    Botafogo and Flamengo face the bay, not the Atlantic. The city’s plan last year gave Flamengo its own show, three barges with 10 minutes of fireworks, and started a special bus operation at the Terminal Enseada de Botafogo at 9pm, but also blocked the Enseada de Botafogo access to Copacabana. These districts are on the metro, so you can reach the beach by train and still have a calmer street to return to.

    Where to stay in Botafogo and Flamengo

    • Windsor Florida, good value. A hotel of 422 apartments at Rua Ferreira Viana 81, a few metres from the Aterro do Flamengo, which it pitches as good value for money.     Barra da Tijuca: its own fireworks, far from the avenue

    Barra is the long modern beach west of the Zona Sul. It does not have a city fireworks show: Riotur said last year that hotels and shopping centres promised fireworks at 12 points in Barra and Recreio. Those are private, and nothing in the city’s list of official sites includes Barra. It suits a night with a pool and a dinner, not the crowd, and the metro’s Line 4 ends at Jardim Oceânico.

    Where to stay in Barra da Tijuca

    • Windsor Barra, comfortable. A hotel of 339 apartments and suites at Avenida Lúcio Costa 2630, with views of the sea and mountains and front-sea apartments from the third floor, plus a pool and a sauna. Choose it for a pool and the beach in Barra. Watch out: its page has no New Year’s Eve package, and Barra is a long way from Copacabana. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Mercure Rio de Janeiro Barra da Tijuca, good value. At Avenida do Pepê 56, with Pepê beach at 0 m by Accor’s page, the Sambá restaurant, a spa and a fitness centre, and some rooms with sea or Pedra da Gávea views. Accor says it is just 10 minutes from Jardim Oceânico subway station. Choose it for a beachfront Barra stay at a lower price. Watch out: its page lists no New Year’s Eve offer, and check-in is from 3pm with check-out at noon. Check rates on Booking.com →

    How to book New Year’s Eve in Rio

    • Decide how close you want to be. On the avenue, you get the stage and the sand but also the closures. Elsewhere you take the metro.
    • Read the party terms. At the Pestana the rooftop is open that night only to party-ticket holders with a wristband, and the Othon’s Skylights party is ticketed separately.
    • Ask about minimum stays. We found no hotel here that publishes one for 31 December, so ask in writing; the Mango Tree’s published terms apply to Carnaval.
    • Book early. We cannot tell you when each hotel sells out, but the Pestana’s own page says New Year’s Eve in Copacabana and Carnival are the most sought-after times.
    • Compare totals. Our hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Rio's Réveillon free?

    Yes. The City's Réveillon on Copacabana beach has no ticket, and last year's plan had 13 stages across the city and 19 barges with 12 minutes of fireworks, with more than 2.5 million people expected in Copacabana. That was the 2025-26 edition; the 2026-27 programme had not been published when we checked in November 2026.

    When does Rio publish the Réveillon programme?

    Last year Riotur dated its party guide 27 November 2025, its stage-lineup page 4 December 2025 and the Prefeitura's operations plan 26 December 2025. We found nothing for 31 December 2026 in November 2026, so expect the detail in December.

    Is the Metro running on New Year's Eve in Rio?

    The 2026 plan was not published when we checked. Last year MetrôRio ran a special Copacabana operation from 7pm on 31 December to 5am on 1 January, with a R$15 round-trip passport sold only through its app or site and a return wristband given at the gate.

    Is Avenida Atlântica closed on New Year's Eve?

    Last year the plan closed Avenida Atlântica completely to traffic from 7am on 31 December and opened the lane beside the buildings from 10am on 1 January, with parking banned from 6am on 30 December. The 2026-27 closures had not been published, so ask your hotel how guests reach it.

    Does Rio have a tourist tax on hotels?

    We found none charged in the city in November 2026. A local news report of 6 June 2026 said a R$15 sustainable tourism fee bill was before the City Council's committees with no plenary vote scheduled. A different fee applies in Angra dos Reis, a separate municipality.

    Do Rio hotels require a minimum stay for New Year's Eve?

    Only the hotels' own pages can say, and most of the 12 here publish nothing for 31 December. The Pestana's page says its rooftop party is open only to ticket holders with a wristband, and the Othon Palace sells its parties separately; none states a minimum stay that we found, so ask in writing.
