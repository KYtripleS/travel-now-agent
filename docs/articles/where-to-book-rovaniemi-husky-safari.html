A Rovaniemi husky safari is not one product, and the headline price hides the thing that matters most: who drives the sled. On the Saturday we priced, a ride where the musher drives and you sit in the sled cost from about €89 a head, while a self-drive safari where you steer your own team cost €189 to €219. The rest of the gap comes from three lines on each listing: how long the ride actually is, how late you can cancel, and whether pickup and winter clothing are in the price.

Two practical points before you compare. Several of the cheaper listings need at least two paying adults to run, so a solo traveller has to read that line first. And a ride can be as short as a few kilometres: the cheapest listing we read is a 1 km sled ride with a farm visit, which is a different outing from a 5 to 10 km safari even though both are sold as a husky experience.

What each seller’s listing says

All read on 22 November 2026.

GetYourGuide: Rovaniemi: Daytime Husky Safari, 5 km self-driven. €189 for one adult on 12 December, shown “all taxes and fees included”, with start times at 9:00, 11:00, 13:00 and 15:00. The activity provider is Safartica, the listing runs 1.5 hours with an English-speaking guide, and it is rated 4.6 from 1,037 reviews and flagged “likely to sell out”. Free cancellation up to 24 hours ahead for a full refund (the booking widget said you can cancel for free until 11 December), and you can reserve now and pay later. Included: winter clothing, transfers, the 5 km self-drive ride (about 20 to 30 minutes, two adults per team, children in the sleigh), hot drinks with biscuits and English guiding. The description says the musher shows you how to steer before the start and that you return to the kennel afterwards for stories about the huskies and hot drinks by a campfire. One recent reviewer, travelling alone, says solo riders drive the whole way rather than swapping halfway.

Viator: Husky Farm Visit & Sled Ride Experience. €89 per adult with two adults selected (€178 in total), “peak season” pricing, start times 09:40 and 10:55, about 2 hours, supplied by Sunny Safari. The ride is 1 km with the musher driving while guests sit in the sled; the listing also includes a husky kennel visit, a talk about the huskies, hot berry juice and biscuits, and pickup from listed locations or a meeting point at the Sunny Safari office in Santa Claus Village. Free cancellation before 09:40 on 11 December, with reserve now and pay nothing until 10 December. The listing says at least two full-paying adults are required for the tour to operate, though a single traveller may join an existing group, that a sled typically holds two to four people with seating decided by the musher on site, and that it is not wheelchair accessible. It shows 3 reviews, and one traveller wrote that the ride was short for the price.

Choosing between a passenger ride and driving yourself

  • Who drives. On the passenger listings the musher drives and you sit in the sled; the KKday €119 listing says passengers cannot steer. On the self-drive listings you steer your own team after a briefing, usually two adults to a sled who swap halfway. A solo rider on a self-drive listing should expect to drive the whole way.
  • Distance. The listings we read run from 1 km to 10 km. The ride itself can be short: GetYourGuide puts its 5 km at about 20 to 30 minutes of the 1.5-hour booking, and KKday puts its 7 to 10 km at 45 to 60 minutes. The rest of the time is the farm, the kit and the drinks.
  • Children. Rules differ. GetYourGuide and KKday put children in the sleigh; Viator’s Apukka listing says children under 14 sit with an adult; Viator’s Sunny Safari listing says children aged 0 to 2 are not recommended; KKday’s €119 listing excludes children under 3.
  • Conditions. Some March reviews on GetYourGuide mention little snow left late in the season, and one Viator reviewer found the 1 km ride short for the price. Treat the distance on the listing as what you are buying, and read recent reviews for the trail.

Clothing and the small print

Winter clothing is stated as included on the GetYourGuide and KKday safari listings; the KKday €119 listing does not list clothing in its inclusions. Wear warm layers under whatever is lent to you. Check each listing’s meeting-point line: pickup applies only from listed locations, and a missed pickup or meeting time is not refunded on the Viator Sunny Safari and KKday safari listings.

Frequently asked questions

Which seller is the safest to book for a husky safari in Rovaniemi?

Of the listings we read, the GetYourGuide 5 km self-driven safari: €189 for one adult on 12 December 2026, free cancellation up to 24 hours ahead, reserve now and pay later, pickup and winter clothing included.

What is the cheapest husky ride in Rovaniemi?

On 12 December 2026 the lowest price we read was €89 a head on Viator for a 1 km sled ride with a farm visit, with the musher driving. It needs at least two paying adults. The lowest self-drive price was €189 on GetYourGuide.

Do I drive the sled myself?

It depends on the listing. On the GetYourGuide, Viator Apukka and KKday safari listings you steer your own team after a briefing; on the Viator Sunny Safari and KKday €119 listings the musher drives and you sit in the sled.

How long is the ride itself?

The listings we read run from 1 km to 10 km. GetYourGuide's 5 km ride is about 20 to 30 minutes within a 1.5-hour booking, and KKday's 7 to 10 km safari is 45 to 60 minutes within about 2 hours.

Can children go?

Rules differ. GetYourGuide and KKday seat children in the sleigh, Viator's Apukka listing has children under 14 sitting with an adult, and the KKday €119 listing excludes children under 3.

How late can I cancel?

On our date, GetYourGuide and both Viator listings allowed free cancellation until the day before, KKday's 1-Hour Husky Safari up to 2 days ahead, and KKday's half-day tour only up to 31 days ahead before a fee applies.
