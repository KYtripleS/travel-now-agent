Hotels around Marina Bay, with prices

The map shows the hotels and apartments around Marina Bay that booking sites such as Booking.com and Expedia list, with nightly prices; our own picks follow further down. It comes from our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book through it.

Singapore placed ninth in Euromonitor International’s Top 100 City Destinations Index for 2025, and it is compact enough that anywhere central is a short MRT ride from anywhere else. What changes from area to area is what you can walk to, and what the room costs: hotels here are not cheap, so where you sleep is as much about value as about views.

This guide compares five areas, then names twelve hotels across them, with the reason to pick each and, where there is one, the catch. They are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating at the start of October 2026. Two of them have changed more than their prices: Hilton Singapore Orchard is the former Mandarin Orchard, and Marina Bay Sands has been rebuilding its rooms into fewer, larger ones. Marina Bay and the Civic District: the first-trip base

Marina Bay is the Singapore of the postcards: Marina Bay Sands, the ArtScience Museum and, behind them, Gardens by the Bay, with its Supertree Grove and glass conservatories. Across the water, the Civic District holds the colonial core: the Padang, the National Gallery Singapore in the former Supreme Court and City Hall, and Raffles Hotel. The MRT’s North-South and East-West lines cross at City Hall, the Circle and Downtown lines meet at Bayfront, and the Thomson-East Coast line stops at Gardens by the Bay. Many first-time visitors spend most of their days around here, so a hotel here saves the most walking.

Where to stay in Marina Bay and the Civic District

  • Marina Bay Sands, splurge. Opened in 2010, three towers joined at the top by the SkyPark, whose 150-metre infinity pool, 200 metres up, is for hotel guests only. A US$1.75 billion renovation has been rebuilding its rooms into about 1,850, some 770 of them suites. Choose it for the pool and the view. Watch out: price, and the crowds in the mall below.   • Raffles Singapore, splurge. It opened in 1887 and reopened on 1 August 2019 after a restoration that began in 2017, as an all-suite hotel with 115 suites around its gardens and verandahs, a short walk from City Hall station.   • Pan Pacific Singapore, comfortable. 790 rooms at Marina Square, linked to the Marina Square mall and the Suntec convention centre, with an outdoor pool. The Singapore River: Clarke Quay and Robertson Quay

The Singapore River runs from the Bay past Boat Quay and Clarke Quay, where old godowns and shophouses are now restaurants and bars, up to Robertson Quay, where the riverside walk is quieter and lined with cafés. At its mouth stands the Fullerton Building, once the General Post Office. Stay here for evenings by the water. Clarke Quay station is on the North East line and Fort Canning on the Downtown line, and the riverside path makes a long, flat walk to the Bay.

Where to stay on the river

  • The Fullerton Hotel Singapore, splurge. 400 rooms in the Fullerton Building, completed in 1928 as the General Post Office, opened as a hotel in 2001 and made a National Monument in 2015, at the mouth of the river. Choose it for heritage between the river and the Bay. Watch out: price. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • The Warehouse Hotel, comfortable to splurge. 37 rooms in a godown built in 1895 on the river at Robertson Quay, opened as a hotel in January 2017, with a rooftop pool and free bicycles. Choose it for character at the quiet end of the river. Watch out: with 37 rooms, there are few room types to choose from. Check rates on Booking.com →

Orchard Road: the shopping street

Orchard Road is more than two kilometres of malls, from ION Orchard and Ngee Ann City to Plaza Singapura, with the Singapore Botanic Gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, beyond its western end. The North-South line runs beneath it at Orchard, Somerset and Dhoby Ghaut, and the Thomson-East Coast line stops at Orchard too. The hotels are big and modern, and the evenings are calmer than at the Bay or on the river.

Where to stay on Orchard Road

  • Pan Pacific Orchard, splurge. A 23-storey garden hotel by WOHA, opened in June 2023, with four open-air terraces planted as forest, beach, garden and cloud. Choose it for the newest hotel on the road, near the Botanic Gardens. Watch out: it is at the western end of Orchard Road, away from the busiest malls. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Hilton Singapore Orchard, comfortable. 1,080 rooms at 333 Orchard Road, the former Mandarin Orchard, reopened as a Hilton on 24 February 2022 after a S$150 million renovation; it is the brand’s largest hotel in Asia Pacific. Choose it for the middle of Orchard Road. Watch out: its size. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • YOTEL Singapore Orchard Road, good value. 610 compact rooms, which the brand calls cabins, over 30 floors at 366 Orchard Road, opened in 2017, with an outdoor pool. Choose it for a lower price on Orchard Road. Watch out: the rooms are small by design. Check rates on Booking.com →

Chinatown and Tanjong Pagar: hawker food and old streets

Chinatown is the old shophouse quarter south of the river, with the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, the Sri Mariamman Temple and hawker centres such as Maxwell Food Centre and the Chinatown Complex. Telok Ayer and Tanjong Pagar, next door, add restaurants and bars among the offices of the business district. Chinatown station is on the North East and Downtown lines, Telok Ayer on the Downtown line, and Tanjong Pagar on the East-West line.

Where to stay in Chinatown and Tanjong Pagar

  • PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering, comfortable. 367 rooms in a tower by WOHA that opened in January 2013, wrapped in some 15,000 square metres of planted sky terraces, about twice the area of its site, on Upper Pickering Street between Chinatown and Clarke Quay. Choose it for a garden in the middle of the city. Watch out: the busy roads around it. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • The Clan Hotel Singapore, comfortable. 324 rooms at 10 Cross Street, opened on 1 March 2021, with direct access to Telok Ayer station. Choose it for the hawker centres and the restaurants of Telok Ayer on foot. Watch out: it is in the business district, so weekday mornings are busy. Check rates on Booking.com →

Sentosa: beaches and theme parks

Sentosa, the resort island off the south coast, has beaches along its southern shore, big resort pools, and Universal Studios Singapore and the other attractions of Resorts World Sentosa. It suits families, or a couple of nights at the end of a trip. The Sentosa Express monorail runs from VivoCity, next to HarbourFront station, and you can also walk across on the Sentosa Boardwalk. The trade-off is distance: the rest of the city is a longer ride away.

Where to stay on Sentosa

  • Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa, comfortable to splurge. 454 rooms with balconies, on the beach at Siloso Road, with two pools and a free shuttle. Choose it for a family beach stay. Watch out: it is at the far western end of the island. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Capella Singapore, splurge. 112 rooms in a resort by Foster + Partners around two military buildings from the 1880s, opened in March 2009, with three outdoor pools in the island’s rainforest. Choose it for seclusion close to the city. Watch out: price, and the hotel’s shuttle to HarbourFront and VivoCity is your link to the MRT. Check rates on Booking.com →

Before you book

  • Read the “++”. Prices marked “++” add a 10% service charge and then 9% GST, the rate since 1 January 2024, charged on the service charge too: about 19.9% in all. Hotels may show prices before GST, so compare totals, not headline rates (IRAS).
  • Fill in the SG Arrival Card. It is free and is done online in the three days before you arrive, through ICA’s own e-service or the MyICA app, and it asks where you are staying (ICA).
  • Choose the station before the hotel. In Singapore’s heat, a hotel by an MRT station saves more than a bigger room further away. If you are still choosing a booking site, our hotel booking sites compared goes through them.

Getting around from your hotel

  • From Changi Airport, the MRT runs into the city with a change at Tanah Merah onto the East-West line, and taxis and ride-hailing cars wait at every terminal (Changi Airport).
  • Around town, tap a contactless Visa, Mastercard or American Express card, or a phone wallet, at the MRT gates and on buses; there is no need to buy a transit card (Visa).

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Singapore for a first trip?

Marina Bay and the Civic District: Gardens by the Bay, the museums and the Padang are on foot, and City Hall and Bayfront stations connect four MRT lines. Choose Orchard Road for shopping or Chinatown for hawker food.

Is it better to stay at Marina Bay or on Orchard Road?

Marina Bay for the sights and the views, Orchard Road for the malls and calmer evenings. The MRT links them in minutes, so choose by what you want outside the door.

Is Sentosa a good place to stay in Singapore?

For families and beach days, yes: there are resort pools, beaches and Universal Studios Singapore. The trade-off is distance from the city; the Sentosa Express monorail runs from VivoCity, next to HarbourFront station.

What does "++" mean on hotel prices in Singapore?

A 10% service charge and then 9% GST, charged on the service charge too, about 19.9% in all. GST has been 9% since 1 January 2024.

Do I need to fill in an arrival card for Singapore?

Yes, the SG Arrival Card. It is free and is submitted online in the three days before you arrive, through ICA's e-service or the MyICA app; it asks where you are staying.

How do I get from Changi Airport to my hotel?

By MRT, with a change at Tanah Merah onto the East-West line, or by taxi or ride-hailing car from any terminal. Contactless bank cards and phone wallets work at the MRT gates.
