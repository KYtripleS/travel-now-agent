The 2026/27 season and the lift pass

Whistler Blackcomb’s hours page gives opening day as 20 November 2026 and says opening and closing dates depend on weather and conditions. It lists no closing date for 2026/27 yet, so do not book a late-April or May trip on an assumed date. Base-area lift hours for the winter:

Dates Lifts open Lifts close
Opening day to 15 January 9:00 am 3:30 pm
16 January to 12 February 9:00 am 4:00 pm
13 February to closing day 8:30 am 4:00 pm

The pass options we could read on the official pages on 25 October 2026. Prices change, and the day-pass and lift-ticket prices vary by date:

Option Price Notes
Epic Pass, adult (31 and over) US$1,169 Unlimited, unrestricted access at Whistler Blackcomb
Epic Pass, young adult (18 to 30) or teen (13 to 17) US$929 The same access
Epic Pass, child (5 to 12) US$599 The same access
Whistler Blackcomb Day Pass, adult, 4 days, without peak dates C$517, or C$130 a day Adding peak dates adds C$93 to the total
Single-day lift ticket, adult up to C$309 a day The highest 7-day-advance price listed this season

The Epic Pass also lists five consecutive days at Hakuba Valley in Japan, which is useful if you are weighing where to stay in Hakuba for the same winter. Epic Mountain Rewards, included with every Epic Pass, lists 20% off lodging among its benefits; check that your hotel takes part before you count on it.

Whistler Village: the pedestrian centre

Whistler Village is where the restaurants, bars and shops are, with the Whistler and Blackcomb gondolas a short walk away. The hotels here range from a resort at the base of Whistler Mountain to a pod hotel 210 m from the gondolas.

Upper Village and Blackcomb: ski-in, ski-out

Upper Village is the base of Blackcomb Mountain, with two resort hotels. The Fairmont describes itself as ski-in, ski-out, and the Four Seasons runs its ski concierge from the Blackcomb base.

Where to stay in Upper Village

  • Fairmont Chateau Whistler, splurge. 4599 Chateau Boulevard, with 519 guest rooms and suites at the foot of Blackcomb Mountain, which the hotel calls a ski-in, ski-out location. It has slope-side pools, whirlpools, the Vida Spa and an on-site golf course. Choose it for the slopes at the door. Watch out: it is at the Blackcomb base, so Whistler Mountain’s gondola is across the Village, and parking costs extra. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Four Seasons Resort and Residences Whistler, splurge. 4591 Blackcomb Way, with suites that have kitchens and living rooms, a spa, and The Library bar and SIDECUT restaurant. Its ski concierge, at the Blackcomb base, is a five-minute walk or a complimentary shuttle ride from the resort. Choose it for a full-service base with space to spread out. Watch out: check the route from your room to the lifts, because the hotel describes a walk or shuttle rather than skiing to the door. Check rates on Booking.com →

Main Street: the quieter edge of the Village

Three hotels sit on Main Street, at the edge of Whistler Village, and they trade a little walking for kitchens, fireplaces and lower prices. Delta lists its address as 4308 Main Street, near Olympic Plaza.

Where to stay on Main Street

  • Delta Hotels Whistler Village Suites, comfortable. 4308 Main Street, an all-suites hotel with studios, one-bedroom suites and two-bedroom connecting suites, an outdoor pool, a complimentary whirlpool and a fitness centre. Choose it for a suite with a kitchen. Watch out: the minimum age to check in is 19, and parking is listed at $32 a day. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Pinnacle Hotel Whistler Village, good value. 4319 Main Street, with suite-style studios that have a fireplace and a whirlpool or deep soaker bath, many with full kitchens, plus a pool and hot tub and three places to eat, Quattro, Alta Bistro and Alpha Cafe. It lists a seasonal mountain base shuttle. Choose it for a studio with a kitchen and a bath at a lower price. Watch out: parking is $29 plus tax and a pet is $40 plus tax; confirm the shuttle runs on your dates. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Summit Lodge Whistler, good value. 4359 Main Street, which the hotel describes as the more sedate, forested edge of Whistler Village, with Queen Deluxe Studios that have a queen bed and a pull-out sofa bed, a stone-fireplace lobby, and a pet-friendly policy. Choose it for a quieter bed near the Village. Watch out: its page gives no walking distance to the lifts, so check the map before you book. On Booking.com it appears as Summit Lodge Boutique Hotel by Paradox. Check rates on Booking.com →

Creekside: a smaller village and its own gondola

Creekside is the south-side base, which Nita Lake Lodge puts about 5 km south of the main Village. It has a small village of shops, cafes and restaurants, and its own gondola station that uploads Whistler Mountain in winter.

Where to stay in Creekside

  • Nita Lake Lodge, splurge. 2131 Lake Placid Road, Whistler’s only lakeside resort and spa, with studio to two-bedroom suites (the one- and two-bedroom suites have private balconies or patios), free seasonal slopeside ski lockers, and a spa with rooftop hot tubs. Choose it for a quiet lakeside base with the Creekside gondola nearby. Watch out: it is about 5 km from the Village, and parking is $35 a night plus tax. Check rates on Booking.com →
  • Legends Whistler, comfortable. A condominium-style lodge with one- to three-bedroom suites and full kitchens, which its operator, Lodging Ovations, puts only a minute from the Creekside gondola, with heated outdoor pools and hot tubs. Choose it for a family or group suite at the lift. Watch out: Creekside is a smaller village than Whistler Village, so a night out in the Village means a bus or taxi. Check rates on Booking.com →

Legacy Way: the lowest price, outside the Village

  • HI Whistler, budget. 1035 Legacy Way: private ensuite rooms and four-bed shared rooms, a shared kitchen and a cafe, and discounts for HI members, including on the Vancouver to Whistler bus. Choose it for the lowest price. Watch out: it is outside Whistler Village and its page gives no distance, so price the local bus, a single ride of $2.75 and an adult DayPASS of $5.50, into your budget. Check rates on Booking.com →

The taxes

Whistler charges three taxes on a room, and all three show up at checkout rather than in the advertised rate.

Tax Rate Applies to
Provincial sales tax (PST) 8% The room price, including added fees
Municipal and regional district tax (MRDT) 3% The room price, including added fees
Goods and services tax (GST) 5% The room price and fees, not the PST or MRDT

That is 16% of the price before tax. Whistler’s municipality describes its 3% hotel tax as in addition to the 8% PST, and the province says GST is not charged on the PST or MRDT outside the City of Vancouver. The PST and MRDT also apply to fees the hotel adds, such as a resort charge. If your stay runs past 26 days, the PST and MRDT stop for the rest of it.

A worked example, our arithmetic: a C$350 room plus the Hilton’s C$40 resort charge is C$390 a night. PST is C$31.20, MRDT C$11.70 and GST C$19.50, so the night costs C$452.40, which is 16% more than the C$390 shown. See hotel taxes around the world for how this compares, and our Vancouver guide, where the city adds a further 2.5% and GST applies to the provincial taxes.

How to get to Whistler

Whistler is about 120 km from Vancouver along the Sea to Sky Highway, Highway 99. Tourism Whistler puts the shuttle trip at 2.5 to 3 hours depending on traffic and weather, and lists three operators: YVR Skylynx, Whistler Shuttle, and Epic Rides, which runs from downtown Vancouver only.

  • By bus from the airport. YVR Skylynx runs every day of the year; its winter timetable runs from 22 November 2026 to 19 April 2027. The Express buses leave YVR at 10:00 am and 1:30 pm and reach Whistler at 12:15 pm and 3:45 pm without stopping; the standard 7:00 am bus calls at downtown Vancouver and Squamish and reaches Whistler at 10:00 am. Meet at its kiosk in International Arrivals, next to the escalators; the buses use Bus Bay 9. Its price page, which is dated to 21 November, lists YVR to Whistler at $55 one way standard, $65 express, and a first set of skis or a snowboard is free. We could not confirm separate winter fares, so check the price when you book. Whistler Shuttle also serves the airport with hotel drop-off.
  • By car. Winter tires are required on the Sea to Sky Highway from 1 October to 31 March, according to Tourism Whistler. The province accepts tires marked with the three-peak mountain and snowflake symbol or M+S, with at least 3.5 mm of tread, and the route can change quickly from sunshine to snow. Check DriveBC before you leave.
  • Once there. Skylynx drops at the Whistler Gateway loop by the Visitor Centre, and Creekside stops are by reservation only, outside the Legends hotel. Whistler Transit is a single ride of $2.75 or a $5.50 DayPASS for adults.

How to book Whistler

  • Count the tax. Add 16% to the room price, plus 16% of any resort charge, before you compare.
  • Watch the season. Opening day is scheduled for 20 November and there is no published closing date, so for a late-season trip hold a cancellable rate.
  • Check what the room includes. Kitchens, pools, ski lockers, parking and pets are priced differently at each hotel; Aava’s ski lockers are free, Nita Lake’s parking is $35 a night, and Pinnacle’s is $29.
  • Check age limits. Delta requires guests to be 19 to check in, and Pangea requires 18.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Whistler for skiing?

For a first trip, Whistler Village: the restaurants, bars and shops are there, with the Whistler and Blackcomb gondolas a short walk away. Upper Village suits ski-in, ski-out stays at the foot of Blackcomb Mountain, Main Street is a quieter edge of the Village, and Creekside has its own gondola about 5 km south.

When does Whistler Blackcomb open for the 2026/27 season?

Whistler Blackcomb schedules winter opening day for 20 November 2026, subject to weather and conditions. At the time of writing its hours page lists no closing date for 2026/27.

How much is a lift pass at Whistler in 2026/27?

An adult Epic Pass (31 and over) is US$1,169 and gives unlimited, unrestricted access at Whistler Blackcomb; young adults and teens pay US$929 and children US$599. A four-day adult Whistler Blackcomb Day Pass without peak dates was C$517, or C$130 a day, with peak dates adding C$93, and the highest 7-day-advance lift ticket price listed was C$309 a day.

What taxes are added to a Whistler hotel room?

Eight per cent provincial sales tax, a 3% municipal and regional district tax and 5% GST, about 16% of the room price. The PST and MRDT apply to added fees such as a resort charge, and they stop once a guest's stay passes 26 days. For example, a C$350 room plus a C$40 resort charge costs C$452.40 a night, by our arithmetic.

How do you get from Vancouver airport to Whistler?

YVR Skylynx runs a daily bus from the airport; its winter timetable runs from 22 November 2026 to 19 April 2027, with Express buses at 10:00 am and 1:30 pm reaching Whistler in 2 hours 15 minutes. Tourism Whistler puts the shuttle journey at 2.5 to 3 hours, and Whistler Shuttle also serves the airport. Driving needs winter tires on the Sea to Sky Highway from 1 October to 31 March.

Which Whistler hotels are ski-in, ski-out?

Of the 12 in this guide, Fairmont Chateau Whistler describes itself as ski-in, ski-out at the foot of Blackcomb Mountain. Legends Whistler in Creekside is only a minute from the Creekside gondola, and Pangea Pod Hotel is about 210 m from the Village gondolas. The Four Seasons describes a five-minute walk or shuttle to its ski concierge at the Blackcomb base.
