The park is the same, the turnstile is the same, and the official price list has no separate foreigner rate. What differs is the price and the paperwork. On the day we checked, for a Wednesday in mid-December, a one-day adult ticket with the Folk Museum cost ₩67,000 on Lotte World’s own site, ₩49,339 on GetYourGuide and ₩42,000 on KKday, a gap of up to ₩25,000 per adult.

That is a large gap for a theme-park day, and it comes with conditions that matter more than the headline: the cheaper tickets are sold to foreign visitors only, you may need your passport at the gate, and both resellers we could read say the ticket is non-refundable. This guide sets out the figures and the terms, and which seller suits which trip. How to read it:

  • KKday was lowest. Our arithmetic from the table: ₩25,000 less than the official park-plus-museum ticket, about 37 per cent, and ₩22,000 less than the official park-only ticket. KKday’s own page shows the official ₩67,000 as the crossed-out price beside its ₩42,000.
  • GetYourGuide sat between the two. It was ₩17,661 under the official park-plus-museum price and ₩7,339 above KKday. GetYourGuide shows won with a long fraction because it converts from another currency, so check the total at checkout.
  • The official site printed no date calendar. The price page lists one figure per age group, so we did not see a cheaper or dearer December day on the official site. It also lists an “After4” ticket for entry after 4 pm: ₩52,000 adult and ₩38,000 child for the park only. We did not price an evening ticket at any reseller.
  • Promo codes were on offer but not applied. KKday’s page advertised a “USD 25 OFF” banner, a “30% OFF” label and a 5 per cent app code. We did not test them, so the real price at checkout may differ.
  • No foreigner rate on the official page. The English price page lists adult, youth, child and baby fares only. The lower foreigner-only prices we saw were on reseller tickets.

What each seller’s listing says

Lotte World Adventure, direct

The English price page, read on 19 November 2026, lists a 1 Day ticket for the park alone and a “Full day pass” that adds the Folk Museum of Korea. The park-only fares are adult (19 and over) ₩64,000, youth (13-18) ₩56,000, children (36 months to 12 years) ₩49,000 and baby (over 12 months, under 36 months) ₩17,000. The full day pass costs ₩67,000, ₩58,000, ₩50,000 and ₩17,000 for the same four groups. Babies up to 12 months are free. Visitors aged 65 and over pay the child price, and the site says other discounts do not apply to them.

Two terms matter for the buying decision. On the full day pass the page says: “Mobile capture screen not available. You need to bring a real ticket”. And on refunds it says cancellations and refunds are not possible “except on the day of use”. The Folk Museum and the park cannot be refunded separately. You must show an ID or document to verify age for the baby and child rates. The page we read does not mention resellers, and we found no official statement on tickets bought elsewhere.

KKday

Check current prices on KKday →

KKday’s listing is titled “Lotte World Seoul Tickets: 1-Day Pass & Magic Pass” and sells six package options. The 1-Day Pass includes admission to the park, “access to attractions” except certain paid rides and games, and the Folk Museum (last admission 6 pm). The QR code gives direct entry. The listing states: “For foreign travelers only; bring your own passport or Alien Registration Card for park entry”, and that Korean nationals “will be denied entry”. It says “Once completed, every order is final, with no cancellations, changes, or refunds, so confirm your date before paying.” The ticket has no child price: the listing says children under 3 enter free with a passport and that “this discounted product has no separate child ticket”, so we did not assume what a child aged 3 to 12 would pay. The Magic Pass and aquarium or Seoul Sky bundles are different: they are exchanged for a paper ticket at the Group Ticket Counter next to the Wild Tour entrance, and mobile tickets cannot be used for them. Not included: the Fantasy Forest, Game Factory, Mirror Maze, Ghost House, Tomb of Horror and the Ice Rink.

GetYourGuide

The listing is “Seoul: Lotte World Adventure Entry Ticket”, provided by BeMyGuest, and includes access to Lotte World Adventure, access to Magic Island and admission to the Lotte World Folk Museum. Its cancellation line reads “This activity is non-refundable.” It says the ticket is “valid only for international travellers and cannot be purchased by Korean citizens”. Its traveller selector has an adult (13 and over) and a child (3-12) type, and says ages 2 and younger are not permitted on the booking, although the page text also says children under 3 are free. The redemption booth is at the Wild Tour Gate on the opposite side of the Main Gate, B1F, and the page says: “your admission pass must be redeemed by 5.30 pm, except if you choose the direct entry option”. We priced the one option shown for our date.

Klook, Viator, Trip.com

Not read, for the reasons above. Do not assume their terms match the others. In particular, check whether the ticket is a direct-entry QR code or a voucher you exchange at the Wild Tour Gate, whether it is for foreign passports only, and the cancellation line on the date you choose.

The park’s own rules that change the buying decision

  • Age bands, not a family ticket. The official site prices by age: baby (12 to 36 months), child (36 months to 12 years), youth (13-18) and adult (19 and over). A family with a 14-year-old pays the youth price, ₩56,000 for park only. KKday’s single-price ticket does not separate these, and its listing sets the rules by the date of birth on the passport.
  • Baby tickets. The official page prices a baby (12 to 36 months) at ₩17,000 and lists the infant rides that pass covers, such as Kidstoria and Undersea Kingdom. Under 12 months is free.
  • After4. From 4 pm the official site sells a cheaper park-only ticket (₩52,000 adult, ₩38,000 child). We did not find an equivalent evening ticket at the resellers we read.
  • Folk Museum closes earlier. The official page gives admission to the museum as closing at 18:00, so visit it first if your ticket includes it.
  • Paid extras. Admission excludes paid facilities such as game facilities; KKday names the Fantasy Forest, Game Factory, Mirror Maze, Ghost House, Tomb of Horror and the Ice Rink.

Which seller for which trip

  • You are an adult foreign visitor with a fixed date: KKday. It was ₩42,000 for 16 December, ₩25,000 under the official price for the same park-plus-museum day, with a direct-entry QR code and no counter.
  • You want a child traveller on the same booking: GetYourGuide. Its listing has a separate child (3-12) traveller type; we did not read the child price, so check it at checkout. The adult ticket was ₩49,339.
  • You already have a reseller account. Our Klook, Viator and GetYourGuide comparison explains how they differ.
  • Before you pay, either way: both reseller tickets are for foreign passport holders (KKday and GetYourGuide say Korean nationals are turned away at the gate) and both are non-refundable.

Five checks before you pay

  1. Same date, same inclusions. The official park-only ticket (₩64,000) is not the same product as the reseller’s park-plus-museum ticket.
  2. Your passport. The reseller tickets are for foreign passports; bring the original.
  3. Direct entry or exchange. A voucher exchanged at the Wild Tour Gate must be redeemed by 5.30 pm on GetYourGuide’s listing; a QR code skips that step.
  4. No refunds. Both resellers we read said so. Look at the weather forecast before you book.
  5. Compare the total in won. Resellers convert from other currencies and may add a fee at checkout.

Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to buy Lotte World tickets from a reseller than from the official site?

For an adult foreign visitor, yes on the date we checked. On 19 November 2026, for Wednesday 16 December, the official park-plus-Folk-Museum ticket was ₩67,000, GetYourGuide showed ₩49,339 and KKday showed ₩42,000. Klook, Viator, Trip.com and Tiqets were not checked. Prices change daily, so compare your own date.

Is there a foreigner price at Lotte World?

The official English price page showed no separate foreigner fare: adult ₩64,000 for the park, ₩67,000 with the Folk Museum. The lower prices we saw were reseller tickets that KKday and GetYourGuide say are for foreign or international visitors only.

Can I cancel a Lotte World ticket?

KKday says every order is final with no cancellations, changes or refunds, and GetYourGuide's listing says the activity is non-refundable. The official site says cancellations and refunds are not possible except on the day of use.

What does a child pay?

The official site charges ₩49,000 for ages 36 months to 12 years (₩50,000 with the Folk Museum). KKday's listing has no child ticket, and GetYourGuide has a child traveller type whose price we did not read. Children under 3 are free on KKday with a passport.

Do I need my passport?

For the reseller tickets, yes. KKday says to bring your original passport or Alien Registration Card, and GetYourGuide says its ticket is valid only for international travellers. The official site asks for ID or a document to verify age for the baby and child rates.

Do I have to exchange a voucher at the gate?

It depends on the ticket. KKday's 1-Day Pass is a QR code that gives direct entry, but its Magic Pass and bundle tickets are exchanged for paper tickets at the Group Ticket Counter next to the Wild Tour entrance. GetYourGuide's listing says the pass must be redeemed by 5.30 pm unless you choose the direct entry option.
