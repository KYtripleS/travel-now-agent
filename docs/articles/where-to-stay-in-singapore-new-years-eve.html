Singapore’s Marina Bay fireworks are a city event whose organiser’s release last year named no tickets, but the hotel bill still carries two extras: a service charge, usually 10%, and 9% GST charged on top of it. Together they add about 19.9% to the headline room rate, and many hotels quote “++” prices that leave both out. The bigger planning question is where the crowd goes. Last year the organisers regulated entry to the waterfront, closed roads around the Bay and warned that some MRT exits would shut, and none of that has been published yet for Thursday 31 December 2026.

This guide covers what the organisers and transport operators published for last year’s countdown, labelled with its year, six areas matched to Marina Bay, the Civic District, the river, Orchard Road, Bugis and Sentosa, 12 hotels, and the terms that catch people out. New Year’s Eve at the Bay: what is published, and what is last year’s

The organiser, the Urban Redevelopment Authority, names each edition for the year it welcomes. The edition that ran on 31 December 2025 was the Marina Bay Singapore Countdown 2026. The one on 31 December 2026 welcomes 2027, and when we checked in November 2026 the organiser’s site still described the earlier edition, so everything below about the Bay is last year’s unless we say otherwise. Check marinabaycountdown.gov.sg before you book anything that cannot be cancelled.

Item Last year (31 December 2025)
Fireworks A show called ELYSIUM: an hour-long score from 11pm in four segments, with 30 minutes of fireworks in total, building to the stroke of midnight
Light shows Brightening Lives on the facade of The Fullerton Hotel, 8pm to 10:30pm from 26 to 31 December; a UOB Plaza 1 projection from 8pm
Festival Togetherland, a Christmas market at the Bayfront Event Space, to 4 January
Admission The release states no admission terms; entry to key areas was regulated and closed at capacity
Cycling Not allowed at key areas around the Bay

The show began an hour before midnight last year, so plan to be in place well before 11pm; the 2026 timing is not published yet. The organiser says it will regulate how many people enter key areas, that those areas close when capacity is reached, that the open-space carpark beside the Bayfront Event Space will be closed, and that certain MRT station exits may close.

Last year’s road closures began early. Bayfront Avenue, Esplanade Drive and part of Fullerton Road closed fully from 6pm on 31 December 2025 to 2am on 1 January, and from 10pm several more roads around Marina Boulevard and Raffles Avenue were partly or fully closed. If you arrive by taxi after 6pm, expect to be set down some way from a Bay hotel.

Last year there were other countdown sites too, so the Bay is not the only choice. The government’s One Countdown release listed Marina Bay, the Singapore Sports Hub in Kallang (a ticketed concert), Sentosa and Clarke Quay, with seven heartland sites for residents.

Getting in from Changi, and home after midnight

From Changi Airport, the MRT reaches the city with a change at Tanah Merah onto the East-West line. Last year SMRT said there would be no extension for the Changi Airport service on New Year’s Eve, so do not count on a late train from the airport on 31 December. Taxis and ride-hailing cars are the fallback, and the closures above apply to them.

Trains were extended last year. SBS Transit extended the Downtown and North East lines and the Sengkang-Punggol LRT by about two hours and fifteen minutes. SMRT’s last trains from City Hall were at 2:15am on the North-South line to Jurong East and on the East-West line to Pasir Ris and Tuas Link,. These are last year’s times; LTA and the operators had not published the 2026 ones when we checked.

The Police warned last year that trains might bypass some stations, such as Bayfront and Stadium, in the event of overcrowding, and that City Hall, Raffles Place, Bayfront, Stadium, Tanjong Rhu and Nicoll Highway stations would be very crowded. They named Esplanade, Promenade, Downtown, Marina Bay, Kallang and Mountbatten as alternatives. Whatever hotel you pick, check which exits are open on the night, and walk to a quieter station.

    Marina Bay: the closest beds to the show

    Marina Bay is where the fireworks happen, so these hotels have the shortest walk and the strictest rules. None of the three below promises a fireworks view from the room in its own words, and the crowd controls may limit how freely guests move, so ask in writing how guests are let through.

    Where to stay in Marina Bay

    How the hotel links work: a hotel’s name goes to its own website. “Check rates” opens it on Booking.com through our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book, at no extra cost to you.

    • Mandarin Oriental, Singapore, splurge. At 5 Raffles Avenue, opposite Esplanade – Theatres on the Bay. Its festive page lists a poolside New Year’s Eve Countdown Party on 31 December 2026, from 10pm to 1am, with prices starting at SGD 148 per person, subject to 10% service charge and prevailing government taxes, and free tickets for guests who reserve Dolce Vita’s New Year’s Eve Night Brunch. Choose it for a hotel party a few steps from the Bay. Watch out: the party is sold separately from the room, and its page does not say the rooms see the fireworks. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Marina Bay Sands, splurge. At 10 Bayfront Avenue, three towers joined at the top by the SkyPark, whose 150-metre infinity pool, 200 metres up, is for hotel guests only. A US$1.75 billion renovation is rebuilding its rooms into about 1,850, nearly 770 of them suites. Choose it for the pool and the skyline. Watch out: Bayfront Avenue closed from 6pm last year, and the New Year’s Eve guide we opened still described last year’s events and said they had finished, so we cannot say what it plans for 2026. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • The Fullerton Hotel Singapore, splurge. 400 rooms in the Fullerton Building at 1 Fullerton Square, completed in 1928 as the General Post Office. Its New Year’s Eve page lists the 2026 programme: a Countdown Party at The Courtyard from 9pm to 1am on 31 December, with a S$118++ cover charge and complimentary access for diners and in-house guests, a six-course dinner at The Lighthouse for S$598++ per person with fireworks over the waterfront, and a dinner feast at Town from S$308++ per adult. Choose it for the most fully published New Year’s Eve programme of the twelve. Watch out: those prices are for dining and parties, not rooms, and Fullerton Road was closed from 9pm last year.     The Civic District: colonial hotels at City Hall

    City Hall is the interchange between the North-South and East-West lines and a short walk from the Esplanade side of the Bay. It is also on the Police’s list of stations expected to be very crowded last year, so plan your return from a different one.

    Where to stay in the Civic District

    • Raffles Singapore, splurge. It opened in 1887 and reopened on 1 August 2019 after a restoration, as an all-suite hotel with 115 suites. Its site has a Festive 2026 section. Choose it for the history. Watch out: price, and we found no New Year’s Eve room terms on its main page. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • Swissôtel The Stamford, comfortable. 1,252 guestrooms and suites at 2 Stamford Road, each with a private balcony, with City Hall MRT at the hotel’s doorstep by its own account. Choose it for a large hotel with the station underneath. Watch out: with 1,252 rooms it is busy on the night, and its main page publishes no New Year’s Eve terms. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Clarke Quay and the river: a countdown of its own

    Clarke Quay was one of four city-centre countdown sites last year. The government’s release said CQ would have live music from local bands and DJs, drone shows over the Singapore River, interactive art, and a signature midnight spectacular. That makes the riverside lively, and loud until late; Robertson Quay upriver is calmer.

    Where to stay at Clarke Quay and the river

    • Grand Copthorne Waterfront Hotel Singapore, comfortable. At 392 Havelock Road, with a riverside restaurant, free parking for in-house guests and newly renovated Grand Deluxe rooms of 26 sq m. Its site lists a Festive 2026 dining programme from 2 November 2026 to 1 January 2027. Choose it for river views at a mid-range price. Watch out: it is at Robertson Quay, not Clarke Quay, and its page states no New Year’s Eve room terms. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • ibis budget Singapore Clarke Quay, budget. A two-star economy hotel at 20 Hong Kong Street, which its own page puts steps from Clarke Quay’s nightlife and dining, with mobile check-in. Choose it for the lowest price beside the river party. Watch out: it is steps from the noise, and its page publishes no New Year’s Eve terms. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Orchard Road: shopping streets, a MRT ride away

    Orchard Road was not among the official countdown sites last year, which is the point of staying here: the hotels are away from the closed roads and the lines of people, and the Bay is a train ride away.

    Where to stay on Orchard Road

    • Goodwood Park Hotel, splurge. A five-star heritage hotel at 22 Scotts Road with 233 guest rooms and suites, two outdoor swimming pools and five restaurants, by its own count. Choose it for a garden-style hotel at the quiet end of Orchard. Watch out: its main page mentions no New Year’s Eve terms, and it is the farthest hotel here from the Bay. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • JEN Singapore Orchardgateway by Shangri-La, comfortable. At 277 Orchard Road, with an award-winning rooftop infinity pool, direct connectivity to public transport. Choose it for an Orchard base with a pool on the roof. Watch out: its main page mentions no New Year’s Eve terms, so confirm stay rules for 31 December. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Bugis and Kampong Glam: lower prices and heritage lanes

    Bugis and Kampong Glam sit north of the Civic District, with the Sultan Mosque and Haji Lane on foot. They are not countdown sites and are served by several MRT stations.

    Where to stay in Bugis and Kampong Glam

    • PARKROYAL on Beach Road, Singapore, comfortable. At 7500C Beach Road, which the hotel puts just steps from Kampong Glam, with a current offer that includes a full breakfast with your stay. Choose it for the heritage quarter on your doorstep. Watch out: the offers change, and its page publishes no New Year’s Eve terms. Check rates on Booking.com →
    • ibis Singapore on Bencoolen, budget. In the Bugis district, with three MRT stations, Rochor, Bencoolen and Bugis, all within a nine-minute walk by the hotel’s own account. Choose it for the lowest price in the middle of the city. Watch out: its rooms page carried a notice about guest-lift upgrade works on weekdays and Saturdays, and says the hotel becomes non-smoking from 1 November 2026. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Sentosa: a beach countdown of its own

    Sentosa had its own official countdown last year, at Siloso Beach, with live performances including Mediacorp’s countdown show and fireworks, plus a movie marathon at Palawan Kidz City Rooftop, a carnival at Palawan Green and installations at Sensoryscape, by the government’s account. It is a separate evening from the Bay. We found no official statement of late-night transport to and from Sentosa for last year, so ask the hotel how you will get back.

    Where to stay on Sentosa

    • Amara Sanctuary Sentosa, comfortable. At 1 Larkhill Road, a resort on a former British military outpost that keeps its historic architecture and grand colonial-style accommodation, with views of the South China Sea. Choose it for a quiet island base. Watch out: you will be away from the Bay and must plan the journey back, and its main page publishes no New Year’s Eve terms. Check rates on Booking.com →

    Singapore’s hotel charges: service charge and GST

    Charge Rate in 2026
    Service charge Typically 10%, added by many hotels
    GST 9%, charged on the total including the service charge
    Combined effect About 19.9% over the headline price

    IRAS, Singapore’s tax authority, says the current GST rate is 9%, that hotels often add a service charge, typically at 10%, and that GST is charged on the total price including that charge. On a room advertised at S$300++, that is S$30 of service charge, then S$29.70 of GST on S$330, for S$359.70 in all (our arithmetic from the published rates). Mandarin Oriental states that its prices are subject to the 10% service charge and prevailing government taxes. Compare totals, not headline rates.

    How to book New Year’s Eve in Singapore

    • Wait for the organiser, but not for long. The 2026 arrangements were not published when we checked. Book a room you can cancel, then confirm closures and station exits when the URA and LTA publish.
    • Read what is a room and what is a party. Mandarin Oriental’s countdown party and The Fullerton’s Courtyard party are priced separately from rooms, and The Fullerton gives diners and in-house guests free entry to its Courtyard party.
    • Count the ++. Add the 10% service charge and 9% GST before you compare.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where is the best place to stay for Singapore's New Year's Eve fireworks?

    Marina Bay has the shortest walk to the show: the Mandarin Oriental, Marina Bay Sands and The Fullerton. Last year the organiser regulated entry to key areas around the Bay, closed them at capacity, closed roads from 6pm and warned that some MRT exits would shut, so a hotel on the Bay does not by itself promise a view. The Civic District, with Raffles and Swissôtel The Stamford at City Hall, is the next closest.

    What time are the Marina Bay fireworks on New Year's Eve?

    The 2026 arrangements were not published when we checked in November 2026. For New Year's Eve 2025 the URA's release described ELYSIUM as an hour-long score from 11pm with 30 minutes of fireworks in total, and the government's One Countdown release said the Bay would culminate at the stroke of midnight. Check marinabaycountdown.gov.sg for the 2026 times.

    Do the MRT and buses run late on New Year's Eve in Singapore?

    Last year they did, with exceptions. SBS Transit extended the Downtown and North East lines and the Sengkang-Punggol LRT by about two hours and fifteen minutes, and SMRT's last trains from City Hall were at 2:15am on the North-South and East-West lines. SMRT said there was no extension for the Changi Airport service. The Police said trains might bypass Bayfront and Stadium in overcrowding. The 2026 timings had not been published when we checked.

    Are there other New Year's Eve countdown sites in Singapore besides Marina Bay?

    Last year the government's One Countdown release listed four city-centre sites: Marina Bay, the Singapore Sports Hub in Kallang (a ticketed concert), Sentosa at Siloso Beach, and Clarke Quay, plus seven heartland sites for residents. The 2026 list had not been published when we checked.

    How much extra do Singapore hotels charge on top of the room rate?

    Many hotels add a service charge, typically 10%, and 9% GST is charged on the total including that service charge, about 19.9% over the headline price. On a room advertised at S$300++ that is S$30 of service charge and S$29.70 of GST, S$359.70 in all (our arithmetic from the published rates). Compare totals, not headline rates.

    Do I need to fill in anything before arriving in Singapore?

    Yes: the SG Arrival Card, which ICA says is free and must be submitted within three days, including the day of arrival, before you reach Singapore, through its e-service or the MyICA app.
