Four things separate one Dubai desert safari from the next, and the headline price is only one of them: whether the vehicle is shared or private, whether dinner and the camp are in the price, what a health or age rule rules you out of, and how late you can cancel. For the same Saturday evening, the shared dune-bashing-and-barbecue tours we read run from about $27 to $55 a head, while a private vehicle, an overnight tent or a conservation-reserve dinner runs from about $139 to over $500.

Two things to know before you pick. Almost every safari listing says it is not suitable for pregnant women and for people with back problems, and some add heart conditions, mobility impairments and very young children. Dune bashing is a rough ride in a 4x4, so read that line on the listing you choose. And several listings say that during Ramadan the live shows, music and alcohol are not offered, which matters for any trip that runs into early 2027.

Health, age and pregnancy rules

These come from the listings, and they differ by operator:

  • Pregnancy. Tiqets (Al Khayma), KKday and the Viator evening listing say not recommended; GetYourGuide’s Red Dune listing says not suitable for pregnant women; Viator’s heritage safari says not recommended in the third trimester. If you are pregnant, the heritage drive is the one whose wording is narrowest, but read it with your doctor’s advice.
  • Back, heart and mobility. GetYourGuide’s Red Dune listing lists back problems, mobility impairments and heart problems as not suitable; Tiqets’ premium and KKday mention back problems.
  • Children. GetYourGuide says under 3 is not suitable; KKday says under 4 is free with no seat; Viator says infants sit on laps.
  • Can I skip the bashing? Viator’s evening listing says yes: wait at the meeting point and rejoin the group before camp.
  • Quad bikes are usually extra. Viator’s quad option describes a 30-minute ride in a fenced area.

Pickup, private or shared, alcohol and dinner

  • Pickup. Viator offers free pickup from hotels and residences within Dubai city limits. KKday names Deira, Bur Dubai, Sheikh Zayed and Marina. GetYourGuide covers Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman. Pickups are roughly 2:00 to 3:00 pm for evening tours.
  • Private or shared. Private is a separate fare: $139 a head on Viator, a per-vehicle price on GetYourGuide, and $555.49 a head for a private heritage car. On GetYourGuide the private-vehicle dinner tour still uses a shared camp.
  • Alcohol. The listings we read treat it as an extra or not included: Viator lists alcoholic drinks as available for purchase; Tiqets’ premium lists alcohol as not included.
  • Dinner. Shared tours serve a barbecue buffet with vegetarian options (Viator, Tiqets). The premium Al Khayma tour offers three buffets; the luxury listing a five-course dinner.
  • Ramadan. Tiqets (Al Khayma) says no live entertainment or alcohol in Ramadan and religious holidays; KKday says no belly dancing, music or alcohol during Ramadan; the Tiqets evening listing marks its belly dance show as unavailable in Ramadan days. Ramadan falls in early 2027, inside this season, so if you travel then, expect a quieter camp evening.

Morning, overnight and premium: who each suits

Morning tours start around 7:00 am (GetYourGuide) and last about four hours, so you are back before the afternoon. They have no dinner or camp. Evening tours run about six to seven hours, with the dinner and shows. Overnight adds stargazing, a night in a tent and a sunrise camel ride; it suits people who want more than a few hours in the dunes. Premium camps and the conservation-reserve tours cost more because the groups are smaller (maximum 5 or 6 on the Tiqets listings) and the dinner is larger.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which seller is cheapest for a Dubai evening desert safari?

    On 12 December 2026 the lowest dollar price we read was $26.72 on Tiqets for the Red Dunes evening safari with dinner. KKday showed €24.51 in euros. Viator was $55 and GetYourGuide $40.57 for comparable shared tours.

    Can pregnant women do a desert safari?

    The listings say it is not recommended or not suitable: Tiqets (Al Khayma), KKday and Viator's evening tour say not recommended, GetYourGuide's Red Dune tour says not suitable, and Viator's heritage safari says not recommended in the third trimester.

    Is a private desert safari worth it?

    It costs more: $139 a head for two adults on Viator, against $55 for the shared tour, and $555.49 a head for a private heritage car. It suits groups who want their own pace; shared tours already include dinner.

    Is alcohol included?

    Not in the listings we read. Viator lists alcoholic drinks as available for purchase and Tiqets' premium tour lists alcohol as not included.

    What changes during Ramadan?

    KKday says the belly dancing show, music and alcohol are not available, while dinner continues. Tiqets says no live entertainment or alcohol during Ramadan and religious holidays.

    Can I do a desert safari overnight?

    Yes. GetYourGuide's overnight safari was $189 a head for 12 December in a private air-conditioned tent, with dinner, stargazing and breakfast, for up to 15 people.
