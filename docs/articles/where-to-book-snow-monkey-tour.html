The same coach day to Jigokudani Yaen-koen, the snow monkey park in Nagano, is sold at $108 on GetYourGuide and $118.40 on Viator for Saturday 12 December 2026, and both listings name the same operator, H.I.S. What differs is everything around the park: whether the day adds Zenkoji Temple or a sukiyaki lunch, where you board, how late you get back, and how long you can cancel for. KKday sells a different day, with Zenkoji and a lunch break on your own, for ¥27,000.

One fact shapes the choice more than price. The park says the monkeys are wild and may not appear at all on some days, so no listing can promise a sighting, and a winter coach day can lose time to snow on the road. That makes the cancellation line worth more than a few dollars.

What the park itself says

These are the park’s own rules; we read them on 21 November 2026 at the park’s English site.

  • Opening hours. The winter season, November to March, runs 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and the park asks visitors to arrive 30 minutes before closing. It has no closing days, but may shorten hours or close without notice because of weather, snow monkey behaviour or disaster.
  • Wild monkeys. The park says its monkeys may not show up at all on certain days, and that they do not bathe at all hours or all year. It publishes a weekly appearance forecast and a live camera.
  • Entry fee. The guide page lists ¥800 for adults and ¥400 for children aged 6 to 17, with children under 6 free if accompanied. It also says admission fees are revised from 1 December 2026; the notice page did not display the new amounts when we opened it, so the ¥800 in the table may not apply on 12 December. Every tour above lists park entry as included.
  • Winter entry. The park’s homepage says that from December to April priority for entry to the garden is given to those who bought tickets online. None of the listings we read explains how that rule applies to a coach group, so ask the seller if your day is a busy one.
  • The path. The park describes the Yumichi Natural Trail from Kanbayashi Hot Springs as about 2 km and about 30 minutes, mostly flat with a few hilly sections. It lists no wheelchairs or carts because of unpaved roads, uneven surfaces and steps, and no pets of any kind.
  • Clothing. It recommends rubber or snow boots in winter and suggests buying simple crampons in advance. Flash is allowed, but drones, selfie sticks and cameras in the bath are not, and bathing with the monkeys is prohibited.

How the days differ

Transport and time. Every Tokyo day here is a coach: roughly 3.5 hours each way on the H.I.S. day, 13 hours door to door on both Tokyo options. That is a long day for a short visit, which is why both the GetYourGuide review and the Viator listing mention rushing and long drives. The Nagano-start tour trades the long drive for 8 hours, but you must be at Nagano Station on time.

Walk on snow. Listings put the walk from the parking area at 30 minutes or more in each direction, and the park’s own figure for the trail is about 2 km. Treat that as an hour of walking on packed snow or ice, and cut the margin if you are slow on ice.

Zenkoji and the extras. KKday and the Nagano-start Viator day include Zenkoji; the H.I.S. day on GetYourGuide and Viator does not, but includes a sukiyaki lunch and a fruit-farm stop. Shibu Onsen is not a fixed stop on any listing we opened.

Children. The park charges children aged 6 to 17 a lower fee and lets under-6s in free. KKday charges the adult fare from age 3 because the coach needs a seat. Viator’s Nagano day takes 33% off ages 6 to 12 and nothing for 0 to 5.

Four checks before you book

  1. Which day do you want? The H.I.S. lunch day for the lowest price, KKday for Zenkoji from Tokyo, the Nagano-start day if you are already in Nagano or Hakuba.
  2. How long can you cancel? 24 hours on GetYourGuide and Viator, 4 days on KKday.
  3. Does your group fit? KKday needs 7 travellers; with fewer it cancels.
  4. Have you packed for ice? Snow boots or crampons; the park says so itself.

You can see the H.I.S. snow monkey day on GetYourGuide, open the Viator version, or look at KKday’s Zenkoji day. If you will stay in Nagano or Hakuba afterwards, where to stay in Hakuba covers the winter bases, and where to stay in Tokyo covers the other end of a day that leaves at 8am.

Frequently asked questions

Is a snow monkey sighting guaranteed on a day tour?

No. The park says its monkeys may not show up at all on certain days, and GetYourGuide's listing says sightings in the springs are not guaranteed. Cancellation terms matter for that reason.

Which seller is cheapest for the Tokyo coach day?

On 12 December 2026 GetYourGuide showed $108 and Viator $118.40 for the H.I.S. day with a sukiyaki lunch. KKday sells a different day with Zenkoji, at ¥27,000.

Is park entry included in the price?

On every listing we opened it was. The park's page lists ¥800 for adults and ¥400 for ages 6 to 17, and says fees are revised from 1 December 2026.

How long is the walk to the park?

The park describes the Yumichi Natural Trail as about 2 km and about 30 minutes. Listings say 30 minutes or more from the parking area. Bring snow boots or crampons.

What are the opening hours in winter?

9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. from November to March, with arrival 30 minutes before closing requested. The park may shorten hours or close without notice.

Can children come, and what do they pay?

The park charges ages 6 to 17 less and lets under-6s in free. KKday charges the adult fare from age 3, and Viator's Nagano day takes 33% off ages 6 to 12 and nothing for ages 0 to 5.
