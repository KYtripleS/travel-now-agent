Buy from KKday. A Tokyo Disneyland or Tokyo DisneySea ticket is the same gate whoever sells it, and the official price is not one price: it is set date by date. On Tuesday 8 December 2026 the official adult 1-Day Passport was ¥10,900; on Saturday 12 December it was ¥12,400. What differs between sellers is therefore less the headline figure than the rules around it: who lets you cancel, and how far ahead you can buy.

On the one date we priced, KKday matched the official ¥10,900 to the yen, with free cancellation up to 3 days before; Disney’s own tickets can be changed but not refunded. GetYourGuide priced the same ticket in US dollars only (US$80.02), with free cancellation up to 14 days before (details below).     What each seller’s listing says

    Tokyo Disney Resort, direct

    Tickets are sold on the official website and in the Tokyo Disney Resort App. Disney’s rules, read on 16 November 2026:

    • Release date. “Tickets will be sold daily from 2:00 p.m. for admissions on the same date two months later.” Our arithmetic: on 16 November the latest date on sale is 16 January 2027.
    • Variable pricing. “Ticket prices are based on variable pricing.” The calendar shows the adult price under each date, and the page says prices may change without notice.
    • Limits. Up to ten park tickets per online purchase. Sales for a ticket type can be suspended “without notice” if the daily limit is reached, and sold-out tickets “may become available for purchase again”.
    • Age bands and lowest prices. Adult is ages 18 and over, junior 12 to 17 and child 4 to 11, with ages 3 and under free. The lowest 1-Day Passport prices in the eligible period are ¥8,900 for an adult, ¥7,400 for a junior and ¥5,300 for a child.
    • Refunds and changes. “Refunds and cancellations of Park tickets due to guests’ personal reasons are not accepted.” Changes to the date or age category are free and can be made while the ticket is valid, but a change to another park or ticket type is not accepted. A ticket bought online is valid for one year from the purchase date, and you cannot move to a date that is sold out.

    Rules that change the buying decision

    Buy only from a seller Disney names. Disney warns that Park tickets sold at unofficial vendors, online reseller shops, auctions or ticket brokers “may be invalid”, and that the holder of an invalid ticket is refused entry with no refund. Its own Park Guide, read on 16 November 2026, lists the online travel agencies where Park tickets may be bought, and KKday, Klook, GetYourGuide and Viator are all on it (with Agoda, Booking.com, Trip.com and others). The same page says only 1-Day Passports are sold on those websites, which is why the After 3 and After 5 Passports appear only on Disney’s own site.

    Two months ahead, not a year. Disney’s sales schedule lets you buy a date only two months out, 2:00 p.m. Japan time. If you are planning further ahead, the date is not on sale on the official site yet; check any earlier listing’s terms carefully. Disney’s purchase page says “Fixed-date tickets for up to two month ahead can be purchased.”

    Sold-out marks mean less than you think. The calendar shows an “X” for tickets unavailable, but Disney’s FAQ says that a ○ or △ mark means some ticket type is still on sale, not necessarily yours: “Even while the ○ or△ mark is shown in the calendar” you can get a “Tickets unavailable” message. Disney’s advice is to change ticket type or date, or check again later.

    No refunds from Disney, free changes instead. Disney does not accept cancellations or refunds for personal reasons, and that applies “even in the event of inclement weather or earthquakes”. A partner package with free cancellation, such as KKday’s or GetYourGuide’s, is the way to get money back. Disney’s page says online changes can be made “only for those purchased through the Tokyo Disney Resort Official Website”, so a reseller ticket does not get the free date change.

    Disney Premier Access is sold in the app only. Disney’s page says it is “Available for purchase on the Tokyo Disney Resort App” and that “The fee above does not include the cost of a Park ticket”. Each park ticket can buy one, and it cannot be shared or given to someone else. On the day we read the page, prices were ¥1,500 for attractions such as Splash Mountain and Big Thunder Mountain and ¥2,500 for Enchanted Tale of Beauty and the Beast; the Christmas show “Toys Wondrous Christmas!” was ¥3,500 for 11 November to 25 December. The ticket seller does not matter for this, as long as you can scan the ticket into the app.

    One ticket, one park. The official page says the 1-Day Passport lets you enjoy “Tokyo Disneyland or Tokyo DisneySea for one day”. There is no multi-day passport: Disney’s FAQ says to buy tickets for each day.

    Which seller for which trip

    • A date you are sure of, in yen: KKday. It showed ¥10,900 for 8 December, the same as Disney’s figure, and its calendar matched Disney’s ¥12,400 for Saturday 12 December.
    • A date that might move: KKday or GetYourGuide. KKday’s Early Bird package said free cancellation up to 3 days ahead; GetYourGuide’s said up to 14 days (24 November for an 8 December visit).
    • A ticket marked “Official ticket”: GetYourGuide. Its calendar showed nothing after 9 December when we looked, so it suits a near date.
    • Seeing the price in another currency before you pay: KKday. It lets you switch currency on the page; confirm the total at checkout.

    Five checks before you pay

    1. Same date, same park, same age band. A junior or child price is lower than adult.
    2. Which park. The 1-Day Passport is one park; confirm Tokyo Disneyland or Tokyo DisneySea on the booking.
    3. The cancellation date. For 8 December, GetYourGuide’s rule meant 24 November.
    4. Date change vs cancellation. Disney’s own tickets: change free, no refund. KKday and GetYourGuide: free cancellation windows, as above.
    5. Whether you want Premier Access. If so, install the app and plan to scan the ticket in.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where should I buy Tokyo Disney Resort tickets?

    KKday. On 16 November 2026, for Tuesday 8 December, it showed ¥10,900, the same as the official adult 1-Day Passport, with free cancellation up to 3 days before. GetYourGuide showed US$80.02, in dollars only, with free cancellation up to 14 days before. Klook and Viator were not priced. Prices change daily, so the figure at checkout for your date is the one that counts.

    How far ahead can I buy Tokyo Disneyland tickets?

    On the official site, tickets go on sale daily at 2:00 p.m. for admission on the same date two months later. On 16 November the latest date on sale was 16 January 2027, by our arithmetic.

    Why does the ticket price change by date?

    Disney uses variable pricing. In December 2026 the adult 1-Day Passport was ¥10,900 on most weekdays, ¥11,900 on Sundays and ¥12,400 on Saturdays (¥11,900 on some holiday dates), with the lowest price in the eligible period at ¥8,900.

    Can I get a refund on a Tokyo Disney ticket?

    Yes, with a partner listing that offers it: KKday's Early Bird package said free cancellation up to 3 days ahead and GetYourGuide's listing up to 14 days ahead. Disney's own tickets are not refunded for personal reasons, even in bad weather, though the date can be changed at no fee.

    Is a ticket from KKday, Klook, GetYourGuide or Viator valid?

    All four are on the list of online travel agencies in Disney's own Park Guide, read on 16 November 2026. Disney warns that tickets from sellers outside its official channels, such as auction sites and ticket brokers, may be invalid.

    Can I buy Disney Premier Access from a reseller?

    No. Disney's page says it is available on the Tokyo Disney Resort App, costs extra and is not included in a park ticket. Each park ticket can buy one.

    Do children need a ticket?

    Children aged 3 and under are admitted free. Child is ages 4 to 11, junior 12 to 17 and adult 18 and over, with a lowest price of ¥5,300 for a child.
