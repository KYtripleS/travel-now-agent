Hotels around Times Square, with prices

The map shows the hotels and apartments around Times Square that booking sites such as Booking.com and Expedia list, with nightly prices; our own picks follow further down. It comes from our partner Stay22, which pays us a commission if you book through it.

Times Square’s New Year’s Eve is free, needs no ticket and is open to anyone who gets there early. On the Times Square Alliance’s published arrangements for its last edition, the viewing areas open at 3pm, first come, first served, and the Ball starts its 60-second descent at 11:59pm. That makes the hotel a question of logistics as much as comfort: inside the closed streets it is your warm room, and outside them it is where you walk back to after midnight. Some hotels sell the view as a room type, with a minimum stay to match: the New York Marriott Marquis’s Ball Drop View Room offer needs three to four or more nights for any stay between 25 December and 1 January.

This guide covers what the Alliance has and has not published for Thursday 31 December 2026, four areas matched to how close you are to the closed streets, 12 hotels, and the terms that catch people out. For the rest of your trip, where to stay in New York covers six areas and 12 hotels, four of which also appear here. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in October 2026. No hotel pays to appear here.

31 December 2026: what the Alliance has published

The Alliance has not yet published the 2026 timetable or performers. In October 2026 its FAQ and its live schedule still describe the 31 December 2025 edition, which ran from 6pm to 12:15am. Last year’s pattern is the best guide to the shape of the night. The Alliance’s FAQ says the Ball is best seen along Broadway from 43rd to 50th Street and along Seventh Avenue as far north as 59th Street. Check timessquarenyc.org/nye before you go.

4am Seventh Avenue closes to vehicles from 42nd to 48th Street, with the side streets from 43rd to 48th closed between Sixth and Eighth Avenues

The rules matter as much as the times. The Alliance says no tickets are needed and there are no public viewing spaces for sale, and it warns that passes promising access to several venues were refused at police checkpoints in past years. All bags are searched, and backpacks, large bags, umbrellas, folding chairs, picnic blankets, large coolers, alcohol and drones are not permitted; public drinking is illegal in New York City. There are no portable toilets and no licensed food or drink vendors. If you leave your viewing area, you will not be able to return to your spot. Once the streets close, no one may cross Broadway and Seventh Avenue, so you enter from Sixth Avenue if you are east of them and from Eighth Avenue if you are west.

For a hotel inside the closed streets, the Alliance says to contact the business for instructions on reaching its entrance. None of the hotels below published how guests are admitted through the checkpoints, so ask in writing before you book.

Inside the closures: the Ball from your room

Staying here means you watch from a room instead of a pen, and the Alliance lists the businesses with a view of the Ball “from portions of their building”: the Renaissance New York, the Marriott Marquis and The Knickerbocker are on it, along with the Hyatt Centric and Tempo by Hilton at TSX Broadway. A view from part of a building is not a view from your room, so ask which rooms face the square.

Brooklyn: the quiet option

Across the East River none of the Times Square closures apply, and the skyline is the view. It is a subway ride from Times Square, and you will not see the Ball from here. We found no organiser announcing midnight fireworks in Central Park or Prospect Park for 2026, so we have not planned the night around either.

Getting around on the night

  • Take the subway, and plan the exit. The Alliance says public transport is the best way to Times Square, and that in the past the MTA has advised revellers not to use the 42nd Street station; exit at a surrounding station and walk in.
  • Last year’s MTA notice is the latest published. On 31 December 2025 the subway ran a weekday schedule, with extra service on the C, D, G, L, N, Q, R and 1 to 7 lines and the 42 St Shuttle from the evening until the early morning; the A ran express until about 2:30am, and the 42 St Shuttle ran all night. Uptown 1 trains skipped 50 St, and N, Q, R and W trains skipped 57 St–7 Av and 49 St, from 11:45am to 12:15am. Street exits at Times Sq–42 St were closed, and the passageway to 42 St–Bryant Park was one-way from 2pm. The 2026 notice was not yet posted when we checked; look at mta.info before you go.
  • The fare is $3 a ride on the subway and local buses in 2026, paid by tapping a contactless card or phone.
  • Leave the car. Sixth and Eighth Avenues close to vehicles as well as pedestrians, and the Alliance says police close the streets northward from 43rd Street as revellers arrive.
  • Sign up for alerts. Last year the Alliance’s FAQ pointed to a city text service for the night, keyword NEWYEARSEVE to 692692; check its page for 2026.

New York’s hotel tax

Three taxes and a fee apply to every hotel room in the city:

Charge Rate
New York State and City sales tax 8.875% of the room rate
New York City hotel room occupancy tax 5.875% of the room rate
City occupancy charge, rooms of $40 or more $2 a room a night
New York State hotel unit fee $1.50 a room a night

Together that is 14.75% of the room rate plus $3.50 a room a night. By state rule the hotel must show the taxes separately on your bill, so compare totals, not headline prices. As our arithmetic, a $300 room costs $347.75 a night, so a four-night minimum stay at $300 a night comes to $1,391 before any resort or service fees.

How to book New Year’s Eve in New York

  • Check the minimum stay first. The Marriott Marquis’s Ball Drop View Room offer needs three to four or more nights for stays between 25 December and 1 January, and 31 December cannot be your check-in date. The other hotels here publish no New Year’s Eve minimum that we found, so ask.
  • Read the cancellation terms before you pay. Under the Marquis’s offer, cancelling within 14 days of arrival forfeits the full stay. The Knickerbocker’s party tickets are separate from a room, and nonrefundable; on 22 October its Silver tier was already sold out.
  • Book a room that faces the square. The Alliance’s list says “portions” of a building have a view of the Ball. Ask for the room, not the hotel.
  • Ignore passes that promise Times Square access. The Alliance warns that multiple-venue passes were refused at police checkpoints in past years, and that some sold on the street were counterfeit.
  • Book as early as your plans allow. We can’t tell you when each hotel sells out; the only data points we found are the sold-out party tier and the Marquis offer’s date window.
  • Compare totals. Our hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and hotel taxes around the world puts New York’s 14.75% beside other cities.

Frequently asked questions

What time is the Times Square Ball Drop on New Year's Eve 2026?

The Times Square Alliance has not yet published the 2026 timetable. Last year's edition ran from 6pm to 12:15am, with the Ball raised at about 6pm and the 60-second descent starting at 11:59pm. The viewing areas opened at 3pm.

Do you need tickets to watch the Ball Drop in Times Square?

No. The Alliance says the event is free, open to everyone on a first-come, first-served basis, and that there are no public viewing spaces for sale. It warns that passes promising access to several venues were refused at police checkpoints in past years.

Where is the best place to stay for New Year's Eve in Times Square?

Inside the closed streets if you want to watch the Ball from your room: the Alliance lists the Renaissance New York, the Marriott Marquis and The Knickerbocker among the buildings with a view of it. If you would rather stay outside the closures, east of Sixth Avenue, west of Eighth Avenue or Brooklyn all work, with the viewing-area access points on Sixth and Eighth Avenues.

Do New York hotels have minimum stays over New Year's Eve?

Some do. The New York Marriott Marquis's Ball Drop View Room offer needs a minimum of three to four or more nights for stays between 25 December and 1 January, and 31 December is not an eligible check-in date. The other hotels in this guide publish no New Year's Eve minimum that we found, so ask before you book.

Can you leave Times Square and come back on New Year's Eve?

Not to the same spot. The Alliance says that if you leave your viewing area you will not be able to return to it. It also says there are no portable toilets or licensed food and drink vendors, all bags are searched, and backpacks, large bags, umbrellas, chairs, picnic blankets, large coolers and alcohol are not permitted.

How does the subway run on New Year's Eve in New York?

Last year the MTA ran a weekday schedule with extra service on the C, D, G, L, N, Q, R and 1 to 7 lines and the 42 St Shuttle, which ran all night. Some trains skipped 49 St, 50 St and 57 St–7 Av from 11:45am to 12:15am, and the street exits at Times Sq–42 St were closed. The 2026 notice was not yet posted; check mta.info.
