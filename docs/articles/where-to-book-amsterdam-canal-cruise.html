Amsterdam gives you two quite different canal cruises in December, and the ticket you want depends on which one you mean. A plain daytime cruise is a 75-minute sightseeing loop of the canal belt, and for Saturday 12 December 2026 the listings we read ask about $14 to $14.71 on Tiqets and GetYourGuide and €13.50 on Viator. A Light Festival cruise is a 75 to 90-minute evening sailing past the festival’s illuminated artworks, and the same Saturday runs from €23.80 to $27.43 depending on the seller and the boat. Two details set the sellers apart more than the headline price: which boat company runs the cruise and whether the sailing is a live guide or an audio guide. And 12 December is one of the festival’s three “Light Nights”, so the evening sailings are busy: the listings we read showed one spot left on Viator’s 5 pm sailing and eight on GetYourGuide’s.

The 2026-27 edition is published. The festival’s own site gives Edition 15 as Thursday 26 November 2026 to Sunday 17 January 2027, with the lights on every day from 4:30 pm to 11:00 pm.

    What the festival’s own pages say about cruises

    Read from amsterdamlightfestival.com on 27 November 2026. The festival names a list of official cruise partners that includes Blue Boat Company and Stromma, among about 45 boat companies; it says official partners carry an Official Partner logo, festival flags on the jetties and stickers on most boats. Tiqets names Blue Boat Company as the provider of its Light Festival listing, and the Viator listing carries the title of a Blue Boat cruise, so those two are the listings that match that list most directly.

    What the pages say that changes how you buy:

    • Dates and lights. Edition 15 runs 26 November 2026 to 17 January 2027. Lights are on daily from 4:30 pm to 11:00 pm, and off at 9:00 pm on 31 December. A morning or early-afternoon canal ticket does not show the artworks.
    • 12 December is a Light Night. The festival lists Light Nights on 26 November, 12 December and 19 December, with extra programme along the route.
    • Sailings and length. Cruises depart every 30 minutes between 4:30 and 9:30 pm and take about 60 to 75 minutes by the festival’s account; the partner listings we read say 90 minutes (Viator, Tiqets) or 75 minutes (GetYourGuide). Be at the departure point 15 minutes ahead.
    • The ticket is for one date and time. You cannot go on another day, and changes are allowed up to 24 hours before departure.
    • Route. The festival says 19 artworks; Tiqets describes 20 and GetYourGuide says more than 20. The route is set and you cannot hop off.
    • On board. Own food and drink are not allowed, and most canal boats have a toilet while most other types do not.
    • Wheelchairs. The festival says Blue Boat Company has some wheelchair-accessible boats and asks you to contact the company a day ahead.

    Light Festival evening or plain daytime: which ticket

    The two products suit different readers, and the price gap is real: on our date the festival cruises we read cost between about €23.80 and $35.20 against $14 to $14.71 for the daytime cruise.

    • The daytime cruise is a 75-minute loop of the canal belt with an audio guide in around 19 to 20 languages, boats leaving every half hour through the day, and the lowest price of anything on this page. It suits a first-time visitor who wants to see the canal houses and bridges in daylight, travelling with children (Tiqets lists activities for kids, GetYourGuide a kids’ audio story), or anyone who wants to choose an hour in the middle of the day.
    • The Light Festival cruise runs only in the evening, past the festival’s artworks, and costs more because it is longer (75 to 90 minutes) and the sailings sell out on busy nights. It suits anyone visiting between 26 November and 17 January who wants the lit route rather than the ordinary canals. Choose between the Blue Boat cruises (Viator, Tiqets) and the Jack Tours listing (GetYourGuide) on three facts: the guide (live on Tiqets, audio-or-live on Viator), the boat (covered and heated on Tiqets, with a drinks option on GetYourGuide), and where it leaves from (Stadhouderskade for Blue Boat, Oosterdokskade 8 for Jack Tours).

    Frequently asked questions

    Are the 2026-27 Amsterdam Light Festival dates published?

    Yes. The festival's site gives Edition 15 as Thursday 26 November 2026 to Sunday 17 January 2027, with lights on daily from 4:30 pm to 11:00 pm and off at 9:00 pm on 31 December.

    What is the cheapest Light Festival cruise for 12 December 2026?

    The lowest figure we read was €23.80 on Viator for the 5:00 pm Blue Boat sailing, against $25.70 on GetYourGuide for the Jack Tours cruise and $27.43 on Tiqets for the Blue Boat cruise. Those are in different currencies, so they cannot be ranked exactly.

    Is 12 December a good night to book?

    It is one of the festival's three Light Nights. On our read, Viator showed one spot left on the 5:00 pm sailing and GetYourGuide showed eight spots left, so book ahead.

    How much is a plain daytime canal cruise?

    For 12 December we read €13.50 on Viator, $14 on Tiqets and $14.71 on GetYourGuide, all for the 75-minute Blue Boat cruise from the Hard Rock Café dock, with an audio guide on the Tiqets and Viator listings.

    Can I cancel?

    Tiqets allows free cancellation and rescheduling until 24 hours before, GetYourGuide free cancellation up to 24 hours before, and Viator free cancellation before 5:00 pm on 11 December for the festival sailing we read. The festival says the date and time on a ticket cannot be changed to another day, and changes are possible up to 24 hours before departure.

    Is there a live guide or an audio guide?

    Tiqets lists a live guide in English and Dutch. GetYourGuide lists a live guide and an English-language tour. Viator's title says "Live Guide" while its description says an audio guide with headphones, so check the option at checkout.
