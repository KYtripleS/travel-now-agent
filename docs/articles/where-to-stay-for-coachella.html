    Coachella is held at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, and the festival’s own hotel and shuttle site gives its 2027 dates as 9 to 11 April and 16 to 18 April. If you are working out where to stay for Coachella, two things shape the bill. The first is the hotel tax: each Coachella Valley city sets its own, from 10% to 13.5% in the cities whose pages we could read, and hotels of 50 or more rooms also sit inside a 3% district assessment. The second is the route, because the festival says its shuttle stops range from about 5 to 25 miles from the polo grounds, so the area you pick sets the length of every ride.

    This guide covers what the festival has confirmed and what it has not, five areas weighed by the trip to the polo grounds, 12 hotels, and the terms that catch people out.     Getting to the polo grounds

    • The Coachella Shuttle (2027). The festival’s hotel site calls shuttles the best choice for getting to and from the festival. They run from 1PM to 8PM to the festival, and return service ends 60 minutes after the music ends. Peak times are 2PM to 5PM going in and 12AM to 2AM coming out. The last stop at the shuttle lot is about a 10-minute walk from the entrance. The shuttle pass is $150 on coachella.com’s passes page, as checked on 30 October 2026, and every festival hotel package includes passes.
    • Which hotels are stops (2027). The list is Hampton Inn & Suites Indio, La Quinta Resort & Club, Embassy Suites La Quinta, Indian Wells Tennis Gardens, Grand Hyatt Indian Wells, JW Marriott Desert Springs, Marriott Desert Springs Villas, Hampton Inn & Suites Palm Desert, Courtyard and Residence Inn Palm Desert, The Shops at Palm Desert, Omni Rancho Las Palmas, Agua Caliente Casino Rancho Mirage, Westin Rancho Mirage and the Palm Springs Convention Center. The Indian Wells Tennis Gardens and The Shops at Palm Desert are marked as official Park and Ride locations. The FAQ says the shuttle is not guaranteed to pick up at your specific hotel, so you may need to walk or take a rideshare to the nearest stop.
    • Rideshare (2026 page). Pick-up and drop-off run Friday to Sunday from 11am to 3am at Avenue 49 and Monroe Street in Lot 1D. The page expects the longest waits between 12am and 2:30am each night. The festival’s FAQ names a different lot for rideshare, Lot 2A, so confirm the lot in the spring.
    • Driving and parking (2026 page). Day parking runs Friday to Sunday from 11am to 2am, is not guaranteed because of capacity, and cars left after 2am are towed. Preferred parking needs a pass, listed at $299 on the passes page for all three days, with no entry after 10pm. Everyone in the vehicle needs a festival wristband.
    • Buses. Coachella lists SunLine Transit, the local bus agency, and Greyhound’s Indio station at 83-100 Indio Boulevard. SunLine’s fares are $1.00 for an adult single ride and $3.00 for a day pass, and its schedules are dated 6 September 2026. We found no festival-night bus service on sunline.org, so treat the bus as a way around the valley, not a plan for the last hour of the night.
    • Camping. On-site car camping is on coachella.com’s passes page at $40 a night plus transient occupancy tax, as checked on 30 October 2026. It is the lowest-priced bed on the grounds.

    Getting in from the airports

    • LAX shuttle (2027). The festival’s hotel site lists four LAX shuttle locations: the Coachella campgrounds, the JW Marriott Desert Springs, La Quinta Resort & Club and the Renaissance Palm Springs. Its FAQ says the drive from Indio to LAX is about 3 hours without traffic, and that LAX shuttles will be available at a later date.
    • Palm Springs International Airport. coachella.com lists Palm Springs, LAX and Ontario as the airports, with Palm Springs at 3400 E. Tahquitz Canyon Way. Omni’s own driving directions from the airport to Rancho Las Palmas add up to about 11 miles. Hilton Palm Springs lists a complimentary airport shuttle on request.

    Coachella Valley hotel taxes in 2026

    Each city sets its own rate, and the polo grounds, the hotels and the airport are spread across them:

    City Hotel tax Added assessment
    Indio 13% for hotels under 50 rooms; 10% for hotels of 50 or more rooms Hotels of 50 or more rooms are levied by the Tourism BID and a Marketing BID; we found the Tourism BID rate (3%) but not the Marketing BID’s
    La Quinta 11% for a group hotel (125 or more rooms, meeting space for 50 or more people and an on-site group sales effort); 10% for all other hotels 3% for hotels of 50 or more rooms
    Palm Desert 11% on the city’s short-term-rental page; we could not confirm the hotel rate from the city’s code 3% for hotels of 50 or more rooms
    Palm Springs 13.5% for group meeting hotels; 11.5% for all other hotels 1% for hotels of 49 rooms or fewer; 3% for hotels of 50 or more rooms
    Rancho Mirage Not confirmed: the city’s site would not open for us 3% for hotels of 50 or more rooms
    Cathedral City 12% on stays of 29 nights or fewer 3% for hotels of 50 or more rooms

    The 3% is the Greater Palm Springs Tourism Business Improvement District, which Visit Greater Palm Springs describes as an assessment on the gross short-term room revenue of hotels with 50 rooms or more, for stays of 27 days or less. It is an assessment on the hotel, not a tax, and the pages we read do not say whether a hotel must list it as a line on your bill, so ask. Cathedral City has no hotel in this guide, and its rate is listed because the city’s own page was readable. We left out Indian Wells, which also has shuttle stops, to keep to the five areas.

    Our arithmetic for a $300 room, if the hotel passes everything on:

    • Indio, hotel of 50 or more rooms: $30.00 plus $9.00, so $339.00 before any Marketing BID. Under 50 rooms: $39.00, so $339.00.
    • La Quinta, hotel of 50 or more rooms: $30.00 plus $9.00, so $339.00, or $33.00 plus $9.00, so $342.00, at a group hotel.
    • Palm Springs, hotel of 50 or more rooms: $34.50 plus $9.00, so $343.50, or $40.50 plus $9.00, so $349.50, at a group meeting hotel. Hotel of 49 rooms or fewer: $34.50 plus $3.00, so $337.50.
    • Cathedral City, hotel of 50 or more rooms: $36.00 plus $9.00, so $345.00.

    Many hotels add a daily resort fee or charge for parking, and those are in the hotel’s own terms, not the tax. The JW Marriott Desert Springs lists a daily resort fee of $45 plus tax.

    Indio: the polo grounds’ own city

    Indio is where the festival is, and its hotels are the closest to the gates after La Quinta’s. The Hampton Inn & Suites is a stop on the 2027 shuttle list, which makes it the most practical Indio base without a car. The tax is 13% or 10%, depending on hotel size, and the festival’s hotel page lists the Fairfield 6.1 miles from the festival.

    La Quinta: the shortest trip

    La Quinta gives the shortest trip on the festival’s own count: La Quinta Resort & Club is listed at 5.3 miles. Two hotels here are shuttle stops, and the resort is also an LAX shuttle location. The hotel tax is 10% or 11%, and a 3% assessment applies to hotels of 50 or more rooms.

    Palm Springs: the town, and a long ride

    Palm Springs is the furthest, at 24.8 and 24.9 miles for the two hotels the festival lists, and a shuttle stop at the Palm Springs Convention Center. Choose it when the weekend is also a Palm Springs trip. The city’s hotel tax is 11.5%, or 13.5% for group meeting hotels, and its page does not say which hotels are group meeting hotels.

    How to book Coachella weekend

    • Choose the weekend first. Weekend 1 is 9 to 11 April 2027 and Weekend 2 is 16 to 18 April 2027. Check-in dates for festival packages are the Thursday or Friday before, with check-out on the Monday.
    • Match the hotel to the shuttle. If you do not have a car, pick a hotel on the 2027 stop list or one within a short ride of it, because the festival says the shuttle is not guaranteed to stop at your hotel.
    • Ask about minimum stays in writing. None of the hotel pages we read lists a festival-weekend minimum. The festival’s own packages are three or four nights, and are non-refundable.
    • Count the tax. Add 10% or 13% in Indio, 10% or 11% in La Quinta, 11.5% or 13.5% in Palm Springs and 12% in Cathedral City, plus any assessment the hotel passes on. For Palm Desert and Rancho Mirage, ask.
    • Add the daily fee and the parking. Parking is a second bill: $25 a night at La Quinta Resort & Club, $30 at the Hilton Palm Springs and $35 at the Renaissance Palm Springs, against free at the Hampton Inns and the Fairfield.
    • Compare totals. Our hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and hotel taxes around the world puts these rates beside other cities.

    Frequently asked questions

    When and where is Coachella 2027?

    The festival's hotel and shuttle site, hospitality.coachella.com, gives the 2027 dates as 9 to 11 April and 16 to 18 April. It is held at the Empire Polo Club, 81-800 Avenue 51, Indio, California. On 30 October 2026 the headers on coachella.com's own pages still showed last year's dates, 10 to 12 and 17 to 19 April 2026.

    Where is the best place to stay for Coachella?

    For the shortest trip, stay in La Quinta: the festival's hotel list puts La Quinta Resort & Club 5.3 miles from the festival, and two La Quinta hotels are 2027 shuttle stops. Indio is the polo grounds' own city and has a shuttle stop at the Hampton Inn & Suites. Palm Desert, Rancho Mirage and Palm Springs are 10 to 25 miles away.

    How do you get to Coachella without a car?

    The Coachella Shuttle runs from 1PM to 8PM to the festival, with return service ending 60 minutes after the music ends, and its pass is $150 on coachella.com's passes page as checked on 30 October 2026. Rideshare pick-up in 2026 was at Avenue 49 and Monroe Street, with the longest waits between 12am and 2:30am. The shuttle is not guaranteed to stop at your specific hotel.

    How much is the hotel tax in the Coachella Valley?

    It depends on the city. Indio charges 13% for hotels under 50 rooms and 10% for hotels of 50 or more, La Quinta 10% or 11%, Palm Springs 11.5% or 13.5% and Cathedral City 12%. Hotels of 50 or more rooms also sit inside a 3% Greater Palm Springs district assessment. We could not confirm Rancho Mirage's hotel rate or Palm Desert's from the cities' own pages. On a $300 room our arithmetic gives $337.50 to $349.50 a night in the cities we could confirm.

    Do hotels near Coachella require a minimum stay?

    None of the hotel pages we read lists a festival-weekend minimum, so ask in writing. The festival's own hotel packages are three nights from Friday or four nights from Thursday, with check-out on the Monday, and they are sold as non-refundable. Most partner hotels require the primary guest to be 21 or over.

    Which hotels are Coachella shuttle stops?

    The 2027 list includes Hampton Inn & Suites Indio, La Quinta Resort & Club, Embassy Suites La Quinta, JW Marriott Desert Springs, Hampton Inn & Suites Palm Desert, Omni Rancho Las Palmas, Agua Caliente Casino Rancho Mirage, Westin Rancho Mirage and the Palm Springs Convention Center, among others. The festival says the shuttle is not guaranteed to stop at your specific hotel.
