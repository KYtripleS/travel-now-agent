Hotels around National Mall, with prices

Washington’s cherry trees have reached peak bloom, by the National Park Service’s count, anywhere from 17 March to 10 April in the 23 years it publishes since 2004, and in 2026 the date was 26 March. Nobody can forecast the 2027 date from here, so the useful question is not which night to book but where to sleep so that an early or a late year still works. The Tidal Basin is on the National Mall, the rest of the city is a Metro ride away, and the Park Service itself tells visitors to come by public transport.

This guide covers the dates to aim for, five areas weighed by their route to the Tidal Basin, 12 hotels to start with, the hotel tax on both sides of the Potomac and the airport links. The hotels are researched picks, chosen for location, what each property publishes and consistent guest feedback, and each was checked as operating in October 2026. No hotel pays to appear here.

When to go: the dates to aim for

The Park Service defines peak bloom as the day when 70% of the Yoshino cherry blossoms around the Tidal Basin are open. Its most likely window is the last week of March to the first week of April, and extreme weather has produced peak bloom as early as 15 March (1990) and as late as 18 April (1958). Here are the last five years from its Bloom Watch table, which is last year’s evidence, not a forecast:

Year Puffy white Peak bloom
2026 22 March 26 March
2025 23 March 28 March
2024 15 March 17 March
2023 18 March 23 March
2022 19 March 21 March

Across the 23 years in the table, 2004 to 2026, our arithmetic gives a median peak-bloom date of 29 March, with the earliest 17 March (2024) and the latest 10 April (2014 and 2015). The Park Service says the trees bloom for a period of several days, that a rainy, windy day can end the display abruptly and that a late frost can prevent blooming; in 2017 a frost on 14 to 16 March cost about half the blossoms. It also says peak bloom is almost impossible to forecast more than ten days ahead, so no 2027 date exists yet.

The National Cherry Blossom Festival has announced its 2027 dates: 20 March to 11 April. Its events calendar lists the opening ceremony on 20 March, the Blossom Kite Festival on 27 March, Petalpalooza at Navy Yard on 3 April, and the Sakura Matsuri street festival and the parade on 10 April. In 22 of the 23 years in the Park Service table, peak bloom fell between 20 March and 10 April, inside the festival window; the exception was 2024. Aim for the last week of March and the first week of April, and stay four or five nights rather than two.

The National Mall and the Southwest Waterfront: the shortest walk

The Park Service puts the Smithsonian station, on the Orange, Blue and Silver lines, about a mile from the Tidal Basin Welcome Area near the Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial, and says the basin’s walkway is 1.8 miles around. To avoid the crowds at the Smithsonian station it suggests walking in from Foggy Bottom, Federal Triangle or L’Enfant Plaza, which adds the Yellow and Green lines. The Southwest Waterfront, with the Wharf’s restaurants and promenade, is served by L’Enfant Plaza, which citizenM says is right by its door. Book early: this is the part of town the blossom crowds head for.

Downtown and Penn Quarter: museums, the White House and the Metro

Downtown, Penn Quarter and Chinatown are the compact core of the city, close to the White House, Capital One Arena and the museums. Federal Triangle, which the Park Service names as a walking starting point, is on the Blue, Orange and Silver lines, and Metro Center, in the middle of downtown, adds the Red Line. This is the best base if the trip is more than the blossom: you can walk to the Mall in the morning and to dinner at night. The Smithsonian’s museums charge no admission, although three locations need a free pass or ticket.

Foggy Bottom and the West End: walking in from the west

Foggy Bottom, around George Washington University, has its own Metro station on the Orange, Blue and Silver lines, and the Park Service names it as a place to start walking to the cherry trees. The West End, north of it, is where the Fairmont stands, near Georgetown’s waterfront. It suits visitors who would rather arrive at the Tidal Basin on foot than by train, and who want a calmer evening than the Mall.

Capitol Hill and Navy Yard: the Capitol and a quieter evening

Capitol Hill is row-house streets around the Capitol and the Supreme Court. Capitol South station, on the Blue, Orange and Silver lines, puts you on the same lines as the Smithsonian station. Navy Yard, to the south-east, is the riverfront by the ballpark, and the festival’s Petalpalooza at Navy Yard is listed on 3 April 2027. This pair suits travellers who plan to spend their days at the Capitol and on the Mall, with the Tidal Basin as one trip by train.

Arlington: Rosslyn and Crystal City

Arlington is in Virginia, across the Potomac, and the reason to consider it is price, which you should compare for your own dates. Rosslyn station is on the Blue, Orange and Silver lines, the same three as the Smithsonian station, so the ride needs no change. Crystal City station is on the Blue and Yellow lines, and Reagan National Airport’s station is on the same two. The trade-off is that you finish your day by train, not on foot, and the Park Service recommends public transport for the blossom anyway.

What the hotel tax adds, in the District and in Arlington

The District’s Office of Tax and Revenue lists one rate for rooms, lodgings and accommodations, and its Notice of Oct. 1, 2025, Tax Changes extends the temporary 15.95% rate through 30 September 2027, which covers a spring 2027 stay:

Place Charge Rate
Washington, DC Sales and use tax on hotel rooms and other transient lodging 15.95% of the room charge
Arlington County, Virginia Transient occupancy tax: 5% county, 0.25% tourism, 3% state regional 8.25% of the room charge

On a $300 room in the District, 15.95% is $47.85, so the room costs $347.85 a night; in Arlington, 8.25% is $24.75, so the same rate costs $324.75 before any other charge. Both figures are our arithmetic, not the cities’. The Arlington page lists only the occupancy tax, so ask the hotel whether anything else is added, and a hotel can add its own fees, so compare the total. The county’s page gives no effective date for its rate, so confirm it with the hotel.

Getting in from the airports, and around

  • From Reagan National (DCA), Metro’s Yellow and Blue lines serve the airport’s station, which Terminal 2 reaches by two enclosed bridges on the concourse level; from Terminal 1, take an airport shuttle bus to the bridges. Weekday daytime fares run from $2.25 to $6.75 depending on distance.
  • From Dulles (IAD), the Silver Line station is about 600 feet from the terminal. The airport states a fare to Metro Center of $6.75 and a ride of roughly 50 to 55 minutes to Rosslyn.
  • In town, Metrorail fares are $2.25 to $6.75 on weekdays between 5 am and 9:30 pm, and $2.25 to $2.50 after 9:30 pm and at weekends; buses are $2.25. Tap the same SmarTrip or contactless card at both ends of a rail trip.
  • To the blossom, the Park Service says parking is extremely limited and warns of severe traffic congestion and road closures. It recommends public transport, with the Smithsonian station as the nearest stop.

How to book for a season that moves

  • Book a refundable rate now for the nights you want, and check each rate’s cancellation deadline; the bloom date will not be known until days before.
  • Stay four or five nights across the last week of March and the first week of April, not two, so that an early or a late year still overlaps with your trip.
  • Count the tax. Add 15.95% to a District room rate and 8.25% to an Arlington one, plus any fee the hotel adds.
  • Check the Metro. Several hotels here do not name a station on their own pages, so look at the walk to the nearest one before you pay for the location.
  • Compare totals. Hotel booking sites compared goes through where to book, and our general guide to where to stay in Washington, DC has more hotels in Georgetown and Dupont Circle.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is peak cherry blossom in Washington, DC?

    The National Park Service puts peak bloom most likely between the last week of March and the first week of April. Its table gives 26 March in 2026, 28 March in 2025 and 17 March in 2024, and its dates since 2004 run from 17 March to 10 April. It says bloom is almost impossible to forecast more than 10 days ahead, so no 2027 date exists yet.

    When is the 2027 National Cherry Blossom Festival?

    20 March to 11 April 2027, according to the festival's website. Its events calendar lists the Blossom Kite Festival on 27 March, Petalpalooza at Navy Yard on 3 April, and the parade and the Sakura Matsuri street festival on 10 April.

    Where is the best place to stay in DC for the cherry blossoms?

    For the shortest route, the National Mall and the Southwest Waterfront, served by L'Enfant Plaza station. Downtown and Penn Quarter suit a first trip with Federal Triangle and Metro Center close, Foggy Bottom suits a walk in from the west, and Arlington, across the Potomac, is on the same Metro lines as the Smithsonian station.

    How far is the Metro from the Tidal Basin?

    The National Park Service says the Smithsonian station is about one mile from the Tidal Basin Welcome Area near the Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial, and the basin's walkway is 1.8 miles around. To avoid crowds it suggests walking in from Foggy Bottom, Federal Triangle or L'Enfant Plaza.

    How much is the hotel tax in Washington, DC and Arlington?

    Washington's rate on rooms is 15.95% according to the Office of Tax and Revenue, which extended it through 30 September 2027: $47.85 on a $300 room, our arithmetic. Arlington County lists an 8.25% transient occupancy tax, which is $24.75 on $300, before any other charge.

    Should I drive to see the cherry blossoms?

    No. The National Park Service says public transport is the best option because parking is extremely limited, and it warns of severe traffic congestion and road closures. Metro fares are $2.25 to $6.75 on weekdays between 5 am and 9:30 pm.
