How to read it:

  • The standard ticket is a three-way tie. At 11:00 AM the official site and Tiqets both showed AED 189, and GetYourGuide’s US$51.46 converts to AED 189 by our arithmetic. At 5:00 PM the official site said AED 219 and Tiqets AED 218.75, a 25-fils rounding difference, not a saving.
  • The evening slots cost more everywhere. The official calendar charged AED 219 for each half-hour slot from 4:30 PM to 7:00 PM and AED 189 either side. Tiqets showed AED 218.75 across the same slots. GetYourGuide’s 5:00 PM price was US$59.63 against US$51.46 at 11:00 AM. Its listing text names 1:00 to 3:00 PM as its peak slots, which does not match the band we saw on the official site and Tiqets, so confirm your slot on the listing.
  • Viator’s from-prices are not slot prices. Its Levels 124 and 125 listing starts at US$47, about AED 173 by our arithmetic and below the official AED 189, but we could not read a price for our date and slot, and the listing is non-refundable (details below). Its Level 148 listing is priced separately.
  • “From” figures are not a quote. For Viator, KKday and GetYourGuide’s SKY ticket we read a starting price, not the price for our slot.

Klook is “not checked” for a plain reason. Its Burj Khalifa listing returned an HTTP 403 error page to our automated browser. We did not try to get round it. We would rather print no number than a guess.

What each seller’s listing says

All of this was read on 17 November 2026.

The official site (At the Top, Burj Khalifa, ticket.atthetop.ae)

The official booking pages list three experiences: Silver (Levels 124 and 125, from AED 189, last entry 8:15 PM), Gold (Levels 124, 125 and 148, AED 399, last entry 7:00 PM) and Platinum (Level 152 to 154 lounge, from AED 769, which we did not price). Gold is described as including priority access with fast-track queues, a multilingual guide, access to the SKY lounge, and Arabic coffee and sweets. It says it does not include canapes or alcoholic beverages.

For cancellation we could not confirm a policy. The site’s general terms say you may be prevented from cancelling or changing an online ticket purchase after confirmation, and its FAQ says there are no refunds or rain checks for bad weather. GetYourGuide and KKday state their cancellation windows below.

Rules from the official site that change the decision

There is no sunset ticket. The FAQ says it has no sunset experience, and recommends buying a Level 124 ticket for entry “approximately 120 minutes prior to sunset time”. With sunset at 5:29 PM on 9 December (timeanddate.com), that points to a slot from about 3:30 PM. In the price band we saw, slots from 4:30 PM cost more, so a 3:30 or 4:00 PM slot at the standard price is the cheaper way to arrive before sunset and stay through it. That is our arithmetic, not an official recommendation, and the band can differ by date.

Weather. The terrace can close in bad conditions, and the FAQ says it gives no refunds or rain checks for inclement weather. Indoor levels stay open.

Bags and children. Large bags, strollers and electronics go to the luggage room near the entrance, and children under 3 enter free.

Time on site. The FAQ puts the average visit at about 1 hour 30 minutes.

Which seller for which trip

  • The standard ticket at the official price: GetYourGuide or Tiqets. Both matched AED 189 at 11:00 AM and about AED 219 at 5:00 PM.
  • A stated refund window: GetYourGuide. It printed “free until Dec 8” for our date, and KKday printed two days ahead.
  • The SKY ticket: Tiqets or GetYourGuide. AED 399 on Tiqets; GetYourGuide started at about AED 400.
  • A fast-track ticket without Level 148: Tiqets. Only Tiqets showed one for Levels 124 and 125, at AED 314.
  • The price in another currency: Tiqets, GetYourGuide or KKday. They let you change the displayed currency, though Tiqets says the currency you are charged in may differ, so confirm the total at checkout.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I buy Burj Khalifa tickets?

GetYourGuide. For Wednesday 9 December 2026 its US$51.46 converts to about AED 189 at 11:00 AM, matching the official price, and US$59.63 (about AED 219) at 5:00 PM; its listing is labelled "Official ticket" and offers free cancellation up to 24 hours ahead. Tiqets matched too, at AED 189.00 and AED 218.75. Viator's Levels 124 and 125 listing started at US$47 but is non-refundable and was not priced for our date. Prices change daily.

How much is At the Top Level 124 and 125?

The official Silver ticket was AED 189 in standard slots and AED 219 in the 4:30 to 7:00 PM slots on 9 December 2026, and GetYourGuide and Tiqets matched those prices. The price depends on the slot you pick.

Is there a sunset ticket for the Burj Khalifa?

No. The official FAQ says there is no sunset experience and recommends a Level 124 ticket with entry about 120 minutes before sunset. Sunset in Dubai on 9 December 2026 is about 5:29 PM, so a slot around 3:30 PM is the official advice; slots from 4:30 PM were in the dearer band.

Can I cancel a Burj Khalifa ticket?

It depends on the seller. GetYourGuide said free cancellation until 8 December for a 9 December visit (up to 24 hours ahead), KKday said up to two days ahead, and the Viator listings we read were non-refundable. The attraction gives no refunds for bad weather.

What does the Level 148 SKY ticket add?

The official Gold ticket at AED 399 adds Level 148, priority access, a guide and refreshments in the SKY lounge to Levels 124 and 125. Tiqets showed AED 399 and GetYourGuide started at about AED 400.

Do children need a ticket?

The official site says children under 3 enter free. We did not check the child price on each reseller's page.
