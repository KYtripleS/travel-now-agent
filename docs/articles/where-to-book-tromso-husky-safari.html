A Tromsø husky safari is not one product, and for Thursday 24 December 2026 the date matters as much as the price. When we looked, the Arctic Adventure Tours self-drive listing on GetYourGuide and Viator was not on sale for that day at all, while Best Arctic’s short self-drive still had 11 places at €259.76 and Tromsø Villmarkssenter’s self-drive had one, at €315.62. The other differences sit in four lines on every listing: how long you are actually on the sled (from 20 to 30 minutes up to 80), who drives, how late you can cancel, and how old you have to be.

Free in the evening? Tromsø northern lights tours compared sets KKday, Viator and GetYourGuide side by side. Need a bed? Where to stay in Tromsø compares the harbour, the centre and two out-of-town lodges.

One practical point: the camps are outside the city, and the transfers the listings state run from 25 minutes to about an hour each way. So the time on the sled is a fraction of the booked duration. Several listings put two people on a sled, one driving and one sitting, which matters if you travel alone.

Which listing is the safe, quick buy

For 24 December it is GetYourGuide’s Easy Dog Sledding Tour & Kennel Visit. It had the lowest price we read for the date (€259.76), two start times and 11 places when every other self-drive we read on GetYourGuide had one place or none. It takes children from 6, you can cancel for free until the day before, and it is a self-drive: two people to a sled, swapping in the middle of the ride. It is a new listing with no reviews yet, run by Best Arctic.

On a date when it is sold, the Self-Drive Husky Dog Sledding Adventure by Arctic Adventure Tours is the one for a longer drive: 70 to 80 minutes and 4.9 from 4,420 reviews, at €325.86 on GetYourGuide for 23 December against €334.27 on Viator. Viator’s next date after Christmas was 3 January 2027.

Where the others win: Husky Sled Self-Drive with Traditional Lunch gives about 45 minutes and a hot lunch on the 24th, if the last place is still there and you are 16 or over. KKday’s Soup & Transfers listing gives a 45 to 60 minute drive on the 24th, instant confirmation and a 2-day cancellation window, for age 12 and over.

Choosing between a passenger ride and driving yourself

  • Who drives. On the self-drive listings you steer after a briefing, usually two people to a sled who swap. On the Villmarkssenter guided listing the musher drives and you sit in the sled. Check this line first if you are travelling alone.
  • Time on the sled. The listings give minutes, not kilometres: from 10 to 15 minutes each way to 70 to 80 minutes in total. The rest of the booked 4 to 7 hours is transfer, kit, the dogs and a hot drink or meal.
  • Children. Minimum ages differ: 4 (Destination Tromsø), 6 (Best Arctic short ride), 7 (Arctic Adventure Tours), 12 (Best Arctic long day) and 16 (Villmarkssenter self-drive). The Villmarkssenter guided listing is the one marked suitable for all ages.

Clothing and the small print

Thermal suits or warm clothes and boots are stated as included on every listing whose inclusions we read; hats, gloves or mittens are included on some and listed as something to bring on others, so read the listing you choose. Missing the meeting time is not refunded on the Arctic Adventure Tours and Villmarkssenter bookings we read.

Frequently asked questions

Which seller is the safest to book for a husky safari in Tromsø on 24 December?

Of the listings we read, GetYourGuide's Easy Dog Sledding Tour & Kennel Visit: €259.76 for one adult on 24 December 2026, two start times with 11 places left, free cancellation until 23 December and a 20 to 30 minute self-drive.

What is the cheapest Tromsø husky safari on 24 December?

The lowest price we read for that date was €259.76 on GetYourGuide for the Easy Dog Sledding Tour & Kennel Visit, with a 20 to 30 minute self-drive; Viator showed €266.46 for the same product.

Do I drive the sled myself?

It depends on the listing. The self-drive listings give you a sled shared with a passenger and you swap; on GetYourGuide's Guided Husky Sledding with Traditional Lunch the musher drives and you sit in the sled.

How long is the ride itself?

The listings state minutes, not kilometres: from 10 to 15 minutes each way up to 70 to 80 minutes, depending on the listing.

Can children go?

Minimum ages run from 4 to 16: 4 on the Destination Tromsø short ride, 6 on the Best Arctic short ride, 7 on Arctic Adventure Tours, 12 on the Best Arctic long day, 16 on the Villmarkssenter self-drive. The Villmarkssenter guided listing is marked suitable for all ages.

How late can I cancel?

GetYourGuide's listings allowed free cancellation up to 24 hours ahead and Viator's the day before; KKday's two listings allow it up to 2 days ahead.
