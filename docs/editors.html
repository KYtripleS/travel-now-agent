Who writes Gently Yonder

Meet the writers

Six pen names, one standard: the answer first, and facts you can check.

Six writers put Gently Yonder together. Casey edits; Martin, Sophie, Mina, Elena and Margaret each take a part of the trip, from the policy wording to the parade route, so every guide is written by the person who cares most about that part.

Casey

Editor

Edits Gently Yonder and writes the hotel and booking guides. Likes a short walk from the station, a clear answer at the top of the page and a price that says what is included.

Martin

Practical travel

Writes the guides you read before you leave home: insurance, money, packing and airports. Reads the policy wording so you can skip to the part that matters.

Sophie

Events and seasons

Covers the dates that fill hotels: carnivals, New Year's Eve, blossom and snow seasons. Starts every guide with when, where and how early to book.

Mina

Cities and food

Writes the first-timer city guides and the food and café pages. Happiest working out which neighbourhood to wander and what to eat there.

Elena

Itineraries

Plans the day-by-day itineraries and the when-to-go guides, with an eye for light, views and the hour when a place is quietest.

Margaret

History and culture

Writes the country profiles, culture and history pieces, and the tax explainers. Reads the sources twice and likes to say what was lost and what survives.

A note on the names

All six names are pen names. Gently Yonder is run by a small team that prefers to stay out of the frame, so we write under pen names: it keeps each voice consistent and the attention on the guides rather than on us. The "I" you read is a narrative voice, a device to keep these guides warm and readable, not a claim about one real person's documented trips. The opinions are genuinely held and, whatever the voice around them, the facts never bend: prices, rules and dates are always verifiable, and every guide lists its sources.

How we work

We read the primary sources first: the aviation authority, the city's tax office, the provider's own terms, the official statistics. We say plainly where our confidence comes from, and we never bend a fact to fit a story. The full version is on our Methodology page, and the standards every article follows are in our Editorial Guidelines.
