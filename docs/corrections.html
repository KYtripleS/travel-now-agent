Corrections

When we get a fact wrong, we fix the guide and record it here: the date, what we said and what is true.

Travel rules and prices change constantly, and sometimes we simply get something wrong. A public log lets you judge how often that happens and how we deal with it. Newest first. It is part of our promise.

2026

27 September: Tokyo to Kyoto by bus

What we said: that the bus costs roughly ¥4,000 to ¥10,000 one way.

What is true: the cheapest seats go lower. On Willer’s booking calendar in late September 2026 the cheapest seat was ¥2,100 on a weekday and ¥4,200 on a Saturday; weekend and premium seats cost more.

Tokyo to Kyoto: Shinkansen, flight or bus

27 September: Japan’s tourist taxes

What we said: that from 2026 the lodging taxes in Tokyo and Osaka also apply to licensed private lodging (minpaku).

What is true: Osaka’s already applied to it. Tokyo’s applies from 1 April 2027, when it also becomes 3% of the room rate on stays of ¥13,000 or more per person per night. The same guide had a photo caption about the tax rather than the photo; it now describes the photo.

Japan’s tourist taxes in 2026

23 September: Renting a boat in Spain

What we said: that in Spain you can rent a boat of under 15 HP without a licence.

What is true: from 1 October 2026, Real Decreto 1188/2025 limits that exemption to private use, so renters need at least the Licencia de Navegación. We also added the small-boat rules for Greece and Croatia.

How to charter a boat for a day

23 September: Tokyo to Kyoto by Shinkansen

What we said: that the Nozomi takes 2.5 to 3 hours.

What is true: it takes about 2 hours 15 minutes.

Tokyo to Kyoto: Shinkansen, flight or bus

23 September: Publication dates

What was wrong: republishing a guide stamped that day as its first-publication date, so guides first published in June claimed September.

What is true: the original dates are restored, and a guide’s “Last updated” date now changes only when its content does.

11 September: Japan’s departure tax

What we said: that Japan’s departure tax is ¥1,000.

What is true: it rose to ¥3,000 per person from 1 July 2026, collected in the price of your ticket.
