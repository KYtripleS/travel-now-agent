Open data

The fact ledger behind our guides

Every price, fare, tax, opening and entry rule in our guides sits in one ledger, with the page it came from and the day we last checked it. Here it is: 3,236 facts for 45 countries and cities, free to download and reuse with a link.

Download

  • fact-ledger.csv: one row per fact, sources and pages joined with " | ".
  • fact-ledger.json: the same facts, with structured values where we hold them.
  • tourist-taxes-2026.csv: the tourist, city and hotel tax rules behind our tourist taxes data report.

Version 2026-11-20. The files are rebuilt every time we publish, so a fact we recheck or correct changes here too.

What is in it

98% of the facts cite the official body, the operator or the venue itself; the rest cite news reports or reference works, and the source kind is in every row. 1,310 facts were checked this month. Each row has:

  • claim: the fact, in the words our guides use;
  • sources: the page or pages it rests on, and what kind of source each is;
  • checked: the day we last confirmed it against that source;
  • note: what we could not confirm, or how we worked something out, when that matters;
  • pages: our guides that state it.

How we keep it current

Each fact has a recheck date. When it comes round we open the source again; if the fact has changed we fix every guide that states it and log the fix on our corrections page. How we verify explains the method.

How to cite it

Reuse it however you like, in articles, research or software, as long as you link to this page (CC BY 4.0). Suggested citation:

Gently Yonder (2026). Travel fact ledger, version 2026-11-20. https://gentlyyonder.com/open-data.html

If you find a fact that is wrong or out of date, tell us and we will correct it in public.

Limits

Facts age. Use the checked date, and confirm anything that decides a booking, a payment or an entry with the source before you rely on it. Entry rules here are preparation guidance, not legal or immigration advice.
