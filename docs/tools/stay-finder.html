How it ranks the areas

Each of our stay guides opens with a short answer: who each area suits, and why. The finder reads those lines and the hotels each guide lists, then adds points you can see:

  • +3 when a priority you picked is in the guide’s line on who the area suits, and +1 when it appears only in the reason or the area’s heading.
  • +2 when at least one of the hotels we checked in that area is in your budget tier.
  • +1 for the area the guide puts first for a first trip, when that is what you are after.

Ties keep the guide’s own order. The finder knows nothing that the guide does not say: no live prices, availability or reviews. Hotel tiers are the guide’s (splurge, comfortable, good value, budget), not a price on your dates, so check the rates for your own nights before you book.

The hotels are the guides’ researched picks, each checked as operating when the guide was last updated; no hotel pays to appear. Read how we research and what we have corrected.
