# Outreach: replace dead links to the IEC World Plugs guide

The IEC retired its World Plugs guide (iec.ch/world-plugs) in 2026. The page that
replaced it tells travellers to use adapter makers or "a trusted travel website".
Many travel blogs, study-abroad pages and visitor guides still link to it, so their
readers now land on a notice. Our hub is a full replacement: same IEC data, 216
countries, a checker, and CSV/JSON under CC BY 4.0.

Page to offer: https://gentlyyonder.com/travel-power/

This is ordinary broken-link outreach: we tell a site that a link is dead and point
to a working replacement. It helps their readers whether or not they pick ours.

## Find the pages that link to it (operator, ~10 min)
1. Bing Webmaster Tools > Backlinks > the "Similar sites" / compare tab: enter
   `https://www.iec.ch/world-plugs` to see the domains linking to it.
2. Bing and Google searches: `"iec.ch/world-plugs"`, `"world plugs" iec travel adapter`,
   `"iec.ch/world-plugs" study abroad`.
3. Keep pages that are about travel prep and still maintained (dated this year or last).
   Skip Wikipedia: editing it to add our own site is a conflict of interest. Skip forums,
   where a reply with our link reads as spam.

## Message (paste, fill the brackets, send from a Gently Yonder address, never a personal one)

Subject: Dead link on your [page topic] page

Hi [name or team],

Your page "[page title]" ([URL]) links to the IEC's World Plugs guide. The IEC retired

In case it helps, we rebuilt the same IEC data as a free table for 216 countries, with
a checker for "do I need an adapter or a converter": https://gentlyyonder.com/travel-power/
Each row notes where other references disagree, and the data is free to reuse as CSV.

Either way, the old link is worth updating.

The Gently Yonder editors

## Rules
- Up to 5 messages a week, each to a page checked by hand. No mail merge, no follow-up
  chains: at most one polite follow-up after two weeks.
- Never claim we are affiliated with the IEC, and never offer payment or a link swap.
- Log each one in `outreach-log.md`: date, site, page, sent / replied / linked.
